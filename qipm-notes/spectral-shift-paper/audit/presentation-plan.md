# Final presentation requirements

The paper should be independently readable without repository notes. Add a
clear introduction, motivation, results overview and literature comparison,
and a concise conclusion. Define every parameter before using it in the
overview. Give a roadmap connecting global unitarity to nonnegative Taylor
limits, the threshold approximation problem, and the pinned upper bound.

The central novelty is the specific unit-normalized spectral-shift problem:
explicit G_r thresholds and complete fixed-K coherent-query staircase,
including exact equality; uniform high-accuracy matching; strengthened
definite-parity comparison; and the exact-central LP access realization.
The even implicit staircase and F1=F2 plateau/degree-six witness, odd
logarithmic refinement, intermediate margin comparison, and exact fixed-error
factor-state bound were developed during manuscript preparation.

Orsucci--Dunjko Section 4.3 is an essential direct antecedent: it already
discusses normalized I-eta*A construction and generic amplification
obstructions. Do not claim discovery of the normalization issue or resolution
of its separate general sparse-value access question. Gilyen et al., Low--
Chuang, Motlagh--Wiebe, Bessen and Laneve supply prior query/synthesis methods.
Eremenko--Yuditskii sign approximation, exterior Chebyshev, Fejer--Riesz,
convex constrained approximation, and amplitude estimation are established
ingredients. Haah 2019 is useful context for synthesis vs degree; its local
full text is available. Dong et al. 2026 is a close constrained-QSP minimax
antecedent and deserves a precise comparison. Read audit/literature.md and
verify final citation metadata in primary sources as needed.

Use a qualified, exact originality statement. Distinguish evidence from a
targeted literature review from a guarantee of priority. Avoid assertions
that generic factor access, sign approximation orders, or parity removal
are new. The implicit F_r theorem does not claim closed formulas for all r.
The joint intermediate bound does not claim a fully optimal multiplicative
law: state the quantified remaining factor and the exact scope of matching.
The even degree-six witness gives an upper bound on F3(2), not its exact
value. Do not plot that witness error as an exact threshold. The F1=F2
plateau means the even class skips the exponent-3/4 tier in this range.

Include one useful publication-quality overview plot/table with reproducible
source, and possibly the existing diagnostic figure if it aids the proof
explanation. Numerical meshes are illustrations, not positivity certificates.
Use mathematical typesetting in plots. Include all required figure PDFs in
the portable LaTeX source. A short appendix may explain reproduction without
depending on this workspace's absolute paths.

Package main.tex, macros.tex, sections, bibliography.bib, main.bbl and figures
as a standalone source archive, with README and a simple build. Keep audit
reports separate from the submission archive. Do not invent author identities
or affiliations; the anonymous manuscript can leave author metadata blank.
Set PDF title/subject metadata. Check visual layout, references, text extraction,
all builds and scripts; no bibliography placeholders or unfinished prose.

All work remains in spectral-shift-paper. Source notes and other concurrent
