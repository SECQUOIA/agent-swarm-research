\section{Products: certificates, compression, and private curvature}\label{sec:products}
Write $C=\prod_{a=1}^h\B_2^{s_a}$, with $s_a\geq2$, and
$M=\prod_a\Sph^{s_a-1}$. The pure support rows of $C$ are the slacks
$1-\ip{x_a}v$, $v\in\Sph^{s_a-1}$. A pure-row certificate represents
such a slack on the entire slice of the original lift, so its pairing has
zero coefficients on the projected coordinates of the other balls. This
is stronger than an identity on a selected \emph{boundary sheet}, a set
of primal lifts of boundary points.

\subsection{The support-join identity}
Idempotents are ordered by their faces; the join $c\vee d$ is the
support of $c+d$. In a product of simple factors, joins and Jordan ranks
are taken factor by factor.
\begin{lemma}[Compression measures new support]\label{lem:join}
For an idempotent $c$, put $f=e-c$. For $y\succeq0$, let
$t=\supp P(f)y$. Then
\[
 c\vee\supp y=c+t,\qquad
 \jrank P(f)y=\jrank(c\vee\supp y)-\jrank c.
\]
For positive weights, $\supp(\sum_a\lambda_a y_a)=\bigvee_a\supp y_a$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
We have $t\leq f$. Set $g=f-t$. Self-adjointness of $P(f)$ gives
$\ip gy=\ip g{P(f)y}=0$. Positive orthogonality implies
$\supp y\perp g$, hence $c\vee\supp y\leq e-g=c+t$.
Conversely let $k=e-(c\vee\supp y)$. Then $k\leq f$ and
$\ip k{P(f)y}=\ip ky=0$, so $k\perp t$. Consequently both $c$ and $t$
belong to $V(e-k,1)$, proving the reverse inclusion. Ranks add on
orthogonal idempotents. Finally a positive element is orthogonal to a
positive sum precisely when it is orthogonal to every summand; their
complementary faces therefore agree. This proves the sum identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Additive existence of genuine certificates]\label{thm:product-existence}
For every finite exact relative-Slater affine lift of $C$ through the
symmetric-cone dictionary of Section~\ref{sec:rank}, there are directions
$u_a\in\Sph^{s_a-1}$ and genuine certificates $Y^a$ of the pure rows
$1-\ip{x_a}{u_a}$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:product-existence}
 Q:=\sum_i\jrank\left(\sum_a\lambda_aY_i^a\right)
 \geq\sum_a q_B(s_a)\qquad(\lambda_a>0).
\end{equation}
Every feasible primal tuple over the simultaneous contact
$(u_1,\ldots,u_h)\in M$ has total Jordan nullity at least $Q$. No
regularity of primal or dual selections is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the rows successively. Suppose the first $a-1$ certificates have
been chosen, and in factor $i$ let
$c_i=\bigvee_{j<a}\supp Y_i^j$, $f_i=e_i-c_i$. Fix the coordinates of
the previous balls at their chosen contacts. Their slacks vanish on the
whole remaining cylinder. Nonnegativity of each block pairing therefore puts
every feasible tuple on this cylinder in $\prod_i V_i(f_i,1)_+$.
Intersect the restricted affine slice with $\prod_i V_i(f_i,1)$;
this preserves its feasible set and its projection.
Fix all later projected coordinates, say at zero. The resulting affine
lift projects exactly onto the $a$th ball.

Compress every original pure-row certificate $Y$ by $P(f_i)$.
Its pairing with every tuple on this slice remains $1-\ip{x_a}v$.
Reduce this restricted lift further to its minimal face $V_i(k_i,1)_+$,
where $k_i\leq f_i$. The further compressed tuple $P(k_i)Y_i$ is a
certificate on the relative-Slater restricted slice. Face reduction
cannot increase the primitive capacity. By the dense-open lower bound of
Theorem~\ref{thm:rank-frontier}, there is a support $u_a$ at which every
such further compression has rank at least $q_B(s_a)$. Slater duality on the
original slice gives a certificate $Y^a$ of the original lift for this
support. Compression cannot increase rank: by
Lemma~\ref{lem:join}
its rank is the Jordan-rank increment of a support join, which is at most
the rank of the added support (equivalently use the usual face-lattice
rank inequality). Alternatively apply a spectral decomposition of $y$
and~\eqref{eq:quadratic-rank} termwise to $P(k)y$.
Thus $\sum_i\jrank P(f_i)Y_i^a\geq q_B(s_a)$.

Lemma~\ref{lem:join} identifies this sum with the increase of
$\sum_i\jrank c_i$. Summing over the rows proves~\eqref{eq:product-existence}.
Every final primal tuple is orthogonal to each chosen certificate, hence
to their positive aggregate. Complementary ranks sum to at most the
ambient rank, proving the nullity assertion.
\end{proof}

The proof compresses certificates of the original lift because a
certificate of a restricted slice need not extend to that lift. The
theorem gives only the existence of pure-row certificates with high-rank
positive aggregates. It gives no lower bound on the rank of every
certificate of the aggregate objective, or on the minimum rank in its full
fiber. Separate
copies of \eqref{eq:rank-attaining-lift} give aggregate certificates of
rank at most $\sum_aq_B(s_a)$ at every support, which confirms the scale
of \eqref{eq:product-existence} without reversing those quantifiers.

\subsection{Private directions in Hermitian factors}
For Hermitian PSD factors, a different argument gives a pointwise bound.
It counts, for each ball, the \emph{private} directions of its
certificate range: those not used by the other balls' certificates. Let
$\mathbb F\in\{\R,\C,\HH\}$, $\delta=\dim_\R\mathbb F$, and use the
real trace pairing. Suppose that $C^1$ maps satisfy the full-row
identities
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full-rows}
 1-\ip{x_a}v=\sum_i\ip{X_i(x)}{Y_i^a(v)},\qquad
 x\in M,\quad v\in\Sph^{s_a-1}.
\end{equation}
The maps $X_i$ are shared by all rows; $Y_i^a$ depends only on its own
row argument.

\begin{theorem}[Private curvature]\label{thm:private}
Suppose the factors in~\eqref{eq:full-rows} belong to
$H_+^{r_i}(\mathbb F)$, $r_i\leq R$, for an integer $R\geq2$, and put
$B=\delta(R-1)$.
At every simultaneous contact $x\in M$, with supports $v=x_a$, write
$p_i=\rank X_i$,
$U_{ia}=\operatorname{Ran}Y_i^a(x_a)$,
$U_{i,-a}=\sum_{b\ne a}U_{ib}$, and
\[
 d_{ia}=\dim_\mathbb F U_{ia}/(U_{ia}\cap U_{i,-a}).
\]
Then
\begin{align}
 s_a-1&\leq\sum_i\delta p_i d_{ia},\label{eq:private-source}\\
 \sum_a d_{ia}&\leq\rank\left(\sum_a\lambda_aY_i^a(x_a)\right)
 \leq\dim_\mathbb F\ker X_i,\qquad\lambda_a>0.
 \label{eq:private-rank}
\end{align}
In particular, both total aggregate rank and total primal nullity are at
least $\sum_a\lceil(s_a-1)/B\rceil\geq h$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Complementarity gives $X_iY_i^a(x_a)=0$. Its cylindrical form, with
$x_b$ varying and $a\ne b$, gives $(D_bX_i)Y_i^a=0$.
Decompose the underlying vector space as $\operatorname{Ran}X_i\oplus
\ker X_i$. Two-sided positivity forces the kernel--kernel compression
of $DX_i$ to vanish, as in Lemma~\ref{lem:peirce-channel}. The only
part entering the row-$a$ mixed pairing is its off-diagonal map from
$\ker X_i$ to $\operatorname{Ran}X_i$. This map annihilates $U_{i,-a}$.
The corresponding derivative of $Y_i^a$ has its other endpoint in
$U_{ia}$; the derivative pairing therefore factors through a real
space of dimension $\delta p_i d_{ia}$. Differentiating the row identity
gives the sphere metric, proving~\eqref{eq:private-source} by rank
subadditivity.

Choose a complement $E_{ia}$ of $U_{ia}\cap U_{i,-a}$ in $U_{ia}$.
These complements are jointly independent: a relation
$\sum_aw_a=0$, $w_a\in E_{ia}$, puts each $w_a$ in
$U_{ia}\cap U_{i,-a}$, hence makes it zero. Their total dimension is
therefore at most $\dim_\mathbb F\sum_aU_{ia}$, which is the aggregate
rank by positivity. This sum lies in $\ker X_i$, proving
\eqref{eq:private-rank}. When $d_{ia}>0$, $p_i\leq R-1$.
Thus~\eqref{eq:private-source} gives
$\sum_i d_{ia}\geq\lceil(s_a-1)/B\rceil$. Sum this inequality in $a$.
\end{proof}

Inequality~\eqref{eq:private-source}, stated for each ball or
\emph{source}, says more than a bound on total rank: it records which
source uses each available kernel direction. It requires
differentiability only at the contact under consideration. Definable
selections of an affine lift are differentiable at generic contacts, but
not necessarily at all of them.

\begin{example}[One factor can serve every source]\label{ex:packing}
Let $p=\max_a\lceil s_a/\delta\rceil$, and embed each $\R^{s_a}$
isometrically into $\mathbb F^p$. Write their images as columns $w_a$
of $W$. The single-factor lift
\begin{equation}\label{eq:column-packing}
 Z=\begin{pmatrix}S&W^*\\W&I_p\end{pmatrix}\succeq0,
 \qquad S\in H^h(\mathbb F),\quad S_{aa}=1
\end{equation}
projects exactly onto $C$. Indeed its Schur complement
$D=S-W^*W$ is positive, giving $\norm{w_a}\leq1$; conversely choose
$S=W^*W+\operatorname{Diag}(1-\norm{w_a}^2)$.
At an extreme point of the product, $D$ has zero diagonal, hence is
zero, so $S=W^*W$. This boundary sheet is polynomial and has nullity
exactly $h$.
For the embedded support $\widetilde v$, put
\[
 Y^a(v)=\tfrac12(e_a,-\widetilde v)(e_a,-\widetilde v)^*.
\]
On the entire affine slice, its pairing with $Z$ is
$\tfrac12(1+\norm{\widetilde v}^2-2\operatorname{Re}(w_a^*\widetilde v))
=1-\ip{x_a}v$. These are genuine rank-one row certificates. Their ranges
are independent because their first $h$ coordinates are the distinct
$e_a$. Thus total aggregate rank and nullity are both exactly $h$,
although the lift has only one cone factor.
\end{example}
