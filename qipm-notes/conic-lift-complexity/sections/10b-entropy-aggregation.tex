\section{Exponential factors and entropy aggregation}
\label{sec:entropy-aggregation}

Changing the ambient cone can lower a barrier parameter even when
coupling the factors of a fixed product cannot.  We compute the exact
optimal parameters for products of exponential cones and for
relative-entropy cones with a nonnegative output map.  The barriers and
lower-bound method are special cases of \citet{FS2023}; we give the
explicit certificates and closure arguments needed to compare
formulations, and claim no new general theorem on optimal barriers.

\subsection{A recession lower bound for products}

For a closed full-dimensional convex set $C$, let $\mathfrak n(C)$ be the
supremum of $\sum_j a_j/b_j$ over interior points $x$, recession directions
$p_j$, and positive numbers $a_j,b_j$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropy-certificate}
 x-\sum_j a_jp_j\in C,\qquad x-b_jp_j\notin\intr C.
\end{equation}
We call such data a recession certificate with value $\sum_j a_j/b_j$,
coefficients $a_j$, and thresholds $b_j$.
Nesterov's recession theorem, in the form
\citep[Theorem 3.9]{FS2023}, gives $\nu\geq\mathfrak n(C)$ for every
self-concordant barrier on $C$, including nonhomogeneous barriers.
Certificates for separate factors combine by padding each recession
direction with zeros; both conditions in \eqref{eq:entropy-certificate}
still hold, so
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropy-tensorization}
 \mathfrak n(C_1\times\cdots\times C_k)
 \geq\sum_j\mathfrak n(C_j).
\end{equation}
This bound applies to every barrier on the product, including coupled
ones; the sum rule for barriers gives only an upper bound.

\begin{lemma}[Orthant-input hypographs]\label{lem:orthant-hypograph}
Let $n,B\geq1$ be integers and $h:\R_{++}^n\to\R^B$ be componentwise concave and homogeneous of degree
one.  For
\[
 H_h=\overline{\{(x,z):x>0,\ z\leq h(x)\}},
\]
every self-concordant barrier on $H_h$ has parameter at least $n+B$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
This is the orthant-output specialization of
\citet[Theorem 3.10]{FS2023}.  We give the certificate explicitly.
For $0<\epsilon<1$, set $v_i=\epsilon\mathbf1+(1-\epsilon)e_i$ and
$x_0=\sum_i v_i=b\mathbf1$, where $b=1+(n-1)\epsilon$.
Concavity and homogeneity imply
$d=h(x_0)-\sum_i h(v_i)\geq0$ componentwise.
Choose $T'>\max_j d_j$ and $T>T'$.  At
$y_0=(x_0,h(x_0)-T\mathbf1)$ use recession directions
$(v_i,h(v_i))$ with coefficients $1$ and thresholds $b$, and
$(0,-Te_j)$ with coefficients $1-T'/T$ and thresholds $1$.
Subtracting these directions, scaled by their coefficients, leaves
$(0,d-T'\mathbf1)\in H_h$.
Each input threshold makes an input coordinate zero; each output
threshold makes an output inequality tight.  Hence each threshold point
is noninterior.  The certificate has value
$n/b+B(1-T'/T)$.  First let $T\to\infty$, then $\epsilon\downarrow0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact exponential-product parameter]\label{thm:entropy-product}
Let
\[
 K_{\exp}=\overline{\{(u,v,w):v>0,\ w\geq ve^{u/v}\}}.
\]
The smallest parameter of any self-concordant barrier on a product of
$R$ primal or dual exponential cones and $P$ rays is $3R+P$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write the primal cone as the hypograph of $h(v,w)=v\log(w/v)$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:orthant-hypograph} gives certificates with values
tending to three.
The compatible barrier
\[
 -\log\bigl(v\log(w/v)-u\bigr)-\log v-\log w
\]
has parameter three \citep{FS2023}.  A ray has a certificate of value one
and barrier $-\log t$.  Equation~\eqref{eq:entropy-tensorization} and the
sum of these barriers give the product assertion.
The map $(a,b,c)\mapsto(a-b,-a,c)$ is a linear isomorphism from
$K_{\exp}^*$ onto $K_{\exp}$: its interior formula is
$a<0$, $c>-a\exp(b/a-1)$, and its closure face $a=0$, $b,c\geq0$
maps to the primal face $v=0$, $u\leq0$, $w\geq0$.
\end{proof}

The same argument gives the optimal parameter $\sum_j(n_j+1)$ for a
product of scalar
hypographs of homogeneous concave $h_j:\R_{++}^{n_j}\to\R$ whenever
the compatible barriers
$-\log(h_j(x)-z)-\sum_i\log x_i$ exist.  This includes the usual
one-sided geometric-mean hypographs.  To check this directly, let $g(x)=\prod_i x_i^{\alpha_i}$, $\alpha_i>0$, $\sum_i\alpha_i=1$.
For a direction put $u_i=h_i/x_i$ and $\bar u=\sum_i\alpha_i u_i$.
Then $-D^2g=g\sum_i\alpha_i(u_i-\bar u)^2$ and
$D^3g=g\sum_i\alpha_i(\bar u+2u_i)(u_i-\bar u)^2$.
Hence $|D^3g|\leq3\norm u_2(-D^2g)$, the compatibility inequality
with the orthant barrier.  The argument needs the output recession
direction, so it gives no exact parameter for an absolute power
cone.  For matrix relative entropy, the published optimal parameter
$2n+1$ also adds over products; restricting the inputs to diagonal matrices
preserves the recession certificates and yields $\sum_j(2n_j+1)$ for a
product of these matrix epigraphs \citep{FS2023}.

\subsection{Positive output maps and their boundary fibers}

Put $\phi_i(x_i,y_i)=x_i\log(x_i/y_i)$ for positive inputs.  Let $N,B$
be positive integers and $A\in\R_+^{B\times N}$.  The cone

\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropy-aggregate-cone}
 C_A=\overline{\{(x,y,z):x,y>0,\ z\geq A\phi(x,y)\}}.
\end{equation}
keeps all $N$ input pairs and all $B$ output coordinates as independent
variables.  Zero or repeated rows and zero columns are permitted.  We
call $C_A$ the intrinsic cone.

\begin{theorem}[Exact intrinsic aggregation parameter]\label{thm:entropy-aggregation}
The optimal barrier parameter of $C_A$ is $2N+B$, attained by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropy-aggregate-barrier}
 F_A=-\sum_{b=1}^B\log s_b-\sum_{i=1}^N(\log x_i+\log y_i),
 \qquad s=z-A\phi(x,y)>0.
\end{equation}
Let $J=\{i:A_{:i}\ne0\}$ (the active indices) and $n_+=|J|$.  An exact
closed affine lift of $C_A$ uses $n_+$ exponential cones, $2(N-n_+)$ input rays,
and $B$ output rays.
The optimal parameter of this ambient product is $2N+n_++B$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
We first verify the compatibility inequality behind the classical
barrier construction.
For one pair and a direction $(a,b)$ write $u=a/x$, $v=b/y$.
Direct differentiation gives
\[
 D^2\phi=x(u-v)^2,\qquad
 D^3\phi=-x(u-v)^2(u+2v).
\]
Therefore $|D^3\phi|\leq3\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\,D^2\phi$.
The common orthant barrier has second derivative
$\sum_i(u_i^2+v_i^2)$, so multiplication by a nonnegative row of $A$
preserves the compatibility inequality.  Nesterov's compatible
hypograph theorem \citep[Theorem 3.3]{FS2023}, applied to the diagonal
output matrix, proves self-concordance of \eqref{eq:entropy-aggregate-barrier}.
Its homogeneity degree is $2N+B$; its Hessian, given below, is
positive definite.  Lemma~\ref{lem:orthant-hypograph} with
$h(x,y)=-A\phi(x,y)$ proves the matching lower bound.

For the exact lift use $q_i\geq\phi_i(x_i,y_i)$ for $i\in J$,
$(x_i,y_i)\geq0$ for $i\notin J$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropy-exact-lift}
 z=A_{:J}q+r,\qquad r\geq0.
\end{equation}
At the boundary, extend each active scalar entropy by
$\bar\phi(0,y)=0$ for $y\geq0$, and
$\bar\phi(x,0)=+\infty$ for $x>0$.
The elementary bound $\phi(x,y)\geq-y/e$ shows that these terms are
uniformly bounded below along convergent input sequences.
If a point belongs to \eqref{eq:entropy-aggregate-cone}, choose an interior
approximating sequence.  Every active $i$ has some $A_{bi}>0$; boundedness
of $z_b$ and the lower bounds on all other terms give an upper bound on
$\phi_i$.  Thus $x_i>0,y_i=0$ is impossible for active $i$.
Lower semicontinuity then gives $z\geq A_{:J}\bar\phi_J$.
Conversely, every such inequality is approximated by positive inputs:
replace each zero coordinate by $\epsilon$ and shift the output by the
resulting change in the active entropies, which vanishes because they
converge to their finite extended values.  Choosing $q=\bar\phi_J$ and
$r=z-A_{:J}q$ gives every point of $C_A$ a preimage with finite $q$ in
\eqref{eq:entropy-exact-lift}, so the lift is exact.  The final parameter
follows from Theorem~\ref{thm:entropy-product}.
\end{proof}

Exactness requires rays for zero columns.  With
$N=B=1$ and
$A=0$, $C_A=\R_+^3$ includes $(x,y,z)=(1,0,0)$; a finite exponential
constraint $q\geq\phi(1,0)$ cannot represent this point.  Keeping an
unused exponential cone gives only a nonclosed image with the right
closure.  Replacing it by two rays restores exactness.

For a partition of $N$ terms into $B$ nonempty blocks, one may instead
sum the local epigraph coordinates within each block, without separate
output rays.  The ambient product then has optimal parameter $3N$,
and $C_A$ has $2N+B$, a difference of $N-B$.  This lift is onto $C_A$,
including its boundary, because a nonnegative residual in each block
output can be absorbed into one local epigraph coordinate.  For a
general positive map, passing from the lift
\eqref{eq:entropy-exact-lift} to the intrinsic cone lowers the
parameter by $n_+$, and the ratio of the usual short-step iteration
bounds is
\[
 \sqrt{\frac{2N+n_++B}{2N+B}}<\sqrt{3/2}.
\]
This compares parameter-based upper bounds, not actual iteration counts.

\subsection{A sparse intrinsic Hessian}

Let $p=(x_1,y_1,\ldots,x_N,y_N)$, let $J_A$ denote the Jacobian of
$A\phi$, and put $R=\operatorname{Diag}(s_b^{-1})$.  Differentiation of
\eqref{eq:entropy-aggregate-barrier} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entropy-hessian}
 \nabla^2F_A=
 \begin{pmatrix}I&-J_A^T\\0&I\end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}G&0\\0&R^2\end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0\\-J_A&I\end{pmatrix},
\end{equation}
where $G$ has independent positive-definite $2\times2$ blocks
\[
 G_i=\operatorname{Diag}(x_i^{-2},y_i^{-2})+
 \left(\sum_b A_{bi}/s_b\right)
 \begin{pmatrix}x_i^{-1}&-y_i^{-1}\\-y_i^{-1}&x_i/y_i^2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The second summand is positive semidefinite.  Assembling $G$ requires the
product $A^T(s_b^{-1})_{b=1}^B$.  After assembly, each solve with
\eqref{eq:entropy-hessian} uses triangular substitution, two products by
$A$ or $A^T$, diagonal scaling, and $N$ two-dimensional solves.  Given
the point and slacks, assembly and one solve take
$O(N+B+\operatorname{nnz}A)$ arithmetic operations.  This is a solve
with the cone-barrier Hessian only; affine constraints add a separate
Schur complement system.
Sparse and low-rank Hessian formulas for ordinary relative entropy are
due to \citet{ChenGoulart2025}.  \citet{HeSaundersonFawzi2026} tailor
entropy cones to positive input maps, including maps with singular
images, and remove redundant determinant terms.  Here the explicit
output map records which coordinates remain independent, and the closure
analysis shows when the elementary lift is exact.
