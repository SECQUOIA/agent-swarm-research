\section{Rigidity from entire boundary fibers}\label{sec:full-fiber-rigidity}

Global regularity of a chosen contact sheet is a substantive hypothesis.
There is, however, a different route to a global support map: constant
nullity on every point of every boundary fiber makes that map intrinsic to
the projection. This section gives the resulting product rigidity theorem.
The hypothesis concerns the whole fiber, including points that no chosen
regular sheet reaches.

\begin{theorem}[Constant-nullity rigidity]\label{thm:full-fiber-rigidity}
Let $C=\prod_{a=1}^h\B^{s_a}$, where $s_a\geq2$, and put
$M=\prod_a\Sph^{p_a}$, $p_a=s_a-1$, and $n=\sum_a p_a$.
Consider an exact semialgebraic affine lift in relative-Slater form,
\[
 C=\pi(\cL\cap K),\qquad K=\prod_iK_i,
\]
where the $K_i$ are simple symmetric cones, rays are allowed, and
$\dim K_i\leq d$ with $d\geq3$. Free variables have been eliminated as
in Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction}. Suppose the full preimage
\[
 E=\{X\in\cL\cap K:\pi(X)\in M\}
\]
is compact. If an integer $L\geq1$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full-fiber-hypothesis}
 n=L(d-2),\qquad
 Z(X):=\sum_i\bigl(r_i-\jrank X_i\bigr)=L\quad(X\in E),
\end{equation}
then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full-fiber-profile}
 \{p_a:1\leq a\leq h\}
 =\{\underbrace{d-2,\ldots,d-2}_{L\text{ copies}}\}
\end{equation}
as multisets. Exactly $L$ cone factors have positive nullity on $E$;
each is $Q_d$ and has nullity one. All other factors are interior on $E$
and contribute zero to every genuine supporting row there.
The profile~\eqref{eq:full-fiber-profile} is attained by the separate
direct Lorentz lift.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Projection $E\to M$ is a continuous surjection with convex, hence
connected, fibers. It follows that $E$ is connected. Indeed, a separation
of the compact set $E$ into two nonempty clopen subsets would give two
disjoint compact images covering $M$: a connected fiber must lie on one
side of the separation. This contradicts connectedness of $M$.

Each nullity $z_i(X)=r_i-\jrank X_i$ is upper semicontinuous and
integer-valued. Near any $X\in E$, all $z_i$ are at most their values at
$X$. Their constant sum forces equality term by term. Thus each $z_i$
is locally constant, and connectedness makes it constant on $E$.
If $X,X'$ belong to one fiber, their midpoint belongs to the same fiber.
In a symmetric cone the support of a positive sum is the join of the
supports. Equality of the three ranks therefore implies
\[
 \supp X_i=\supp X'_i\qquad\text{for every }i.
\]
The support map is continuous on a fixed-rank stratum. Since a continuous
surjection from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is a quotient map,
these support maps descend to continuous maps on $M$.

Next choose primal and genuine pure-row dual selections on a common
dense open semialgebraic contact set; Lemma~\ref{lem:selection} supplies
such selections with locally constant ranks. Summing the pure rows gives
the joint kernel $\sum_a(1-\ip{x_a}{v_a})$, whose mixed contact form is
the product metric of rank $n$. Write $u_i$ and $v_i$ for the primal and
aggregate dual Jordan ranks at one such contact. The Peirce bound gives
\begin{align}
 n&\leq\sum_{i:r_i\geq2}a_i u_i v_i
 \leq\sum_{i:r_i\geq2}a_i(r_i-1)z_i
 \leq\sum_{i:r_i\geq2}(m_i-2)z_i\notag\\
 &\leq(d-2)\sum_{i:r_i\geq2}z_i
 \leq(d-2)Z=L(d-2)=n,\label{eq:full-fiber-chain}
\end{align}
where $m_i=\dim K_i$. For the second inequality, complementarity gives
$v_i\leq z_i$ and a nonzero primal rank is at most $r_i-1$ whenever
$z_i>0$; if the primal vanishes, its mixed contribution is zero.
The third inequality follows from
\[
 m_i-2-a_i(r_i-1)
 =(r_i-2)\left(1+\frac{a_i(r_i-1)}2\right).
\]
Equality throughout~\eqref{eq:full-fiber-chain} implies that rays have
zero nullity and every positive-nullity non-ray factor has rank two,
dimension $d$, and primal and dual ranks one. Consequently $z_i=1$
in exactly $L$ factors. Constancy of each $z_i$ extends this conclusion
to all of $E$. The other factors have interior primal coordinates;
complementarity forces their dual coordinate to vanish for every genuine
pure supporting row at every point of $M$.

The descended supports in the active Lorentz factors define
\[
 \Sigma:M\longrightarrow(\Sph^{d-2})^L.
\]
This map is injective. If $\Sigma(x)=\Sigma(x')$, take any genuine
certificate for the row $1-\ip{\pi_a(X)}{x_a}$ and evaluate it at any
lift over $x'$. Equal active support rays make every active pairing zero,
and all other dual coordinates are zero. Hence
$1-\ip{x'_a}{x_a}=0$ for every $a$, so $x'=x$.
Source and target have equal dimension. Invariance of domain makes the
image open, and compactness makes it closed. The target is connected;
thus $\Sigma$ is a homeomorphism. In the mod-two cohomology ring of a
product of positive-dimensional spheres, the quotient of the
positive-degree ideal by its square has one generator in each sphere
dimension, with multiplicity. A graded ring isomorphism therefore gives
\eqref{eq:full-fiber-profile}, including any degree-one generators.
Conversely, under that profile the lift
$(1,x_a)\in Q_d$ has compact singleton fibers and one unit of nullity
per source, as asserted.
\end{proof}

For a single $s$-dimensional ball with $d<s+1$ and
$s-1=L(d-2)$, Theorem~\ref{thm:full-fiber-rigidity} is an impossibility
statement. For products it is a sharp classification: equality permits
only the profile of separate direct factors. This conclusion requires
neither a continuous chosen primal lift nor a continuous chosen dual
certificate.

\begin{definition}[Accessibility by regular contacts]
A point $X\in E$ is contact-accessible if there are contact points
$x^{(j)}\to\pi(X)$ and local $C^1$ primal and genuine pure-row dual
contact selections near $x^{(j)}$, on constant-rank strata, whose primal
values $X^{(j)}$ satisfy $X^{(j)}\to X$.
\end{definition}

\begin{corollary}[Accessible equality cases]\label{cor:accessible-fibers}
Retain the lift and compactness assumptions of
Theorem~\ref{thm:full-fiber-rigidity} and suppose $n=L(d-2)$.
If every $X\in E$ is contact-accessible and the restricted standard
barrier satisfies $\nustd\leq L$, then the profile
\eqref{eq:full-fiber-profile} is necessary.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:boundary-order} gives $Z(X)\leq\nustd\leq L$ at
every $X\in E$. At each approximating regular contact the curvature
chain~\eqref{eq:full-fiber-chain}, without its final equalities, gives
$Z(X^{(j)})\geq L$. Upper semicontinuity then yields
$Z(X)\geq\limsup_j Z(X^{(j)})\geq L$. Thus $Z=L$ throughout $E$, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:full-fiber-rigidity} applies.
\end{proof}

A standard-barrier bound alone supplies the upper bound on nullity,
not accessibility or the reverse inequality at projection-singular
fiber points. A single global section likewise controls only its own
image. The distinction is essential: the unrestricted barrier brackets
in Section~\ref{sec:restricted} are not closed by this corollary.
