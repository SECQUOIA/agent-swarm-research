## Main mathematical checks

- **Whole rows and minimum dimension.** The contact-cylinder function
  rank is `1 + dim(F_a) + sum_{b != a} n_b`. Arbitrary boundary polar
  normals decompose into extreme polar normals, so their contact faces
  cannot exceed the chosen maximum. This justifies the exact face size of
  the shared homogenization. The equality proof for arbitrary full slack
  factorizations uses spanning on both sides and dualizes the second cone
  inclusion in the correct direction. The finite relatively closed cover
  of a connected extreme set suffices for the normalized-base bound even
  if the extreme set is not closed. The recession-level proof correctly
  chooses a level meeting the original relative interior before restricting
  the barrier. Its closedness and line-free assumptions are substantive
  and are retained. The simple-vertex lower bound is not extrapolated to
  nonsimple vertices.

- **Face sharing.** Cross-source differentiation holds with only first
  derivatives, because the fixed-source dual derivative vanishes on the
  whole contact cylinder. The rank-detecting subspaces and the primal ray
  form a direct sum. The global increment uses compact exact-active-label
  pieces and proper normalized-phase neighborhoods, not pointwise counting
  alone. The monotonicity of `2L + max(R0,kp-L,L)` is correct. The ambient
  lower bound comes from a boundary-inheriting orthant section and applies
  to coupled barriers. The shared-square cone's dual description includes
  the strict-support case and gives maximal face dimension `2q-1` even
  when `q=1`. Its star-completion barrier is consistent with 09e.

- **All-EJA balance slices.** A rank-two simple-cone face has dimension
  at least three, so two affine equations leave exactly the stated one-
  and two-block extreme points. Opposite strict moment signs are necessary
  for two positive weights. The cross-factor path construction handles
  the disconnected two-point zero set in the real rank-two/spin case.
  The Albert matrix uses only the associative subalgebra generated by its
  two octonion coordinates, so the idempotence check is valid; it does not
  assume arbitrary octonionic associativity. In the first-coordinate
  chart, zero height is `S^7 x R^8`; outside that chart positivity leaves
  only `c3`. Sending the second affine coordinate to infinity gives the
  stated continuous limiting paths, including approach from either strict
  sign region. The alternate moment has signature `(1,r-1)` and is treated
  separately rather than being declared equivalent to the Peirce moment.

  Every vanished diagonal coordinate gives a nonconstant supporting
  functional, so the orthant and simplex restrictions inherit boundary
  divergence. The Jordan Hessian identity and projection off the trace
  normal give precisely `rho - 1/sum tr(X_i^2) <= rho-1`; imposing the
  balance tangent can only decrease this dual norm. The lower sections
  apply to arbitrary coupled barriers. The sharp-cap argument correctly
  uses all full slack rows, not a whole-row grouping assumption. The
  matched-dimension comparison explicitly concerns different bodies.

  I independently recalculated the rejected logarithmic candidate: the
  `-log(q2)` derivatives are `10,52`, and those of `log(q1+q2)` are
  `-27/4,-27`, yielding `13/4,25`; thus the self-concordance inequality
  fails strictly.

- **Lp and power cones.** The positive-curvature patch is restricted to
  nonzero coordinates, where the Hessian is positive definite. The chain
  construction uses exactly the stated number of leaves and dimensions;
  zero-coordinate padding preserves full Slater faces. The half-cone
  counterexample therefore disproves global cone rigidity without
  invalidating local equality. The global proof differentiates the primal
  and polar manifolds independently and does not assume a C1 polarity map
  at coordinate zeros. The Young factors are genuine certificates on the
  whole affine slice. In the two-dimensional exception, the projective
  non-C2 count distinguishes a non-Lorentz power cone, and the vanishing
  second fundamental form distinguishes the remaining Lorentz case for
  `p>2`. The generalized power-cone compact section has a positive second
  fundamental form of rank `m+k-2`; the weighted-variance and spherical
  terms have exactly the stated simultaneous kernel. No denominator or
  unrestricted scalar priority claim is inferred.

- **Spectral and completion cones.** The mixed spectral rank counts the
  relative-phase `delta-1` directions in addition to the off-axis and tail
  directions, giving `delta*c-1`, including quaternionic matrices. The
  real/complex whole-row parameter result is not silently extended to
  quaternionic spectral barriers. In the shared spectral lower bound,
  the displayed positive recession coefficients sum to the chosen interior
  point, and each backward step is a boundary point. Their product
  placement is a single valid certificate for a coupled competitor.
  On the fixed-scale slice, the invariant real diagonal Hessian block
  yields the asserted gradient norm, while the cube supplies the matching
  arbitrary-barrier lower bound. Maximum-determinant completion exists
  because the diagonal bounds compactify the completion set; the inverse
  is sparse at its stationary point. The signed Legendre formula has the
  correct `-n` additive constant. The block-star dual rank-one factor is
  phase invariant and represents every normalized nuclear-polar row.
  The reduced Newton systems agree only after the stated coordinates and
  affine data are identified; no ambient oracle-cost equality is claimed.
