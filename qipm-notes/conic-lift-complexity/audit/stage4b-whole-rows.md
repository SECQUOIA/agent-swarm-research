## Reconstruction and scope

The section independently derives the following results, without assuming the workbench's audit conclusions:

- Arbitrary-body whole-row bounds `m >= 1 + sum n_a` and `f(K) >= 1 + sum n_a - min lambda_a`, from affine function rank and rank on a contact cylinder. No regularity is needed. The exact shared homogenization face computation proves simultaneous attainment.
- Exact dimension-and-face capped grouping, including the identical-body formula and heterogeneous partition characterization. The model assigns each full source row family to one block; it does not assume that a generic split-row factorization satisfies these bounds.
- Strong NP-completeness of the integer grouping decision problem, by a complete reduction from 3-PARTITION with `F=D-1`. Explicit ball descriptions also have polynomial size on strongly bounded instances. This is a standard packing reduction, not a claimed new complexity mechanism.
- Minimum-dimension rigidity in both exact-lift and arbitrary-full-slack-factorization forms. The proof for affine outputs is careful: the dimension count excludes an affine slice through zero, after which an affine output extends linearly from the nonzero hyperplane. No incorrect globally linear extension is assumed in advance.
- Connected extreme sets exclude multifactor equality at total dimension `N+1`, giving only `M>=N+2` under a strict factor cap. No additive tax for arbitrary affine slices is claimed.
- The unique positive result for a projection of one normalized product-cone base: `M>=N+L`, proved by a finite relatively closed cover of the possibly nonclosed extreme-point set and its connected intersection graph.
- A complete recession-height normalization criterion. Strict positivity of a functional on the pointed recession cone makes every sublevel compact; fiber minima are therefore attained. A fixed recession ray raises each fiber minimum to any greater height. A level above one relative-interior point meets the relative interior, so restriction preserves a given barrier before the existing bounded-fiber marginal theorem is used.
- Transfer of intrinsic barrier lower bounds through arbitrary closed line-free lifts of polytopes. Finitely many chosen vertex lifts give the needed uniform height bound. The exact value `N` is asserted only for a polytope with a simple vertex, with classical lower and upper endpoints. Independent normals at a nonsimple vertex are explicitly insufficient.
- A stronger concrete replacement for the source's long normalization counterexample: the already reviewed escaping PSD disk has recession exactly the positive last-coordinate ray. Any affine restriction eliminating it is bounded and cannot contain lifts of the escaping boundary sequence. This gives the failure of projection-preserving affine normalization over three 2-by-2 PSD factors with Slater feasibility. It does not claim failure of optimal-barrier monotonicity by another method.
