# Stage 2 corrections

The separate correction author read the root assessment and all five independent reports. All seven accepted consolidated corrections have been implemented. No future-stage material or files outside `conic-lift-complexity/` were changed.

1. **Whole-slice validity after compression.** In the proof of Theorem `product-existence`, the restricted affine slice is explicitly intersected with the product of complementary-face spans before certificates are compressed. Cylindrical complementarity puts all its feasible tuples in those spans, so the intersection preserves feasibility and the exact projection. Pairing with quadratic compression then agrees on every tuple of the resulting affine slice, including infeasible tuples.
2. **Rank terminology.** The same proof now calls the support-join increment a Jordan-rank increment, not a dimension increment.
3. **Positive-capacity indices.** The global-saturation proof restricts its balanced-rank conclusion and support-differential argument to positive-capacity factors. Zero-capacity factors have zero mixed form; their individual primal and dual ranks are not required to sum to their ambient rank.
4. **Integer caps.** The smooth Lorentz theorem and everywhere-differentiable proposition explicitly require an integer capacity cap. Both all-symmetric dimension-cap statements explicitly require an integer dimension cap.
5. **Nontrivial private-curvature cap.** The private-curvature theorem specifies an integer order cap `R >= 2`, so its denominator `B = delta (R - 1)` is positive.
6. **Nonsmooth comparison scope.** The exact nonsmooth resource comparison explicitly ranges over finite exact relative-Slater affine lifts. It explains the common generic regular contact by intersecting the primal regular locus with inverse images of each dual regular locus. It does not claim the same argument for arbitrary unstructured maps.
7. **Exact PSD2 pure-row frontier and source accounting.** Immediately after Example `tree-regularity`, a coefficient-matching argument proves uniqueness of every genuine tree support certificate. The root head is one, each leaf coordinate is the negative support coordinate, and each internal-child coordinate is the negative child head. Induction up the tree bounds each head below by its subtree norm. Equality at the unit root propagates down through sums of squares, including zero subtrees. Corollary `psd2-worst-pure-row` states and proves the exact infimum over lifts, supremum over simultaneous supports, and supremum over genuine pure-row certificates. The lower bound uses the already proved selection-free product theorem at `B = 1`. For the independent-tree upper construction, evaluating a pure-row zero slack at a contact of its source and strict interior points of all other source trees makes the other-source dual blocks zero; uniqueness on the remaining tree proves the upper bound for every pure-row certificate choice. Generic supports attain it. The text explicitly distinguishes this supremum from a minimum over the full aggregate-objective certificate fiber. The PSD2 source-map entry now records this complete result. The entry for `joint-saturated-block-covering-rigidity` now records partial inclusion and defers its arbitrary-proper-cone/arbitrary-contact-manifold theorem to Stage 4.
