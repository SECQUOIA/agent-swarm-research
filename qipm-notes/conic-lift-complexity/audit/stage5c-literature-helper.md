| `Scheiderer2025SOC` | Claus Scheiderer, *Smooth Hyperbolicity Cones Are Second-Order Cone Representable*, arXiv:2509.17121v2, 5 October 2025. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17121v2) and [Theorems 1.1–1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.17121v2) checked. Theorem 1.2 gives a lift with blocks of order at most two for compact convex semialgebraic sets whose boundary is Nash-smooth and everywhere strictly positively curved. This existence result does not state the sharp factor count or support-certificate minimax considered here. Positive curvature must not be presented as an obstruction to SOC representability. |
| `FGPPST2022Survey` | Hamza Fawzi, João Gouveia, Pablo A. Parrilo, James Saunderson, and Rekha R. Thomas, *Lifting for Simplicity: Concise Descriptions of Convex Sets*, SIAM Review 64(4) (2022), 866–918, DOI 10.1137/20M1324417. [Publisher metadata and abstract](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/20M1324417) checked. General reference on lift constructions, slack factorizations, and facial and algebraic obstructions. Retain primary theorem citations for specific results. The requested key is used literally despite its unusual initials. |
| `Averkov2019` | Gennadiy Averkov, *Optimal Size of Linear Matrix Inequalities in Semidefinite Approaches to Polynomial Optimization*, SIAM Journal on Applied Algebra and Geometry 3(1) (2019), 128–151, DOI 10.1137/18M1201342. [Publisher metadata and abstract](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/18M1201342) checked. Gives lower bounds on maximal PSD block size even when arbitrarily many finite blocks are allowed, including optimality of the standard SOS formulation. This is an extension-degree obstruction, distinct from counting blocks when the permitted dictionary admits a lift. |
| `Saunderson2020` | James Saunderson, *Limitations on the Expressive Power of Convex Cones without Long Chains of Faces*, SIAM Journal on Optimization 30(1) (2020), 1033–1047, DOI 10.1137/19M1245670. [Publisher metadata and abstract](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/19M1245670) checked. Neighborliness rules out finite product lifts over cones with sufficiently short face chains; this extends the PSD block obstruction. Its nonexistence statements must be distinguished from local quantitative resource bounds for representable bodies. |
| `Kummer2016` | Mario Kummer, *Two Results on the Size of Spectrahedral Descriptions*, SIAM Journal on Optimization 26(1) (2016), 589–601, DOI 10.1137/15M1030789. [Publisher metadata and abstract](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/15M1030789) checked. The ball lower bounds concern direct real LMI descriptions. They cannot be identified with projected lifts over products of cones. |
| `Hildebrand2022Projective` | Roland Hildebrand, *Projectively Self-Concordant Barriers*, Mathematics of Operations Research 47(3) (2022), 2444–2463, DOI 10.1287/moor.2021.1215. [Publisher metadata and abstract](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/moor.2021.1215) and [arXiv version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.01883v2) checked. Projective self-concordance corresponds to affine self-concordance of a logarithmically homogeneous conic extension. Root's recorded full-text checks identify Theorem 1.5 and Sections 6–7 for restriction and projective operations. The ambient/restricted distinction is established context; the specific exact restricted parameters are the relevant comparison. No numerical projective parameter formula is inferred from OCR. |
| `CardosoVieira2006` | Domingos Moreira Cardoso and Luís Almeida Vieira, *On the Optimal Parameter of a Self-Concordant Barrier over a Symmetric Cone*, European Journal of Operational Research 169(3) (2006), 1148–1157, DOI 10.1016/j.ejor.2004.11.027. [Publisher metadata and introduction](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0377221705002791) and [author-deposited preprint](https://optimization-online.org/2003/11/774/) checked. Gives the symmetric-cone optimal barrier parameter through Jordan rank and the Carathéodory number. This ambient theorem does not compute the parameter after an arbitrary affine restriction. |
| `MohammadisiahroudiEtAl2026` | Mohammadhossein Mohammadisiahroudi, Brandon Augustino, Pouya Sampourmahani, and Tamás Terlaky, *Quantum Computing Inspired Iterative Refinement for Semidefinite Optimization*, Mathematical Programming 217 (2026), 3–42, DOI 10.1007/s10107-024-02183-z. [Publisher metadata, abstract, and introduction](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10107-024-02183-z) checked. First online 11 January 2025; the journal citation year is 2026. Iterative refinement improves high-precision QIPM complexity. Avoid treating inverse-accuracy dependencies of earlier methods as universal current limitations. The manuscript's matched-access and output-contract claims require their own hypotheses. |
