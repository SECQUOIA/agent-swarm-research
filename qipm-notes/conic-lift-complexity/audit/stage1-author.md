## Independent mathematical reconstruction

The key exceptional-cone step was reconstructed without octonionic matrix
multiplication. For primitive `c` and `w` in its half-Peirce space,
`w² = t c + P(w)c`, where `t=||w||²/2`. Positivity and rank at most one of
`P(w)c` follow by approximating `w` by invertible elements and using quadratic
representation automorphisms. Its trace is `t`, so `P(w)c=t k` for a primitive
orthogonal idempotent. Spectral support then places `w` in the rank-two
subalgebra with identity `c+k`. This proves the determinant and positivity
formulas in every simple EJA, including Albert. The rank-one dual completion
was expanded directly from `P(c-g/(sqrt(2)A))c`.

The mixed-channel proof does not need locally constant ranks: two-sided
positivity kills the derivative on the primal kernel subalgebra, and likewise
for the dual. Their only common derivative channels are cross-Peirce spaces.
Differentiated Jordan complementarity makes the mixed form a Gram form.
The minimum-rank selector is used only to extend its rank lower bound to every
member of the entire dual fiber at generic supports.

## Errors and corrections

No error invalidating the Stage 1 headline was found. The following traps were
corrected or explicitly excluded:

1. The formula cannot include `s=1`: its formal ceiling is zero but every
   certificate is nonzero. The text gives a separate interval construction.
   Root caught an initial draft phrase claiming an interval section of a
   primitive face; a ray cannot give that section. Corrected to the diagonal
   section generated by two orthogonal primitive idempotents, with certificates
   `2c` and `2k`.
2. A rank-one favorable dual completion does not imply every certificate has
   rank one. Zero support groups and restricted half-Peirce coordinate groups
   leave additional certificates. The proof uses tail trace only to force each
   block nonzero and constructs one rank-one completion.
3. Original non-Slater ambient certificates need not be positive or attained.
   All certificate fibers in the manuscript are those of the minimal-face
   reduced lift. This convention is explicit.
4. Generic regularity is not global smooth regularity. No Stage 1 theorem
   invokes the global topological premium.
5. Source bibliography metadata had two drafting inaccuracies, corrected
   against primary records: Soh–Varvitsiotis pages are 427–456, and the
   Fawzi–Safey El Din DOI is 10.1137/17M1142570.
