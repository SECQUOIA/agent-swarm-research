## Supersession and essential scope distinctions

- `exact-lorentz-curvature-budget` and `psd-block-curvature-capacity` are
  specializations of `symmetric-cone-curvature-capacity`; the universal bound
  extends to all definable proper cones but does not supersede the sharper
  Jordan rank-sensitive formula. Both appear in Stage 1.
- `selection-free-hermitian-exposed-rank-frontier`, the real PSD2 frontier,
  `exceptional-albert-sourcewise-exposed-rank`, and the one-ball part of
  `hermitian-sequential-contact-range-frontier` are unified by the all-EJA
  single-ball theorem. Product results still need sequential compression.
- `two-lorentz-factor-compact-ball-barrier-rigidity` is superseded in its
  quotient-two range by the arbitrary-factor wide-cap theorem. The latter
  does **not** close the narrow-cap range. The analogous Hermitian notes have
  the same limitation. Smoothly selected standard-slice frontiers cannot be
  promoted to arbitrary affine-lift frontiers.
- The PSD2 nullity-seam question was refuted by the rotated compact-lift
  counterexample. The exposed-rank premium counterexample and fiber-topology
  obstructions must accompany any fiberwise rigidity discussion.
- `global-smooth-saturation-topology`, sphere/Grassmannian/idempotent-orbit
  saturation, active-ray refinements, and cylinder exclusions form a refinement
  chain. Retain the strongest justified theorem rather than duplicating every
  intermediate claim. A generic dense-open regularity locus is insufficient
  for the global topological premium.
- `c1-euler-no-sharing-product-balls` sharpens analytic no-sharing where its
  whole-row and full-product hypotheses apply. Joint selected contact kernels
  are a different model; a local joint factorization is not automatically a
  whole affine lift. Open-vertex switching and connected-extreme counterexamples
  prevent silently extending these statements.
- `psd-column-packing-*`, Hermitian private-nullity, and balance slices show
  that few large cone factors can coexist with a large restricted barrier.
  Newton-forest/oracle-equivalence results are computational context, not
  evidence for a barrier or rank lower bound.
- `conditioned-approximate-curvature-capacity` and robust topology variants
  require derivative/control assumptions. Hausdorff closeness alone supplies
  none. Condition numbers of chosen factors are not invariant KKT condition
  numbers, as two explicit counterexample notes establish.
- Existing central-path paper material on support-principal minors, metric
  distances, and bounded local movement is background for Stage 5. The new
  paper must attribute that overlap and focus its novelty on optimization over
  formulations; the movement mechanism itself is not a new algorithm lower
  bound. `conditioning-paper/` and `paper/` are comparison sources and must
  not be modified.
