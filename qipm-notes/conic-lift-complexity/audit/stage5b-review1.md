## Independent verification of NEW12c

I rederived `newt:quotient`, `newt:linear-transfer`, `newt:reduced-gradient`, and `newt:obstruction` independently.

For a residual direction `B`, the allocation functional has Riesz representative `D E_a D`, so its Gram matrix is precisely `M(D)`. Minimizing its squared norm under the tangent allocations gives the term `4 z^T M(D)^{-1} z`. The remaining directional term contains one inverse of `D`.

The new first-power comparison is valid. For `E = sum theta_a E_a`, the matrix `E D E` is positive semidefinite even when `E` is indefinite. Thus the residual comparison can be used in one trace factor. Since `D^0` is constant on each source's column labels, exact allocations give

`sum tr(D^0 E D E) = sum theta_a^2 (rho_a/h_a) rho_a`.

This is exactly the centered allocation Gram form. It proves `mu M_0 <= M(D) <= L M_0`, rather than only a squared comparison, and hence the claimed linear bounds for both nonnegative pieces of the quotient. Inversion, congruence, and restriction give the stated dual and tangent-space consequences.

The reduced Newton gradient also checks. Completing the auxiliary square shifts `B` to `B-D`; its constraint becomes `-2z-rho`. Expanding the minimized quadratic therefore produces `2 rho^T M(D)^{-1}z` plus the common objective term. This is an essential distinction from identifying Hessians alone, and the text explicitly retains it.

The two-source example at `W=0` has relative eigenvalues `1/(1+xi)` and `1/(1-xi)`. It attains both linear comparison endpoints and condition `L/mu`. The Newton directions and both trace-distance formulas are correct. Approximate allocations introduce the additional factors stated in `newt:approx-transfer`.

As an independent computational check, I constructed the full affine-coordinate Hessian and gradient, eliminated the auxiliary variables directly, and compared them with the displayed quotient and reduced-gradient formulas for 100 feasible examples with heterogeneous source group counts. The maximum absolute discrepancy was `1.53e-13`; the linear matrix bounds passed in every example. These numerical checks support, rather than replace, the preceding algebraic verification.
