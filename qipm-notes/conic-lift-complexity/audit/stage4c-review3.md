## Exact compilers (10d)

- The one-translate criterion is correctly restricted to proper cones and to same-space, same-oriented translates. The extreme-ray argument uses the lattice decomposition property correctly; the compact-base argument closes the finite-dimensional simpliciality proof. The retained-input-apex condition correctly reduces to a total order and excludes dimension at least two by connectedness of the sphere.
- The simultaneous minima statement distinguishes scalar transformed-entry access from physical-coordinate access, charges simultaneous success in the upper bound, and uses the zero-or-one marked promise needed for a minimum-value lower bound. The cited adversary direct-sum theorem applies to a vector of decision outputs. The randomized lower bound can be justified by coupling under the independent column distribution, as stated.
- The Lorentz obstruction uses an actual open arc from each original circle. Every alternative disk has positive radius because the common intersection has interior. Its conclusion is appropriately not extended to arbitrary lifts or custom barriers.
- The continuous-summary lower bound really proves injectivity before applying invariance of domain. Decoder continuity is unnecessary. The upper bound's coefficient functions are continuous by a finite invertible evaluation matrix. The matrix-group proof of the dilation classification is valid under continuity alone, including the stated real dimension after conjugate pairing and the zero-function case.
- The two-moment relative-entropy identity is valid without matrix commutativity, extends under the indicated support convention, and has the correct extra trace-Y term for the corrected divergence. It applies to a weighted sum and fixed scales, not to an intersection of separate inequalities. The unique-search barrier-value example is an invertible pullback of the scalar relative-entropy barrier, with the claimed log(2) separation.
- The three-cone binomial representation eliminates exactly to the full epigraph. Its coefficients have the claimed encoding size, whereas signed-feature rank is proved against arbitrary competing real nodes. These are distinct output contracts.
- The product-cosh lower bound R >= r is sound. The compact truncation retains a full r-dimensional curved graph patch near (0,1), and the displayed Hessian is positive definite. The foundational reduction removes free variables and ensures relative Slater feasibility. All reduced exponential-cone faces have dimension at most three, and the added truncation ray has zero curvature capacity. The original ambient barrier charge correctly excludes that artificial ray. The resulting interval [r, 2r+1] is not described as an exact optimum.
- The piecewise-affine approximation has the correct relative error estimate and epigraph inclusions. It correctly defeats a putative approximate exponential-factor lower bound when arbitrary scalar inequalities are uncharged.

## Projected compilers (10e)

- The projection keeps the compatibility equations in the orthogonal complement of range(W), so lineality is not silently discarded. The dual cone in the effective range is pointed even when it is not full dimensional. An extreme ray still requires effective-dimension minus one independent active inequalities, giving the stated combinatorial count, with rank-zero blocks separately handled.
- Canonical recourse is only asserted on the relative interior of the image cone. The coercive strictly convex objective gives a unique positive minimizer, and the KKT matrix is invertible after deleting dependent equality rows. The smoothness assertion is local to the compatible affine domain, as required.
- The conditional LP reduction explicitly retains initialization, precision, coherent row access, and a separate recourse-output task. It does not infer an algorithm merely from a small barrier parameter.
- The scalar quadratic barrier proof is valid, including its exact parameter one and the quotient needed for a singular quadratic form. The matrix proof correctly normalizes first and second Schur-complement derivatives; the scalar cubic inequality gives self-concordance, and an orthant slice supplies the arbitrary-barrier parameter lower bound s. The block Gram expansion and its order are correct.
- Safe Gram inflation uses the actual orientation of the published Loewner inequalities. The relative objective conclusion is restricted to pure least squares; the analogous matrix statement does not claim such a conclusion for arbitrary SDP objectives. Row and block queries are distinguished.
- The convolution obstruction is correctly limited to scalar normalizations depending on the number of blocks and explicitly includes degenerate point-mass blocks. Both conjugacy identities and the limiting derivative ratios check out. It says nothing false about all barriers of an interval.
- The reusable-query contract is operational and requires joint successful recovery. The quantum Fourier-span argument applies after purification, and the dimension-versus-identification calculation supplies an Omega(N) preprocessing bound. Membership separation is explicitly vertical. The weighted local-logarithm lower bound follows from isolated active arcs and the vertical limit at infinity, while the existential parameter-two alternative follows from the universal-barrier theorem on a proper planar domain.
