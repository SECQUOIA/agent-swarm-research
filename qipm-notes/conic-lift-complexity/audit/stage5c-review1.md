## Minor issues

### 1. State that the dimension cap is an integer

- **Location:** `sections/00-introduction.tex:27–35`, especially line 28 and the displayed factor-count/dimension formulas.
- **Reason:** The introduction assumes only a cap `d >= 3`, whereas Corollary `cor:dimension-resources` explicitly assumes an integer cap. The displayed exact formulas are false for a general real cap. For example, `s = 4` and `d = 3.5` allow only cone dimensions at most three; the curvature bound requires at least three factors and total dimension nine. Substitution into the introductory formula instead gives two factors and total dimension seven.
- **Concrete correction:** Change “a cone-dimension cap $d\geq3$” to “an integer cone-dimension cap $d\geq3$.” No theorem or proof change is needed.

### 2. State positivity of the residual comparison constants

- **Location:** `sections/00-introduction.tex:107–113`, the premise immediately preceding `eq:intro-transfer`.
- **Reason:** The displayed Loewner sandwich alone does not ensure `mu > 0`, which is required for inversion and for the condition-number conclusion. A centered feasible residual `D = D^0`, together with `mu = -1` and `L = 1`, satisfies the stated sandwich but makes the claimed upper Hessian bound false. With `mu = 0`, the conclusion is undefined. Theorem `newt:linear` correctly includes `0 < mu <= 1 <= L`.
- **Concrete correction:** Write the introductory premise as “$\mu D^0\preceq D\preceq L D^0$, with $0<\mu\leq1\leq L$”. No change to the quotient theorem is needed.
