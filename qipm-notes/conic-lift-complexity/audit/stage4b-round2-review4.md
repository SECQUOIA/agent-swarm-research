## Revised sharing arguments

nonzero, annihilates the primal contact vector, and annihilates every
primal derivative because its nonnegative pairing attains zero there.
The rank-detecting derivative spaces together with the primal ray are a
direct sum by the cross-contact identities. Their inclusion in that one
hyperplane gives total rank at most m_i−2. No symmetry of the separate
mixed forms, second derivatives, or strict complementarity is used.
The exposed-face inequality and the incidence bound survive unchanged.

For the global product-ball argument, the summed labelled dual map is
cone-valued and C1. Its kernel is exactly k−sum_a<x_a,z_a>, with the
identity contact map and nondegenerate product-sphere pairing. The
earlier joint-cover theorem therefore applies at R=kp. Its capacity
multiset conclusion rules out equality under c<p because p≥2. This
establishes the additional integer unit. I also checked the compact
exact-active-label cover argument for the two face-weighted budgets.

The formulas for R_* and L_* correctly combine independent lower
bounds, the inequality R≤cL, and integer rounding. At f=1 they recover
R_*=k tau and L_*=kh; the manuscript does not claim attainment for
general f. The wedge-section argument bounds arbitrary ambient barriers,
not merely logarithmically homogeneous ones.

The same hyperplane argument applies to the spectral contact manifolds
even when their dimensions differ. Recomputing the channels gives
delta(r−1)+delta(c−r)+(delta−1)=delta c−1, including the phase channel
over the complex and quaternionic fields. The strengthened summed
capacity and the two factor-count ceilings follow. No unsupported
spectral topological premium was introduced.
