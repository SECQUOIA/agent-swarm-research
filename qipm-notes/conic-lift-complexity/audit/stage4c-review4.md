## Required minor correction

**10b, lines 198–202 (the operation count following the entropy Hessian factorization).** The stated two products by `A` or `A^T` are correct for a solve after `G` has been assembled. From only the point and slacks, forming the coefficients of `G_i` additionally requires `A^T (1/s)`. Thus the literal two-product claim currently leaves out setup. The asymptotic `O(N+B+nnz A)` bound is correct. Either say “after assembling G, each solve uses two products…” and account for the additional setup product, or replace “two products” by “a constant number of products.” This is minor and does not change any theorem or asymptotic resource bound.

## Mathematical checks

Zero columns do not reduce the intrinsic parameter because their input coordinates remain independent. The active-column closure proof correctly prevents an infinite positive entropy term from being cancelled by the other terms: all other terms have uniform finite lower bounds on a convergent input sequence. The construction with input rays for inactive columns gives finite fibers at every boundary point. The zero-map counterexample to retaining unused exponential cones is correct. Repeated and zero rows do not invalidate the certificate or the Hessian.
- Recomputed the perspective dual by convex conjugation and the characteristic-integral Jacobian. The differentiated scaling exponents are `3/2+(r-1)/p` and `1+(r-1)/p`. The necessary self-concordance inequality is consequently justified, rather than inferred from an undifferentiated asymptotic. The restriction arguments meet the ambient interiors. ## Literature and novelty checks

I checked primary sources online and local literature independently of the author audit:

- Fawzi–Saunderson, *Optimal self-concordant barriers for quantum relative entropies*, primary full text at <https://arxiv.org/html/2205.04581v3>: Theorems 3.3, 3.9 and 3.10 support compatibility and arbitrary-barrier recession lower bounds; the scalar/diagonal specializations used here are properly attributed. Corollary 1.7 supports the matrix relative entropy parameter. The manuscript does not claim this machinery as new.
- Hildebrand, *A lower bound on the barrier parameter of barriers for convex cones*, primary PDF at <https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/3068.pdf>: the root formula and LH qualification agree with Corollaries 7.1–7.2. The manuscript explicitly handles the branch difference via duality. Its claims concern the derived product certificate and family-specific asymptotics, not a newly solved optimal-barrier problem.
- Apers–Gribling, primary manuscript at <https://arxiv.org/html/2311.03215v2>: Theorem 3.1 has exactly the Loewner convention used in 10e, and Section 6 states the initialization requirement retained in the conditional LP reduction. Query counts are not presented as full arithmetic costs.
- Local `literature/papers/lee2021-universal-barrier-is-n-self/paper.md` and the primary manuscript linked there confirm dimension-parameter existence separately from efficient evaluation. Both 10a and 10e maintain that distinction.
- A targeted additional search found Hildebrand's published 2026 survey, *Three Different Views on Barrier Functions in Conic Optimization*, JOTA 209, article 36, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10957-026-02986-4>. Its publisher abstract describes geometric interpretations of LH barriers; the full publisher text was not accessible. It is a potentially useful final literature-synthesis reference, but the abstract supplies no basis to change or challenge a specific theorem or novelty claim here. I do not make a required correction from that abstract.

I found no unsupported claim of an exact nonsymmetric optimum, no transfer from Hausdorff accuracy alone to curvature capacity, and no transfer from barrier parameter alone to oracle/runtime complexity. Subject to the small solve/setup wording correction, this stage is ready to proceed.
