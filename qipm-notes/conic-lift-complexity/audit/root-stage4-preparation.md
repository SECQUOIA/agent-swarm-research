## A useful development suggested by the literature comparison

The general local-curvature theorem appears to give a clean additional corollary for weighted geometric means. This is a candidate for independent development and review in Stage 4, not yet an accepted manuscript theorem.

For positive rational weights `alpha_i` summing to one, let `g(x)=prod x_i^{alpha_i}` and consider the compact section `sum x_i=1`, `x_i>=0`, `0<=t<=g(x)`. It has dimension `m`; on its positive upper boundary,

`-h^T Hess(g)(x) h = g(x)[sum alpha_i(h_i/x_i)^2 - (sum alpha_i h_i/x_i)^2]`.

The right side is positive for nonzero `h` with `sum h_i=0`: equality requires `h` proportional to `x`, and the affine tangent condition then forces zero. Thus the boundary has curvature rank `m-1`. Restricting any exact affine lift of the geometric-mean cone to this compact section and applying the Stage 1 generic contact bound should yield `sum (d_i-2)>=m-1`; in particular any lift through copies of the three-dimensional rotated Lorentz cone, even with extra rays, has at least `m-1` nonray factors. Verify all definitions and proper-face reductions independently before including.

This matters for the prior-work comparison: Wang's journal Theorem 3.15 (preprint Theorem 16) gives `ceil(m/2)` for arbitrary such lifts by face-chain length, whereas his `m-1` bound is stated for the smaller class of simple mediated-set representations. The candidate curvature corollary extends that dimension bound to arbitrary affine lifts. It does not supply Wang's denominator-sensitive bound, which must remain confined to its proved representation class. Do not claim an exact arbitrary-lift count for all weights.

## Topology consolidation

The general normalized-base covering theorem is stronger than the abstract tangent-bundle obstructions for an exact global factorization. Under a nondegenerate contact pairing, equality `sum max(dim K_i-2,0)=dim M` normalizes each nonzero primal factor onto the boundary of a compact cone base. Its derivative has the full available rank. The joint map is a local diffeomorphism and hence a finite covering of the product of those base boundaries. The boundaries are topological spheres even for nonsmooth proper cones; the constant-rank image argument must supply their differentiable structure rather than assume it. On a single source sphere, the product-covering obstruction allows only one positive-capacity factor, with no Hopf exception. This gives the general proper-cone single-ball dimension/count/ambient-barrier frontier. State the compact-contact-manifold version as well.

The Steenrod/Adams partial-sum obstruction remains useful as an abstract splitting consequence. Do not imply that a low-rank summand itself is trivial: the theorem gives existence of a frame from existence of a plane field. With `s=rho(n+1)-1`, every subset rank at most `n/2` is at most s. If `s<n/3`, the largest rank is at least `n-s`. For actual exact global factorizations, explain that the stronger joint theorem subsumes these necessary conditions. A single saturated factor without total saturation induces a sphere submersion; the classical dimension pairs `(3,2),(7,4),(15,8)` concern this weaker setting.

The all-EJA product saturation proof needs the full cylinder argument, not merely a reference to notes. Equality produces a covering of the product of balanced idempotent orbits despite finitely many scalar rays. Complex and quaternionic non-spin Grassmannians have nonzero mod-two squares; the Albert plane does too. Real balanced orders at least six have `pi_2=Z/2`, incompatible with the universal cover of a product of spheres. Real order five has quadric relation `x^2=2y`. One can exclude it by integral indecomposables as in the source, or by the simpler rational invariant: the square-zero classes span degree two in a product of spheres, whereas their projections onto the quadric's one-dimensional degree-two space are all zero. Real orders three and four remain topologically possible. Integral square-zero degree-two generators assign their S2 rulings to distinct literal source S2 slices. A fixed unrelated dual range restricts an order-four support to an oriented `Gr_2(R3)` diagonal/antidiagonal inside `S2 x S2`, incompatible with either ruling. Applying both rulings eliminates every dual row, a contradiction. For order three, cylinders isolate one S2 source and reduce to the already proved PSD3-plus-rays affine-kernel obstruction. The final spin-only covering and degreewise indecomposables assign each cone to one source; no product splitting of the covering is assumed.
