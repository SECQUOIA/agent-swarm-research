## Mathematical checks

- **12a, exact ledgers.** The order-budget exchange proof supports the closed formula for `V_R(N)`, including the relaxed order-one remainder. The ambient dimension recurrence, the order-four/order-five special cases, and the simultaneous order-two/order-three triples agree with the constructions. The finite packing ledger distinguishes full cone coordinates, free coordinates before allocation equations, ambient rank, and restricted parameter correctly. Its new intrinsic-parameter assertion transfers through bounded completion fibers to the grouped domain; I checked the relevant statements in Section 07. The nullity-budget truncation, Hölder constant, cap exponent, continuous aspect ratio, and width-four Schur comparison are consistent.

- **12b, composition of work and movement.** The argument uses the same aggregate certificate rank for both the mixed-derivative capacity and movement estimate. In particular, it does not substitute the restricted barrier parameter for that rank. The per-round charge is expressly a premise. The displayed central path and the checkpoint schedule give the claimed metric budget, and cached Gram matrices support the stated serialization cost. The text retains preprocessing, scalar evaluation, and output representation as separate costs. The phase-oracle example correctly prevents converting this into an unconditional fresh-query lower bound.

- **12c, centered and off-center Newton equations.** I independently differentiated the lifted barrier and eliminated the constrained residual directions. This reproduces both the quotient Hessian and the reduced gradient, including the factor two in the latter. The new allocation-aware proof is valid: for diagonal `E(theta)`, the matrix `E(theta) D E(theta)` is positive semidefinite even when `E(theta)` is indefinite; exact allocations then turn the intermediate trace into `theta^T M_0 theta`. This gives the first-power matrix comparison. The two-source example attains the relative eigenvalue bounds and gives the stated trace distances between normalized directions. I also tested 500 independently generated feasible correlated residual matrices with heterogeneous source group counts and 10 random directions per matrix; all 5,000 directional comparisons passed. These numerical checks supplement the algebraic proof rather than replace it.

- **12c, access and classical comparison.** The canonical-oracle equivalence is explicitly restricted to identical complete oracle unitaries, projected output, and recentered calls. The reconstruction of original auxiliary directions is excluded. The missing-scale lower bound uses valid interior points, disjoint relative-error intervals, and a vector-output adversary direct sum. The joint-output/online distinction is retained. The Lorentz augmentations, signs, positive downdated base, regularization error, and scalar graph counts check. The exact-field result is kept separate from finite-precision stability, and the CGLS residual bound uses `kappa_2(K)`, not `kappa_2(K^T K)` as its iteration multiplier.

- **12d, query and precision laws.** The Fourier-span proof bounds the dimension of the final-state ensemble and yields linear approximate sign recovery under the stated success and error constants. The common EJA instance has rank-one certificates in every group and determinant scale one; I checked the Peirce normalization against Section 02. The disk observable has the claimed adjacent-weight gap. The product and direct-ball value formulas, checkpoint rescalings, normalized objective scales, and readable-output thresholds check. Unequal-magnitude state preparation is correctly described as heralded exact with constant expected queries, or constant error with a fixed query budget. Norms of hidden-dependent projections are excluded from the supplied interface. The independent-search diagnostic uses a correct tensor-sum adversary and known-cardinality exact search avoids a joint-success logarithm. The conditioning statement applies to the scaled restricted Hessian, not an arbitrary KKT system. The product/direct comparison explicitly changes the feasible body, and movement/query lower bounds combine only by a maximum.

- **12e, exact compilers.** The reusable output contract rules out continued source queries. Exact radius acquisition, chain minima, Pareto essentiality witnesses, and the growing-dimensional decoder support the claimed query bounds. The planar witness identity and the adjacent-contact identity have the correct signs. The growing-dimensional optimizer is unique, and its coordinate spacing exceeds twice the stated error threshold. The natural/compiled barrier multiplicities and path-length orders check. The entropy identity is valid on the closed perspective domain, and the projected paths coincide at matched total gaps; their exact lengths use the correct numbers of source and aggregate slack coordinates. The text keeps compilation separate from solving a coupled post-compilation problem and from explicit output/loading costs.
