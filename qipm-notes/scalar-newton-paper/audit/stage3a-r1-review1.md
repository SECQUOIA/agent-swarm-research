## Findings

1. **Minor — expose the central-path multiplier in the box-centering tolerance.** Location: `sections/07-scalar-realizations.tex`, lines 404–412, after the box subproblem. The target accumulator is `rho(eta) h Phi`, whereas the available perturbation estimate is `sqrt(K tau)`. Thus the uniform sufficient gap is `tau <= c rho(eta)^2 (beta-alpha)^2 delta^2/K`. The prose currently asserts `c (beta-alpha)^2 delta^2/K` “for any fixed public eta>0” without saying that c depends on eta. For an arbitrary small eta, the center can already have a small subproblem gap while revealing no amplitude, so a universal c would not work. Concrete fix: retain the rho-squared factor, or write `c_eta` and explicitly permit dependence on the fixed multiplier. If eta is confined to a fixed interval bounded away from zero, say so instead. This does not affect the main optimizer-coordinate or direct-decrement theorems.

2. **Minor — state the sparsity scope of the imported coherent lower bound.** Location: `sections/06-coherent.tex`, equation `eq:sparse-quantum-gap` and its following sentence (lines 85–93). The invoked sign-block family is two-sparse, so the displayed comparison, as currently proved by the cited corollary, is for sparsity budgets `d>=2`. The preceding phrase only says “d-sparse,” leaving d=1 formally included. Concrete fix: say the lower bound already holds at d=2 and hence for all d>=2. A separate diagonal lower bound under a specified sparse-entry-only interface is possible, but unnecessary here; do not silently transfer the full-SQ sign-block proof to d=1.

3. **Minor — quantify the reusable-setup success margin.** Location: `sections/07-scalar-realizations.tex`, lines 609–617. A constant number of independent setups and majority vote follows when good-setup probability is bounded above 1/2 by a fixed positive margin, not from the bare condition that it exceeds 1/2 (which could approach 1/2 with the parameters). Concrete fix: state success at least `1/2+c_setup` for an absolute positive constant, with the conditional source decision made accurate enough relative to that margin. Then the number of repetitions and the constant in `O(1/p)` remain independent of the growing parameters. The setup-plus-draw ledger itself is correct.
