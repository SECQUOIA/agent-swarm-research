## Neighboring programs deliberately outside this paper

- The normalized spectral-shift staircase, joint gap–accuracy law, adaptive lower hierarchy, pinned gates, and exact two-cluster conversion form a separate matrix-access conversion project. This paper uses only the access-model lesson needed to interpret its own inverse-form bounds.
- Cone-lift minimality, exposed-rank and curvature capacity, product-ball packing, arbitrary-barrier centrality taxes, Dikin movement, and spectral-ball subgeodesicity are covered by the conic-lift and central-path manuscripts. They do not become new scalar-query results because a Newton decrement appears in a proof.
- Product-disk, product-ball, spectral-ball, one-sharing-cone, and symmetric-cone scalar/state hierarchies are comparator output contracts already substantially covered by other manuscripts. Reproduce only a concise comparison if needed; their entire cone geometry is not part of this inverse-form paper.
- Entropic-risk exponential-cone compression, tilted jets, moment compilation, positive-atom support, relative-entropy compiler results, and generalized cone barrier-parameter optimization are independent projects.
- The parked Lorentz-condensation note is explicitly a corollary of an existing two-by-two PSD result and is not a fresh scalar contribution.

## Coverage rule for later stages

Where a source claim cannot be proved under its written interface, correct and prove it, or state the exact narrower theorem with the failed extension documented in the audit. Do not silently omit an invalid claim and still mark its source fully incorporated. Partial parameter matches, oracle assumptions, and open universal tradeoffs must be stated as limits of proved theorems, not promoted to complete optimality claims.

## Stage 4 integration

All included developments above now have a route into the standalone
manuscript. Section 1 explains their relationship: positive relative-form
bounds and composition; scalar optimization and repeated-query outputs;
and structured classical comparisons. Table 1 separates the one-scalar
rates by access model and regime. Section 2 supplies the barrier,
predictor-decrement, and equality-restriction conventions used by the
optimization constructions. Section 11 synthesizes their implications.

The literature comparisons distinguish inherited polynomial/SQ,
Forrelation-clock, variable-time quantum, cone-algebra, recycling, and
elimination ingredients from the precise refinements proved here. They
include scalar LP value hardness predating this manuscript, recent spectral
sum and robust dequantization work, and the separate streaming-space
solution-observable model. Scope exclusions above remain exclusions;
integration does not turn neighboring or superseded programs into proved
claims in this paper. Separate lower bounds are not multiplied, and the
unmatched general regimes remain explicit.
