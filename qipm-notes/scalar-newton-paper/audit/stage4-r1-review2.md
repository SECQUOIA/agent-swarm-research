## Finding

1. **Minor — credit the earlier explicit block-versus-entry observation.** Locator: `sections/06-coherent.tex`, lines 166–177, especially the statement that the contribution includes “the explicit boundary between unitary-block and exact-entry access.” Alase et al. already make this boundary explicit immediately after their Theorem IV.13: for a fixed computational-basis input, block-encoded expectation estimation retains its lower bound, whereas the corresponding sparse-entry problem is solved with one entry query. Their Appendix A proves the fixed-state block lower bound. The manuscript correctly distinguishes their observable-query/additive task from its system-query/relative inverse-form task; its mathematical claim is sound. Nevertheless, attributing the boundary only as part of this theorem's contribution leaves an unnecessarily broad impression of originality. **Fix:** add one sentence crediting that prior fixed-state observation and describe the contribution here as its explicit realization for the relative inverse form, together with the matching condition-number dependence. Primary source: [Alase et al., arXiv:2111.10485](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.10485), discussion after Theorem IV.13 and Appendix A; journal DOI [10.1103/PhysRevResearch.4.023237](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.4.023237). No theorem or proof needs changing.
