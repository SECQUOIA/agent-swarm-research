## Findings

1. **Minor — restate full row rank for the profile result.** At the start of “Eccentricity profiles with full output,” explicitly assume that the concatenated matrix `A` has full row rank. That assumption appears inside the earlier conditional cone-system corollary, but it is not a section-wide convention and is not stated again in this subsection. Without it, the proposition's claim `P_theta > 0` and the inverses used in its certificate need not exist (take a zero row of `A`). The intended setting is clear, and adding this hypothesis repairs the statement without changing its proof or cost. It also justifies `S-VV^T > 0` in the later augmentation.

2. **Minor — specify sparse access in both directions for the one-entry maps.** In the general model, the sentence “Its locations and values are queryable” should say that the unique nonzero position/value, or its absence, can be obtained given either a row index or a column index of `T_j`. The proof uses both `T_j` and `T_j^T`, and materializes rows and columns of their composed transforms. A forward-only partial injection oracle does not automatically supply its inverse. Diagonal maps and the cone embeddings satisfy the intended stronger model.

3. **Minor — keep the second rank-sharpness statement explicitly inside the worst-case family.** Immediately after the proof of `eq:profile-rank-lower`, the statement that blocks with `chi_j > theta` force `b_theta` positive and negative update directions should begin “In the same `A=I` family, allowing unequal block eccentricities, ...”. The context suggests this restriction, but the sentence as written can be read as applying to arbitrary `A`. Such an interpretation is false: `A` can annihilate all high/low radial eigendirections. With `A=I`, restriction of the Loewner inequalities to the high and low eigenspaces gives the claimed inertia bounds directly.

4. **Minor — make the exact cited solve theorem and decomposition preprocessing visible.** In the proof of the supplied latent-width proposition, cite Fürer–Hoppen–Trevisan's Corollary 3 for solving the system, alongside Theorem 1 if desired. Add a sentence that the supplied compact decomposition is first converted to an `O(N)`-node nice decomposition in `O(k(|T|+N)) = O(k^2 N)` time, with `N` the augmented dimension and `k=O(tau_L+1)`. This fills an exposition gap: the cited solve corollary explicitly assumes `O(N)` nodes, whereas the manuscript assumes `O(kN)` total bag entries. The published preprocessing makes the manuscript's bound correct; no stronger input hypothesis is needed.
