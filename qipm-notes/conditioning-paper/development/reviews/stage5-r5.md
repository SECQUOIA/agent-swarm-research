## Decision

No major issues found. The assembled paper has a clear mathematical subject, appropriately delimited originality claims, and a genuinely standalone reproduction package. I found two minor wording issues in the abstract/introduction. Neither affects a theorem or numerical conclusion.

## Minor findings and precise corrections

1. **The abstract should allow an empty weak spectral cluster.** `main.tex:40` says “For linear programs, every fixed barrier has two spectral scales.” At a unique optimum, Theorem 8.1 instead has only the `Theta(g^{-2})` scale: its first case is explicitly empty when `f=0`. This distinction is especially relevant because the paper emphasizes unique and degenerate LP optima elsewhere. Replace “has two spectral scales” with “has at most two spectral scales,” or state the two possible scales and identify the weak cluster's multiplicity as the optimal-face dimension. The subsequent projector statement remains valid with the theorem's zero-projector convention.

2. **The introductory uniformity summary should say that residuals stay uniformly below one.** `sections/01-introduction.tex:31–32` refers to “bounded centrality residuals,” and contribution 1 similarly says “bounded barrier parameters and residuals.” Mere boundedness of residuals below one permits a family `rho(g) -> 1`, whereas the displayed constant `(nu+2 sqrt(nu)+rho)/(1-rho)` then diverges. The mathematical sections supply the proper fixed upper bound. Replace both introductory descriptions with “bounded barrier parameters and centrality residuals bounded uniformly below one,” or write `nu <= nu_max` and `rho <= rho_max < 1`. This is a summary-precision issue, not a missing proof.

## Scope and integration assessment

The introduction makes the main geometric result understandable before reviewing related work. It distinguishes the classical same-point Hessian/chord comparison from comparison at different centers with equal gap, explicitly credits Peña's gap parameterization and Schur conditioning analysis, and credits Sturm's underlying example and classical LP endpoint results. Its qualified priority statement is limited to the stated combination of operator, equal-gap comparison, residual-dependent constants, and matching diameter law. The discussion maintains the fixed-metric/fixed-representation scope and does not turn the Hessian condition-number law into an unsupported quantum runtime claim.

The numerical evidence checks proved formulas and finite-gap behavior, with no unsupported exponent fits. The package is reproducible without parent-repository code or network access; optional provenance capture is distinguished from ordinary reproduction. Leaving author identities and journal-specific metadata to the submitters is appropriate and is documented. I found no additional publication-readiness or scientific-completeness issue in this stage.
