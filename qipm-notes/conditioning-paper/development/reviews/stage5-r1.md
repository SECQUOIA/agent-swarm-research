## Minor findings

1. **Qualify the number of LP spectral scales in the abstract.** `main.tex`, abstract sentence “For linear programs, every fixed barrier has two spectral scales” omits the unique-optimum case, in which the bounded weak cluster is empty and every eigenvalue has order `g^{-2}`. The theorem itself states this correctly. Write “at most two spectral scales, with multiplicities determined by the optimal-face dimension,” or state the positive-dimensional-face qualification. This preserves an important distinction emphasized elsewhere in the paper.

2. **Use “exact central point” in the numerical SDP description.** `sections/11-numerics.tex:17` says the figure evaluates “the analytic center” for a sequence of gaps. The analytic center as defined in the setup is the unconstrained minimizer of the barrier and has a single gap; the computation evaluates the exact central point at each prescribed gap. Replace this with “the exact central-point and stable quadratic-root formulas,” or explicitly say “the analytic center of each fixed objective slice.” The former is simpler and matches the theorem's established terminology.

3. **Keep the simplex perturbation symbol consistent.** The simplex theorem uses `vartheta`, whereas `sections/11-numerics.tex:45,58` and the generated simplex figure legend use `theta`. Earlier classifications already use `theta` for a Hölder exponent. Use `vartheta` in the numerical prose/caption and in the legend generated by `repro/reproduce.py` (the internal Python/CSV variable name may remain `theta`, provided its documentation is clear). This avoids silently changing the meaning of an established mathematical symbol.
