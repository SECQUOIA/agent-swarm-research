3. **Spectral distance.** The Hermitian dilation supplies both signs of every singular value and two copies of `g''=b''/2`; this gives the exact required coefficient. 4. **Jordan formula.** The Peirce off-diagonal coefficient is `(1-lambda_i)^(-1)(1-lambda_j)^(-1) + (1+lambda_i)^(-1)(1+lambda_j)^(-1)`, equal to the displayed divided difference. The argument is valid for the Euclidean Jordan setting stated, including direct products and the exceptional algebra.

Simultaneous boundary limits attain the product supremum asymptotically. The manuscript correctly separates positive scales for metric assertions from scales at least one for standard self-concordance.
