## Simpler proof of the operational dimension bound

For an exact affine image of an affine slice of a proper cone of dimension M, a compact full-dimensional image C in R^D forces M>=D+1 without a slack-factorization theorem. Surjectivity of the image's affine span gives M>=D. If M=D, the projection's linear part is invertible; the slice's affine hull must be the entire operational space, so there are no effective affine constraints. Its image is then an affine image of the whole cone, which is unbounded for D>=1. Contradiction. This proof applies after minimal-face reduction and elimination of irrelevant free directions. Alternatively use the classical slack rank D+1 with the proper-lift hypotheses stated explicitly. The direct proof is shorter and avoids unnecessary certificate-selection questions.
