## Completed scope

The five developed candidates are physical water management, cyclic oxides, tungsten coordination/retention, clean-feed polymer catalyst use, and the conditional liquid Ti-zeolite test. The formaldehyde, lactate and fluorochemical searches already underway have closed with their negative findings. Earlier Ag, CHA and zirconia studies retain their existing source audits and stated scope; they are not reopened by this round.

| Candidate | Concrete contribution sought | Evidence that decides expansion |
|---|---|---|
| [Physical water management](working/program-development/physical-water-management.md) | Predict when a neighboring hydrophobic additive changes useful catalyst response through water exposure or operating history. | A reproduced formulation contrast, qualified hot water measurement and an independently specified functional prediction. Outlet response alone does not identify local exposure or validate a shaping rule. |
| [Cyclic oxides](working/program-development/cyclic-oxides.md) | Determine whether steam-purge chemistry creates a persistent loss that prevention avoids more effectively than later restoration. | An informative equal-dose CO2 timing comparison, intermediate material balances, common final reset and useful output under the intended purge duty. |
| [Tungsten coordination/retention](working/program-development/oxygenate-program-alternative.md) | Connect productive sugar binding and W export to a useful catalyst or operating choice. | A consequential limitation of a strong reference and a chemically supported intervention. Neither a hot leaching assay nor the present epimer contrast supplies this by itself. |
| [Polymer catalyst use](working/program-development/polymer-clean-feed.md) | Reduce fresh-catalyst demand at maintained polymer-carbon output and complete cycle time. | A useful retained-inventory response in one reactor mode, verified carbon origin, and a makeup saving that is not simply deferred or transferred to W replacement. |
| [Liquid Ti-zeolite](working/program-development/liquid-zeolite.md) | Test whether an established solvent/network description predicts useful product-rich epoxidation. | Failure or success of the fixed component-based prediction under an independently tested trajectory, with peroxide and product balances. Generic solvent or inhibition observations do not justify expansion. |

The apparatus dependencies are specific: pressurized FT and quantitative hot-water handling for the water work; representative looping material, gas switching and recoverable Li analysis for cyclic oxides; hot aqueous handling with carbon/W accounting for tungsten; an established polymer reactor with product-resolved carbon analysis for polymer; and an existing Ti-zeolite liquid-reaction platform for the conditional epoxidation test. These capabilities have not been confirmed. The proposals state the observations required before larger commitments; they do not presume access to specialist local measurements.
