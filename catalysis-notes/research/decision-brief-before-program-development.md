## Priority bounded investigation: selective-output retention on promoted Ag

**Question.** Does Ni change fresh combustion, make a promoted state easier to recover, or prevent loss that persists through a defined recovery—and can that distinction predict when operating recovery substitutes for Ni or when Ni remains useful?

**Why it could matter.** Retaining selectivity reduces valuable reactant losses, but an endpoint gain can be outweighed by lower production or long conditioning. The research targets a choice between material stabilization and an operating intervention, with integrated net EO output and resource use as the practical test.

**Prior-art correction.** A [1995 patent experiment](reviews/ag-older-ni-prior-art.md) already reports dilute Ni/Cs/Re/S catalysts under pressurized chloride-containing feeds, with less selectivity loss through 50 days of accelerated aging. It also reoptimizes the catalyst after aging. Generic compatibility, an extra aging curve, or a return to reference is insufficiently original. Its control gaps limit causal attribution but do not erase the reported result.

**Updated evidence.** Iyer’s steady reactor model already predicts local chlorine coverage and integral output from separately assessed kinetic components. Santos supplies elevated-pressure chlorine/product response families. Jalil’s newly supplied SI documents preparation sensitivities, existing proximity/processing controls and transient chloride responses; Esposito’s SI constrains transfer of propylene kinetics to ethylene. These precedents strengthen the baseline without establishing Ni-dependent long-term retention.

**First commitment.** One functioning promoted pair with matched composition and processing, ordinary-operation drift measurements, and independent preparation replication. A predefined ordinary-retention horizon includes pairs with comparable fresh performance when both meet the useful-output floor, because a difference may emerge only during operation. If a consequential difference appears, test one credible lower-chloride or other documented operating policy before a substantial excursion campaign. The [modern operating-aging review](reviews/ag-operating-aging-prior-art.md) shows why that comparator matters and why its extrapolated lifetime claims remain unproven. Use matched conditions for causal interpretation and separately selected policies for useful choices. Add further materials or measurements only when they resolve the consequential alternative.

**Requirement for expansion.** A chemically supported explanation must predict a useful intervention or limit under one new history or product burden. Compare the intervention with continued ordinary operation and the competing material over the same period, including recovery downtime and consumables. A familiar chloride adjustment or unsupported upset scenario is insufficient justification. Failure to recover in a finite test establishes persistence under that protocol, not absolute structural irreversibility. Ordinary EO cofeed gives net product sensitivity, not a unique secondary-oxidation rate; oxygen-isotope tracing remains optional.

**Confidence.** Scientific explanation: plausible, unresolved. Informative matched-pair comparison: moderate conditional on materials and reactor capability. Originality: a narrow prospective Ni-retention/operation comparison remains plausible; generic chloride modeling, Ni proximity and recovery are already established. Remaining source gaps and unmeasured outcomes prevent a broader claim. Meaningful modern practical improvement: low evidentiary confidence. Demote the direction if the result is explained by known conditioning without a new useful intervention or requires a broad synthesis/facility campaign.

## Alongside Ag: exposure limits for polyolefin ethenolysis

**Question.** Does one delivered ketone leave W metathesis tolerant or recoverable but cause persistent loss of Na/alumina function, and can those different responses predict useful polymer throughput and a better feed-preparation or catalyst-use choice?

**Why it merits a bounded study.** The recovered patent documents high initial solids inventory, clean-feed reuse losses, fresh-Na rescue, contaminant vulnerability and solvent-prepared waste samples. Those are concrete burdens. The new contribution would be an exposure relationship that changes a useful decision; rescue and dirty-feed inhibition themselves are established.

**Published comparator.** The Science sequence uses three 1 g PE charges, initially 0.4 g each of W- and Na-containing solids, with **0.4 g fresh Na solid added before charge 2 only**. This is the clean-feed makeup control. The [material balance](calculations/polymer-makeup-burden.md) gives conditional `B = 2f` over those three charges; it is not a measured economic benefit. Science SI’s 80.1% apparent scale-up conversion and the patent’s 69.8% refer to different source-specific residue records and must remain separate.

**First commitment.** Establish clean reuse and handling losses against that published schedule, then measure operating-component responses to 2-pentanone and recovery after its measured removal. Compare two measured exposure histories and predict the tandem response plus a subsequent clean charge before testing one characterized-feed connection. Equal input dose does not guarantee equal effective dose or integrated output. Close this branch if clean-feed decay or transport dominates, or if the molecular result does not transfer to a relevant feed. A negative result can still establish a useful feed-acceptance limit.

**Confidence.** The asymmetric W/Na hypothesis is low to moderate confidence; informative controlled measurements are moderately feasible on a functioning platform. Practical improvement remains low confidence. The Science main and SI now define the reuse benchmark. The unretrieved dissertation and original patent regeneration figures leave narrower prior-art limits. Reproduction is better specified, while the hypothesis and practical benefit still require experimental validation.

## Lower-priority diagnostic: NO dependence of wet methane sulfur tolerance

**Question.** Does the wet sulfur tolerance reported for a strongly anchored Pd/SSZ-13 catalyst survive NO removal, and does the result change its useful methane-slip advantage over a credible reference?

**First commitment.** Reproduce the preparation contrast and bridge the published feed to a thermally controlled dilute condition. Compare NO and sulfur histories with sulfur-free and sham controls. Predict a lower-NO history only when a jointly specified application envelope justifies it. Measure cumulative methane slip at useful conversion and rates at a diagnostic condition. Total retained sulfur and nonlinear conversion changes do not uniquely identify sulfur redistribution or chemical coupling.

**Value and confidence.** Generic NO promotion is established; the specific cluster’s dependence remains testable. Chemical confidence is low to moderate and an informative operational test is moderately feasible with reliable wet-gas analysis. The [application review](reviews/wet-methane-low-no-relevance.md) lowers practical confidence: the engine’s roughly 60 ppm NO occurs with reportedly negligible sulfur, while 2 ppm SO2 comes from another study. Those numbers do not define a demonstrated low-NO sulfur challenge. Zero-NO testing can qualify a material claim but cannot yet establish a deployment advantage. A jointly specified real feed must justify further application work.

## Lower-priority diagnostic pilot: zirconia's self-cleaning cycle

**Question.** During ethylbenzene dehydrogenation, where does the oxygen removed from water-poisoned Zr–O pairs go, and does its subsequent chemistry matter for sustained styrene production?

**Why investigate it.** The published catalyst can recover under purified ethylbenzene, but the cleaning products are unassigned. Its model excludes their further reaction. A measured oxygen pathway could determine whether ordinary drying and product separation suffice, or whether cleanup simply creates another source of inhibition. This bears directly on useful operation of an abundant-metal oxide catalyst and could provide a method for assessing chemical cleaning in other catalytic systems.

**Additional knowledge sought.** Connect an independently measured oxygen balance to recovered catalytic function and to the actual removal or return of identified products. Generic self-cleaning, oxygenate poisoning, and guard beds are prior art. A new peak alone would be insufficient.

**Requirement for expansion.** A chemically specified maintenance pathway must predict sustained catalytic function under an untested feed or product-removal change, using independently measured pathway behavior. Identify that prospective prediction after the first repeatable oxygen/function result. If the measured-water model already predicts useful function and X is benign, close this project at its diagnostic scope. Unexplained product-rich damage should be investigated on its own evidence; it does not automatically justify further oxygen attribution.

**First commitment.** Reproduce the reference under measured moisture, then test sustained **net styrene output** in a small matched styrene/H2 cofeed experiment with favorable reaction affinity and return-to-reference controls. Existing recovery tests contain little styrene; high forward rates do not guarantee useful net output. If that gate supports the application, cross water/sham treatment with recovery exposures with/without ethylbenzene and measure incremental oxygen output. Validate the activity assay because ethylbenzene can erase the state being measured. Use isotopes for a specific remaining provenance question. A quarter of the reported 2.4 μmol pair inventory would imply only about 0.6 μmol oxygen under a one-water-per-pair assumption; actual analytical precision must support the intended claim.

**Useful negative results.** Products leave harmlessly through ordinary separation; apparent labelling is exchange rather than cleaning; only water regenerated by products matters; or analytical bounds cannot localize the oxygen. These outcomes narrow the viable chemistry and prevent unnecessary synthesis or cleanup hardware.

**Confidence.** Published recovery: high. Organic oxygen export as its cause: plausible but unassigned. Informative product/kinetic measurements: moderate after calibration. Attribution to particular pairs: harder. Meaningful process improvement: low and conditional. No energy or lifetime advantage has been established.

## Secondary: validate whether repeated acid assays change later zeolite aging


**Question.** Does inserting the NH3/water/thermal assay between H-CHA steam exposures change the catalyst's subsequent loss trajectory, compared with a sham interruption and uninterrupted aging?

**Reason for reduced priority.** NH3 protection, post-steaming recovery, and testing-induced changes are established. Luo’s reversed-order aging experiment included intermediate NH3 assays in both arms and therefore does not resolve assay-versus-no-assay causality. Ladshaw already predicts aged adsorption responses from a population-change model; prediction across assay schedules is the remaining specific target. The focal SI leaves this specific causal comparison open, but a different protocol alone is not a substantial advance. Expand only if the assay has a reproducible carryover effect after ligand removal and accounting for added exposure, and representing that effect improves predictions at an untested assay spacing or changes a useful material-selection decision.

**First commitment.** One material batch; repeated full assay, matched NH3-free sham, and sacrificial uninterrupted endpoints. Separate pre-assay function from common-terminal-assay capacity. Verify real temperature/water histories and endpoint variance. The first comparison can reject a consequential history effect without a large spectroscopy program.

**Confidence.** An effect is plausible; its direction and magnitude are unknown. The bounded comparison is relatively feasible. A useful dry-recovery window and process benefit remain low-confidence. Dry pulses are a later conditional branch, not the current recommendation.

## Why the other directions have not displaced these

The Pt/LOHC idea faces established support scavenging, hard-to-measure native impurity flux and strong durability benchmarks. Water-rich emissions concepts encountered extensive dosing and cleanup prior art. A [fresh N2O review](working/n2o-mechanism-opportunity.md) also found that the compared models permit overlapping nitrate chemistry: an isotope residence-time bound would not alone select a material intervention. These are retained as documented alternatives, not promoted by adding more hypotheses.

The current order ranks **next experiments for useful knowledge**, not commercial value. The independent [CO/CO2 hydrogenation challenge](working/co2-practical-challenge.md) retained only a conditional CO-transfer diagnostic and did not identify a stronger practical program. A [thermodynamic screen](reviews/styrene-cleaning-thermodynamics.md) and [fresh independent review](reviews/styrene-thermodynamics-independent-review.md) add a conditional coproduct-pressure test with explicit stationary-inventory and product-sink requirements. The [liquid-phase challenge](working/liquid-phase-practical-challenge.md) also did not find a stronger supported intervention. These retained checks define what could justify reconsideration; no additional exploration is scheduled.

Later screens also declined to promote [Ni–Ag transfer to propylene epoxidation](working/propylene-transfer-screen.md) and [lower-recycle solid-acid alkylation](working/stronger-practical-program.md). Both target important problems, but their generic composition/confinement ideas have direct prior art. Remaining working-state questions require a specific informative contrast and useful-output benefit before they merit a substantial program.

The [light-olefin oligomerization challenge](working/oligomerization-value-challenge.md) likewise found direct transport, site-placement, operating-state and tandem precedents. Its [independently checked benchmark audit](reviews/oligomerization-benchmark-accounting.md) shows that high ethylene conversion can coexist with declining desired-product output and quality. That distinction is useful, but neither a new substantial program nor a demonstrated advantage of the proposed material contrast follows from it.

A fresh [Cu-zeolite methane-to-methanol extraction/regeneration screen](working/stronger-direction-challenge.md) found close prior art and no supported full-cycle improvement. It also identified conflicting primary evidence on water-driven Cu reoxidation; the source note and Cu-zeolite topic have been qualified. The negative screen does not strengthen the existing pilots by elimination.

The [methane dehydroaromatization challenge](working/mda-consequential-uncertainty.md) also declines a new active-carbon or regeneration program: carbon participation and prolonged H2 cycling are already established. Recovering retained carbon as useful aromatics is a speculative screening question, subject to complete carbon/H2 balances, aromatic washout controls and the cost of rebuilding activity afterward.

The [independently reviewed carbonylation screen](reviews/carbonylation-benchmark-review.md) retains only a conditional wet-feed versus drying decision. Established dry durability and hydrophobic protection constrain originality. A predictable water penalty smaller than an actual drying burden can be a useful operating result, while providing no reason for a larger mechanistic campaign. Reported CO-free organic selectivity is distinct from overall feed-carbon yield.

The [ammonia-cracking review](reviews/ammonia-hydrogen-inference.md) identifies a conditional catalyst/separation selection test: measure oxygen-free rates at the hydrogen pressure a real device can attain. The published large ideal gains are model extrapolations; oxygen-cofeed promotion and post-purge hydrogen desorption do not uniquely validate them. Direct kinetic membrane precedents prevent a generic new-program claim.

The [propane-dehydrogenation review](reviews/dehydrogenation-fresh-value.md) retains a conditional Ga regeneration study. Its strongest question is whether two aging histories need different regeneration endpoints despite similar bulk carbon or oxygen uptake, and whether that distinction predicts better complete-cycle propylene output on a competitive material. Oxygen enrichment, chlorine-assisted conditioning and separating redox from coke removal already have direct precedents. The recovered SI confirms an incomplete reported oxygen subtraction but does not establish a practically significant error.

## Direction search of 2026-09-16: no stronger direction found

Thirteen unscreened candidates were examined against the literature, with independent review of the three strongest outputs and a portfolio-level review ([outcome](reviews/direction-search-outcome.md)). None reached the [promotion threshold](standards.md#promotion-threshold) now written into the standards. Two candidates survive as documented diagnostics without displacing the studies above: the desulfation-atmosphere comparison for Cu-SSZ-13 (per-cycle Cu speciation versus NH3-containing regeneration; intervention is 2013–2024 prior art, He 2025 reports a minor atmosphere effect at the conversion level) and a water-only exposure/recovery arm for m-ZrO2 ketonization that can attach to the zirconia pilot. A cross-reaction Zr–O water-titration rule spanning ketonization and dehydrogenation was rejected: the ketonization water effect is not pair titration, and Iglesia's patent WO2024177986A2 already transfers water management between dehydrogenation and hydrogenation. The search's lasting products are the corrected standards (promotion threshold; during-reaction titration counts titratable sites, with a [plug-flow model](calculations/titration-front-regime.md)) and the documented negatives. The portfolio statement remains: no substantial lead established; bounded studies retained.

## Readiness and stopping rules

The detailed plans define the first scientific decision, controls, alternatives and expansion gates. Before collecting decision data, each team must confirm material and reactor access, reproduce its reference, measure analytical/ordinary-operation variability, and set a useful contrast and comparison horizon from the actual decision. Choose independent preparation/bed replication to resolve that contrast; repeated measurements of one specimen are not independent preparations. If practical equivalence cannot be resolved with available precision, improve the measurement or report the bounded uncertainty rather than interpret a nonsignificant difference as equality.

Only a result that changes a specified material, operating, cleanup or measurement decision justifies the next stage. A descriptive spectral change, better fitted curve or familiar promotion effect alone does not. Failed recovery establishes persistence under the tested protocol. Unknown molecular assignments, missing source definitions and actual facility/cost information remain explicit research limits; they cannot be removed by editorial revision.
