## Tungsten: the supporting study is complete as a proposal, the program thesis is not selected

The current distinction between process retention and permanent atomic attachment is correct. Positive downstream reaction establishes exported catalytic capacity. A downstream null can reflect decay, a changed matrix or sampling losses; persistent upstream function after washout can still reflect continuously generated mobile species. The retained-inventory/mobile-species counterexample in the proposal makes this identification limit explicit. More hot-transfer measurements cannot guarantee a unique surface/mobile partition.

The practical comparator is strong enough that another active-leachate result or improved dilute reuse would not by itself justify the program. I reread Li's local original-derived SI and visually checked original Table S3. Table S2 reports seven effective cycles with EG carbon yield changing from 68.7% to 66.9%; Table S3 gives 29.5% EG for preleachate plus Ni/M and includes AMT controls. The preleachate was prepared in water/H2 without cellulose. These observations establish retained useful function and exported capacity; the yield ratio is not a homogeneous turnover fraction. The missing main article still prevents a full audit of working-state linkage, recovery handling and any covariates. [[li2020-supporting-information-for-enhanced-ni]] p.8–9.

The [carbon-comparator review](tungsten-carbon-comparator-review.md) and [ash-recovery audit](../calculations/tungsten-ash-recovery-comparator.md), including its [independent review](tungsten-ash-recovery-review.md), must be read together. The carbon studies leave concentration, output and hot-export questions open without showing that extraction caused their decline. The ash patent demonstrates active recovered W in a product-rich continuous laboratory test. Its model-waste origin, short duration, inconsistent W-loading record and incomplete overall recovery do not erase that capability. The absence of working examples in two other inspected patents cannot be generalized to soluble-W recovery.

I also reread El Mohammad's SI and visually checked original Figures S7 and S9. Figure S7 directly shows glycolaldehyde formation from hot mannose feeds. Figure S8 records epimerization; neither separates direct mannose cleavage from conversion after epimerization. Figure S9 shows early carbon loss in the glucose experiment, so a short contact time is not automatically an interpretable window. The proposal has correctly withdrawn the inactive-mannose-reservoir premise. [[mohammad2024-supporting-information-for-identification-of]] p.6–8.

The [process accounting](../calculations/tungsten-process-burden.md) correctly distinguishes gross export, return fraction, inventory, makeup, recovered function and recovery duty. Its equations define measurements, not an economic advantage. No further algebra can determine whether retaining W will improve a real process before the relevant reference burden is measured.

**Closeout boundary:** the immediate deliverable is an interpretable assay and reference comparison, not an anchor library. If a consequential limitation and a chemically specified intervention do not emerge within that finite study, retain or close the exploratory proposal. Do not turn source retrieval, increasingly elaborate mannose assays or atomistic characterization into substitutes for the missing contribution. Missing original protocols limit faithful reproduction; their absence is not evidence that the comparator omitted controls.

## Polymer: pursue one operational distinction, without claiming a function assignment

The clean-feed limitation is real at the reported observation scope: the original Science Figure 4B shows poorer reuse output and rescue after one 400 mg Na/alumina addition before charge 2, with PE alone added before charge 3. I reread the main article's caption and isotopic discussion and the SI semibatch procedure. The benchmark uses 400 mg each of Na/alumina and W/silica initially. Thus the three-charge fresh-solid total is 1.2 g rather than 2.4 g for independent fresh charges, and the [audited calculation](../calculations/polymer-makeup-burden.md) gives B = 2f. This is a conditional material-productivity result, not measured economics or an indefinite supplementation policy. [[conk2024-polyolefin-waste-to-light-olefins]] p.3–5; [[conk2024-supporting-information-for-polyolefin-waste]] p.16–17.

The first campaign fairly addresses handling, solids addition, polymer recovery and changed chain populations. Unsaturated-feed rescue still cannot identify selective initiation loss: substrate supply, W activation, adsorption and polymer access all change. Failed rescue excludes the intervention in its tested domain, not initiation loss itself. An observed operational benefit is worth reporting without forcing a molecular attribution.

The pressure alternative is appropriately weakly directional. The recovered Chen dissertation concerns another catalyst system; its plotted reference is on the increasing part of the ethylene-response curve. The thesis motivates testing a response, not predicting that lowering pressure improves Na/W. Its model-derived metallacycle does not identify a W intermediate. These limits were independently checked in the [pressure review](polymer-pressure-intervention-review.md), and the proposal now accepts them. The corrected dissertation year is 2023; the stable package identifier remains `chen2025`.

The pressure experiment also changes operating mode relative to the published closed-batch rescue. Its first requirement is a reuse/Na-rescue contrast in the actual semibatch mode. Twelve-minute sampling and gas/product storage can prevent rapid reversal from being interpretable; independently replicated complete charges are an acceptable finite comparison. Because ethylene contributes product carbon, gross propylene, mass loss and total carbon closure do not themselves assign polymer-derived accepted output. The decisive reference and beneficial condition need validated carbon attribution before a makeup claim.

**Closeout boundary:** the benchmark/handling/dose stage is common. Pressure and matched-feed tests are alternatives for the first new response; executing both is not necessary to complete the proposal. Choose the feasible one on the available platform, stop a precisely negative intervention, and expand only a reproducible distinction into one prospective catalyst-use prediction. A three-charge saving remains a three-charge result until additional operation tests deferred makeup and W replacement. No dedicated apparatus, full spectroscopy program or new impurity branch follows automatically.

The Conk dissertation body and unambiguous original regeneration results remain source dependencies. They limit overlap and regeneration comparisons, without making every bounded operating result unpublishable or justifying an inferred DME-restoration benchmark. The main Xu alloy article is now read; its 120 h are cumulative daytime operation. Detailed SI carbon/energy accounting is still unresolved. It is a serious external benchmark with different feed, product, thermal and inventory conditions, not a directly matched Na/W superiority comparison. [[xu2026-interfacial-configurational-entropy-tuning-strategy]] p.1.

## Liquid Ti-zeolite: a valid model test can end without a new mechanism

The proposal has identified a coherent conditional question, but no existing unexplained result that establishes headroom for the proposed extended solvent effect. Potts already treats product occupancy and solvent-sensitive rates. I reread the local primary text's kinetics and conclusions: the practical qualifications include epoxide ring opening, separation and long-term stability. That source's differential behavior is not proof of useful concentrated operation, and its first-order substrate response does not generally exclude internal diffusion. [[potts2023-engineering-intraporous-solvent-environments-effects]] p.1, kinetics and Conclusions.

The proposed component experiments, peroxide-equivalent balance, activity/phase controls and transport checks make a meaningful prediction test possible on an existing platform. Neither an inverse product order nor a product spectral band establishes extended solvent organization. Known site occupancy, secondary oxidation, product-induced site geometry and persistent catalyst changes remain alternatives. Carbon and peroxide losses must be smaller than the claimed discrepancy, including uncertainty in identifiable model parameters.

A consequential baseline correction was needed: productive epoxidation and peroxide decomposition generate water, while hydrolysis can consume it. The liquid composition therefore evolves even before any proposed product-induced pore effect. Since the primary prior art already establishes strong water/solvent sensitivity, the baseline must include measured composition, activities and independently constrained known solvent dependence. Holding all component constants fixed despite that established dependence would manufacture an apparent new effect. The proposal now distinguishes fixed model equations/parameters from fixed liquid composition.

A separate procedural correction was needed: the Gate 1 integral run selects the realistic product envelope and is therefore development data. It cannot also be the held-out trajectory used to claim predictive success or failure. The revised Gate 3 now reserves a separate trajectory. Before validation, the experiment must specify which prediction error is large enough to change an actual material or operating decision. An underconstrained model with a very wide prediction interval provides an inconclusive test, not evidence of new chemistry.

**Closeout boundary:** Gates 1–3 are the finite first commitment. A successful known-network prediction closes the proposed excess-effect branch in the tested domain. A consequential, independently reproducible discrepancy permits the already specified Gate 4 controls. Inability to distinguish its molecular cause should close that attribution rather than trigger a general survey of solvent, topology or spectroscopy. There is no requirement to revive the earlier sugar/lactate or wet-VFA ideas.
