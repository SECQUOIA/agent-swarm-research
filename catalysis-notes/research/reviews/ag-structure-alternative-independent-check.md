# Independent check: chlorine-dependent Ag structure as an alternative

2026-09-15. Fresh, bounded independent review of [the root source assessment](ag-chlorine-structure-alternative.md). No experiments performed. This review checked the original PDFs and text; it did not independently reproduce the reported experiments or calculations.

**2026-09-16 source-status correction:** the later [SI boundary review](ag-new-si-boundary.md) directly inspected the Artiglia micrographs and other SI evidence. Its qualified observations supersede the original SI-unavailable statements below. The van Hoof2018 SI package retains an archive but remains unprocessed/unread in the inspected KB; no findings from it are inferred.

## Verdict

**The assessment is factually sound and supports the proposed controls without earning another structural campaign.** Chlorine-dependent Ag restructuring is established in the van Hoof formulation. It is a credible alternative to a purely adsorptive explanation of functional recovery, but neither source establishes that restructuring controls Ni-dependent retention on Cs/Re-promoted Ag.

The Artiglia matched-conversion improvement is correctly expressed as **about 20 percentage points**, despite the preprint prose saying “20%.” Figure 1b, inspected directly in the original PDF, shows selectivities of approximately 50–52% without VC and 70–73% with VC at comparable conversion. These are visual estimates, not digitized data. They support the assessment's wording; they do not support a 20% relative improvement. The separate 38% to 69% comparison changes conversion and must remain separate.

## Source checks

### Van Hoof et al., 2018

Checked [main article](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.8b03331), [local full text](../../literature/papers/hoof2018-reversible-restructuring-of-silver-particles/fulltext.md), and the local original PDF, especially Table 1 and pp. 11795–11798.

| Claim in the assessment | Independent check |
|---|---|
| 8.2 wt% Ag/α-Al2O3; 5% ethylene, 10% oxygen; 20 bar; 225 °C | Correct. Loading is explicitly nominal. Pretreatment was 10% O2/He for 3 h at the same pressure and temperature. |
| After 60 h without VC, adding 1 ppm VC changes mean size 172 → 136 nm in 2 h, subsequently to 119 nm | Correct. Table 1 places 119 nm after a further 60 h with VC; the intervening 20 h value is 127 nm. |
| An additional experiment supports redispersion | Correct as reported in the main text: 175 → 121 nm after the second 60 h interval with VC, with part of the first-stage material removed for microscopy. SI Figure S15 itself was not read. |
| Wetting, surface-energy changes or grain-boundary breakup are possible mechanisms | Fair. They are interpretations, not atomistically established pathways. |
| Pores add little external area and their catalytic role remains uncertain | Correct. Main text reports less than 2% of overall external Ag area and separately proposes a possible oxygen-diffusion effect. |

A useful concrete illustration strengthens the assessment: **the pretreated and 60 h + 60 h VC samples have the same reported mean particle size, 119 nm, while the latter has 2.48% porosity and a 31 nm XRD grain size versus no observed initial voids and a 27 nm grain size.** Thus restoration of one mean does not establish restoration of the whole structure. These differences do not by themselves identify a causal determinant of catalytic performance.

The paper also reports increasing total reaction rate despite an estimated loss of external Ag area during the chlorine-free treatment. A simple “larger particles mean less activity” interpretation would therefore misrepresent this source. The root assessment does not make that mistake.

### Artiglia et al., 2025 preprint

Checked [Research Square v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-7141633/v1), original PDF at `/tmp/ag-aging-prior-art/chlorine-xps-preprint.pdf`, especially main results, methods and Figure 1. This remains a preprint; no journal version or supporting information was established in this review.

- The 15 wt% Ag loading, 0.5 ppm VC kinetic comparison, 38% → 69% reported selectivity, and greater suppression of CO2 than EO are correctly represented. Methods give 230 °C, 15% ethylene and 3% oxygen, with nitrogen balance. These conditions differ substantially from the powder AP-XPS measurements.
- Powder AP-XPS used a 1 mbar, ethylene:oxygen = 2:1 feed, with or without 2 ppm VC. The chlorine signal remained weak after 210 scans over more than 2 h. The stronger chlorine characterization used silver foil and 10 ppm DCE. Treating this as a different sample/moderator combination is essential and correctly done.
- Calculated initial dehydrogenation barriers of 0.71 eV at the selected reconstructed-oxide edge and 1.30 eV on Ag(111) with adsorbed dioxygen agree with the text. Direct epoxidation barriers are 0.41 and 0.24 eV on the two selected oxide reconstructions. These calculations establish plausible pathways on the chosen models, not their unique predominance in the promoted working catalyst.
- The short TEM treatments lasted 3 h. The text reports voids after treatment without VC, greater void formation at 230 °C than 200 °C, and suppressed void formation with 0.5 ppm VC at 230 °C. The actual supporting micrographs were not independently inspected because the SI was unavailable.
- The root assessment fairly separates observed spectral changes from assignments and mechanistic interpretation. In particular, lower intermediate abundance is not a direct measurement of its reaction flux. The preprint's proposed distinction between combustion and direct epoxidation is not sufficient to exclude every common-intermediate mechanism under other conditions.

## Are the controls warranted?

**Yes, with the conditional scope already stated.** Ordinary continuation and sham histories answer whether apparent recovery exceeds the trajectory without the treatment. They also preserve a valid comparison when treatment changes later behavior. Chlorine-driven restructuring gives a concrete physical reason why treatment carryover is possible, but these sources do not establish its duration or importance after the exact proposed recovery protocol.

Separate matched histories are warranted when prior treatment would contaminate the selected comparison; they need not become a mandatory large factorial design. Functional recovery should be defined by the measured rates, selectivity and integrated useful output. No unique atomic state follows from that definition.

Fresh/exposed/recovered morphology comparisons are justified only after a consequential performance contrast survives and when their possible results can change an interpretation or intervention. Particle distributions and representative sampling are appropriate because a mean alone can hide relevant changes. Quench preservation is a measurement-validity requirement, not a reason to presume every ex situ measurement fails. Neither source obligates tomography, synchrotron spectroscopy or an exhaustive search for minor structures before the functional comparison.
