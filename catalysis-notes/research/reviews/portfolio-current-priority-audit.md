## Decision

**No current candidate has earned selection as a substantial original program. Ag remains my first choice for one bounded decision experiment, conditional on a functioning promoted-catalyst platform.** That is a defensible reading of the current recommendation; it is not evidence that Ag is likely to yield the requested substantial contribution.

The next Ag investment should establish whether Ni changes a consequential ordinary-operation loss or fresh useful-output relationship after a credible operating alternative is considered. It should end with a specific material/operation decision or a stop. A program to explain every residual Ni, Re, chlorine or oxygen-state difference is not justified.

This conclusion survives all three corrections: actual Ni-containing aging experiments in 1995; modern lower-chloride and support-dependent operating-stability disclosures; and quantitative oxygen-return isotope prior art. These remove much of the original novelty argument. They leave an empirical opportunity to discover a consequential relationship, without supplying evidence that this relationship exists.

## The affirmative Ag case, and its limit

Ag has three positive features independent of the failure of other screens. There is a reported Ni-associated retention phenomenon; the recent promoted-Ag study reports slow, reversible changes in useful performance; and loss of EO selectivity directly consumes reactants without making the desired product. The modern operating disclosures also provide a real competing intervention. These make a limited comparison more grounded than a wholly conjectural poisoning or regeneration mechanism.

The recent evidence does not yet connect those facts causally. [Jalil 2025](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adt1213) reports a fresh Ni response on a different formulation; [Hwang 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcat.2026.117021), Section 3.3 and Figure 5, reports reversible chloride-dependent behavior without testing the proposed Ni pair. Different formulations and histories cannot establish that Ni preserves the state inferred from Hwang's kinetics. The [historical review](ag-older-ni-prior-art.md) likewise shows why the old table cannot supply the missing mechanism or a normal-operation lifetime benefit.

**The weakest step in the present positive recommendation is the inference that explaining these differences will change a useful decision.** Classifying fresh chemistry, conditioning and persistent loss is sound experimental reasoning. It becomes an original substantial result only when a newly established relationship explains and prospectively predicts a consequential response. No present observation establishes that such an unresolved response survives familiar chloride adjustment, product effects and preparation differences.

A substantial fundamental contribution need not outperform a proprietary commercial catalyst. A validated chemical limit or rule could matter even when it recommends keeping an existing material. Conversely, a historical control correction or another selectivity curve does not become substantial merely because EO is important. Industrial importance and unresolved chemistry are necessary context, not sufficient selection criteria.

## My next investment: one Ag material/operation decision

I would fund the following bounded sequence before a new isotope, spectroscopy or synthesis campaign. Calling it cheap is justified only relative to an already functioning platform. Pressurized EO operation, controlled chloride delivery, suitable analytics and reproducible promoted materials have not been confirmed; the present record cannot rank absolute laboratory costs.

1. **Qualify one matched pair and its operating window.** Use one functioning promoted reference, one justified Ni treatment, and its processing blank. Preserve the relevant promoter inventories and document preparation reproducibility. Establish overlapping useful chloride conditions and net EO/CO2 rates, with transport and balance checks. Distinguish a matched-condition Ni effect from the performance of separately chosen policies. Do not begin a promoter/loading library to force a positive result.
2. **Measure the decision-relevant ordinary behavior over a finite horizon.** Follow useful output at documented normal working conditions. Predeclare the observation horizon and the smallest decision-relevant difference after commissioning establishes uncertainty and response times. Neither the historical accelerated-aging duration nor Hwang's conditioning time supplies a universal duration. A null within that horizon bounds the contrast there; it does not warrant indefinitely extending the run until drift appears.
3. **If there is a consequential difference, challenge it with one operating alternative.** Select a useful lower-chloride setting for the Ni-free reference from its measured curve, or one documented alternative if lower chloride provides no useful window. Compare integrated net EO output per initial catalyst or Ag inventory, reactant losses, temperature and intervention costs over the same observed period. Retain ordinary continuation so elapsed-time recovery is not credited to a treatment. Do not forgive a present disadvantage using a distant fitted crossover.
4. **Stop for a selection review.** Identify the single unresolved chemical distinction that would now change the preferred material or policy. State one prospective prediction for a new duration, product burden or recovery history before selecting the measurement that tests it. Add only the contrast needed for that prediction. A complete elementary mechanism is unnecessary.

The first comparison can proceed without a full Ni × Re × support × chloride factorial. But a practical claim that Ni adds value requires testing the relevant competing choice; it cannot rest only on equal chloride settings that favor one material. A causal Ni claim still requires matched conditions and processing. Those are different comparisons, and neither substitutes for the other.

### Results that change priority

| Observed outcome | Investment decision |
|---|---|
| No resolved useful Ni effect or relevant drift within the agreed horizon | Close this bounded comparison. Do not commission a new structural campaign to explain an unresolved performance difference. |
| A familiar chloride adjustment reproduces the useful output, without a new predictive relationship | Record the operational result and demote Ag. This is useful information but does not earn the substantial program. |
| Ni retains a consequential advantage under the competing policy, and a supported state/exposure relationship predicts its survival or failure under one new history | Promote a focused mechanism-and-intervention program. The observed advantage alone is an entry signal; the prospective prediction earns expansion. |
| A measured recovery relationship predicts when operating intervention replaces Ni, including its production cost, under a new history | Promote a focused operating-state program even if Ni is unnecessary. Reproducing known recovery without that new relationship remains insufficient. |
| The only advantage requires an arbitrary severe upset, an assumed recurrence frequency, or extrapolated long-run benefits | Withhold the practical claim and demote the proposed retention program. A separate fundamental question would need its own justification. |
| Reproducing the pair requires open-ended synthesis or new facility development | Reassess the allocation before that expansion. This review does not authorize a large setup commitment merely to preserve Ag's ranking. |

## Comparison with the retained alternatives

These are comparisons of the existing proposals, not verdicts on whole fields. Expertise fit is strong throughout: quantitative kinetics, working-state populations and reaction engineering connect Ag and zirconia to Iglesia; Al siting, acid-site counting and durability connect CHA to Gounder; component cooperation and confinement connect the alternatives to both groups. Fit alone does not select a program.

To move above a diagnostic pilot, the identified chemistry must predict sustained function under a new composition or removal change better than the existing measured-water account. Ag is my first bounded choice because it begins with an observed selective-output loss and a material intervention already associated with retention, then asks whether a concrete operating alternative makes that intervention unnecessary. Zirconia first needs to show that its missing oxygen chemistry controls an unresolved prediction; CHA first needs to show consequential carryover; polymer first needs a verified quantitative baseline and identifiable damage pathway. This is an affirmative information argument for Ag, not an inference that the last surviving field must be promising. Without access and setup information, it is not a defensible cost-adjusted ranking of these pilots.

The latest negative screens do not strengthen Ag:

- [Oligomerization](../working/oligomerization-value-challenge.md): transport, external acidity, operating state and regeneration already have direct precedents. The [benchmark audit](oligomerization-benchmark-accounting.md) shows a real useful-product durability problem, but does not demonstrate that the proposed intermediate external activity solves it. Reopening requires a matched existing-material contrast with improved integrated accepted-product output and retained branching, beyond the close directed-siting work.
- [MDA](../working/mda-consequential-uncertainty.md): retained-carbon participation does not itself select a new intervention. Reopening the narrow carbon-harvesting idea requires enough isotope-resolved aromatic recovery to survive a full-cycle carbon/H2/productivity bound and subsequent re-induction costs. A label exchange or methane-rich regeneration pulse is insufficient.
- [Peroxide tandem oxidation](../working/peroxide-tandem-challenge.md): strong integration and solvent/material interventions are already reported. Reopening requires a measured consequential zeolite-associated loss at the actual coupled reference composition. A better accounting denominator or an unresolved Ti assignment does not establish that burden.

No candidate should be promoted automatically if Ag stops. A finite pilot can return a useful negative result while the portfolio still has no selected substantial program.

## Evidence boundary and remaining source work

For the selection-changing Ag evidence, it reread the retained primary patent experimental text and tables and the Esposito SI S9 text, alongside the independent quantitative isotope review. Numerical isotope equations were taken from the existing source-checked equation audit; this review did not independently rederive them. Other fields are compared at the evidence level documented by their existing reviews, without silently promoting unread abstracts to full-text evidence.

The immediate literature discriminator is specific: do the already queued chlorine-deposition/removal and product/chloride studies, and the industrial chlorination study, already predict the proposed recovery/retention response under the relevant promoted formulation? Relevant identifiers are `10.1016/j.jcat.2019.09.037`, `10.1002/chem.201801356`, and `10.1021/acscatal.4c01764`. A mechanistic account covering that response would narrow Ag further; source silence would leave the experiment open without proving novelty. The main Esposito article remains needed for the optional isotope branch, not as a prerequisite to the retention comparison. Root is handling these existing source requests with the sole literature worker; this audit identifies no new source for intake.

**Portfolio wording supported by this audit:** no substantial program selected; Ag first for a bounded material/operation decision, with explicit exit conditions; zirconia, CHA and polymer retained as conditional alternatives. The next evidence required is a consequential observed contrast and one successful prospective prediction, not a longer list of measurements.
