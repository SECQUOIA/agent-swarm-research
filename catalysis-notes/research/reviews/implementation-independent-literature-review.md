# Independent verification of the literature-note corrections

2026-09-16. Fresh, bounded review of the existing literature curation requested during implementation. No new research direction or literature search was started. The reviewer did not edit the knowledge base or run its maintenance check; all source-note corrections were routed to the sole literature maintainer.

## Scope and method

The review checked ten rewritten notes against their retained original PDFs: Conk main and SI, Iyer2023, Napolitano2025, Dimian2019, and five fixed samples from the [126-note screening inventory](post-upload-note-quality-inventory.md): Kinnunen2018, Zhou2025 CoS-1, Sadokhina2017 gas composition, Lu2026 and Qiu2025. These samples were selected before reading their rewritten notes, to cover both misleading reference fragments and benchmark/modeling risks across the existing portfolio. They are a purposive sample, not a statistical estimate of the accuracy of all 126 notes.

The original PDFs were extracted independently for comparison; Conk Figure 4 and its caption were also inspected visually. Checks covered catalyst/material identity, operating conditions, experimental versus modeled findings, denominators, time and regeneration basis, page locators, mechanism qualifications and practical limits. Itoh2014's original Eq.3 was additionally checked to verify the cross-paper hydrogen-recovery comparison. Removing a repeated phrase or passing `KB_CHECK` was not accepted as scientific validation.

## Corrections caught during implementation

The fresh review found one major cross-paper attribution error in an intermediate rewrite: Qiu2025 had acquired Qiu2026's O2-cofeed results, 76.4% ideal-model illustration and 14/57/633 model ratios. The maintainer removed these from the 2025 note and restored its actual experimental domain. The corrected note was re-read against the 2025 original. The review also required explicit credit for its commercial-catalyst pure-NH3 validation, avoiding an overbroad claim that all concentrated-feed evidence was merely projected.

Other corrections addressed a still-ambiguous Dimian cost boundary, Napolitano's equation locator, Conk's main-versus-SI citation, Lu's cumulative time wording, Kinnunen's volumetric loading and matched-volume comparison, Sadokhina's monolith-versus-powder flow rates, and Zhou's page locators and description of cobalt as a metal.

## Conclusion and limits

The ten-note review is complete. The identified source-attribution, accounting, protocol and locator corrections were applied by the maintainer and rechecked. No unresolved material error was found in the sampled final findings and comparisons.

This review supports the accuracy of these sampled notes for the stated comparisons. It does not certify every claim in all 126 rewritten notes or the entire knowledge base, and it does not validate any proposed experiment or practical benefit. Chemical hypotheses, platform feasibility, long-term durability, device boundaries and unavailable supplements retain the uncertainties stated in the existing research plans. No new idea or program is recommended here.
