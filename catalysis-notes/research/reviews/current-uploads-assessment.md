Three numerical corrections deserve particular attention. Li's Ni/M-plus-preleachate experiment gives 29.5% EG yield, while its main article gives 27.1% for Ni/M alone. The full 29.5% cannot be called leachate-derived activity, and the 2.4-percentage-point difference lacks the matched uncertainty needed for a firm catalytic-contribution claim. Zhang reports combined EG+PG yield declining from 70.4% to 61.4% over five cycles; the abstract's stability description must not replace the main result. Zhao's 1:1 catalyst/polymer mixture gives exactly half the reported water uptake per gram; under total-mixture normalization, dilution alone predicts that ratio. This is a denominator caveat, not proof that the catalytic promotion is absent.

The new originals do not change the existing Fang cumulative-output integration, the water two-pool identifiability derivation, or the cyclic output/time arithmetic. They strengthen the limits on what those calculations establish. They do not supply an improved catalyst, a closed practical material balance, or validated equipment access.
