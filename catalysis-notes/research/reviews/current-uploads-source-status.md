## Additional known artifact and version limits

Missing main-article metadata is not the complete description of source limitations. The earlier [completion ledger](program-development-literature-completion.md#retained-sources-with-reading-artifact-or-version-limits) preserves separate missing supplements for the read Xu liquid-alloy paper, Gao carbonate-shell paper, Brody looping paper, Mougel activation paper, Young carbonate manuscript, Pang 2021 amine paper and Zhang 2023 Ga-assisted CF4 paper. These remain source-specific limits unless a later retained supplement explicitly resolves them; they are not evidence that the studies lacked controls. The [current correction report](current-uploads-literature-corrections.md) records the newly identified Zhao, Kim, Murphy and Chi supplement requests and their actual outcomes.

The Young and Pang accepted manuscripts are readable even when a separate publisher version is not stored. LIDSEN HTML originals are readable despite unavailable PDF versions. The NSF award record describes proposed work and does not establish successful project results; no unseen final report is presumed to exist. Such version/format boundaries must not be conflated with unread main articles.
