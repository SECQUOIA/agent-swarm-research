### The polymer calculation needs a substantive update

For the disclosed three-charge sequence, initial catalyst solids are 0.8 g and the single later addition is 0.4 g. Three completely fresh charges would consume 2.4 g. Using the existing definition of mean accepted polymer-derived output `f`, the conditional material-productivity ratio is therefore **`B = 2f`**. The earlier illustrative policy of adding Na before *each* later charge gives `B = 1.5f` over three charges; its algebra is valid, but it is no longer the closest known comparator.

Neither expression establishes a measured economic benefit. The published bars do not close polymer-carbon attribution, retained inventories, complete cycle time or all resource burdens. The updated result strengthens the existing makeup alternative and raises the bar for the proposed intervention.

Science SI's **80.1% apparent scale-up conversion** must likewise be separated from the patent's **69.8% apparent conversion**. Both arise from source-specific residue accounting. The new SI does not prove which patent number is erroneous, or that the two documents describe identical experimental runs. See the polymer audit for the original masses and collection assumptions.

## Literature-note corrections are a priority

The source files are valuable, but part of the note layer needs scientific curation. A text scan found **126 `paper.md` files sharing the same generic findings/limitations template**; the [linked inventory](post-upload-note-quality-inventory.md) preserves the screening rule and affected notes. This count identifies a review set, not 126 proven scientific errors and not proof that nobody read the sources. However, inspection found concrete failures:

- **Conk SI:** a cited reference's title is presented as the file's central outcome; the actual makeup and scale-up results are omitted.
- **Kinnunen2018:** a keywords/reference-list fragment is presented as a central outcome.
- **Sadokhina2017:** general support background substitutes for the main NO/water result.
- **Lu, Xu, Qiu2025 and Coelho notes:** generic prose omits the operating domains and distinctions necessary for fair comparisons.
- **Iyer2023:** the summary needs the explicit upper-bound qualification on TFE uptake.
- **Napolitano2025:** the purity range and recovery denominator require correction; maxima from incompatible conditions must not be combined.
- **Dimian2019:** distinguish roughly 73% hot-utility reduction, 48% annual energy-cost reduction, and 36% reaction-section annualized-cost reduction. These are different quantities and boundaries.

**Recommended repair order:** first the sources that control the current Ag/polymer decisions and the definite overstatements above; then other templated notes actually used in comparisons. Each repaired note should state the material, operating conditions, observed result, denominator/time basis, interpretation and limitation, with useful page locators. Do not substitute a title, introductory claim or reference-list fragment for a finding.

A successful `KB_CHECK` establishes structural and deterministic note compliance. It cannot establish that the chosen text supports a scientific claim. Likewise, the final intake check's `READ_UNCITED=168` is a synthesis-maintenance signal, not evidence that 168 papers are scientifically unreliable or that all require forced citation.
