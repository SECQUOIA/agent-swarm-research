## Primary-source checks and retained limits

The substantive additions were checked against original PDFs, using text extraction directly from those originals:

- **Peng SI:** pp.21–22, Figures S15–S16 explicitly compare 0.5 wt% Mo at 800 °C with 2 wt% at 700 °C. Reactor/assignment context was checked in the retained SI text, pp.2–14. These controls support the species assignment but do not isolate site density from temperature or determine obligatory steady-state aromatic flux.
- **Koninckx SI:** pp.2–3 specify cross-study conversion of total acidity to estimated Brønsted acidity and the measured/modeled conversions. The stabilization relation above C9 is a model assumption. No new statistical confidence interval or mechanistic uniqueness is claimed.
- **Nozik SI:** p.3 gives the 5 min-to-10 h rate-retention comparison. Sections S.9–S.10, pp.23–29, contain reactant-transfer estimates and restricted-space-time/deactivation sensitivity analysis. Those controls do not establish universal absence of product transport or retained-oligomer effects.
- **Van Minnebruggen:** original pp.4–5, Tables 2–3 and regeneration discussion, specify feed, initial inventory, formulation and cumulative yield. Product mass, accepted C8 mass and complete-cycle productivity are not interchangeable.
- **Lin:** original p.6, Figure 7 and conclusions, give the pilot history and operating ranges. This is a source-reported performance record with variable conditions, not an independent complete-process comparison.

The [peroxide accounting](../calculations/peroxide-coupling-accounting.md) was retained unchanged: its numerical examples are explicitly hypothetical, and its balances distinguish gross generation, inventories, net target product and destruction. No new test suite is needed for these prose corrections.

## What this pass does not establish

This is a targeted repair of known contradictions and consequential newly available controls, not an exhaustive rereading of every source cited in the retired alternatives. It does not prove that the ideas contain no remaining scientific uncertainty. None of these nonselected proposals has acquired an experimentally demonstrated practical advantage through the uploads or these edits.

Two definite KB-note concerns were sent to the coordinating agent for the sole literature maintainer: Peng SI's matched-conversion summary needs the changed-temperature qualification, and Tengco's note used a reference-list fragment as a central result. This pass did not edit the KB or run its maintenance command. No additional literature was discovered or requested.
