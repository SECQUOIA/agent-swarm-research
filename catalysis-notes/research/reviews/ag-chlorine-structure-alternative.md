# Chlorine-driven Ag restructuring as a competing explanation

2026-09-15. Root source assessment, subsequently checked in a [fresh independent review](ag-structure-alternative-independent-check.md). No experiments performed. This qualifies the [Ni-dependent retention program](../programs/ag-selective-oxygen-use.md); it does not select another campaign.

## Decision

**Restored EO performance need not mean restoration of the original catalyst structure.** Chlorine can change Ag morphology while changing surface chemistry. A recovery experiment therefore establishes recovery of a measured function under its stated protocol. It does not by itself distinguish chlorine coverage, redistribution of oxygen, restructuring of Ag or a Ni–Re interaction.

This is established competing physics, not a new hypothesis that earns a structural campaign. Gross Ag morphology belongs among the alternatives only if a consequential performance contrast survives the ordinary-operation and competing-policy gates. The original preparation-size comparison cannot exclude subsequent restructuring.

## Direct prior evidence: van Hoof 2018

The existing [open primary article](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.8b03331), [local full text](../../literature/papers/hoof2018-reversible-restructuring-of-silver-particles/fulltext.md), reports nominal 8.2 wt% Ag/α-Al2O3 under 5% ethylene/10% oxygen at 20 bar and 225 °C. These are pressurized catalytic measurements but not the proposed Cs/Re/Ni formulation. Main text pp.11795–11798 and Table 1 were inspected here.

Without VC, particles grew and developed internal voids. After 60 h, adding 1 ppm VC decreased the mean particle diameter from 172 to 136 nm in 2 h; 60 additional hours with VC brought it to 119 nm. An additional experiment supported the redispersion trend. The paper proposes changes in surface energy, wetting or grain-boundary breakup but does not uniquely identify the atomistic route. The main text reports little added external area from the pores and leaves their catalytic role uncertain.

Thus, morphology can evolve on a timescale that overlaps conditioning. Particle-size recovery is not equivalent to proof of productive-site recovery. Table 1 makes the distinction concrete: the final 119 nm mean matches the pretreated mean, but reported porosity is 2.48% and the XRD grain size is 31 nm, versus no listed pores and a 27 nm grain size before reaction. Conversely, recovered catalytic performance cannot be assigned solely to reversible adsorption. The causal contribution of morphology on a fully promoted catalyst remains to be tested.

## Additional evidence: Artiglia 2025 preprint

Source: *Reaction Mechanism and Role of Chlorine in Ethylene Epoxidation Revealed by in situ XPS*, [Research Square v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-7141633/v1), posted August 12, 2025. The CC-BY original was retrieved; main results and methods were read. The SI was subsequently recovered and read, with relevant original figures checked in a [fresh independent review](ag-new-si-boundary.md). Main and SI are now centrally archived; a journal version was not established.

The detailed lattice/dioxygen O1s fits in the SI concern foil with DCE. Powder O1s difference spectra chiefly support OH/water assignments, with overlap and normalization limits; a negative support-region difference is not direct evidence of removed support oxygen. The temperature-programmed retained-carbon control produces CO2 without a visible EO increase under that sequence, but does not uniquely exclude a shared earlier intermediate during steady catalysis.

The preprint proposes distinct routes for direct epoxidation and combustion and argues against a common oxametallacycle account. Treat that as its mechanistic interpretation. It does not establish a universal mechanism across silver formulations. The AP-XPS pressure gap is especially large, while the powder and foil experiments also differ in surface preparation and moderator.

## Consequences for experimental design

1. Keep the recovery claim functional: specify which rates, selectivity, response times and useful-output integral recover. Do not call this a return to an identical material state.
2. Preserve ordinary continuation and sham histories. Chlorine treatment can change the catalyst's later trajectory; a crossover on the same bed may retain that treatment's structural history. Use separate matched histories when this carryover would invalidate the selected contrast.
3. If a consequential residual contrast warrants morphology measurements, compare fresh, exposed and recovered aliquots from the relevant histories. Quantify a distribution rather than treating a single mean or selected micrograph as an active-site count. Sampling/quenching must preserve enough of the relevant state to make the comparison meaningful.
4. A matching final particle-size distribution does not exclude a minority interfacial change, and a different size distribution does not prove causation. Add a structural measurement only when its outcomes would change a particular interpretation or intervention. Do not expand automatically to tomography or operando spectroscopy.

**Confidence:** high that chlorine-dependent Ag restructuring is real in the 2018 formulation; moderate that it is an important confound for a functional recovery interpretation; unestablished that it controls the proposed Ni-dependent retention. The new preprint strengthens the alternative mechanism to consider but does not increase confidence in a practical Ni benefit.
