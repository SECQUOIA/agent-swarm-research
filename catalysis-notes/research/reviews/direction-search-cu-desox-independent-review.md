# Independent review: desulfation-atmosphere diagnostic for Cu-SSZ-13 (direction-search screen)

2026-09-16. Fresh review of [`working/direction-search/cu-pd-zeolite-durability.md`](../working/direction-search/cu-pd-zeolite-durability.md) against [`standards.md`](../standards.md). I did not write the screen. No experiments were performed; nothing under `literature/` or the screen itself was changed. Labels: (E) established from a source I read, (I) inference, (J) my judgment. Each source states what I actually read.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The precise open boundary survives: no retrieved study measures per-cycle irreversible Cu loss (EPR-active Cu, CO-titratable dimers, H2-TPR CuOx) as a function of desulfation atmosphere on one sulfated material. But the boundary is narrower than the screen states, for three reasons the screen missed or misread: (i) the with/without-reductant comparison of sulfur removal and conversion recovery already exists in Cummins' 2013-priority patents, not only as a 2024 claim; (ii) He/Ding 2025 did regenerate with and without NO + NH3 and reports a minor atmosphere effect on conversion; (iii) two unread papers (Shen 2019; Gao/Mossin/Janssens/Vennestrøm 2025) may already contain Cu-speciation-resolved regeneration comparisons. The practical dosing stake is smaller than presented; the accelerated-aging-protocol stake is real but is protocol knowledge. I agree with "bounded diagnostic, not a program", disagree with "clearer operating decision than wet methane", and rank it alongside the wet-methane diagnostic, below Ag and polymer.

**Does a study already answer the question?** No, not at the level the screen defines (per-cycle irreversible Cu accounting versus atmosphere on one material). But the screen's "no matched lean-only comparator" is wrong for sulfur removal and conversion: Cummins 2013 Figs. 3–5 and He 2025 Fig. S5 are matched comparators at that level, and He 2025 reports a minor atmosphere effect, which is an H3-like prior at the conversion level. (I) Hammershøi 2018a (SCR-gas regeneration: irreversible loss fully accounted by residual S) and Deka 2025 (static air: 20% loss persisting after 80% S removal) already form a crude cross-study contrast in the H1 direction, confounded by material, sulfation route and inventory; the screen does not use it.

## 2. Hypothesis and first campaign

- **Two-sided and falsifiable:** yes. H1/H2/H3 exhaust the sign; H4 lists the right artifacts. (J) Sound structure.
- **Wrong practical comparator.** In a DPF-regeneration event the SCR inlet carries engine-out NOx whether or not urea is dosed. The calibration alternative is "lean + NO, dosing off", not "lean only" (arm A). Arm A tests chemistry; it does not test the decision. Add arm A' = lean + NO without NH3, or replace A.
- **Sulfation route starves the mechanism.** The irreversible pathway (Deka 2025; Mandal 2024) concerns CuSO4 clusters formed from sulfate on Cu at ≥400 °C or from impregnated sulfate. SO2 at 250 °C in SCR feed forms mainly ammonium (bi)sulfate (He 2025 SO2-TPD; Jangjou 2018), which leaves below 400 °C. The primary arm may therefore enter desulfation with little of the species whose fate is in question, and a null would be uninformative. (J) Make the 400 °C SO2 exposure (Rani 2025's choice) or an SO3/H2SO4 exposure the primary arm, and the 250 °C SCR-feed exposure the reference.
- **Per-event framing.** Deka 2025 shows a one-event loss set by the first calcination of a heavily sulfated (nominal S/Cu 1) sample; growth with cycles rests on Rani 2025 (abstract level). The screen should say so.
- **Precision.** Deka 2025 reports no replicate uncertainty for the EPR double integration. (J) Quantitative EPR on Cu-CHA is usually reproducible to several percent relative between preparations, so the screen's 5-percentage-point target sits near the noise floor; 10 pp is a defensible minimum. The H2-TPR CuOx feature (Deka: ~260 °C) neighbours Z2Cu reduction (~348 °C) and is semi-quantitative. CO titration counts dimers, not CuOx. TOR at 200 °C to ±15% is achievable in a differential bed. The realistic discriminating readouts are TOR and EPR, with H2-TPR as support.
- **Exotherm.** (J, estimate) Complete oxidation of 500 ppm excess NH3 (ΔH ≈ −317 kJ mol−1) in a gas with heat capacity ≈ 32 J mol−1 K−1 gives an adiabatic rise of about 5 K; SCR of 500 ppm NO adds a similar amount. In a diluted bed with an internal thermocouple this is small against 550 °C. The exotherm-matched arm is cheap insurance; the main feasibility risks are reproducible sulfation to matched S/Cu and matched sulfur speciation, not temperature.
- **SO3 omission.** Field sulfur reaches the SCR largely as DOC-generated SO3/H2SO4 below 400 °C, and Hammershøi 2018a shows SO3 raises the irreversible part at 200 °C. If the primary arm is made Cu-sulfate-rich as above, a first campaign without SO3 remains decision-relevant for the mechanism; it does not support a deployment claim, which the screen already concedes.
- **Size.** Four arms × three sacrifice points × ≥3 replicates, plus three sham arms, an exotherm-matched arm and a second batch, each with EPR, CO titration, H2-TPR, 27Al NMR and kinetics, is roughly 50 specimens. That is not smaller than the Ag first commitment. The screen calls it small; I do not.

## 3. Practical stake

- **Dosing during deSOx.** Reductant-assisted deSOx is prior art three times over (Cummins 2013 patents with data; Kumar 2016 and BASF 2016; Cummins 2024 ANR > 1). H1 confirms current practice and changes nothing. H3 changes nothing (dosing chosen on slip/N2O grounds). Only H2 changes a calibration (an NH3-off window in the 350–500 °C part of the ramp), and it would conflict with the documented sulfur-removal benefit; He 2025's minor atmosphere effect on recovery lowers its prior. (J) The expected calibration stake is small and asymmetric.
- **Accelerated-aging protocol.** Real and specific: Deka 2025 and Rani 2025 both ask for cycling protocols, and whether the lab desulfation step must contain NH3/NO to reproduce field Cu speciation is exactly the kind of question a matched comparison settles. This is protocol knowledge, the same category as the CHA assay-history secondary, which the portfolio audit treats as valuable but not a practical regeneration opportunity.
- **Formulation interaction** (Al-rich/high-Cu): He 2025 already establishes the compositional advantage; its interaction with desulfation atmosphere is speculative and should not be counted as stake.

## 4. Standing against the portfolio

- **Below Ag (first priority)** and below the polymer bounded study. Ag starts from an observed selective-output loss, a material intervention with a genuine open material/operation decision, and an economically direct outcome. This screen starts from a fresh mechanism but an intervention already claimed and practiced; its most likely outcome (H3) leaves practice unchanged.
- **Alongside the wet-methane diagnostic**, not above. The screen claims a clearer operating decision; I disagree because the decision arm (dosing on/off with NO present) is not what the design tests, and because He 2025 already supplies an atmosphere-insensitivity prior at the conversion level. In its favour against wet methane: the application envelope is established (regulatory useful life; field post-mortems agree on sulfur aging), and a null is more useful here (it fixes aging-protocol design). Against: larger campaign, prior-art density, and two unread papers that could narrow it further.
- (J) Its natural home is the zeolite-durability-and-measurement program as a protocol-knowledge option, not a new portfolio entry.

## 5. Promotion and closure

- **Promote to a substantial program only if:** at matched residual S and closed sulfur balance, the NH3-on (with NO) and NH3-off (with NO) arms differ in EPR-active Cu loss by ≥10 pp or in 200 °C TOR by ≥15% at cycle 6 across independent replicates and two batches, the sham and exotherm-matched arms are flat, and a frozen prospective prediction (different peak temperature or SO3-rich sulfation) succeeds. An H2 result of that size is the only outcome that changes a calibration.
- **Close if:** cycle-3 arms differ by less than replicate scatter with closed sulfur balance (H3); the exotherm-matched lean arm reproduces the NH3-arm result (H4b); sulfation cannot be matched within modest effort; or reading Shen 2019, Gao 2025 (ChemCatChem) or He 2025 SI Fig. S5/S17 shows a Cu-speciation-resolved atmosphere comparison already exists.
