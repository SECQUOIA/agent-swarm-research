# Independent review after the current literature uploads

2026-09-17. Fresh review of existing proposals only. No new research directions, literature discovery or experiments were undertaken. This review independently checked decisive original-source text before comparing the branch audits. It separates a useful bounded experiment from a substantial original program.

## Decision

**The new literature requires scientific corrections and a narrower account of originality. It does not justify promoting any current candidate to an unrestricted program.** Water management can remain the first bounded feasibility priority, with cyclic oxides a conditional reserve, but that ordering is justified by the next affordable information gain and existing functional motivation. It is not evidence that either already has a strong, experimentally secured original mechanistic thesis.

The reason to retain water first is specific: the low-dose separately mixed additive has an already reported sustained-output benefit, and late addition after common conditioning could test an operational boundary with useful formulation-level consequences. Neither another geometry–stability correlation nor another water-history experiment would suffice. The existing identifiability counterexample remains consequential: fitting outlet-water responses does not uniquely determine exposure at the cobalt sites. A substantial program still needs a consequential prospective functional prediction or independent evidence that resolves the claimed mechanism.

Cyclic oxides address an unvalidated process translation, namely substituting steam for the Ar purges used in the long-duration experiment. That is a credible process question, but neither consequential coating damage in five minutes nor useful prevention is established. Its generic prevention-versus-repair rationale now has more direct prior art. Retain only the specified steam/LSF timing and material/function comparison; do not call carbonate protection or delayed non-recovery a new general discovery.

Tungsten remains on hold. The new originals strengthen retained-W comparators and describe specific chemical stabilization hypotheses. They make a generic retention study less original while leaving the repository's missing causal intervention unresolved. Polymer and liquid-zeolite work should remain bounded by their existing-platform and model-transfer conditions. None of these results is a reason to reopen closed alternative searches.

### Independent check of the Li cumulative-loss caution

Figure 4b was visually inspected: the seven concentrations are 28, 22, 19, 21, 18, 20 and 19 ppm, summing to 147 ppm. If ppm is treated as mg/L, each collected liquid volume is 40 mL, and initial W is the nominal `0.15 g × 20 wt% = 30 mg`, the summed liquid W is `147 mg/L × 0.040 L = 5.88 mg`, or **19.6% of nominal initial W**. Using the Figure 3 caption's 100 mL instead gives **49%**. These calculations are conditional reconstructions, not a measured cumulative balance; collected volume, actual loading, washes and handling remain unresolved. The original's 100 mL caption must not be silently reconciled with its 40 mL methods.

Thus low endpoint ppm and nearly retained EG yield do not establish negligible catalyst-inventory loss. This materially sharpens the retention comparator, while not proving a mobile active species or supplying the repository's missing chemical intervention. The hold remains appropriate for that separate reason.

These comparisons do not transfer an observed rate, threshold or mechanistic assignment across different supports, pressures, temperatures or salt compositions. They narrow novelty and improve controls; they do not validate the repository hypotheses.

## Assessment against the selection standards

The water question remains scientifically important and its limited intervention is feasible in a capable existing FT laboratory. Confidence in reproducing some formulation response is stronger than confidence in identifying sustained local-water protection, and stronger than confidence in improving an optimized process. The first experimental commitment is justified only at the documented finite scope. A measurement calibration that cannot support an independent functional prediction should end the mechanistic expansion rather than invite an indefinitely growing collection of outlet curves.

For cyclic oxides, confidence in carbonate/hydroxide perturbation is moderate; confidence in consequential loss from this coating at the proposed duty is low. Confidence in an informative comparison is conditional on representative steam switching, intermediate material measurements, recovery of transported Li and verification of reset chemistry. Practical benefit is uncertain because extra gas, purge duration and restoration may consume the available output gain. The prevention-value calculation retains its status as a planning bound on an observed Ar-aged yield decline, not an estimate of steam damage.

For tungsten, the stronger original comparators make the hold more defensible. They do not resolve whether the current proposal has a chemically selective intervention that predicts productive cleavage and W export. Hot-transfer measurements can constrain exported catalytic function but cannot supply that missing thesis merely by being technically careful.

The selection standards do not require success before a first test. They do require a consequential contribution beyond prior work. The available documents identify conditional routes to such a contribution, not already secured substantial programs. The appropriate recommendation is to complete the defined decision experiments on an existing platform if resources allow, with expansion contingent on their stated outcomes. There is no need to generate new ideas within this audit.

## Documentation and source-note quality

Present-tense claims that these primary works are unavailable or unread must be replaced in active proposals, the decision brief and current status pages. Historical reviews should remain dated records with a link to the superseding audit.

A retained, processed PDF does not make every curated assertion sound. The decisive notes need conditions and inference limits, not abstract-only descriptions. Specific examples found here are the original's identifiable Hanssen authors despite a retained “unknown authors” metadata field, the methane-specific and post-water-treatment scope of the SSITKA result, and Zhang's actual glycol decline. These were reported to the lead for the sole literature maintainer; this reviewer did not modify the knowledge base.
