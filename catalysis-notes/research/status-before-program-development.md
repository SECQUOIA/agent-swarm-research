An [independently reviewed reversible-cycle check](reviews/reversible-cleaning-independent-review.md) further narrows the coproduct hypothesis: with complete matched local products, reverse cleaning alone predicts greater steady free-pair loss in dry operation. A wet-only effect can arise from forward-cleaning inhibition or unmatched coproducts; it is not sufficient evidence of thermodynamic reversal.
