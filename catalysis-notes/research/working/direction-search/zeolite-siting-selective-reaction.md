# Direction screen: Al-pair control of Zn/Ga cation retention across regeneration in light-alkane aromatization

Date: 2026-09-16. Candidate territory: zeolite reactions where framework Al pairing, heteroatom identity, external passivation or void environment could resolve a consequential uncertainty in a reaction the repository has not screened. This is a screen, not a selected program; selection happens in `research/decision-brief.md` after independent review.

**Decision: do not promote to a substantial program. Retain as a bounded, materials-conditional diagnostic** on Zn/MFI (Ga/MFI as a secondary check): does a synthesis-controlled paired-Al fraction at fixed Si/Al predict how much exchanged metal returns to intrazeolitic divalent cation sites after each oxidative regeneration, and does that change cumulative BTX per cycle? Which cation state carries the dehydrogenation flux is already established for propane by Bell, Lercher and Lobo/Xu, and for ethane the pairing question is weaker because both Ga hydride states have similar turnover. The regeneration-cycle dependence on pairing is a published suggestion, not a result, but the practical stake of ethane aromatization is unestablished commercially and the expected outcome is a synthesis rule, not a demonstrated process gain.

## 1. Candidate choice and practical problem

### Why this reaction and not the others in the territory

- **Light-alkane aromatization on Ga/Zn-MFI (chosen).** The reaction network is well mapped by Iglesia (1990s), Bell, Lercher and Lobo/Xu, which makes the remaining boundary narrow and checkable. The process runs with repeated oxidative regeneration, so cation re-speciation across cycles is the operational variable, and Gounder's Co2+-titrated Na/TPA synthesis series is the specific tool nobody has applied to it.
- **MTO/CHA Al pairing:** Gounder's group and others already connect paired Al in CHA and MFI to dehydration, cracking and oligomerization/hydrogen transfer (local notes [[bickel2021-developing-quantitative-synthesis-structure-function]], [[kester2021-effects-of-brnsted-acid-site]], and the MFI acid-density/transport work in `literature/topics/c1-and-hydrocarbon-conversion.md`). A further MTO pairing study would be incremental.
- **Aromatics alkylation/transalkylation and n-butene skeletal isomerization:** mature commercial catalysis whose remaining uncertainties are feed-impurity and regeneration engineering, not site placement; a new siting rule would not change an operating decision without a plant partner.
- **Ethanol dehydration:** Iglesia, Bhan and Gounder have resolved the confined-dimer and water-inhibition kinetics (`literature/topics/confinement-solvation-transport-and-deactivation.md`); no consequential uncertainty remains at the site level.
- **Pt/zeolite hydrocracking of polyolefins:** dense 2023–2025 prior art on Pt–acid intimacy and site isolation (Tian et al., JACS 2025, DOI 10.1021/jacs.5c09153; a Chem. Eng. Sci. 2025 site-isolation paper; mordenite crystal-engineering and Beta-mesoporosity studies), and Gounder's own external-site quantification for PE cracking ([[hullfish2026-reaction-based-quantification-of-surface]]). Transport and external-surface chain activation dominate; framework Al siting is a second-order variable there.

### Practical problem and scale

US ethane supply is at record levels, with EIA forecasting net ethane exports to grow 14% in 2025 and 16% in 2026 on expanding terminal capacity ([EIA, Today in Energy, 14 Oct 2025](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=66344), read). Search-surfaced EIA/API summaries give 2024 production near 2.8 million b/d and exports near 0.49 million b/d; those figures were not re-read on the original pages and are quoted as context, not as verified numbers. Converting ethane to BTX rather than exporting it or cracking it to ethylene is the long-standing motivation for Cyclar-type Ga/MFI and Zn/MFI catalysts.

The catalytic limitations are established: dehydrogenation is thermodynamically limited and H2-inhibited at 600–650 °C, methane forms by cracking/hydrogenolysis, coking is fast, and the metal function re-speciates during reaction and oxidative regeneration. For Zn, loss of zinc by reduction and sublimation is a recognized obstacle alongside coke (Li et al., ChemCatChem 2025, DOI 10.1002/cctc.202500085, abstract read). For Ga–Pt/HZSM-5 in ethane dehydroaromatization, metal agglomeration and Pt leaching were the main non-coke deactivation causes across air regenerations (Bai, Samanta, Robinson, Li, Hu, Ind. Eng. Chem. Res. 2018, DOI 10.1021/acs.iecr.7b05094, search summary only). A Cyclar-type commercial unit is described in reviews as operating on propane/butane, with ethane activity of that catalyst described as low (Saito & Sekine, RSC Adv. 2020, DOI 10.1039/d0ra03365k, metadata only; the capacity and dates surfaced by search were not verified). No currently operating commercial ethane-aromatization unit was verified in this screen.

### Connection to Gounder and Iglesia

- **Iglesia.** Ga in H-ZSM5 supplies a recombinative H2-desorption function that relieves high hydrogen surface fugacity so dehydrogenation can proceed without concurrent cracking (Iglesia, Baumgartner, Price, J. Catal. 1992, 134, 549, DOI 10.1016/0021-9517(92)90342-F); in situ Ga K-edge XAS showed reduced Ga present as highly dispersed monomeric hydride under working conditions (Meitzner, Iglesia, Baumgartner, Huang, J. Catal. 1993, 140, 209, DOI 10.1006/jcat.1993.1079); Zn/H-ZSM5 active-species density and Zn elution as vapor from reduced bulk ZnO were established by Biscardi, Meitzner, Iglesia (J. Catal. 1998, DOI 10.1006/jcat.1998.2177). All three read as abstracts/search summaries; local notes are absent.
- **Gounder.** Co2+-titratable paired-Al fractions in MFI can be set from about 0.12 to 0.44 at near-constant composition by the Na+/TPA+ ratio, and held near 0.06 with a neutral pentaerythritol SDA ([[nimlos2020-experimental-and-theoretical-assessments-of]] p.2-18; [[norfleet2026-pentaerythritol-structure-directing-agents-bias]] p.2-5). External-site counting ([[ezenwa2024-quantification-of-extracrystalline-acid-sites]]) and MFI pair-loss kinetics under steam ([[wang2024-evidence-of-preferential-aluminum-site]]) are the matched controls this diagnostic needs.

**Open boundary (inference from the table):** the dehydrogenation-site identity is settled for propane and is insensitive to pairing for ethane at the rate level; the untested question is whether the paired-Al fraction, set by synthesis rather than by Si/Al or post-treatment, controls (a) the fraction of Zn/Ga that returns to divalent intrazeolitic sites after each oxidative regeneration, (b) metal loss per cycle, and (c) cumulative BTX over cycles, once the higher local acid density of pair-rich MFI and the faster steam loss of pairs are accounted for.

## 3. The consequential uncertainty

After a coke burn (which produces steam) and the subsequent reductive re-start, exchanged Zn or Ga can return as divalent cations at paired Al, as [MOH]+ at isolated Al, as intrazeolitic oxo-clusters, or as external oxide; isolated Ga+ is water-oxidized while pair sites are not (Yuan 2021), and [ZnOH]+ sublimates while pair-anchored species survive (Yuan 2023). If the paired-Al fraction set at synthesis controls the post-regeneration cation population and metal loss, two decisions change: the **synthesis recipe** (Na+ co-SDA to raise pairs, versus a low-Na or PET route that minimizes pairs to limit hydrogen transfer) and the **regeneration protocol** (oxidative burn alone versus burn followed by a controlled H2 re-reduction, and the metal make-up rate). If it does not, pairing is irrelevant to durability and anti-loss additives remain the only lever.

## 4. Hypothesis, live alternatives, discriminating observations

**H1 (falsifiable).** At fixed Si/Al, crystal size, external acidity and M/Al, MFI with a higher Co2+-titratable paired-Al fraction retains a larger fraction of exchanged Zn (or Ga) as intrazeolitic divalent cations after each oxidative regeneration, loses less metal per cycle, and therefore keeps a higher cumulative BTX output per gram over five reaction–regeneration cycles.

**A1 — acid-density penalty.** Pair-rich MFI has higher local proton density, which raises hydrogen transfer, paraffin formation and coke (MFI evidence in `literature/topics/c1-and-hydrocarbon-conversion.md`); cycles shorten and the retention gain is offset. Discriminator: per-cycle coke (TPO), CH4 and C2–C4 paraffin selectivity, and time to a fixed conversion loss, compared at matched initial conversion.

**A2 — pair erosion.** Coke-burn steam removes paired Al faster than total Al, so a synthesis-set pair contrast collapses within a few cycles and any early advantage decays. Discriminator: sham cycling of the two H-forms without metal through the same burn/steam history, followed by Co2+ re-titration and 1H DQ–SQ NMR; if the pair fractions converge, H1 cannot hold over cycles.

**A3 — pair-anchored M2+ is less active.** Isolated Zn2+ on pairs is less active and selective than [ZnOH]+ and [Zn–O–Zn]2+ (Yuan 2023), so a higher retained divalent fraction gives lower per-Zn turnover; cumulative BTX may not rise even if retention does. Discriminator: per-cycle initial BTX rate normalized per retained intrazeolitic Zn (in situ IR/BAS-consumption census after in situ reduction) versus per total Zn.

**A4 — pairing-independent redispersion.** Oxide formed during the burn re-enters pores on reduction regardless of pairing (Ga-CHA precedent), so retention depends on regeneration atmosphere, not on Al distribution. Discriminator: interaction between pair fraction and regeneration arm (burn-only versus burn + H2 re-reduction); if only the arm matters, A4 holds.

**A5 (Ga-specific).** Because [GaH2]+ on isolated Al is as active for ethane as [GaH]2+ (Phadke 2021), pairing changes neither rate nor useful output for Ga unless re-speciation after the burn differs; this makes Zn the primary material and Ga a check rather than a second full arm.

Established evidence supports the ingredients (**E**); H1 itself is a hypothesis (**H**); the cumulative-BTX gain is an expectation (**X**).

## 5. Smallest informative first campaign

**Materials (no new synthesis library).** Two MFI from the existing Na+/TPA+ series at Si/Al about 50 with paired-Al fractions near 0.1 and 0.4, matched in crystal size (SEM), external acidity (DBE or pyrenemethanol probe) and micropore volume; independent second preparation of each. Zn exchanged at Zn/Al about 0.15 (below the pair capacity of the pair-poor sample so that speciation, not saturation, differs). Controls: the two H-forms (metal-free sham cycling), a conventional Zn/H-ZSM-5 on commercial MFI at the same Zn/Al (benchmark), and, if available, a P-stabilized Zn/ZSM-5 as the anti-loss comparator.

**Reaction and cycling.** Ethane, 600–650 °C, 1 atm, about 10 kPa ethane in inert with H2-free feed, in a SiC-diluted bed with an internal thermocouple; one differential and one integral space time. Five cycles; two regeneration arms: (i) dilute O2 burn at 550 °C with product steam retained, and (ii) the same burn followed by H2 re-reduction at reaction temperature. Balances: carbon balance above 95% per cycle by online GC; per-cycle BTX, ethylene, methane, C3–C4 paraffins, and coke by TPO; Zn balance by ICP of the bed after cycles 1, 3 and 5 and of a downstream cold trap.

**State measurements.** In situ transmission IR after in situ reduction (BAS consumption and Zn–OH/Ga–H bands per the Yuan 2021 protocol; ex situ counts are unreliable), H2-TPR of regenerated samples, and Co2+ re-titration plus 1H DQ–SQ NMR of the sham-cycled H-forms (A2).

**Transport and replication checks.** Weisz–Prater and Mears estimates at the integral condition; a crystal-size or bed-dilution sensitivity test at cycle 1; independent preparations (not repeat runs of one bed) define the scatter against which a contrast is judged.

**Decision informed.** Whether to adopt Na+ co-SDA synthesis for a pair-rich support and whether regeneration should include re-reduction; a null result closes the pairing lever and leaves additives.

Numerical targets above are design choices, not predictions.

## 6. Confidence judgments

- **Hypothesis (H1):** plausible, low-to-moderate confidence. The Zn speciation sequence and the stability contrast between [ZnOH]+ and pair-anchored species are established, and Lobo explicitly suggests the pair strategy. Against it: pairs are the Al population lost fastest under steam, pair-rich MFI carries an acid-density penalty, and for Ga the ethane rate is pairing-insensitive.
- **Feasibility and informativeness:** moderate. The synthesis series, the in situ speciation protocol and the cycling reactor are standard in the two groups' capability envelope; the main risks are isothermality at 650 °C, quantifying sublimed Zn, and separating Zn loss from pair loss, which the sham arm addresses. A clean negative (pair fractions converge, or retention differs but cumulative BTX does not) is itself informative.
- **Practical improvement:** low. No operating commercial ethane-aromatization unit was verified; a positive result yields a synthesis rule whose value depends on process adoption, and Zn loss already has additive countermeasures. Even a favorable retention result would not demonstrate a better complete-cycle economics against a P-stabilized benchmark.

## 7. Benchmark and stop/pivot rule

**Benchmark.** Conventional Zn/H-ZSM-5 prepared identically on commercial MFI, and the P-stabilized Zn/ZSM-5 anti-loss precedent where available, compared on cumulative BTX per gram over five cycles including regeneration time, Zn retained, and coke burned per cycle. Cumulative output, not endpoint conversion, is the metric (`research/reviews/oligomerization-benchmark-accounting.md` explains why).

**Stop.** Close the diagnostic if, after five cycles, retained intrazeolitic Zn and cumulative BTX differ between the 0.1 and 0.4 pair-fraction materials by less than the independent-preparation scatter; or if the sham-cycled H-forms converge in pair fraction within two cycles (A2); or if the pair-rich material's first-cycle coke/paraffin penalty exceeds its multi-cycle retention gain at matched initial conversion (A1).

**Pivot.** If retention differs but cumulative BTX does not (A3), report the result as a bounded Zn-loss measurement and do not expand. If only the regeneration arm matters (A4), the useful outcome is a regeneration-protocol choice, not a synthesis rule; hand it to the propane-dehydrogenation Ga-regeneration screen rather than opening a new program.

## 8. Recommendation

**Do not promote for full development.** The site-identity question is answered for propane and is rate-insensitive to pairing for ethane; the remaining question is a durability/re-speciation rule with unestablished process value. **Retain as a bounded diagnostic** only if the pair-controlled MFI series already exists in hand and a 650 °C cycling reactor with online GC and in situ IR is available; do not start a synthesis library or a spectroscopy program to manufacture the contrast. Rejection of the pairing lever after five cycles would be a legitimate, useful outcome.
