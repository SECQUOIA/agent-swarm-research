# Candidate screen: synthesis-set Al pairing as a durability specification for commercial-composition Cu-CHA

Direction search, 2026-09-16. Territory: zeolite synthesis and post-synthesis as an enabling capability (Al-pair control by OSDA/inorganic cation mixtures, interzeolite conversion, heteroatom siting, defect control). This is a screen, not a selected program. No experiments were performed, no facility or material access is assumed, and nothing under `literature/` was modified. Companion screens cover Zn/MFI aromatization ([zeolite-siting-selective-reaction](zeolite-siting-selective-reaction.md)) and the desulfation-atmosphere question for Cu-SSZ-13 ([cu-pd-zeolite-durability](cu-pd-zeolite-durability.md)); neither is re-proposed here.

**Decision in one paragraph.** Of the candidates in this territory, the one with a real material-selection decision and an open boundary is **whether the paired-Al fraction set at synthesis, at fixed commercial-like composition (Si/Al about 12–15, Cu/Al about 0.3), predicts how much redox-active Cu a Cu-CHA catalyst keeps after hydrothermal aging and after combined hydrothermal + SO2 duty.** The ingredients are established: Na+ or K+ co-occlusion with TMAda+ sets 6-MR paired versus isolated Al at fixed Si/Al (Gounder); Cu at paired Al (Z2Cu) survives steam better than ZCuOH (Gao/Szanyi); survivors after aging sit near paired Al (Chmelka/Gounder/BASF). What is not established is whether pre-pairing by synthesis buys anything that aging and Cu/Al selection do not already deliver, because (i) aging itself converts ZCuOH to Z2Cu within hours, (ii) pair-rich H-CHA frameworks lose crystallinity faster under steam, and (iii) the Na route adds extra-framework Al and non-redox Cu. **Recommendation: do not promote to a substantial program; retain as a bounded diagnostic only if the pair-controlled CHA series already exists, with an explicit promotion gate.** Reasons are in Section 8. The other candidates in the brief are weaker (Section 1).

## 1. Practical problem, scale, and fit

Cu-CHA (Cu-SSZ-13) is the standard NH3-SCR catalyst for diesel NOx control, and its regulated useful life is rising (the companion screen records the CARB Omnibus 435,000 → 600,000 → 800,000-mile schedule as cited in Deka 2022; not independently verified here). Field-returned bricks lose activity through hydrothermal dealumination, Cu aggregation to CuOx/Cu-aluminate, and sulfur chemistry; the current industrial levers are Si/Al, Cu/Al, topology (AEI, LTA), and washcoat sizing.

The synthesis lever at issue is cheap in principle: at fixed Si/Al, the fraction of Al in 6-MR pairs is set by the Na+/TMAda+ ratio in the gel (Di Iorio & Gounder 2016; 2020), by the FAU precursor fraction (Nishitoba 2018; Devos 2021), or by Li+-assisted interzeolite conversion up to "fully paired" (Robijns 2023). K+ co-occlusion does the opposite (mostly isolated 6-MR Al) and allows more than tenfold higher inorganic-to-organic ratios, i.e. less OSDA per gram of CHA (Di Iorio 2020). So a manufacturer choosing between a cheaper K-assisted route and a Na-assisted route is, without knowing it, choosing between ZCuOH-rich and Z2Cu-rich Cu-CHA at the same bulk composition. That is the material-selection decision this screen targets.

**Fit.** This is Gounder's core synthesis capability (Na/K/TMAda CHA series, Co2+ pair titration with validated site balances, FAU→CHA transformation, Ba2+ complement), combined with Cu-CHA speciation and redox kinetics from the Ribeiro/Schneider/Gounder line, and Iglesia-style site-normalized rate measurement. Gounder co-authored the 2020 SDA-occlusion paper with BASF (Moini, Prasad, Kunkes, Vattipalli) and the 2025 aged-survivor paper with BASF and Chmelka; those are intellectual connections and also a warning that industrial data on exactly these series may exist unpublished.

### Other candidates in the brief, and why not chosen

- **(b) Al-pair or heteroatom control in AEI/AFX.** For SSZ-39 the Al-pair placement versus cis/trans OSDA isomer ratio and Na is so far a DFT prediction (Umhey et al. 2024, [10.1021/acs.chemmater.4c02175](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.4c02175), abstract read), not a demonstrated synthesis series; AEI already outperforms CHA in aged Cu retention for a topological reason (Wenig 2025, local note, CuII_st 28% vs 46%). A pair-control study in AEI would need the synthesis tool built first; no consequential decision is blocked only by that. Cu-KFI seed-assisted Al-distribution control is published (Lee et al. 2022, [10.1039/d2cy00745b](https://doi.org/10.1039/d2cy00745b), one-line abstract only). Not chosen.
- **(c) Steam stability of paired-Al populations and synthesis levers (B, P, defects).** The premise "steam removes paired Al about twice as fast as total Al" comes from HZSM-5 at 450 °C ([[sun2025-identification-and-quantification-of-al]] p.6-9) and is consistent with random survival (a pair needs both Al to survive; [[wang2024-evidence-of-preferential-aluminum-site]] p.3-4 explicitly reports similar per-site propensity). In Cu-CHA the opposite is observed after 750 °C aging: Q4(2Al) correlations increase ([[schmithorst2025-origins-of-the-hydrothermal-stability]] p.4). So the premise is framework-, Cu- and protocol-dependent, and the metal-free question is not a stand-alone program; it is folded into this screen as alternative A2 (a metal-free sham arm) because the answer only matters if it changes the Cu-CHA decision. B- or P-stabilization of pairs was not found as prior art in the searches run here and would be a second-stage lever.
- **(d) Sn/Ti/Zr/Hf siting for aqueous Lewis-acid catalysis.** Declined in the companion [liquid-phase-oxygenate](liquid-phase-oxygenate.md) screen (loss modes and regeneration already densely mapped; the open/closed CD3CN census is not a working-state count, [[abidi2025-challenging-the-distinction-between-open]] p.2-7). Not re-proposed.
- **MTO on pair-controlled CHA.** Nishitoba 2018 already reports Al-distribution effects on MTO and hydrothermal stability in H-CHA; Gounder's dehydration/cracking pair kinetics are established. Incremental.

## 2. The one consequential uncertainty

**At fixed Si/Al (≈12–15), fixed Cu/Al (≈0.3), matched crystal size and matched extra-framework Al, does a higher synthesis-set 6-MR paired-Al fraction give a Cu-CHA that retains more redox-active isolated Cu and a higher low-temperature SCR rate after (i) hydrothermal aging and (ii) hydrothermal + SO2/desulfation cycling, or do aging-driven ZCuOH→Z2Cu conversion and faster dealumination of pair-rich frameworks erase the synthesis contrast?**

Why this is consequential and not "characterize more": the answer selects a manufacturing route (Na-assisted or FAU/Li-IZC pair-enriching versus K-assisted OSDA-lean), decides whether Al distribution should join Si/Al and Cu/Al as a specification, and decides whether accelerated-aging protocols must control Al distribution (Deka 2025 asks for protocol design; Rani 2025 shows single-exposure tests do not mimic engine aging). A null result is directly useful: it licenses the cheaper K-route and removes a variable from protocol design.

**Open boundary (inference from the table).** No retrieved study varies the synthesis-set paired-Al fraction at fixed Si/Al, Cu/Al, crystal size and EFAl, and then measures, on the same specimens, retained redox-active Cu (EPR, CO titration, H2-TPR), Brønsted inventory, and low-temperature turnover after hydrothermal aging and after hydrothermal + SO2 cycling. Composition-based Z2Cu enrichment (lower Si/Al, lower Cu/Al) and aging-based enrichment (ZCuOH → Z2Cu) are prior art; synthesis-based enrichment at fixed composition, and its interaction with the framework-stability penalty of pair-rich CHA, is the untested cell.

**Negative or constraining results.** Nishitoba 2018 (pair-rich H-CHA less steam-stable), Mossin 2024 (Na route: more EFAl, less redox-active Cu), Luo 2017/Zhang 2020 (aging converges speciation and immobilizes Cu), Krishna 2023 and Paolucci 2016 (pairing does not change fresh kinetics at fixed Cu/Al), and Wang 2020 (framework-Al protection "less important" than Cu-ion stabilization) all cut against a large synthesis-derived gain.

Patent literature (BASF, Johnson Matthey, Umicore, Cummins) was not searched; given the BASF co-authorships, unpublished or patented data on this exact series is plausible and would lower originality further.

## 4. Falsifiable hypothesis, live alternatives, discriminating observations

**H1 (proposed).** At Si/Al ≈ 13, Cu/Al ≈ 0.3, matched crystal size and EFAl, Cu-CHA with a higher synthesis-set 6-MR paired-Al fraction retains a larger fraction of redox-active isolated Cu and a higher 200 °C turnover rate after 750 °C/10% H2O aging and after HTA + SO2/desulfation cycling, because Cu pre-sited as Z2Cu bypasses the ZCuOH → CuOx (Song 2017) and sulfated-pair → CuOx (Deka 2025) pathways and Cu at pairs raises the framework hydrolysis barrier (Singh 2024).

**A1 — aging convergence (redundancy).** Aging converts ZCuOH + H+ → Z2Cu within the first hours (Luo 2017; Zhang 2020), so after the first interval all materials have similar Z2Cu fractions and durability; synthesis pairing only pre-empts what aging does. Discriminator: Z2Cu/ZCuOH by H2-TPR and NO2-TPD (Villamaina 2019 protocol, [10.1021/acscatal.9b02578](https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.9b02578)) at 0, 2, 16 h; convergence in speciation *and* in aged TOR within scatter supports A1. A subtlety: A1 can hold for speciation while H1 holds for inventory if the conversion route itself produces CuOx; therefore CuOx (H2-TPR ≈240–260 °C feature) must be counted separately.

**A2 — framework penalty.** Pair-rich CHA dealuminates faster (Nishitoba 2018; Singh 2024's barrier spread), so the Brønsted-site loss that sets early rate loss (Singh 2026) is worse in the pair-rich material and outweighs the Cu-site benefit. Discriminator: metal-free H-forms of each material through the identical aging history (sham arm); 27Al MAS NMR framework-Al fraction and NH3-TPD Brønsted count versus time; if pair-rich H-CHA loses more framework Al and the Cu-form shows the same ordering, A2 holds.

**A3 — synthesis confounder.** Differences track EFAl, crystal size, silanol density or residual Na (Mossin 2024) rather than pairing. Discriminator: fresh 27Al NMR EFAl within a preset tolerance across materials; two independent pair-enriching routes with different confounders (Na/TMAda direct synthesis and FAU-derived or Li-IZC) versus the K-route isolated control; agreement of both pair-rich routes against the K-route supports pairing as the variable.

**A4 — sulfur sign.** Z2Cu sulfates only with NH3 as reversible ammonium sulfate (Jangjou 2018), but sulfated Cu pairs are the irreversible trap (Deka 2025) and SO2 already drives ZCuOH → Z2Cu (Zhang 2022). Pair-rich material may therefore be more tolerant per exposure yet lose the same irreversible fraction per desulfation. Discriminator: irreversible EPR-active Cu loss per cycle and CO-titratable dimers in the SO2 arm, compared across pair fractions at matched S/Cu.

**A5 — Cu/Al saturation artifact.** With paired-Al fraction ≈0.4, only ≈0.2 Cu/Al can be Z2Cu, so at Cu/Al 0.3 all materials contain ZCuOH and the contrast is diluted; at Cu/Al 0.15 the pair-rich material is all-Z2Cu and the K-route material all-ZCuOH. Discriminator: run both Cu/Al levels; H1 predicts the contrast is largest at Cu/Al 0.15 and still present at 0.3.

Established ingredients are **E**; H1 is **H**; the practical durability gain is **X**.

## 5. Smallest informative first campaign

**Materials (assumes the pair-controlled series exists; do not start a synthesis library to manufacture it).** Three H-CHA at Si/Al ≈ 13–15: K+/TMAda+ (isolated, ≈0.05 paired fraction), Na+/TMAda+ (≈0.2), Na+/TMAda+ or FAU-derived (≈0.4). Matching criteria set before Cu exchange: crystal size by SEM, micropore volume, EFAl by 27Al MAS NMR within a preset tolerance, silanol by IR, residual Na below detection after NH4+ exchange. Paired fraction by Co2+ titration with saturation check and by Ba2+ complement. Cu exchange to Cu/Al ≈ 0.30 (commercial-like) and 0.15 (below pair capacity); independent second preparation of each. Fresh speciation: H2-TPR, NO2-TPD, OH-region IR (ZCuOH ≈ 3660 cm−1 / 900–950 cm−1 bands), EPR.

**Aging arms.** (i) 750 °C, 10% H2O/air, sacrificial aliquots at 2, 16 h (Schmithorst conditions, which allows comparison with the local aged-survivor data). (ii) HTA 16 h, then six sulfation/desulfation cycles (gas-phase SO2 in SCR feed at 250 °C to a matched, ICP-verified S/Cu; lean desulfation at 550 °C; identical to the companion screen's arm A so results can be shared). (iii) Sham: metal-free H-forms through arm (i). (iv) Fresh and degreened references through the final assay.

**Assays on each specimen.** Differential 200 °C standard-SCR TOR (< 15% conversion, SiC-diluted sieved fraction, internal thermocouple, Weisz–Prater and Mears recorded); EPR-active Cu; CO-titratable dimers; H2-TPR (ZCuOH, Z2Cu, CuOx); 27Al MAS NMR; NH3-TPD Brønsted count; Cu and S by ICP; XRD and micropore volume as bulk checks only (Caballero 2017 shows they are not sufficient durability metrics).

**Balances and replication.** Cu balance before/after (no volatilization expected; must be excluded). Sulfur balance by inlet integral, outlet SO2, and ICP. Independent preparations (not repeat runs of one bed), at least three aged aliquots per arm at the final interval. Freeze the minimum consequential difference before unblinding: a design choice, not a prediction, of ≥10 percentage points in retained EPR-active Cu or ≥20% in aged 200 °C TOR between the ≈0.4 and ≈0.05 pair-fraction materials against independent-preparation scatter.

**Decision informed.** H1 supported at both Cu/Al: specify Al distribution (Na-assisted or FAU/Li-IZC) for durability-critical Cu-CHA and add it to accelerated-aging protocol variables; test one held-out prediction (a different aging temperature or Cu/Al) before any application claim. A1 or A2: license the OSDA-lean K-route and remove Al distribution from protocol design. A3: report that the route, not pairing, matters, and stop.

## 6. Confidence judgments

- **Hypothesis (H1):** low to moderate. The Z2Cu stability advantage and aged-survivor enrichment near pairs are established, and the DFT hydrolysis argument is mechanistically plausible. Against it, and at least as strong: aging already converges speciation toward Z2Cu; pair-rich H-CHA frameworks are less steam-stable; the pair-enriching route carries EFAl and exchange-capacity penalties; and fresh kinetics are pair-insensitive, so any gain must show up only through durability.
- **Feasibility and informativeness:** moderate to high, conditional on the series being in hand. Synthesis, pair titration, Cu speciation and aging are all standard in the two groups' envelope. The main risks are matching EFAl and crystal size across three routes (Mossin 2024 shows the Na route shifts both) and resolving few-percent differences in EPR/CO titration. A clean A1 or A2 outcome is informative and cheap.
- **Practical improvement:** low to moderate. The decision is real (cheap K-route vs Na-route; Al-distribution as a specification), but the magnitude is unknown and industry co-authors likely already hold relevant data. Existing levers (Cu/Al, Si/Al, AEI/LTA topology, H-zeolite blending) already deliver durability gains, and a positive result would need to beat those on a per-cost basis to change a formulation.

## 7. Fair benchmark and stop/pivot rule

**Benchmark.** A commercial-like Cu-SSZ-13 (Si/Al ≈ 12, Cu/Al ≈ 0.3, conventional synthesis) through the identical arms, plus the two established alternatives that reach Z2Cu richness or Cu dilution without changing Al distribution: an Al-rich Cu-SSZ-13 near the He 2025 composition, and a Cu-SSZ-13/H-SSZ-13 physical blend (Gao 2026). Report retained redox-active Cu per gram and per Cu, aged TOR, and Brønsted inventory with the sham arm shown alongside. A pairing effect that is smaller than the effect of moving Si/Al or blending is not a formulation lever.

**Stop.** After the 16 h HTA interval, if the three materials agree in Z2Cu/ZCuOH fraction, CuOx, and aged TOR within independent-preparation scatter (A1), stop; do not add cycles or spectroscopy to rescue the contrast. Stop also if EFAl or crystal size cannot be matched within the preset tolerance (A3), or if the sham H-forms show pair-rich framework loss that the Cu-form reproduces (A2 with the same ordering).

**Pivot.** If HTA shows a pairing advantage but the SO2 arm shows none, report a hydrothermal-only result and hand the sulfur question to the companion desulfation diagnostic rather than expanding here. If the advantage appears only at Cu/Al 0.15 (A5), the result is a low-loading design note, not a commercial-composition lever.

## 8. Recommendation

**Do not promote for full development. Retain as a bounded diagnostic only if the Na/K/TMAda CHA series (or FAU/Li-IZC equivalents) already exists at Si/Al ≈ 13–15, with a promotion gate: pair-rich material must retain more redox-active Cu after HTA than the K-route material by more than independent-preparation scatter, the metal-free sham must not show faster framework loss in the pair-rich material, and the effect must be comparable to or larger than the established Si/Al or blending levers.**

Reasons against a program: (i) the mechanistic ingredients are all prior art, and two of Gounder's own results (pair-insensitive fresh kinetics; survivor enrichment after aging) make the outcome guessable in either direction; (ii) the two strongest published levers for Z2Cu-rich Cu-CHA (composition and aging) already exist, so the marginal value of a synthesis lever is a cost/route question that industry partners on the 2020 and 2025 papers are better placed to answer; (iii) the pair-enriching route has documented confounders (EFAl, exchange capacity) that could absorb most of a positive result. In its favor: the question is sharply posed, the decision it informs (route choice, Al-distribution specification, aging-protocol variable) is concrete, the campaign shares materials and arms with the companion desulfation diagnostic, and a negative result is directly useful.

Relative standing: comparable to the companion desulfation diagnostic in decision clarity and lower in originality; weaker than the retained Ag and polymer studies because no practical superiority over composition or topology levers is even suggested by the literature yet.
