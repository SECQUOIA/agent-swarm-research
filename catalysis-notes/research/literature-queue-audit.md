# Read-only DOI queue audit

**Historical snapshot: 2026-09-15.** Superseded for current access status by [literature status](literature-status.md) and the [post-upload inventory](reviews/post-upload-missing-sources.md). Do not treat this old unmatched-DOI list as an active queue.

Original scope: This audit compares DOI identifiers in the [source registry](literature-queue.md) and its linked [propylene handoff](working/propylene-transfer-screen.md#exact-literature-handoff-retained-for-the-root-registry) against existing package DOI fields. It does not modify or validate the knowledge base.

A missing package match means **still queued or needing identity reconciliation**, not that the full text is unavailable. The [literature status report](literature-status.md) separately records completed batches and confirmed retrieval gaps. Patent IDs, web records, theses without DOI, linked SI and newly arriving requests are not exhaustively covered by this DOI-only check. A preprint may appropriately be retained with its journal article rather than as a separate package.

Identifiers checked: 103. Without a current exact package DOI match: 51. These counts are a dated snapshot.
