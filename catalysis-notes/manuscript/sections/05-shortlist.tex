\section{Reserve ideas and withdrawn claims}
\label{sec:shortlist}

The six ideas below are ranked for possible reconsideration, not proposed as six further full campaigns. Each entry gives the question, the prior work it builds on, and the next step. Existing equipment may change the order in which a small test is worthwhile.

\paragraph{1. Tungsten coordination and retention in sugar conversion: exploratory hold.}
\emph{Question:} can a controllable anchoring or feed variable link productive sugar coordination, W export and useful glycol output? \emph{Prior work:} Ti--O--W stabilization and catalyst reuse already have strong precedent. In Li's study, the supported-Ni control without intentionally added W (Ni/M) gives 29.5\% ethylene-glycol yield with preleachate, which must be compared with 27.1\% for Ni/M alone; 29.5\% is not a leachate-derived yield.\citep{li2020w,li2020wsi} \emph{Next step:} if the idea is reopened, first reproduce a retained and a soluble-W reference with hot export and carbon balances. Transferred activity can show exported function but cannot uniquely assign all upstream turnover. The idea stays on hold: a chemically grounded intervention addressing an important limitation of the reference is still missing.

\paragraph{2. Product-rich liquid Ti-zeolite epoxidation: conditional model-transfer test.}
\emph{Question:} does a calibrated reaction network predict an independent product-accumulation trajectory, including peroxide loss and epoxide ring opening? \emph{Prior work:} product-induced solvent changes already have direct spectroscopic precedent, and related Ti-zeolite secondary kinetics contain unresolved behavior.\citep{torres2023thesis,kwon2024} \emph{Next step:} validate component kinetics in the chosen liquid catalyst/solvent system before fixing the prediction; a vapor-fed model is not a validated liquid baseline. If the established network predicts useful output adequately, drop the claim of an additional mechanism. A residual alone does not identify solvent reorganization, fouling or Ti loss.

\paragraph{3. Zirconia styrene self-cleaning: limited chemical-balance pilot.}
\emph{Question:} does ethylbenzene exposure remove inhibiting water from Zr--O acid--base pairs, and does the exported oxygen track recovered function? \emph{Prior work:} this hydrocarbon-assisted water removal is already proposed, but the cleaning products were not identified in the source's dilute-feed regime.\citep{artsiusheuski2025} \emph{Next step:} first establish useful net output with measured moisture and relevant styrene/hydrogen burdens. Then cross water history with ethylbenzene exposure to relate oxygen export to recovered function. Control oxygen exchange and perturbation by the activity assay before isotope attribution. Expand only if the chemistry predicts a useful feed or removal policy; omitting steam alone does not establish process savings.

\paragraph{4. Does an acid-site assay change subsequent zeolite aging? Conditional protocol study.}
\emph{Question:} compared with uninterrupted aging and a matched thermal/water sham, do repeated NH\textsubscript{3}/water assays change the later aging of protonic chabazite (H-CHA)? \emph{Prior work:} repeated assays are part of the relevant published protocol. A Cu/SSZ-13 aging-order comparison assayed both arms, so it does not compare assay with no assay.\citep{classmartinez2026,luo2018} \emph{Next step:} measure function on separate matched specimens before terminal titration; a common terminal assay then compares recoverable capacity. A useful result would predict later function under a withheld assay spacing. Immediate uptake recovery is not enough, and a null schedule effect cannot by itself determine the lifetime of an unseen Al intermediate.

\paragraph{5. Wet methane oxidation after NO removal: application-dependent diagnostic.}
\emph{Question:} does the sulfur-tolerance advantage of Pd/SSZ-13 treated by ST-CO-N2-O2 over its ST-CO-O2 counterpart (which lacks an extra N\textsubscript{2} treatment between CO and O\textsubscript{2}) survive removing NO at matched water, sulfur history, temperature and precious-metal inventory? \emph{Prior work:} the reported sulfur interval includes NO, and history-dependent NO promotion on other Pd catalysts is already known.\citep{ryu2024,auvinen2021} \emph{Next step:} run the small crossed NO/sulfur test on both variants only where a real low-NO, sulfur-containing duty could change the material choice. Without such an application, another NO response is a diagnostic, not a full program.

\paragraph{6. Ga-based propane dehydrogenation regeneration: conditional cycle study.}
\emph{Question:} does a measured restoration endpoint predict a shorter useful regeneration for a withheld spent history? \emph{Prior work:} redox-related deactivation and shortened oxygen treatments are already reported.\citep{wu2025,buchbinder2022} Oxygen consumption minus \COtwo{} alone is not a selective Ga-redox inventory, because oxidation of retained hydrogen and other oxygen-containing products also enters the balance. \emph{Next step:} calibrate that balance before assigning sites. Then compare complete-cycle propylene output, gas use and heat duty with the best simple regeneration policy. Correcting an assay does not by itself establish a better catalyst cycle.

\subsection*{Withdrawn claims}

Several attractive shortcuts do not survive scrutiny. A faster outlet-water transient does not uniquely establish lower active-site water exposure (Section~\ref{sec:water}). Fresh-Na rescue does not identify selective loss of initiation (Section~\ref{sec:polymer}). Ni compatibility and retention on promoted Ag are already prior art (Section~\ref{sec:silver}). The chapters replace these claims with narrower functional and predictive questions; none of the corrections invalidates its catalytic field.

For tungsten, the premise that mannose forms an inactive reservoir is also withdrawn. Published hot mannose feeds produce glycolaldehyde, although epimerization complicates assignment of the direct cleavage route.\citep{elmohammad2024} This does not exclude an inactive fraction, but such a reservoir cannot be assumed.

This section thus separates missing evidence from demonstrated contradiction. An incomplete idea can be reconsidered after its specific missing test; an invalid inference should not be kept as the default mechanism.
