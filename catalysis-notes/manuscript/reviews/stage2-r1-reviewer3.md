## Minor issues

1. **Distinguish the coated-carrier inhibition result from the reversibility experiment.** `manuscript/sections/02-cyclic-oxides.tex:82` says Gao reports “reversible CO2 inhibition on the coated carrier.” Gao's original Fig. 4D compares coated-LSF conversion/yield with and without CO2; the explicit CO2 switch-on/switch-off time trace demonstrating recovery is Fig. 4E, for molten Li2CO3 with ethane/O2 cofeed at 730°C. The adjacent reversibility sentence follows that second experiment. I checked the original XML and the open original PDF, p.6/Fig.4, obtained from `https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1803998` without changing the library. **Fix:** say Gao reports CO2 inhibition on the coated carrier; if reversibility is mentioned, identify the separate molten-salt/O2 experiment. The washout requirement remains well motivated and unchanged. Make the same distinction in `manuscript/evidence/stage2-cyclic.md:7`.

2. **Keep the benign-steam conclusion conditional on the tested restoration history.** `manuscript/sections/02-cyclic-oxides.tex:118` says benign steam can rule out “unnecessary protection.” Both wet diagnostic arms receive CO2, and all arms subsequently receive the common carbonation/oxidation reset (lines 57–60 and 78). A null persistent difference therefore supports compatibility *after that reset* and can rule out a consequential benefit from concurrent CO2 over delayed restoration; it cannot by itself show that CO2 treatment altogether is unnecessary. **Fix:** qualify the closing conclusion as ruling out added concurrent protection over the tested restoration policy, or explicitly restrict the benign-steam statement to the complete tested gas/reset history. This is a wording correction, not a request to add another experimental arm.

The distinction between resolution examples and predicted losses is explicit.

The alternating held-out schedule tests a genuine history prediction rather than another calibration fit. Measurement feasibility and positive experimental outcomes remain explicit dependencies, not missing manuscript results.
