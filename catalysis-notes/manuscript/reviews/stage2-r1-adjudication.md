## Accepted major correction

Preserve the meaningful original process question by adding a bounded, independently replicated ordinary-cycle comparison of the selected post-oxidation steam purge versus Ar, without supplementary CO2. This can be a preliminary qualification and the untreated comparator for the eventual policy comparison, not a broad new campaign. Freeze exposure/cycle counts and reset cadence; don't increase severity until failure appears. Narrow every null to its actual gas, duty, state, and recovery policy.
