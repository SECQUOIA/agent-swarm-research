## Minor issue

**Use the direct contrast to close the persistent timing-advantage claim.** `manuscript/sections/02-cyclic-oxides.tex:126` says that if both timing arms recover within the consequential-loss bound, a persistent concurrent-over-delayed advantage closes. Individual recovery bounds do not necessarily exclude a consequential difference between the arms. For example, with reference output normalized to 100 and a tolerance of 5, outputs of 104 and 96 are each within the tolerance but differ by 8. Sampling uncertainty further separates individual recovery statements from a bound on the treatment contrast.

Base this closure on the uncertainty interval for the direct post-reset wet P–R difference excluding the predeclared consequential advantage. If the claim is specifically that steam modifies that advantage, also use the interval for `D_steam`. Keep the individual recovery comparisons as distinct statements. The chapter already defines and measures these contrasts at lines 70–76, so this requires a sentence-level correction rather than another experiment.
