## Inference and decision checks

- **Ordinary compatibility is now measured directly.** Lines 47–49 compare steam with Ar without supplementary CO2 or special reset during the operating block. The output is measured before an added dry-conditioning intervention, so reversible inhibition and carryover remain part of the actual operating result. The subsequent common dry assay establishes function after a specified terminal treatment. The opening and closing claims correctly restrict compatibility to the selected post-oxidation purge, conditioning, ordinary oxidation, and finite cycle history.
- **The timing experiment supports its narrower conclusion.** Lines 51–78 retain dry early/late controls and add the explicit wet-minus-corresponding-dry contrasts. The text distinguishes a timing benefit, steam dependence of that benefit, and residual loss relative to the dry schedules. A null after CO2 treatment and reset is no longer used to infer untreated steam tolerance. Validated termination and sampling are required before assigning changes to the first exposure segment.
- **Reset cadence and value accounting are consistent.** Lines 82–86 qualify the common reset and actual gas washout. Lines 96 and 102 distinguish a single terminal analytical reset from any periodic operating treatment. An analytical reset after the counted output block cannot improve the policy's reported productivity. Untreated steam and Ar remain direct operating comparators, and any supplementary treatment, washout, replenishment, or heating belongs in the policy burden.
- **Carbon and oxygen attribution are bounded.** Line 88 includes carbonate-carbon storage and release, distinguishes total COx from hydrocarbon-derived COx, and does not call regeneration CO2 a coke assay. This matches the accounting difficulty documented in Chacko's original PDF p.116, checked during the preceding review. Direct ethylene measurement preserves the functional decision even if carbon-source attribution remains unresolved. Water alone is not treated as an unambiguous lattice-oxygen balance.
- **Prospective prediction is genuinely outside calibration.** Lines 122–124 require a fixed alternating history, operating-treatment cadence, prediction model, and intervals. They predict both operating output and function after one terminal reset, without silently resetting the material between cycles. Inventory-only prediction can fail, and additional measured state must earn value on another independent block. Successful correlation is not promoted to causal dominance.
