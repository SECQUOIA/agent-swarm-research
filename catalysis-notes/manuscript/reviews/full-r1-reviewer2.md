## Findings by program

**Water management — `sections/01-water.tex`, lines 44–109; PDF pp. 6–9.** The causal intervention occurs after common conditioning and uses independent charges in a crossed additive/challenge design. Quantitative solid recovery, retained-liquid handling, identical restart, and an uninterrupted pilot control address the main feasibility problem introduced by late repacking. The challenge qualification, time-matched controls, absolute-rate reporting, and integrated-output endpoint separate protection from a favorable retention ratio caused by low initial activity. The discussion of internally generated water correctly covers recovery as well as the high-water plateau. The transport argument does not mistake an outlet time constant for active-site exposure: storage, source feedback, and source location are explicitly separate. The withheld pulse must predict a consequential functional endpoint and improve upon a simple calibrated baseline. Failure of a mechanism claim does not erase a valid formulation result. These are appropriately bounded decisions.

**Cyclic oxides — `sections/02-cyclic-oxides.tex`, lines 43–126; PDF pp. 11–16.** The ordinary steam/Ar comparison precedes any special reset, so terminal recovery cannot masquerade as untreated steam tolerance. The dry timing arms distinguish a steam-dependent treatment-order interaction from an ordinary CO2 timing effect. Direct wet contrasts and absolute outputs remain available when the interaction is zero. Equal inlet doses are not misrepresented as equal uptake or equivalent coating states. Sister specimens, qualified state preservation, washout checks, and separate Li/carbon inventories appropriately restrict temporal and mechanistic attribution. The policy comparison includes untreated steam and Ar, permits credible delayed restoration, and charges treatment time and circulating gases. The proposed alternating-history validation is independent of fitting and keeps the analytical reset outside ordinary cycling. Compatibility is explicitly restricted to the post-oxidation purge, tested duty, and finite cycle count; no missing post-ethane campaign is implied by that scope.

**Polymer ethenolysis — `sections/03-polymer.tex`, lines 18–105; PDF pp. 18–22.** Carbon origin, cumulative retained inventory, actual fresh-solid consumption, and complete time define the resource decision. Continuous isotope enrichment across conditioning and every charge avoids misclassifying carbon carried from earlier cycles, subject to the explicitly required source and fractionation qualification. The source’s once-only supplement is preserved. The semibatch bridge is a real entry gate because sealed-batch reuse does not automatically transfer. Crossing both pressures with all three makeup doses avoids attributing a dose effect to pressure, and the independent intermediate-dose test at both pressures distinguishes a pressure-enabled saving from an adequate smaller dose. The bounded extension charges full-mixture replacement, failed charges, residue, and complete time, so a short three-charge gain cannot silently become a sustained catalyst-demand claim. No selective-initiation mechanism is needed for the operational result.

**Promoted silver — `sections/04-silver.tex`, lines 18–101; PDF pp. 24–28.** Untreated, sham, and Ni-treated materials address the principal historical processing confound. Untreated operation continues across the full horizon, rather than serving only as a fresh-material check. The common-setting causal comparison and separately tuned useful policies answer different questions and are kept distinct. Both materials receive bounded tuning opportunity, ordinary drift supplies the primary burden, and an unproductive acceleration campaign cannot manufacture the missing practical question. Accepted EO, physical EO flows, admissibility, downtime, and resource use are distinguished. Recovery comparisons retain separate trajectories and rank all tested Ni and sham policies in absolute campaign output, alongside untreated ordinary operation. The calibrated chlorine/product baseline, limits of bulk-state assays, and withheld-duration prediction prevent a familiar conditioning response from being presented as a new oxygen-transfer mechanism. Claims concern the finite tested policies, not a globally optimized or industrial-lifetime comparison.

**Portfolio and reserves — `sections/00-introduction.tex` and `sections/05-shortlist.tex`; PDF pp. 1–3 and 29–30.** The ranking states its scientific rather than scheduling basis and acknowledges unknown equipment access and achievable uncertainty. All six reserves remain short, identify a missing decision or invalid inference, and do not dilute the four main programs into unbounded campaigns. The overview’s proposed contributions agree with the detailed chapters.

## Decisive primary-source checks

- Fang et al. (2026), original HTML, Fig. 1 and methods: 0.025 g PDVB per 0.5 g catalyst, separate 40–60 mesh granules, and matched nominal bed volume support the specified platform. The source’s approximately 80/175 s water decays follow switching from syngas to Ar; the manuscript correctly limits what those decays identify.
- Brody et al. (2022), original PDF, experimental cycle description, process-model section, and yield table: the 16-minute experimental cycle uses Ar purges; steam appears in modeled heat-exchange purge steps. The reported 53.2% and 47.24% yields are C2+ quantities. The manuscript preserves these distinctions and labels its throughput calculation as an illustration.
- Conk et al. (2024), original article Fig. 4B and original SI pp. S16–S18: the additional 0.4 g Na/alumina is supplied before charge 2 only, with PE alone before charge 3. Semibatch methods use continuous gas delivery and 12-minute samples; the specified 19 bar is gauge pressure in the relevant method. These support the manuscript’s reuse comparator and reactor-mode/time-resolution gates.
- Kemp (1995), original patent HTML, catalyst preparations and Table III: the 50-day losses are 4.3 and 6.1 percentage points for A and C; the ethanol-treated B control was not aged because of poorer initial performance. The manuscript does not overstate this as an isolated Ni effect or an integrated-output advantage.

The principal practical uncertainties are real—repacking reproducibility, steam-compatible state preservation, isotope/mass-balance resolution, and access to a functioning promoted-Ag reference—but the existing entry gates already address them. No added positive result, broad materials library, or optional mechanism campaign is necessary for this manuscript to be complete as a research proposal.
