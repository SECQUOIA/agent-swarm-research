## Minor correction

**Use the direct timing contrast, rather than recovery of each arm, to close the persistent-timing-benefit claim.** Location: `manuscript/sections/02-cyclic-oxides.tex:126`, sentence beginning “If both timing arms recover within the consequential-loss bound”. Related summary: `manuscript/evidence/stage2-cyclic.md:43`.

Recovery or noninferiority of each arm against a reference does not establish absence of a consequential P–R difference. For example, if normalized post-reset outputs are 1.10 for concurrent treatment and 1.00 for delayed treatment, both arms have recovered without loss, but concurrent treatment retains a 10% output advantage. Even two arms close to the reference can differ by more than a separately chosen consequential timing threshold. The direct comparisons are already properly specified at lines 70–76; the closing sentence should use their intervals.

**Remedy:** replace that sentence with: “If the uncertainty interval for the post-reset concurrent-minus-delayed output excludes the predeclared consequential benefit, the claim of a useful persistent timing advantage closes for that history and reset. Recovery of both arms alone does not establish this; neither result shows that all CO2 treatment is unnecessary.” Use the interaction interval separately when the claim is specifically that steam changes the timing benefit. This is a decision-rule correction, not a request for another experiment.
