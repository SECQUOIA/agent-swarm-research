## Minor issues

1. **Specify the reset cadence when accumulating a small effect.** `manuscript/sections/02-cyclic-oxides.tex:88` proposes accumulating captures over repeated cycles if one exposure is unresolved, but does not state whether the special common carbonation/oxidation reset in lines 78–82 occurs after every exposure or only after the accumulation block. Those histories can differ materially: repeated complete reset may suppress a cumulative chemical change, whereas ordinary reoxidation followed by a single terminal reset tests recovery from accumulated exposure. Add the intended sequence in one sentence and freeze it before the accumulation test. Keep the diagnostic reset distinct from ordinary cycle reoxidation, and report the chosen cadence when assigning any operational loss rate.

2. **Name a feasible starting measurement route for the conditional spatial/state branch.** `manuscript/sections/02-cyclic-oxides.tex:78,84,114` asks for carbonate-sensitive spectra, suitable Fe-sensitive measurements, and calibrated Li-sensitive spatial analysis, but leaves every technique in the final category unspecified. The qualification language is sound, yet readers cannot assess the proposed spatial inference without knowing the intended probe and its limitation. Name one candidate method for Li distribution and the spatial scale it must resolve, along with the proposed Fe-state method. For example, a cross-sectional secondary-ion mapping approach would require matrix/geometry controls and separate bulk Li quantification; it would not automatically provide absolute coating coverage. Preserve the option to close the mechanistic branch if that measurement cannot be qualified. This does not require adding a characterization campaign to the initial compatibility test.

3. **Narrow the opening promise to the tested purge and conditions.** `manuscript/sections/02-cyclic-oxides.tex:4` describes replacement of “an inert purge” and an “operating boundary,” while lines 45 and 92 correctly restrict the work initially to the post-oxidation purge and one selected steam-rich condition. The carrier enters the post-ethane purge in a different state, so an opening sentence that names the post-oxidation purge would prevent readers from carrying the broader substitution claim through the chapter. Also describe a recoverability or performance result under specified histories unless a boundary across a defined variable is actually being located. The existing bounded program is sufficient; a new severity or composition sweep is not necessary.
