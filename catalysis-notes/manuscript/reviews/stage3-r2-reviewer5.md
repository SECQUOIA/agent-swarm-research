The catalyst-solid inventory table and conditional savings arithmetic are unchanged and correct. No new result is presented as measured.

Common first-charge history, independent sequences, fresh controls, and dose-specific contrasts preserve an interpretable operating comparison. The null conclusion is restricted to the tested doses and pressures.

Preserving the validated first-charge history avoids silently changing the policy after replacement. Claims of sustained demand are limited to this bounded extension and replacement rule.

The proposal is sufficiently developed without requiring a pressure library, speculative selective-component assay, or positive experimental results. Its remaining dependencies are appropriate matters for the stated qualification experiments.
