## Basis for approval

At `manuscript/sections/04-silver.tex:10–16`, the novelty boundary is credible. The chapter acknowledges historical Ni-dependent retention on fully promoted Ag and established chloride-management alternatives. It seeks a controlled relationship that predicts when the operating alternative can replace Ni or when a useful Ni advantage survives. Better preparation controls or another selectivity curve alone are explicitly insufficient.

At lines 23–25 and 30–48, the material and operating comparisons are distinct and interpretable. Untreated, sham, and Ni-treated preparations address gross processing damage without claiming identical promoter placement. Common-setting histories estimate the complete Ni-addition effect under matched exposure. Separately chosen useful policies answer the operator's question and receive comparable tuning opportunities. The Ni-free lower-chloride alternative is required within an admissible operating window, with a scheduled-adjustment fallback if that choice cannot function.

At lines 34–50, complete-clock output, work-rate and conversion requirements, unfiltered EO, and resource histories prevent a high-selectivity but poorly productive catalyst from winning by definition. The distinction between policy inadmissibility and physically usable EO is explicit. Lower-chloride penalties and temperature compensation remain visible throughout the observation horizon.

At lines 56–64, the competing explanations are handled carefully. The chlorine/product baseline must be calibrated on the actual formulation. Curve collapse does not eliminate a difference in recovery speed, and curve departure does not identify promoter relocation. Axial states, self-generated products, net EO cofeed response, inventory changes, and minority-site sensitivity constrain the mechanism claims. Optional structural and isotope measurements cannot replace the useful-output comparison.

At lines 68–81, the withheld-duration test predicts an integrated decision rather than a recovered peak or an unobserved microscopic state. Recovery and continuation begin at matched operating age; the benefit includes intervention and return time; and absolute full-clock output must rank the candidate against the best ordinary policies. The manuscript correctly refuses to transfer recovery across different prior histories without validation or infer a periodic-service policy from one successful recovery. A familiar chlorine model may support the operating choice while closing the additional-mechanism claim. These are sound stop and expansion rules.
