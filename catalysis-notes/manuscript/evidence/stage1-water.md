## Design developments beyond existing briefs

- Replaced an assumed common-parent wax-bearing pooled split with preweighed independent charges under a common conditioning protocol, randomized within preparation/run blocks. The parent pilot selects a duration beyond startup using rate/selectivity drift over a defined window, with tolerances set from pilot variability. Initial conditioning uses syngas H2/CO/Ar=64/32/4 at 220°C and 2 MPa; the complete feed/flow/temperature/pressure schedule and duration are frozen before randomization. Pre-handling drift and retained-liquid observations define the reported operational age without implying stationary water affinity. Full-charge solid recovery and matched handling are qualification requirements.
- Initial quartz dilution is identical and stays with each recovered whole charge. Repacking adds either PDVB or the same measured bulk volume of quartz to equal final bed volumes, avoiding selective removal of wax-bearing diluent. Heat/flow behavior and solid/liquid recovery are included in the existing handling qualification; no separate packing experiment is added.
- Made time-matched low-water shams part of the decisive 2x2 design, not an optional afterthought. Defined a difference of retention differences that separates challenge-associated loss from formulation-specific drift; both pre-challenge and post-recovery absolute useful rates for all four conditions and integrated bed-volume output remain required endpoints.
- Specified complete restart/challenge/recovery output horizon and retained-inventory/collection-delay accounting, so an initial PDVB penalty cannot disappear through a favorable retention denominator.
- Kept4bar water as a parent-calibrated planning plateau, with explicit stop if it lacks suitable dynamic range. Fourbar is not justified as a cobalt damage threshold by Wolf's substantially different source.
- A whole-bed reaction-water source explanation of post-recovery retention remains viable unless parent checks bracket the measured formulation-dependent water histories, with uncertainty, during both challenge and recovery. Plateau-only bracketing cannot reject it.
- Added a concrete held-out short-pulse validation on independent charges. Its endpoint is output deficit over a fixed production window, with duration selected for consequential functional separation and a sufficiently narrow prediction interval. The decision is whether the proposed humidity excursion stays within a preselected output budget. A baseline using inlet humidity, the calibrated steady product response, and apparatus delays uses the same data; no added campaign is required. No claim that external water calibration reports local cobalt water; no extrapolation from reversible small pulses to a damaging challenge.
- Narrowed the contribution to a controlled test at one defined operational state and disturbance, plus conditional prediction within the calibrated range. The proposal does not locate an operating boundary across age, dose, or challenge severity. Prior PDVB removal is now explicitly credited, and the reaction, C5+ product designation, and carbon-based selectivity are introduced before the output calculation.
- Explicitly close the unsupported guaranteed stationary mechanism, while retaining a scientifically coherent direct functional program.
