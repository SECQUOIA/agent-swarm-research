## Primary source checks for the shortlist

All paths below are local primary originals. Knowledge-base summaries were navigation aids, not the authority for decisive claims. Reading was targeted to the listed passages, not a complete reanalysis of every article or supplement.

### Tungsten

- `literature/papers/li2020-enhanced-ni-w-ti-catalyst/original.pdf`: Table 1 and discussion, PDF pp. 4–6 / printed pp. 9653–9655. Ni/M is the supported-Ni control without intentionally added W and gives 27.1% EG; retention/reuse and the proposed Ti–O–W linkage are substantive prior art. Bibliography checked from the original first page: ACS Sustainable Chemistry & Engineering 8, 9650–9659, DOI 10.1021/acssuschemeng.0c00836.
- `literature/papers/li2020-supporting-information-for-enhanced-ni/original.pdf`: Table S3, printed p. S9, gives 29.5% EG for Ni/M plus solution. The 29.5% value is not a leachate-only contribution. The manuscript does not infer that the small difference is zero, statistically resolved, or uniquely W-mediated.
- `literature/papers/mohammad2024-identification-of-key-active-species/original.pdf`: first page verifies title, authors, ACS Catalysis 14, 10998–11013, DOI 10.1021/acscatal.4c02791.
- `literature/papers/mohammad2024-supporting-information-for-identification-of/original.pdf`: PDF pp. 6–7, Figures S7–S8. Figure S7 was rendered and visually inspected: mannose feeds produce glycolaldehyde at 150 °C with 5 mM sugar and 1.3 mM metal, under 40 bar N2. Figure S8 documents epimer formation. Productive feed conversion does not establish direct mannose cleavage or exclude an inactive fraction. It does invalidate treating the inactive-reservoir premise as the default explanation. The shortlist states this unsupported premise directly, without referring to an unexplained abandoned extraction prediction.

The preliminary missing test remains reference/export/carbon qualification, conditional on reopening. Positive hot-transfer activity can establish exported function; it cannot uniquely assign all upstream turnover or exclude continuously supplied short-lived species. A chemically grounded anchor/ligand intervention is still missing, so no full tungsten program is claimed.

### Liquid Ti-zeolites

- `literature/papers/record2023-torres-thesis-on-liquid-zeolite/original.pdf`: PDF p. 2 verifies Chris Torres, *Solvent and Surface Effects on Alkene Oxidation Catalysis over Transition Metal Incorporated Zeolites*, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, 2023. PDF pp. 58–60 / printed pp. 42–44, Figure 2.6, checked directly: 0.1 M epoxyhexane changes solvent OH spectral features in Ti-MFI with 1 M water at 313 K. This is nonreacting adsorption spectroscopy, not an independent liquid integral-performance validation.
- `literature/papers/kwon2024-influence-of-ti-incorporated-zeolite/original.pdf`: PDF pp. 6–7, Figure 5 and related discussion, checked directly. Vapor-fed pore-condensed secondary reaction kinetics exhibit sublinear epoxide dependence and cannot uniquely allocate all product inhibition. The new shortlist requires component adequacy in the chosen liquid system and does not transplant vapor parameters as established liquid behavior. Original first page verifies Angewandte Chemie International Edition 63, e202405950, DOI 10.1002/anie.202405950.
- Also checked `literature/papers/torres2024-solvent-stabilization-of-alkene-epoxidation/original.pdf`, PDF p. 12: adsorption enthalpy is an incomplete descriptor, and reaction versus calorimetry environments differ. This informed interpretation; it was not necessary to add another citation for the compact shortlist.

### Zeolite assay history

- `literature/papers/martinez2026-consequences-of-non-mean-field/original.pdf`: PDF pp. 4–5, experimental procedures checked directly. The same sample receives repeated aging and NH3-TPD sequences, with water purge and thermal histories. The original first page verifies Class-Martínez as the surname, Journal of Catalysis 458, 116848, DOI 10.1016/j.jcat.2026.116848.
- `literature/papers/luo2018-nh3-tpd-methodology-for-quantifying/original.pdf`: PDF pp. 5–6, Section 3.4.2 and Figure 7 checked directly. Both aging-order specimens undergo NH3-TPD before receiving their second aging exposure. This is meaningful cumulative-aging evidence but not an assay/no-assay test. DOI 10.1016/j.ces.2018.06.015.

The shortlist explicitly separates function measured on matched specimens before terminal titration from recoverable capacity after a common terminal assay. This prevents the endpoint assay from silently erasing the effect being investigated. An immediate uptake increase does not establish lasting protection.

### Wet methane

- `literature/papers/ryu2024-robust-palladium-oxide-nano-cluster/original.pdf`: Figure 6m and associated feed caption/discussion checked directly. The comparison is between Pd/SSZ-13 variants ST-CO-N2-O2 and ST-CO-O2, with the former receiving an extra N2 treatment between CO and O2. Both variants are now named in the proposed crossed NO/sulfur test. The sulfur introduction accompanies NO; the source does not provide the proposed NO-free sulfur comparison. Original first page confirms author order. DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-52698-4.
- `literature/papers/auvinen2021-effects-of-no-and-no2/original.pdf`: original first-page abstract and source audit support fresh inhibition versus sulfur-history-dependent promotion. Chemical Engineering Journal 417, 128050, DOI 10.1016/j.cej.2020.128050. No unique hydroxyl-removal mechanism is assigned in the shortlist.

A real low-NO sulfur duty is required before calling the diagnostic an application program. Relative material ranking, not generic NO promotion, is the intended decision.

### Ga dehydrogenation

- `literature/papers/wu2025-the-role-of-reducibility-of/original.pdf`: Figure 1 and associated oxygen/CO2 subtraction passage checked directly. Oxygen assigned to coke is taken as CO2; the remaining oxygen is assigned to reoxidation. Hydrogen oxidation and other oxygen-bearing products can change that subtraction. This is a balance ambiguity, not proof that the reported redox assignment is quantitatively wrong or that the independent performance result is invalid. Original first page verifies Angewandte Chemie International Edition 64, e202506704, and author order (David Linke precedes Evgenii V. Kondratenko).
- https://patents.google.com/patent/US20220126281A1/en : original description and Examples 1–7 inspected online. It discloses shortening/removing a later oxygen treatment, enriched-oxygen regeneration, and endpoint conversion examples. This is direct intervention prior art, not a measured complete-cycle productivity comparison.

The retained missing test is a frozen regeneration policy/state relationship tested on a withheld spent history with complete-cycle output, gas and heat accounting. No new oxygen amount or site census is claimed.
