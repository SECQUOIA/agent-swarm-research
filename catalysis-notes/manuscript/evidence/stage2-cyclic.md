## Arithmetic and interpretation checked

- Balanced reactions: C2H6 + [O] → C2H4 + H2O; Li2CO3 + H2O ⇌ 2 LiOH + CO2. The reverse requires the coefficient 2. Rearrangement of the equilibrium expression gives the manuscript activity quotient. The fugacity ratio is approximated by partial pressures only for the atmospheric-pressure diagnostic. No equilibrium constant or loss rate is transferred to the coating.
- Using Li = 6.94, C = 12.011, and O = 15.999 gives Li2CO3 molecular mass 73.888 g/mol. At 0.1 g carbonate per gram final catalyst: 1.3534 mmol carbonate and 18.7852 mg Li. A 0.1% Li change is 18.7852 micrograms. This convention is explicit and does not resolve the source loading discrepancy.
- A five-minute exposure at 100 standard cubic centimetres/min delivers 0.5 L total gas. With 22.414 L/mol at 0°C, this is 22.3075 mmol; 5% CO2 is 1.1154 mmol. Conversion of 0.1% of the illustrative carbonate inventory corresponds to 1.3534 micromoles CO2, or 60.67 ppm of the total gas dose against a 50,000 ppm inlet. These are resolution scales, not predicted losses.
- A one-minute ethane step in each 16-minute cycle gives 62.5 scheduled hours of ethane contact in 1000 elapsed hours. Elapsed longevity is not continuous ethane exposure.
- Complete hypothetical recovery of Table 1 yield: 53.2/47.24 = 1.1261643; 16 × (ratio − 1) = 2.0186283 additional minutes/cycle. A five-minute addition to a 16-minute cycle requires 31.25% greater output, versus the illustrated 12.6164% recovery. This is Ar-aged endpoint headroom, not lifetime-average prevention or predicted steam damage.
- At unchanged purge flow and a one-minute 81.6% ethane pulse, a five-minute purge with CO2 fraction 1/3 circulates 5/(3 × 0.816) = 2.04248366 mol CO2 per mol ethane fed. Maintaining steam/CO2 = 2 in a binary mixture requires CO2 fraction 1/3. Fresh demand differs from circulation. Holding the steam dose fixed instead raises total molar flow by 50%.
- The productivity inequality follows from positive cycle times and positive recovery-policy output. The q variables denote measured mean product amounts per cycle, on equal initial catalyst mass and feed bases. They are not assumed proportional to Li inventory. The diagnostic interaction now also uses amounts per cycle and initial mass, avoiding a yield/amount notation inconsistency.

## Developments beyond the existing protocol

1. Added an independently replicated ordinary-cycle comparison of one selected steam-rich post-oxidation purge against Ar, with no supplementary CO2 or special reset during the fixed block. Conditioning, gas/thermal duty, cycle count, and averaging windows are frozen from pilot qualification. Measure cycle output and cumulative ethylene before any special reset or added dry conditioning. This endpoint includes carryover and reversible inhibition; it is operating performance rather than proof of permanent damage. Each completed steam and Ar history then receives one qualified terminal reset and a common dry-cycle assay. Recovery under this treatment differs from tolerance without it.
2. Retained dry early-CO2 and dry late-CO2 counterparts to the wet timing pair, with an Ar-only reference. The dilute ten-minute timing diagnostic remains distinct from the selected five-minute ordinary purge. Require the timing interaction, wet-concurrent minus dry-early, wet-delayed minus dry-late, every arm's absolute output, and Ar comparisons with uncertainty. Timing nulls apply after the specified CO2 and reset history; they cannot establish untreated steam compatibility.
3. Made the common reset operationally explicit: a pilot-qualified shared duration accommodating the slowest response, a CO2/O2 endpoint, net uptake plus carbonate-sensitive evidence, and measurements through reset and actual washout. In situ observation or validated termination/quench/controlled transfer now applies to first-segment, completed-exposure, reset, and washout endpoints. Document timing and gas history; withhold temporal species/spatial attribution for observables altered by sampling. Do not force equal retained carbonate or assume a dry inlet prevents internally generated water. Post-reset assays qualify both CO2 and O2 carryover against a consequential first-pulse output effect. Persistent difference means failure of the stated reset, not universal irreversibility.
4. When accumulating unresolved diagnostic changes, ordinary ethane/reoxidation intervals and assigned timing segments occur each cycle, with only one special terminal reset after the fixed block. This cadence is frozen and reported with any history-specific loss rate. The diagnostic block's extra five minutes are not mislabeled as the ordinary-cycle duty.
5. Separated diagnostic standardization from policy cost. Compare concurrent protection, delayed restoration, untreated steam, and Ar. The ordinary pre-reset blocks can supply the last two only when histories, block length, and preparation/run blocks match; otherwise matched controls are required. Special operating treatments have a frozen reported cadence, and their time/gas/heat costs enter block productivity. A terminal analytical reset follows the counted output block. Untreated steam and Ar have ordinary oxidation only during that block. The steam-rich condition is selected for a defined gas and heat duty, not extrapolated from the dilute diagnostic ratio.
6. Direct ethylene is the primary output. Carbonate capture/release and retained-carbon changes enter the cycle carbon balance. Bound hydrocarbon-derived COx before assigning combustion selectivity or coke; total regeneration CO2 alone is not a coke assay. Report attribution limits when the carbonate and hydrocarbon contributions cannot be resolved.
7. Named conditional spatial/state methods: cross-sectional NanoSIMS for Li-rich patches and rim-to-interior gradients, with submicrometre feature resolution, matrix/geometry/beam controls, and microscopy correlation; Mössbauer for Fe-state/phase indicators, with references, repeatability, and uncertainty sufficient to separate the proposed change. Neither method establishes unresolved interface equivalence or absolute coating coverage. Access and adequate sensitivity remain dependencies.
8. Defined a prospective follow-on: calibrate continuous protected and delayed histories, then withhold an alternating schedule with frozen period and treatment cadence. Predict cycle output and Li transfer through the operating block and standardized function after one terminal diagnostic reset. A successful Li/output relationship is a useful predictor within the tested range, not proof of causal dominance; replenishment can change composition, coverage, and carrier structure as well as Li inventory. An inventory-only model fails if equal inventory gives different function. Any added state must earn value through independent prediction.
9. Narrowed the opening and closing scope to the post-oxidation purge, defined gas/duty/state/cycle-count and recovery histories, and conditional prediction. A useful ordinary-cycle null supports compatibility without supplementary CO2 only over that tested block. Recovery after the terminal reset supports recovery under that treatment. The uncertainty interval for the predeclared direct wet P-minus-R contrast must exclude a consequential advantage before closing the persistent timing-benefit claim after the stated reset. Individual arm recovery bounds do not establish that comparison; they remain separate conclusions. The Dsteam interval tests whether steam modifies the timing benefit. Neither contrast rules out the need for all CO2 treatment. This direct-contrast stopping rule implements the sole accepted round 2 minor correction. Post-ethane steam purging and lifetime tolerance remain untested. Source Ar aging remains a separate conditional branch. No broad coating, salt, temperature, or isotope campaign is included.

## Remaining experimental dependencies

Reproducible measured formulation, contact-material compatibility, gas switching, ordinary-cycle steam response, a demonstrated reset endpoint, Li assay and capture recovery, adequate state preservation and method resolution, and prospective output prediction all require experiments. The chapter closes branches when they cannot resolve a consequential contrast. The source equation typo and loading inconsistency are corrected or explicitly retained; neither invalidates the bounded compatibility program.
