# Stage 5 coordinator literature audit

This audit supports the synthesis stage. It distinguishes mathematical verification from historical priority. A negative search is not proof that a result is new. The manuscript must make specific contributions without claiming that finite reservoirs, Gaussian ensembles, phase selection, or correlated equilibrium subsystems were introduced here.

## Assessment of the developed results

Targeted searches combined finite reservoirs, phase coexistence, total variation, optimized calibration, Gaussian mixtures, and the three-halves capacity scale. No inspected primary source was found that gives the same collection of weak-support necessity, optimal two-phase physical capacity, complete microscopic phase-tail realization, and global boundary-error optimization. This is a bounded literature finding, not an exhaustive priority guarantee.

The strongest distinctions to retain are:

- Optimization over every admissible physical composite energy, with a fixed canonical target.
- Sharp necessity from weak support and separate positive-phase conditions for sufficiency.
- The microscopic short-range sufficient bound, which requires positive interfacial-mass and phase-moment control.
- Full microscopic marginal, joint, and information limits under a shared external reservoir.
- Optimization of the physical boundary error including phase abandonment, and of finite Gaussian multiphase loss including unbounded fields.

The conditional capillarity theorem remains conditional on its stated large-deviation input. Its square-droplet example is an explicit isotropic model, not a proved microscopic two-dimensional Potts morphology theorem. Existing limitations of these input models are mathematical scope conditions, not silently unresolved proofs.
