## Actionable issue

**Minor M1 — describe the truncation without implying smoothness.** In `appendices/short-range-proof.tex:114`, the sentence “This positive smooth cap ensures uniform convergence” immediately follows the definition of the minimum-truncated activity. The exponential upper-cap function itself is smooth, but the minimum with the original activity need not be differentiable. This sentence can therefore mislead the reader about the central regularity issue. Replace it with, for example, “This positive cap ensures uniform convergence of the cluster expansion.” The later proof correctly uses absolute continuity for the truncated model, removes every cutoff, and only then differentiates twice. This is a wording correction, not a gap in that proof.

There are no major revision requests. No additional theorem or numerical experiment is required by this review.
