\section{Canonical marginals and correlated phases under a shared reservoir}
\label{sec:shared-baths}

This section proves that, for $N\ll c_N\ll N^2$, a reservoir shared
by two identical copies under the centered calibration selects
opposite phases (Theorem~\ref{thm:shared-selection}) and that the
mutual information of the two full states tends to one bit
(Proposition~\ref{prop:shared-one-bit}). With balanced phase weights,
each one-copy marginal is asymptotically canonical while the joint TV
tends to $1/2$. Joint canonical accuracy requires $c_N\gg N^2$
(Corollary~\ref{cor:shared-joint-threshold}).
Section~\ref{subsec:shared-balancing} balances the phase weights with
a temperature shift of order $1/N$.
Thermally coupled systems with negative specific heat were shown to
develop different subsystem phases and to exchange their
assignments~\cite{RamirezHernandezLarraldeLeyvraz2008}; we give the
reservoir-size conditions for this behavior in the full microscopic
law.

\subsection{Opposite-phase selection by the exact reservoir}

Let $P_N$ be the canonical law of one copy of size $N$ at the
specified inverse temperature $\beta_N\to\beta>0$. Assume two disjoint
measurable phase events $A_{-,N},A_{+,N}$ partition its state space,
with
\begin{equation}
 w_{i,N}:=P_N(A_{i,N})\longrightarrow w_i>0,
 \qquad w_-+w_+=1.
 \label{eq:shared-phase-weights}
\end{equation}
Let $e_{-,N}<e_{+,N}$ be deterministic energy centers, with
$\Delta_N=e_{+,N}-e_{-,N}$, chosen so that
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_N/N\longrightarrow\ell>0,\qquad
 (E_N-e_{i,N})/\sqrt N\mid A_{i,N}\quad\hbox{is tight}.
 \label{eq:shared-tightness}
\end{equation}
These assumptions allow discrete phase laws and a degenerate
conditional energy limit.

Two noninteracting copies, with full microscopic states $X_1,X_2$
and phase labels $S_1,S_2$, have the reference law
$\Pi_N=P_N\otimes P_N$. Their combined energy $E_N(X_1)+E_N(X_2)$ is
coupled to one exact reservoir with the centered calibration
\begin{equation}
 M_N=e_{-,N}+e_{+,N},\qquad
 \mathcal E_N=M_N+c_N/\beta_N.
 \label{eq:shared-centered-energy}
\end{equation}
The centered weight from~\eqref{eq:centered-weight} is
\begin{equation}
 W_N=
 \begin{cases}
 \exp[\beta_N T_N]
       (1-\beta_N T_N/c_N)^{c_N},&\beta_N T_N<c_N,\\
 0,&\beta_N T_N\ge c_N,
 \end{cases}
 \quad
 T_N=E_N(X_1)+E_N(X_2)-M_N.
 \label{eq:shared-weight}
\end{equation}
Let $z_N=\E_{\Pi_N}W_N$ and $\dd Q_N/\dd\Pi_N=W_N/z_N$. By
\eqref{eq:global-centered-bound}, $0\le W_N\le1$ for all states.

\begin{theorem}[Opposite phases and full marginal accuracy]
\label{thm:shared-selection}
Under~\eqref{eq:shared-phase-weights}--\eqref{eq:shared-tightness},
suppose
\begin{equation}
 N\ll c_N\ll N^2.
 \label{eq:shared-window}
\end{equation}
For $O_N=\{S_1\ne S_2\}$, the shared reservoir with the centered
calibration~\eqref{eq:shared-centered-energy} satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \TV\bigl(Q_N,\Pi_N(\cdot\mid O_N)\bigr)\longrightarrow0.
 \label{eq:shared-selection-tv}
\end{equation}
Consequently, with $Q_N^{(j)}$ denoting either one-copy marginal,
\begin{align}
 \TV(Q_N,\Pi_N)&\longrightarrow w_-^2+w_+^2,
 \label{eq:shared-joint-tv}\\
 \TV(Q_N^{(j)},P_N)&\longrightarrow |w_--1/2|,
 \qquad j=1,2.
 \label{eq:shared-marginal-tv}
\end{align}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On either opposite-phase assignment $T_N=O_{\Pi_N}(\sqrt N)$
conditionally. The Taylor expansion
$x+\log(1-x)=-x^2/2+O(|x|^3)$, together with $N/c_N\to0$, gives
$\log W_N\to0$ in its conditional probability; the cutoff lies beyond
every fixed fluctuation window. On the same-phase assignments,
$T_N=\pm\Delta_N+O_{\Pi_N}(\sqrt N)$. Because $c_N/N\to\infty$,
the Taylor expansion still applies in conditional probability, but now
\[
 \log W_N=-\frac{\beta_N^2\Delta_N^2}{2c_N}[1+o_{\Pi_N}(1)]
            \longrightarrow-\infty.
\]
Thus $W_N-\ind_{O_N}\to0$ in probability and, by boundedness, in
$L^1(\Pi_N)$. In particular,
$z_N\to p_O:=2w_-w_+>0$. Normalizing the two nonnegative weights
proves~\eqref{eq:shared-selection-tv}.

Conditioning a probability measure on an event of probability $p$
changes it by $1-p$ in total variation, which gives
\eqref{eq:shared-joint-tv}. The exact marginal of
$\Pi_N(\cdot\mid O_N)$ is
\[
 \tfrac12P_N(\cdot\mid A_{-,N})+
 \tfrac12P_N(\cdot\mid A_{+,N}).
\]
The two phase supports are disjoint, so its distance from $P_N$ is
$|w_{-,N}-1/2|$. Total variation contracts under projection, proving
\eqref{eq:shared-marginal-tv}.
\end{proof}

With the copies in opposite phases, the reservoir absorbs only
within-phase energy fluctuations. No density approximation, contour
decomposition, or bound on the probability of a particular interfacial
configuration is needed: tightness controls the atypical canonical
mass, and the weight~\eqref{eq:shared-weight}, bounded by one, cannot
amplify it.

\subsection{The full-state mutual information}
\label{subsec:shared-information}

Total variation does not control relative entropy on growing or
continuous state spaces, so mutual information needs a separate
argument. For $Q\ll P$, define
$\KL(Q\Vert P)=\int\log(\dd Q/\dd P)\dd Q$, with $0\log0=0$.
Logarithms are natural; $\log2$ nats is one bit. The mutual
information of a joint law $Q$ is
$I_Q(X_1;X_2)=\KL(Q\Vert Q^{(1)}\otimes Q^{(2)})$.

\begin{proposition}[One bit in the full state law]
\label{prop:shared-one-bit}
Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:shared-selection},
\begin{equation}
 I_{Q_N}(X_1;X_2)\longrightarrow\log2.
 \label{eq:shared-one-bit}
\end{equation}
This limit is independent of the original positive phase weights.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Since $x\log x$ is continuous and bounded on $[0,1]$,
$\E_{\Pi_N}W_N\log W_N\to0$ by the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:shared-selection}. Hence
\begin{equation}
 \KL(Q_N\Vert\Pi_N)
 =z_N^{-1}\E_{\Pi_N}W_N\log W_N-\log z_N
 \longrightarrow-\log(2w_-w_+).
 \label{eq:shared-joint-kl}
\end{equation}
Each marginal likelihood $r_{j,N}=\dd Q_N^{(j)}/\dd P_N$ is at most
$1/z_N$, hence uniformly bounded for large $N$. By
\eqref{eq:shared-selection-tv} it converges in $L^1(P_N)$ to the
finite-$N$ conditional marginal ratio
\[
 r_N^O=\frac{\ind_{A_{-,N}}}{2w_{-,N}}
             +\frac{\ind_{A_{+,N}}}{2w_{+,N}}.
\]
Both ratios lie in a common compact interval of $[0,\infty)$, on which
$r\log r$ is uniformly continuous, so convergence in probability
implies convergence of its integrals:
\begin{equation}
 \KL(Q_N^{(j)}\Vert P_N)
       \longrightarrow\tfrac12\log\frac1{2w_-}
                        +\tfrac12\log\frac1{2w_+}.
 \label{eq:shared-marginal-kl}
\end{equation}
All divergences are finite because the likelihood ratios are bounded,
and the relative-entropy chain rule gives
\begin{equation}
 I_{Q_N}(X_1;X_2)=\KL(Q_N\Vert\Pi_N)
                   -\sum_{j=1}^2\KL(Q_N^{(j)}\Vert P_N).
 \label{eq:shared-kl-chain}
\end{equation}
Substituting~\eqref{eq:shared-joint-kl} and
\eqref{eq:shared-marginal-kl} proves~\eqref{eq:shared-one-bit}.
\end{proof}

This $\log2$ comes entirely from the uniform, perfectly anticorrelated
phase labels; in the window~\eqref{eq:shared-window}, within-phase
fluctuations add nothing in the limit. At capacity proportional to
$N$, under Gaussian phase fluctuations with positive variances, they
do (Proposition~\ref{prop:shared-linear-information} in
Appendix~\ref{app:shared-extensions}).

\subsection{Joint canonical accuracy requires a quadratic-size reservoir}

For a fixed number of copies, some calibration achieves joint
canonical accuracy exactly when $c_N\gg N^2$.

\begin{corollary}[Optimized joint threshold]
\label{cor:shared-joint-threshold}
Suppose only that, for some centers $a_N$, $(E_N-a_N)/N$ converges
weakly to a law with exactly two distinct positive-weight atoms.
For any fixed number of
independent identical copies $m\ge2$, a shared
reservoir~\eqref{eq:bath-dos} can reproduce their full-state canonical
product law in total variation,
by some calibration, if and only if $c_N\gg N^2$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
After a common centering, the total energy divided by $N$ converges
to the $m$-fold convolution of the two-atom law. It has $m+1\ge3$
distinct support points, all of positive weight. Apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:three-support} with $b_N=N$ to the combined subsystem.
Since $m$ is fixed, using $N$ rather than $mN$ does not change the
condition.
\end{proof}

Under the assumptions of Section~\ref{sec:shared-baths},
$(E_N-e_{-,N})/N$ has limiting atoms $0,\ell$ with weights $w_-,w_+$,
so the corollary applies; for $m=2$ the combined energy
$[E_N(X_1)+E_N(X_2)-M_N]/N$ has atoms $-\ell,0,\ell$ with weights
$w_-^2,2w_-w_+,w_+^2$. No central limit theorem within the phases is
needed.

For the microscopic models, an isolated copy has the sharp threshold
$c_N\gg N^{3/2}$, yet with balanced phase weights a shared reservoir
with $c_N=N^{5/4}$ (too small for an isolated copy) gives canonical
one-copy marginals, because the copies trade the latent heat with
each other: when one is in the lower phase the other is in the upper
one, so their total energy stays within $O(\sqrt N)$ of $M_N$ and the
reservoir absorbs only within-phase fluctuations.
Appendix~\ref{app:shared-extensions} also treats a fixed phase count
among $m$ copies (Proposition~\ref{prop:shared-count}).

\subsection{Phase events for the microscopic models}
\label{subsec:shared-microscopic}

Both microscopic models satisfy
\eqref{eq:shared-phase-weights}--\eqref{eq:shared-tightness}. For the
mean-field model of Section~\ref{subsec:meanfield}, take the ordered
and disordered Voronoi cells; Lemma~\ref{lem:mf-exponential} gives
conditional exponential moments on the $\sqrt N$ scale, hence
tightness, with or without momenta. For the short-range model of
Section~\ref{subsec:short-range}, take the midpoint energy events
$B_{o,N}$ and $B_{d,N}$ defined in
Appendix~\ref{app:short-range-proof}; their conditional standardized
energies converge weakly by \eqref{eq:sr-conditional-clt}, hence are
tight.

\subsection{Balancing the reference phase weights}
\label{subsec:shared-balancing}

The microscopic models have unequal limiting phase weights at
coexistence, but $\beta_N$ may be any specified sequence, so the
canonical reference can be taken at a nearby temperature. A shift of
order $1/N$ changes the limiting phase weights but leaves the
phase-conditional laws asymptotically unchanged in total variation.

\begin{lemma}[A bounded-energy temperature shift]
\label{lem:shared-temperature-shift}
Let the spin energy $U_N/N$ be uniformly bounded and suppose the phase
conditional laws at $\beta_c$ concentrate at $u_-<u_+$, with positive
limiting weights $w_-,w_+$. For
$\beta_N=\beta_c+\delta/N+o(N^{-1})$, the new limiting weight ratio is
\begin{equation}
 \frac{w_-^{(\delta)}}{w_+^{(\delta)}}
       =\frac{w_-}{w_+}\exp[\delta(u_+-u_-)].
 \label{eq:shared-temperature-ratio}
\end{equation}
The new spin law conditional on either phase approaches the old
phase-conditional law in total variation. Hence any conditional weak
fluctuation limit, with the same center and scale, persists.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The unnormalized likelihood $\exp[-(\beta_N-\beta_c)U_N]$ of the
change of canonical measure is bounded above and below by positive
constants uniformly in $N$, and on phase $i$ it converges in
probability to $e^{-\delta u_i}$. Bounded convergence gives the
limiting phase normalizations and~\eqref{eq:shared-temperature-ratio}.
After normalizing within either phase, the likelihood converges to
one in $L^1$, proving the conditional total-variation assertion.
\end{proof}

Writing $\ell=u_+-u_-$, asymptotic balance is obtained by
\begin{equation}
 \beta_N=\beta_c-\frac{\log(w_-/w_+)}{N\ell}+o(N^{-1}).
 \label{eq:shared-balanced-temperature}
\end{equation}
The negative sign means warming when the ordered, lower-energy phase
has excess weight. For the short-range model $w_-/w_+=q$, giving the
shift $-\log q/(N\ell)$; for the three-state mean-field model the
ratio is $3r_*$ from~\eqref{eq:mf-phase-weights} and $\ell=J/12$.
Both spin Hamiltonians are bounded per particle, so
Lemma~\ref{lem:shared-temperature-shift} applies.

The shift also preserves the isolated-copy $N^{3/2}$ thresholds.
For the short-range positive contour decomposition, retain the
conditional bond kernel $K_{\beta_c}(\dd A\mid\sigma)$ of the
Edwards--Sokal law at $\beta_c$ (it need not be the kernel at
$\beta_N$) and define the auxiliary law
\[
 \widehat P_N(\dd\sigma,\dd A)
   =P_{\beta_N}(\dd\sigma)K_{\beta_c}(\dd A\mid\sigma).
\]
Its likelihood relative to the old joint law is
$\dd P_{\beta_N}/\dd P_{\beta_c}$, bounded above and below by positive
constants, so the fixed contour events still give a positive
decomposition of the new physical spin law; their conditional
exponential moments remain bounded, and their exceptional mass
increases by at most a constant factor. For the mean-field model, the
same bounded spin likelihood directly preserves the positive phase
moment bounds.

Independent momenta, taken at the same $\beta_N$, contribute a
partition factor common to both spin phases, which cancels from the
weight ratio. Their law has relative entropy with respect to the
kinetic law at $\beta_c$ (from the product Gaussian momenta or their
Gamma energy law)
\[
 aN\left[\frac{\beta_c}{\beta_N}-1
                     -\log\frac{\beta_c}{\beta_N}\right]=O(N^{-1}).
\]
By Pinsker's inequality the two kinetic laws converge in total
variation; the mean energy changes by $O(1)$, and the $\sqrt N$
Gaussian fluctuation limit is unchanged. No globally bounded kinetic
likelihood is asserted.

With this reference for every copy and the same $\beta_N$
in~\eqref{eq:shared-centered-energy}, the phase events of
Section~\ref{subsec:shared-microscopic} keep their conditional limits
by Lemma~\ref{lem:shared-temperature-shift}.
Theorem~\ref{thm:shared-selection} then gives canonical one-copy
marginals and joint TV error $1/2$, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:shared-one-bit} gives one bit of mutual
information. The law reproduced is the balanced canonical law at
$\beta_N$, not the unbalanced law at $\beta_c$.
