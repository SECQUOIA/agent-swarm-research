2026-09-07. This is an editorial and mathematical-status audit of [working-paper-kinetic-defects.md](working-paper-kinetic-defects.md), read against the current result index and supporting reviews. It does not add a research result.

The draft's five theorem statements remain consistent with their supporting records. These statements concern tested designs, and do not imply that an optimized predetermined design is worse than uniform. No unsupported experimental or manufacturing claim was found.

Later positive-moment, generic-fold and finite-precision results expand the repository beyond the scope of the current paper; their absence from its five theorems is not a mathematical inconsistency. The general logarithmic bulk lemma also gives a broader proof route than the special trial estimates summarized in Section 7. Those earlier estimates remain valid, and the draft does not claim that they are the only possible transfer argument.

## Recommended manuscript division

Keep the current draft centered on the physical transport model, exact finite-bulk reduction, weak-surface-diffusion crossover, and explicit mobility placement with a finite rate floor. Its disorder and observation sections can serve as a short bridge, or move to a companion manuscript during later editing.

The subsequently prepared [companion draft](working-paper-uncertain-mobility.md) follows this recommendation. Its main question is: how do the chosen disorder moment and the precision of observing a pattern change the best small-budget transport response? The existing material comprises the [predetermined and adaptive expected-design theorem](robust-mobility-design.md), [positive-moment design theorem](risk-sensitive-mobility.md), [generic fold extension](generic-kinetic-folds.md), and [finite-precision theorem](finite-precision-mobility.md), with the general finite-bulk transfer as a shared supporting result. This is a recommendation for organizing completed results, not a proposal to start additional research.

Such a companion should distinguish order theorems from sharp equivalents, identify which sharp constants use the cosine ensemble, and preserve fixed positive bulk diffusivity and one-dimensional wall assumptions. The generic theorem excludes unsupported higher degeneracies and unsampled root-containing fold sides. Exact finite-budget optimizers and profile convergence must not be inferred from the asymptotic values. Primary-source novelty comparisons and remaining access gaps should accompany both manuscripts.

No change to the current paper's central theorem statements was needed in this audit. The companion contains only existing results. The critical-coefficient review is complete and its verified status is integrated.
