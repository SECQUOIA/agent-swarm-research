## Finding

### R5-01 — Minor: distinguish the fold operator scale from the integrated energy scale

**Location:** `sections/05-exact-observation.tex`, lines 411–420, in the fold-layer policy construction.

**Reason:** The sentence “The derivative energy has the same scale r^4” uses the operator coefficient scale for the energy. For a test of unchanged amplitude under `s−s_0=rx`, the integrated derivative and reaction energies both carry a factor `r^5`; their differential-operator coefficients carry `r^4`. The subsequent response scale `r/r^4=r^(−3)` is correct, so this is a wording/normalization clarification rather than a flaw in the estimate.

**Remedy:** Say that the rescaled operator is `r^4[-(2B)^(−1)∂_x²+V_(r,mu)]`, or explicitly state that the integrated energy has scale `r^5` and the source has scale r. Either formulation transparently gives response scale `r^(−3)`.
