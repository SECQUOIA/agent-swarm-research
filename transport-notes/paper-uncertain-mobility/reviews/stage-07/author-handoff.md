## Numerical work and distinctions

`numerics/reproduce.py` is the manuscript-owned driver. It imports the existing sound tridiagonal half-wall solver, explicit observed trial and graded design construction by resolved repository paths. The README lists those dependencies. Unlike the old circle comparison, **every sampled field is normalized to exactly the same discrete budget**, including uniform and observed fields. This changes some finite-grid numbers slightly without changing their interpretations.

The script reran 11 circle trial cases for mean, critical and third moments; 9 circle refinement cases separate spatial and offset quadrature changes; 4 primary 400-cell uncertain-center optimizations; and 5 additional center cases for mesh, domain and deliberately inadequate scenario quadrature. All 9 optimizations report success, nonnegative feasible masses to floating tolerance, and nonnegative tangent gaps. Stored data are `data/numerical-evidence.json`; all intermediate printouts are in `reviews/stage-07/numerical-run.log`. Figure PDFs and generated table rows are regenerated by the same driver.

Circle methodology is fully specified in the manuscript: midpoint reaction, face mobility, zero flux on the reflected half-wall, exact discrete mass, full-circle doubling, offset density factor, budget-scaled quadrature subdivisions, and every trial's formula. Mean observed-trial values are explicitly distinct from the policy used to prove the sharp exact-observation limit. The figures never call those finite-budget trials optimal.

The uncertain-center calculation specifies mobility support `[-L,L]`, free interior endpoints and exact exterior reciprocal-potential response. The derivative gives a global tangent lower value for the **finite-dimensional, finite-quadrature** convex objective, evaluated numerically. The paper makes clear that this does not bound continuum discretization error or certify floating arithmetic. Re-evaluation on 384 nodes, a separate 200→400-cell study, and `L=22,n=400` versus `L=33,n=600` at fixed mesh spacing are included. The larger-domain change lies below optimizer-gap uncertainty and is described as a diagnostic only. At eta = 0 the computed value lies below exact Cpl, explicitly demonstrating the distinction.

The negative 24-node case at eta = 32 is retained: it has a small discrete tangent gap but its value rises 11.6% on independent 384-node quadrature. No computed value of S_q or global H(tau) is claimed. The uniform and optimized asymptotic constants shown as guides come from proved formulas, not numerical fits.
