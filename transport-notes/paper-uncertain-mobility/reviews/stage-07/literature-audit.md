# Stage 07 primary literature and contribution audit

Date: 2026-09-07. This record accompanies the manuscript synthesis. It distinguishes primary material inspected in this stage, prior-stage inspections carried forward, and access limits. It does not claim an exhaustive proof of originality. No literature knowledge-base package or generated literature bibliography was modified; no downloaded source PDF is redistributed in this folder.

## Contribution being compared

The paper's claims are the sharp small-budget moment values for random vanishing reaction and unrestricted nonnegative integrable mobility; generic transverse-fold orders; the exact-information mean; the simultaneous quantization–budget law and attained local crossover; and the same-information finite-bulk optimal-value comparison. It does not claim to invent compliance duality, measure relaxation, gradient saturation, power-law allocation, rare-bifurcation moment selection, or decision making under partial information.

Unlike older repository audit notes, the manuscript now proves both the sharp supercritical variational coefficient and the finite positive resolution-to-budget ratio limit. Historical statements that those questions are open refer to the earlier notes and do not describe the accepted Sections 03 and 06.
