## Scope and assumptions that must survive every theorem

The problem is a dilute, linear bulk–surface transport model with fixed adsorption affinity and a reversible transverse process. Longitudinal advection need not be reversible. The wall has zero longitudinal advective drift; axial molecular diffusion is a separate contribution and need not vanish. These assumptions give the mean velocity and constant surface source used below. The kinetic pattern is quenched: sampled once for each wall and fixed during a particle's motion. Disorder moments concern differences among wall realizations, not moments of a single tracer's displacement. The physical dispersion convention is the stationary long-time coefficient `Var X(t) ~ 2 D_eff t`, where existence and any needed ergodicity assumptions must be proved or precisely cited. The whole-line source problems are local asymptotic models, not normalized stationary systems.

The canonical dimensionless ensemble has wall `Γ=R/(2πZ)`, arclength `s∈[0,2π)`, desorption rate `k_c(s)=(c+cos s)^2`, and offset `c` uniformly distributed on `[-2,2]` with density `1/4`. Equal-bin observation means observing only the index of the bin containing `c`: for an integer `N≥1` and `Δ=4/N`, bins have endpoints `-2+jΔ`, `j=0,…,N`. Take bins half-open except for the final endpoint; this convention has no effect on expectations. A mobility field may depend on that bin index, with the same budget `M` in every bin. Exact observation reveals `c`, and predetermined design reveals no information about `c`.

The physical cross-section is fixed, smooth enough for the stated trace map, and bounded and connected, with a connected closed one-dimensional wall. The main full-bulk transfer uses fixed positive bulk transverse diffusivity, a fixed L² velocity field with nonzero stationary mean, constant positive affinity, and uniformly bounded nonnegative rates with a uniformly positive wall integral. Connectedness of the bulk alone does not imply connectedness of the wall. A multiple-component extension requires anchoring each component and is not silently included.

The admissible scalar fields are arbitrary nonnegative L¹ mobilities with a fixed integral budget for each observation; unbounded spikes, zero sets, and budget-dependent fine structure cannot be ruled out merely for proof convenience. Upper-bound constructions may use positive backgrounds. Measurability of the response and policies, the countable-test reduction, and the closed weighted form used to identify an actual transport coefficient are proved in accepted Stage 01. Exact-observation notation `E inf` must be justified against the infimum over measurable policies, or replaced by a definition/proof that avoids that interchange.

Physical quantities must be nondimensionalized before canonical constants are transferred. If the physical wall length is `P_phys`, let `ℓ_ref=P_phys/(2π)` and choose a reference rate `k_ref`. Then `D'=D_phys/(k_ref ℓ_ref²)`, `M'=M_phys/(k_ref ℓ_ref³)`, and `J_phys=(ℓ_ref/k_ref)J'`. A canonical coefficient multiplying a power of `M'` acquires the response factor `ℓ_ref/k_ref` before the dimensional prefactor `χ=KV²/Z`. Writing only `χ` times a dimensionless constant would be incorrect. Accepted Stage 01 fixes this convention for the entire paper.

## Numerical and scientific claims

N1 ([reviewed Stage 07 numerical support](reviews/stage-07/acceptance.md)) covers the uniform-design moments, explicit graded trials, slow critical convergence, finite-budget blind-profile reversal, and finite-dimensional uncertain-center optimization. Source scripts/data are linked in [numerical verification](../notes/numerical-verification.md). Existing data are evidence to reproduce, not a substitute for new independent certificates. A continuum optimum, a discrete optimum, and an evaluated trial are different quantities. Sparse scenario quadrature can overfit a design and must be checked on independent parameter nodes. Quartic whole-line tail integrals require explicit truncation and tail-error accounting; uncertified numerical digits must not be called rigorous constants.

The principal scientific interpretation is a mathematical relationship among kinetic information, an integral mobility budget, and stationary transport spreading. The model does not establish that mobility, exchange rates, and affinity are independently adjustable in a real coating. It has no mobility cap, fixed fabrication length, measurement price, adsorption saturation, moving interface, or noisy-measurement model. Numerical budgets have no experimental units until a physical normalization is chosen.

Existing transport work includes Dill–Brenner and Levesque et al.; conductivity/reinforcement and stochastic compliance precedents include Bouchitté–Buttazzo, Buttazzo–Oudet–Velichkov, and Buttazzo–Maestre; rare-bifurcation moment selection includes Berry–Keating–Schomerus; observation-channel/quantization precedents include Yüksel–Linder and Saldi–Yüksel–Linder. The 2026 Alphonse–Kunštek–Vrdoljak risk-aware conductivity preprint is inspected and bibliographically verified in the Stage 07 literature record. The oscillator, Morse normal form, inverse-form convexity, Hölder allocation, and elementary value-of-information inequality are not new inventions.

The novelty candidates are the unrestricted singular transport-design laws, their sharp constants where proved, generic-fold extension, and budget-dependent measurement-resolution theorem. The Stage 07 literature record compares the results against primary sources; any later change in scope must reopen the relevant comparison. Search failure is not proof of novelty. Older inaccessible full texts are a literature uncertainty to investigate and transparently scope, not a reason to fabricate coverage.

## Excluded material

Traveling/compliant channels, periodic-removal no-go results, homometric inverse constructions, general degenerate-mobility gamma formulas, anomalous finite-time renewal preparation effects, deterministic finite-rate-floor phase diagrams, and joint optimization of total mobility and flow are outside this paper's core problem. Relevant definitions or a necessary quadratic placement lemma are rederived here. Exclusion avoids combining unrelated research questions; it does not imply those notes are incorrect or uninteresting.
