\section{Conditional recourse}\label{sec:recourse}
The condition number $\kappa$ of Section~\ref{sec:growth} is built from the
largest diagonal curvature of $F$. Stiff convex terms, which are easy to
optimize, can make it large, and so can a large residual problem whose
nonconvexity has a combinatorial rather than a metric structure. This
section shows how exact optimization over part of the variables, which we
call recourse, fits into the corrected-grid framework.
