## Detailed quantitative checks

1. **Residual transfer.** In the canonical extension the only potentially violated fixed equations are the addition pin and inversion pin D, with residuals `|x+y−z|` and `|y−1/x|`. For an arbitrary box point, each complement step adds at most epsilon; at most `6m` steps therefore give delta=`6m epsilon`. At C_I the error is at most epsilon+delta, and dividing the I residual is legitimate because its voltage is at least 1/2. The bound `|h'|<=2` holds on the entire required interval. Combining the D error (epsilon+3 delta) with the I/W error yields `10 epsilon+10 delta` for `|xy−1|`. This verifies both constants in the theorem, including the unconstrained case `m=0`.

2. **Tiny infeasible family.** The first equations force `t=1`, `h=1/2`, `x_0=3/2`. The recurrence gives `x_j=1+1/d_j` with `d_0=2` and `d_{j+1}=d_j(d_j+1)`, so the final inversion is impossible. All other recurrence coordinates stay in their source intervals. The canonical network extension violates exactly the final D equation by `1/(d_k+1)`, not by the larger source residual `1/d_k`. The counts `4k+3` variables and `4k+4` equations imply the advertised network size bounds; the incidence graph is connected. Compactness proves strict positivity of the minimum independently of the exhibited upper bound. The input-length and accuracy-bit conclusions are appropriately limited to residual thresholds, with no decision-time lower bound inferred.

3. **General separation.** The epigraph set has exactly `4n+2` weak constraints in dimension `q=n+1`, is nonempty and compact, and is connected through its full top face. Thus it is itself a compact connected component, as required by the published Jeronimo–Perrucci–Tsigaridas Theorem 1.1 (page 242). Positive denominator clearing preserves it. Substitution of degree 2 gives exponent `q 4^q` and base `2^(4−q/2) Htilde 2^q`; the manuscript's logarithmic relaxation is valid. Fixed data and bounded degree allow a constant cleared coefficient bound, with only the theorem's constraint/dimension term growing. The recurrence family and this upper bound establish the stated worst-case scale in the number of buses. The cited paper's repeated-power separation example is appropriately credited; restricted electrical realization is the additional development.

4. **Certificates and stability.** The row gradient bound gives the stated sup-norm Lipschitz constant. Dyadic rounding followed by clamping preserves rational singleton endpoints and polynomial encoding length, so the specified promise verifier has completeness and soundness. The energy/Poincare proof gives the angle and active-discrepancy bounds, and the graph-only eigenvalue lower bound follows from the pairwise-difference identity with the displayed factor 2. Substituting `ell=1/2`, `U=4`, and `gmin>=1` gives the corollary constant 256. Componentwise treatment and the isolated-bus exception are correct.
