## New AC development

The crossing orientation and the potential sign agree. With the manuscript's orientation `j -> i`, `delta_ij = alpha_i - alpha_j + 2*pi*k_ij`, so `a_i-a_j=k_ij` gives `theta_i=alpha_i+2*pi*a_i` and exactly the desired line difference. On a forest, a zero root and the edge equations uniquely determine each potential as an integer sum. Non-tree edges impose exactly the fundamental-cycle conditions. Real quantification of the potentials therefore needs no separate integrality restriction. Isolated vertices have their own zero root; the zero-vertex case is also consistent.

The linear count in the theorem is justified: four variables per vertex, one per edge, constant-size crossing formulas per edge, one edge difference equation, and incident-line power expressions whose total number of monomial occurrences is linear. Clearing rational denominators changes coefficients but not degree or monomial count. Polynomial total bit length, rather than linear bit length, is the correct claim and is what the manuscript states.

Unsquared positive magnitude variables correctly permit negative cosine limits, and `c>-1` excludes antipodes. The lift criterion describes zero winding over the reals, not only vanishing modulo `2*pi`. The new graph-potential representation is appropriately presented as elementary rather than as a new graph or winding theorem.

## Focused quantitative audit

**Residual transfer.** At an exact voltage-pinned copy bus, injection error is complement-sum error. At most `6m` path extensions therefore give copy error at most `6m*epsilon`. The addition error is at most `epsilon+3*delta`. For inversion, the tie error is `epsilon+delta`, dividing the nonlinear equation costs at most two because `v_I>=1/2`, and `|h'|<=2` on the actual interval `[1/2,2]`. The final weighted pin contributes `epsilon+3*delta`; multiplication by the root value costs at most two. Their sum is exactly the conservative `10*(epsilon+delta)` used in the proof. The forward construction is valid even on source nonsolutions because its auxiliary `h(x)` stays in the network box. It violates only addition pins and the final inversion pins, with factor at most two. Empty equation sets are handled.

**Tiny residual family.** The recurrence gives `delta_(j+1)=delta_j^2/(1+delta_j)` and, with `delta_j=1/d_j`, gives `d_(j+1)=d_j*(d_j+1)`. It remains strictly positive, so the final equation contradicts the preceding equations. All displayed variable ranges hold, including `c_j<=5/3`. The last electrical inversion residual is `1-1/x_k=1/(d_k+1)`, not the different source residual `x_k-1`; the manuscript correctly distinguishes them. The counts `4k+3`, `4k+4`, and the resulting network upper bounds are consistent. Incidence connectedness survives the ordinary gadget replacement. Compactness proves the minimum is positive, while the exhibited rational point provides only an upper bound, precisely as stated. The graph's linear size in `k` supports the claimed exponential accuracy-bit scale in bus count.

**General separation.** I read the published primary Theorem 1.1 in `revision-20260907/sources/jeronimo-published.txt`, including the dimension, compact connected component, even degree, and common coefficient-height hypotheses. The epigraph set in the manuscript is nonempty and compact; it is connected because all vertical fibers reach the convex top face. It is therefore itself a compact connected component of the basic closed set being supplied to that theorem. There are exactly `4n+2` inequalities. The objective is the integer polynomial `t`; including it in the height bound is correct. Substitution `q=n+1`, `d=2` gives exponent `q*4^q`, with base `2^(4-q/2)*Htilde*2^q`. Its logarithm is bounded by the displayed `tau+q+4`. Fixed data and bounded degree make the cleared coefficient bound constant; the dimension term in `Htilde` still gives `tau=O(log(n+2))`. No stronger single-exponential positive-residual lower bound is incorrectly asserted.

**Rational certificates.** The derivative row-sum bound `4UD` is safe, including zero conductance-degree at isolated buses. **Reactive stability.** Pairwise summation gives the signed energy `-theta^T q`. The short real differences ensure `t*sin(t)>0` away from zero and the quantitative bound `t*sin(t)>=(2/pi)t^2`. Substitution of `ell=1/2`, `U=4`, `g_min>=1`, and `||q||^2<=n*eta^2` gives `256*n*(n-1)^2*eta^2`. The componentwise and singleton cases are handled correctly. Symmetric nonzero reactive tolerances are not promoted to exact hardness.
