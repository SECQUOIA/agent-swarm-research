## Changes

In `sections/03-ac.tex`, the real-lift lemma now gives an equivalent system with real vertex variables: one zero root per component and `a_i-a_j=k_ij` on every oriented edge. Forest propagation makes all vertex variables integers automatically and determines them uniquely. A non-tree edge is consistent precisely when its fundamental-cycle crossing sum vanishes. The explicit angles `theta_i=alpha_i+2*pi*a_i` verify the sign and interpretation of these shifts. Isolated vertices and the empty graph are addressed.

The membership proof uses these vertex equations instead of explicitly listing fundamental cycles. It establishes linear counts of real variables, atomic predicates and monomial occurrences, with maximum degree two. Total binary encoding length remains polynomial, including graph indices, rational data and denominator clearing. This is an elementary graph-potential representation, not a new winding theorem; the body credits Farivar–Low and Jafarpour et al. near the winding discussion. The accepted introduction and bibliography were preserved.

`checks/check_ac_exact.py` now compares exact rational propagation against an independent enumeration of every simple cycle. All 4,166 labeled simple-graph and three-valued crossing-count cases through four vertices agree; 1,090 are consistent and have integral shifts. It also verifies invariance under orientation reversal, empty and disconnected graphs, isolated vertices, an inconsistent cycle, rejection of the actual winding four-cycle, and nonzero integral shifts across the branch cut for a zero-winding phasor cycle. These test the equivalence and its risky sign/boundary cases, not just the code's internal operations. `appendices/verification.tex` records this additional coverage.
