The common top face makes it connected, and finite voltage and epigraph bounds make it compact. The mesh exponent and clamped coordinate representations have polynomial bit length in the rational input and tolerance encoding. Exact rational evaluation supplies a uniform polynomial verifier. - **Reactive stability (`prop:reactive-stability`).** The energy identity, short-angle sine bound, and centered Laplacian inequality give both the angle and active-discrepancy constants.
