# Computational evidence audit and companion design

Audit date: 2026-10-06. Scope: the authorized intersection-cut streams and their retained records. No optimization experiment, solver call, benchmark, literature search, project-wide check, or CI inspection was performed for this audit. Reading, parsing, counting, checking transcription, and computing file metadata are distinct from rerunning the experiments. Source HEAD at the start of this audit was `5c13cc58f7f4cebbe54e496c7b04cc6c30901261`; hashes in `companion-inventory.json` identify the actual files audited and take precedence over that commit identifier.

The existing records support a paper about geometric limits, exact certificates, and the limits of a local selection criterion. They do not establish a generally useful replacement for SCIP's rule or a stock-SCIP timing benefit. Existing research-agent reviews are useful checks, but are not journal peer review. The manuscript must contain the essential mathematical arguments; the companion supplies saved certificates and computational records.

## Evidence classes

| Class | Meaning in this paper | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematical proof | An argument under explicit hypotheses, including any stated external theorem. | Experimental frequency, runtime, or publication novelty. |
| Exact certificate | Saved rational or symbolic data checked by exact arithmetic, under an explicit input data model. | Correctness of an unrelated floating-point cohort or a solver policy. |
| Numerical diagnostic | Saved floating-point steps, corners, re-solves, or solver-reported bounds. | A rational global optimum or an exact geometric classification. |
| Post hoc policy evaluation | Paired trajectories evaluated after exploring rules, with stated test families. | Confirmation on fresh data or causal identification. |
| Withdrawn comparison | An observed historical experiment whose design does not support its original conclusion. | A valid stock-SCIP performance comparison. |

In particular, “exact up to floating point,” a reproducible trajectory, a bitwise rerun, and a solver's `optimal` status must not be called exact certificates. A finite mutation test gives confidence in a verifier's checks; it is not a proof that every corrupt certificate will be rejected.

### Set selection inside SCIP

The set-selection experiments use a patched SCIP 10.0.3 build and heuristic orbit searches, not globally optimal set search. In two λ dimensions the search samples 24 half-circle directions and refines locally; higher dimensions use projected subgradient ascent. Candidate validity and search optimality are separate claims.

Across the three root seeds, point-rule intersection cuts improve the root-gap fraction over the disabled setting by 0.114, 0.104, and 0.106, with added root CPU cost. Relative to the point rule, the proposed corner criterion changes that fraction by −0.008, −0.007, −0.010; efficacy changes it by +0.001, +0.003, −0.004. The full root scan covers 305 instances and 109,389 corners; 81.6% of the floored surrogate minima occur at a zero-rate ray. A positive floored surrogate is not a positive gain in the true corner bound. The acceptance-threshold fallback applies to the fully degenerate 9% class.

The full-solve row capture changes tree search. The 120 stock reruns retain 77 optimal outcomes and 43 limits, but batches were not comparable for CPU timing. The claim that stock intersection cuts improve solve time is withdrawn. The attribution of a patched/stock timing gap to instrumentation distortion is also withdrawn: batch speed explains that gap, while row capture changes some paths. Same-path comparisons do not repair the cross-batch timing design. Preserve instrumented solved counts and paths as historical experiment records; do not present them as stock-SCIP efficacy.

The 300 debug-solution runs are an audit population, not ordinary seed-0 full-solve benchmarks. Five settings times 60 instances use seed 0, 30 CPU seconds, and disabled heuristics and symmetry. There are 170 optimal reports, 129 limits, and one numerical failure; 299 return zero. There are 45 runs with ERROR diagnostics and 215 unknown-variable warnings. None of 299 available final dual bounds or 286 root bounds excludes the reference at the stated tolerance. The 73 intersection-row messages and nine other-row messages all concern `st_glmp_fp2`: correcting the missing nonlinear objective helper to 7.6275 makes all 73 rows satisfied (minimum slack 0.01754381880081013); the nine local secants exclude the supplied point's domain. These specific false alarms are resolved, while other bound/implication/node-cutoff diagnostics remain unresolved. The three downward-discrepant `kall` debug optima were not individually primal-checked; the saved ordinary-full-solve primal check is a different run. A separate `tricp` debug rerun has no row exclusion but two terminal node-cutoff ERROR messages. Do not call the grid a clean validity certificate.

Root cohort accounting is 2,429/1,220/1,220 runs for seeds 0/1/2, on 347/305/305 instances; complete across settings 305/305/304, referenced complete 302/302/301, and common defined root-gap closure 251/250/248. The phrase “335 with a root run” is a reference-eligible union. The 305 full-scan summaries retain 89,307 floored-minimum zero-ray corners and 10,040 all-zero-cost corners; raw corner dumps and per-instance exit codes were not retained. Selection's floor is `max(w_j,1e-6 max(w_max,1e-9))`, distinct from the fidelity and multiround thresholds. ### Multiround paired experiment and policy exploration

The main paired sample contains 220 constructed instances: 60 each at sizes 4×4 and 6×8, 50 each at 8×12 and 10×20. These are external root cutting loops using cached instances and full-bilinear reference values, not an integration into SCIP's search. The `logs/main/` and `logs/new/` cohorts together retain 74 JSONL files and 5,160 trajectories; the audit found 220 baseline instance IDs, no duplicate/conflicting trajectories, no non-ok statuses in those cohorts, and exact matches to cached denominators.

The corrected orbit-minus-SCIP pooled fraction of root gap closed is −0.001 with interval [−0.016,+0.014] after round 1 and −0.012 [−0.017,−0.006] after rounds 10 and 20. After round 10 the size-specific losses are −0.011 on 6×8 and −0.025 on 10×20. The corrected-minus-bug-affected orbit rule changes round 1 by +0.011 [+0.004,+0.019], but round 10 by −0.001 [−0.003,+0.002]. The claim that the bug explains about 40% of the later loss is withdrawn.

One orbit root round followed by SCIP-model rounds (`o1s`) already has pooled round-10 deficit −0.006 [−0.011,−0.002], about half the main deficit. This is a switching experiment in the external loop. No actual best-orbit SCIP pass was located in the authorized records: selection explicitly did not implement its full-orbit LMI candidate in SCIP. The CLOSEOUT `o1s` comparison must not be relabelled as an actual SCIP integration.

The post hoc efficacy rule has pooled AUC advantage +0.004 over rounds 1–10, with Holm-adjusted p=0.025 in the stated 14-test family; its round-10 Holm p is 0.33. Continued-orbit recovery gives pooled `o3s` raw paired-t p=0.00369 and 12-test Holm p=0.0443; adjustment over 36 tests is 0.133, or 0.122 over 33 distinct tests after removing three duplicates. A 12-test Wilcoxon adjustment gives 0.056, while the 10×20 `o3s` adjustment is 0.035. These choices materially change the threshold conclusion. Give the family and test beside the claim, and retain the full family in the companion. This is exploratory evidence of some recovery, not an established general repair or a gain over the baseline.

Ties and support explain some local degeneracy but do not establish the observed trajectory mechanism. Nonattaining rules also lose. Shorter steps are a plausible common factor, while the `maj2` versus `rnd0.33` intervention is inconclusive. Their orbit shares differ (0.219/0.273/0.287/0.321 versus 0.337/0.333/0.335/0.329); selection and exposure are confounded. The contrast is positive in two sizes, approximately zero in one and negative in one, rather than positive in three as one note sentence says. Within-rule correlations do not identify causality. Tie proxies exclude normalized costs below 1e-4 and use relative tolerance 1e-3; they are not exact dual-degeneracy certificates. Multiround floors weights at `max(w_j,1e-9 max(1,max w))`. A surviving correction paragraph in multiround §6 says strict improvement is true “per cut and per round from the same state”; this must not imply full-LP dominance. The same note reports orbit re-solves worse at a 10×20 root (0.677 versus 0.689) and later orbit states (0.241 versus 0.250).

The uniform-depth convergence theorem is mathematical evidence, not demonstrated by these trajectories. Their observed pointedness falls to about 0.001; no uniform-depth hypothesis is verified. The adversarial maximal-cut counterexample is not a failure theorem for SCIP's natural rule.

## Portable companion design

The companion should have a small default download and optional bulky diagnostics. Preserve repository-relative paths, or rewrite imports once and record the exact hashes of both original and submitted files. A manifest is an inventory, not a claim that every inventoried file is packaged. Packaging was initially outside this audit and was subsequently authorized. The prepared companion preserves original bytes under a neutral wrapper; its own manifest records the selected population.

1. **Exact certificates:** include saved rational certificate data, the current standalone verifiers, certificate input models, mathematical derivations, and already-retained successful verification receipts. Include all direct local imports. The ratio verifier imports `certify_zB.py`; several higher-level scripts import sfree `core.py`, `bilinear.py`, and `scout_sfree.py` using relative repository paths. The closure saved-cut `--verify` path uses standard-library rational arithmetic and must remain distinct from its optimization-based generation path. It can be made portable without shipping a solver installation.
2. **Compact numerical records:** include corrected one-cut logs with ten-case mapping, sampled fidelity analyses, attempt indexes needed for denominators, root/solve summaries with their cohort lists, all main/new paired trajectories and cached denominators, minor summary JSONL, and the policy analysis scripts/test-family definitions. Include compact records for failures and limits. A read-only table-transcription script may derive manuscript rows; it must not import an experiment entry point or launch a solver.
3. **Historical diagnostics:** retain or index the old incorrect corner summaries, withdrawn timing data, debug run summaries, incomplete checkpoints, failed box bounds, and old minor solver output. Mark them clearly as superseded, withdrawn, diagnostic, or incomplete. They should not populate the main result tables.
4. **Optional original traces:** existing `artifacts/manifest.json` already records the48 raw SCIP gzip traces in `scip-fidelity-traces`, archive size2,193,209,777 bytes and SHA-256 `81a2a2a07c08b0137364f40ebbd5d438f4de2e62026af4d044e6f49cb9a816a9`. The archive splits into ordered1992294400-byte and200915377-byte parts. Original gzip members total2,199,648,289 bytes. Compact transcription does not require restoration. Raw piece diagnostics do. Avoid bundling them by default.

The starting recorded environment is Python3.13, NumPy2.5.1, SciPy1.18.0, CVXPY1.9.3, Clarabel0.11.1, SCS3.3.1, SymPy1.14.0, HiGHS/highspy, PySCIPOpt6.2.1/SCIP10.0.3. Versions reported in notes describe the original work, not a newly reconstructed environment. Fidelity solver bounds additionally depend on Gurobi and its license. SCIP source/build flags, Ipopt availability, seeds, gap denominators, tolerances, and implementation variant belong with each numerical cohort. An exact verifier should list only dependencies it actually imports, not the full experiment stack.

No root repository LICENSE was found during the scoped inventory. Local experiment scripts consequently need an explicit redistribution license from the owner before public release. SCIP10.0.3 C headers declare Apache-2.0; local C source snapshots are outside the repository, at the source-tree paths recorded by the manifests. If shipping those sources, retain notices and the license. MINLPLib downloads being publicly accessible/open data does not, by itself, document a redistribution license. Source manifests do not establish redistribution permission for retained literature PDFs or extracted text. Prefer bibliographic references and upstream retrieval instructions unless a license is established. Gurobi binaries/licenses and local virtual environments must not be shipped.

For anonymous submission, existing archive READMEs/manifests expose an author-named repository URL and absolute username paths. Submit neutral metadata with relative paths and anonymous retrieval instructions. Keep original identities/provenance in an internal mapping; do not change the evidence files silently.

## Recommended manuscript tables and interpretation

Use four compact tables and an appendix inventory:

- **Certificate examples:** exact input model, proved/certified orbit or closure bracket, and explicitly numerical SCIP value. Include bilinear thm14/prop16, the corrected near-boundary bracket, and minor A/B/S1. Keep one-sided rounded cases in the appendix.
- **Fidelity and rejection:**610,193 comparisons/1,211 classified discrepancies, sampled degeneracy3,347/4,578, full attempts76,914 and generated29,022, plus first-piece-only scaling passes. Put a denominator and evidence class in each row.
- **Local and later-round behavior:** corrected120 one-cut corners and220 paired trajectories in separate panels. Give corrected means and the pooled round1/10 interval. Their denominators and implementations differ.
- **Inside-SCIP selection:** three-seed root-gap deltas; a text row stating full-solve timing withdrawal; optionally debug counts with all unresolved categories. Do not give a performance conclusion from withdrawn timing or debug runs.

The empirical conclusion should be that maximizing the local corner criterion can improve a one-cut bound and still lose after reoptimization; numerical degeneracy and implementation rejection frequently limit its practical discrimination. The retained records support neither causal attribution of the whole multiround loss nor a general recommendation to enable quadratic cuts or replace SCIP's rule.
