Affine restrictions, output subtraction, and quotient maps retain exact graph coverage at each lower-bound interface.

These yield the stated zero pattern and precision exponents. The fourth-moment covariance expansion has the claimed nonnegative discarded terms; the determinant estimate and volume normalization give the stated finite constant. The shared residual products meet every nonzero coefficient's exponent condition. The cross-product example distinguishes maximum scalar rank from joint nc-rank for the claimed reason.
- The smooth lower bound genuinely controls contacts of arbitrary aspect ratio: the blow-up witness gives an invertible mixed Hessian, the localized oscillatory operator has the required norm bound, and applying it to the product indicator cancels the blow-up dimension in the final exponent. The constant-rank upper proof constructs affine tubes in original coordinates and controls the whole tube by a transverse Taylor estimate. It does not silently treat nonlinear coordinate substitution as an admissible lift operation. The perspective proof uses binary products and positive homogenization with the original rows; its lower slice transfers are valid separately for both models.
- In the finite algorithm, the exact penalty rescales to a feasible covariance, and its normalized branch gradients have trace one. The convergence estimate telescopes on the stored rational iterates and accounts locally for projection, branch error, and rounding. The block and scalar allocation oracle proofs include a known interior ball and an exact convex-combination repair.
