## Additional online screening

Searched exact combinations of “noncommutative rank”, “quadratic”, “approximation”, and “integer dimension”, along with exact titles for the analytic and rational spectral sources. Absence of a matching search result was not treated as proof of novelty.

- [Choi et al., *Convexification of mixed-integer quadratic optimization via decision diagrams*, arXiv:2608.22815v1, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22815v1): abstract describes optimization with indicator variables, ideal conic convex-hull formulations, and approximate diagrams for structured quadratics. This is adjacent to formulation design but does not state the graph-containing minimum-integer-dimension law under study. Screened at abstract/model level only; no new citation is needed to claim a stronger comparison to its unreviewed full results.
- [Hirai et al., *Algebraic combinatorial optimization on the degree of determinants of noncommutative symbolic matrices*, Mathematical Programming 213 (2025), 941–984](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10107-024-02158-0): publisher abstract and introduction describe weighted noncommutative determinant/minor degrees and combinatorial optimization applications. This provides no basis for claiming novelty of algebraic rank algorithms; the manuscript continues to credit its precise imported rank algorithm and claims the geometric/formulation connection.

These searches support maintaining the narrow, qualified priority statement established in stage 1. They do not establish absence over all published and unpublished literature.
