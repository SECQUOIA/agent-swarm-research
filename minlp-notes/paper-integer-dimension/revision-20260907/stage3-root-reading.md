chord/packing proof, truncated mass and potential estimates, signed-polynomial
analytic panel and quantile arguments, +7/+11 accounting, separable packing,
positive allocation and sparse powers, inverse interpolation, and both encoding
boundaries. Author changes and frozen review findings are assessed separately.

The substantive checks included the rounding-down comparison in the greedy grid
proof (use the last original knot having the chosen rounded value); the bound on
failed analytic panels per depth and root separation termination; graph coverage
by a continuous input path even with unordered computed knots; the distinction
between numerical degree and exponent bit length; and the fixed-witness LP
argument whose determinant depends on continuous columns while witness size
changes only integer right-hand sides. The conic homogeneous value gadget also
handles zero interpolation weights using primal and dual feasibility.

No unsupported proof step was identified in this reading. This is a critical
reading, not a formal verification or a substitute for the five independent
reviews. Literature positioning and published-version locators are being audited
by the author and will be checked in the independent stage review.
