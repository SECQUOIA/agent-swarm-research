## Online search scope and limits

Targeted searches included `"integer dimension" "approximation" "noncommutative"`, `"mixed-integer convex representability" "approximation" graph`, `"noncommutative rank" "quadratic" "approximation" integer`, and exact titles for compact quadratic formulations, minimum-breakpoint approximation, LinA, and constructive nc-rank. Exact-title publisher/arXiv pages supplied metadata, not indirect aggregator summaries. We did not treat missing search matches as proof of novelty.

A current adjacent hit, [Peiris, Sukhorukova, Pham, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/imamat/hxag015), was screened through the publisher's abstract and introduction (published June 11; corrected July 2). Its stated topic is one-free-knot Chebyshev linear-spline fitting and optimality conditions. This is not used as evidence for a claim about arbitrary-lift minimum integer dimension, and no assertion about its full theorems is made.

The only explicit qualified priority sentence in the revised introduction concerns identification of nc-rank/2 as the minimum integer-dimension precision coefficient for fixed quadratic systems, accompanied by the exact model, cited antecedents, and the manuscript theorem. Other contributions are phrased as the particular complete comparisons proved here; primitive methods are credited. This focused search cannot certify absence across all literature. Later stages must check technical imports and section-specific novelty at the level needed by their proofs.
