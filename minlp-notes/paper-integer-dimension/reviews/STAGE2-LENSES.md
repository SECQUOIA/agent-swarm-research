# Stage 2 reviewer lenses

All fifteen reviewers read the entire stage. These lenses guide additional depth, not exclusive assignments.

1. Adversarial end-to-end validity; counterexamples, hidden assumptions, and exact scope.
2. Covariance lower bounds, parity supports, topology, volume constants, arbitrary general integers.
3. Shared residual grids, exact graph containment, endpoints, rational coordinate identities, output and row counts.
4. Rational nc-rank construction, rational basis heights, integral capacity constants, uniform input-encoding overhead.
5. Geodesic exact penalty, gradient and curvature formulas, projected inexact recurrence, feasibility repair.
6. Rational Jacobi contraction, common denominators, matrix-function conditioning, bit complexity and iteration counts.
7. PSD grouped budgets, l1 SDP surrogate, normalized gradient, weak-optimization interface and exact repair.
8. General symmetric error bodies, effective output image, classical rounding and oracle/radius conventions.
9. Common nonlinear input rank, affine fibers, zonotope oracles, rational LDL normalization and volume losses.
10. PSD block and diagonal structure, unconditional budgets, direct logdet oracle, commuting matrices and certificates.
11. Forest and integer-feature domain geometry, exact volume/minor bounds, thin-domain and benchmark counterexamples.
12. Hardness reductions and positive-optimum amplification; construction-time versus solution-time distinctions.
13. Full repository coverage, omitted distinct developments and audits, cross-stage consistency and scope.
14. Primary sources, publication metadata, imported theorem hypotheses, careful credit and bounded novelty claims.
15. Reader comprehension and coherent standalone exposition, with independent reconstruction of the mathematical chain.
