# Stage 4 reviewer lenses

Every reviewer reads the entire stage, including synthesis. The lens adds depth; it does not narrow scope.

01. Finite continuous-convex geometry: arbitrary-factor level cuts, parity hulls, interval covers, output/facet overlays and all real-data degeneracies.
02. Implicit common partitions: ordered uncertain mass bisection, common denominator, multiset selection, duplicate endpoints, containing source cells and compilation counts.
03. Box and facet curvature rank: quotient by affine functions, original-row spanners, signed coefficients, rank-zero case, coupled bands and every constant.
04. Unconditional maximum-product allocation: supporting inequality, approximate-product factor seven, oracle specialization, rational inverse scales and direct count.
05. Generic rational spanner: seed conditioning, objective bounds, weak optimization convention, exact feasibility repair, fixed global denominators, polynomial exchange complexity.
06. Positive-polar oracle and effective image: inner/outer balls, weak separation, feasible support, rank and seeds, pullback separators and absence of a strong polar assumption.
07. Oracle graph formulation: two spanners, positive scalarizations, primal parallelotope, rounding effective coordinates, affine restoration and full error/count calculations.
08. Separable vector theory: one concatenated basis, Jensen product packing, all six finite/compiled comparisons, inactive coordinates and shared output selection.
09. Positive-power obstruction: all scalarizations versus joint approximation, midpoint and thirds estimates, residue counts, refinement failure and three-witness repeated convexification.
10. Cap-set restriction and exact convex box separation: imported cap theorem, generating-function constant, modular product lower bounds, entire integer sections and rational upper formulations.
11. Nonconvex scalar family and arbitrary polynomial overlay: Bernstein error, degree/encoding, two-integer representation, all binary lower bounds, curvature root brackets and sign-directed bands.
12. Tilted error bodies and finite conditioning: affine shears, common quadratic, radius growth, Euclidean simplex bound, nonsymmetric extension and unconditional normalization.
13. Lattice-free open boundaries and completeness: strict violation sets, unions, Helly versus covering, integral Radon limitations and all distinct precursor/support arguments.
14. Primary-source credit and publication positioning: classical spanners, weak/anti-blocker oracle equivalence, shared SOS2, proportional fairness, cap sets, Bernstein/DC, bibliography and qualified novelty.
15. Full-paper synthesis and standalone exposition: abstract/introduction/conclusion match proved scope; definitions, result roadmap, notation, dependencies and meaningful reader explanations.
