**Primary lens: implicit common partitions.**

- `lem:implicit-overlay`, lines 83–107: the right-endpoint multiset, including its multiplicities and one prepended zero, gives exactly `sum K_j` cells. Nested source-index and numerator searches compute the least numerator whose cumulative count reaches the requested rank. The known common denominator is essential and is supplied by the actual construction. All search depths and arithmetic lengths are polynomial in the encoded counts and denominator, rather than in the number of cells. For a positive-length merged cell, the last source knot at most its left endpoint has a successor covering its right endpoint. At repeated endpoints any containing source cell works, including the final source cell at one.
- Lines 109–123 and the accepted mass compiler: the deterministic mass approximation and uncertainty width are fixed at each bisection node independently of the target. The left, stopping and right target ranges are ordered, and their returned input ranges are ordered. Induction on the fixed depth therefore proves monotonicity without requiring the approximate oracle values at different nodes to be monotone. Exact forced endpoints, padded dyadic outputs, reflection with index reversal, and concatenation of consecutive pieces preserve order. Products of the polynomially many rational piece-endpoint denominators, together with one largest local dyadic denominator, provide a common grid of polynomial bit length.

Downward endpoint evaluation, one shared input cell and one shared interpolation weight give the displayed asymmetric bands and error at most `15/16` of tolerance. Zero-length cells retain this property. The aggregate count is below `2048m 2^p`; invalid codes are excluded, and internal flags, output bits and products need no additional integrality declarations. The algorithm uses its computed counts, not the unknown comparator lift.

Source-cell metadata selects downward rounding for convex/bracket cells and upward rounding for concave cells. I checked the three center-error intervals and both band directions.
