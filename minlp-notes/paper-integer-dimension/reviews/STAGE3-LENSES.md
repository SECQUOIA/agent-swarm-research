# Stage 3 reviewer lenses

Every reviewer reads the entire stage. These lenses assign additional depth, not exclusive scope.

01. Scalar continuous-convex geometry: parity spans, chord refinement, finite maximal packings, interval trimming, and finite real versus constructive scope.
02. Truncated curvature mass: Taylor and potential inequalities, constants, endpoint cases, raw curvature and allocation-gap counterexamples.
03. Positive-coefficient curvature integration: branch isolation, analytic sectors, Gaussian positivity/exactness, rational node/weight/function evaluation and inverse moduli.
04. Signed monotone-curvature integration: Taylor certificate, complex-root proximity, discriminant separation, polynomial adaptive tree size and quantile conditioning.
05. Compiled mass quantiles and dense convex-polynomial hybrid: uncertain comparisons, reversed knots, exact greedy predicates, interval perturbation, branch threshold and global count.
06. Indexed computation-to-MILP compiler: only external bits integral, uniform circuit generation, fixed denominators, signed outputs, invalid indices and exact interpolation.
07. Separable scalar sums and independent outputs: Jensen superadditivity for continuous convex functions, product packing/code, finite and compiled dimension constants.
08. Positive-polynomial allocation geometry and oracle: nonnegative supporting multipliers, normal cones/caps, transformed covariance, unconditional domination and rational repair.
09. Dense dyadic layers and sparse positive mixtures: exact prefix powers, uniform curvature, rounded exponentiation, shared output errors and sparse encoding.
10. Integer and rational inverse-power algorithms: uncertainty termination, rational log/exp series, scaled Jensen/chord estimates for1<alpha<2 and all endpoint/size cases.
11. Stieltjes approximation and rational endpoint gadgets: truncation, uniform complex panel bounds, positive rationalization, exact normalization, signed P/Q denominator bounds and no-new-bit recurrences.
12. Relative-error boundaries: arbitrary modulus residue classes, convex/concave threshold, zero endpoint, truncated-domain lower/upper order and one-sided scope.
13. Rational MILP versus MISOCP encoding: unbounded integer witnesses, LP basis denominators, four-bit upper, primal-dual value gadget including theta=0, explicit squaring-chain dual.
14. Coverage and primary-source attribution: all canonical/supporting developments, imported theorem hypotheses, bibliography metadata, accurate predecessor credit and bounded priority claims.
15. Standalone exposition and full proof reconstruction: dependencies, definition consistency, symbol scope, statements versus proofs, and reader understanding across the entire stage.
