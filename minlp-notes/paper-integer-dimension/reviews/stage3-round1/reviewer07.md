**Primary-lens reconstruction.** In `lem:jensen-superadditivity` and `thm:separable-scalar` (approximately lines 557–643), the long-interval midpoint tent dominates each short-interval midpoint tent. Integrating against a nonnegative second-derivative measure proves the inequality for piecewise-linear convex functions; uniform convex piecewise-linear approximation passes it to every continuous convex function. This does not require differentiability or strict convexity. The positive-polynomial feature comparison is consistent with this more general argument: coordinate increments are nonnegative, so squared secants are superadditive along an ordered chain.

The maximal scalar packing argument is finite by uniform continuity. Its compatible neighborhoods are intervals, and splitting them at the selected points gives the claimed chord-cover comparison. For independent outputs, any two distinct product-packing points violate the tolerance in a coordinate where their packing indices differ; affine terms depending on other inputs cancel from the midpoint gap. Thus the product packing applies to the full lifted formulation, not just to a chosen separable formulation.

For scalar sums, at local tolerance `eta/r`, an index difference of `h` contributes a midpoint gap strictly greater than `h eta/r`. The greedy code deletes at most `6^r` lattice points per selected word: the generating-function estimate bounds the integer l1 ball of radius `r` by `2^r (sum_z 2^{-|z|})^r = 6^r`. The resulting selected words have l1 distance greater than `r`, so their graph points are pairwise incompatible. This supplies the necessary whole-formulation lower bound; merely multiplying independent scalar lower bounds would not have done so.

I checked the actual numbers of binary codes, including ceilings and affine coordinates. The local code counts are bounded by `12 P_i`, `481 P_i` and `5832 P_i` in the continuous finite, positive compiled and general dense convex compiled cases. The inequalities `12<2^4`, `481<2^9`, `5832<2^13`, and their products with six being less than `2^7`, `2^12`, `2^16`, respectively, give exactly the stated independent-output and scalar-sum constants. Local output errors sum to at most the global scalar tolerance. Coordinates occurring only affinely require no bits; the zero-active-coordinate case is exact.
