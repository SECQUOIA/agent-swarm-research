2. **Quadratic asymptotics and joint rank.** I checked the maximum-simplex argument behind the indefinite contact-volume bound, the principal rank restriction and eigenspace upper construction, the one-sided inertia argument, and the distinction between an upper epigraph construction and a claim of exact optimal bit count.
