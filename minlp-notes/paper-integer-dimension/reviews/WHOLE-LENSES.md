# Whole-paper reviewer lenses

Each reviewer reads and reconstructs the whole manuscript; these lenses add depth.

01. Foundational model and lower bounds: graph sandwich, arbitrary convex lifts, parity/modulus contacts, nonclosed and nonmeasurable sets, covariance/volume, zero-bit and affine cases.
02. Quadratic asymptotics: scalar rank, bilinear graph fractional covers, one-sided inertia, noncommutative rank, symmetric shrunk subspaces and joint versus scalar ranks.
03. Algebraic imports and rational nc-rank construction: capacity/rank hypotheses, rational field issues, determinant denominators, optimization accuracy and polynomial bits.
04. Finite quadratic covariance: both comparison directions, feasibility certificates, geodesic optimization, rational approximation and count/size/time distinctions.
05. General error geometry and structured quadratics: oracle radii, effective input/output images, ellipsoids, PSD blocks, coordinate allocation, forests/features and correlated domains.
06. Smooth, perspective and hardness results: local/global rank, constant-rank affine fibers, oscillatory estimates, domain hypotheses, reductions, asymptotic and computational quantifiers.
07. Scalar convex shape: chord refinements, truncated curvature mass, raw-curvature obstructions, positive/signed certified integration and the hybrid greedy construction.
08. Rational compilation: exact gate behavior with continuous internal wires, indexed endpoints, shared interpolation, common denominators, signed P/Q gadgets, invalid codes and all encoding claims.
09. Positive separable polynomials and powers: support allocation, shape lower bounds, layers, sparse huge exponents, inverse-power arithmetic, Stieltjes approximation and dimension constants.
10. Relative error and conic/linear separation: residue thresholds, endpoint behavior, LP basis denominator lower bounds, conic-value zero-weight case, primal/dual certificates and short/long encoding quantifiers.
11. Vector rank and oracle proofs: original-output bases, max product, fixed-grid spanners, positive-polar weak access, effective coordinate rounding and complete bands/counts.
12. Separable vector theory and implicit overlays: one shared basis, product packing, local capacities, signed curvature pieces, sorted random access and rational construction.
13. Separation examples and unresolved boundaries: cap sets, repeated convexification, exact product counts, Bernstein nonconvex graphs, tilted conditioning, Helly/Radon limits and scope of open questions.
14. Full coverage and primary-source positioning: all 43 canonical results, every distinct supporting development, imported theorem assumptions, original-source metadata and honest contribution claims.
15. Standalone exposition and publication assessment: abstract/title/introduction/conclusion consistency, result map, symbol/definition consistency, proof readability, redundant material and material reader obstacles.
