## Finding

1. **MINOR — define the ellipsoid matrix convention.** Location: `sections/02-quadratic-finite.tex`, lines 817–824, first use of `E(A)` in the rounding import; also lines 940–948 in the input-quotient proof. The manuscript defines `E_0` by a quadratic inequality but does not explicitly define `E(A)`. The later choices `W=d(d+1)^2 A` and `R=diag(b_i)L^T` rely on the convention `E(A)={z:z^T A z<=1}`, rather than the also common covariance convention involving `A^{-1}`. The cited primary paper uses the former convention: [Dadush–Peikert–Vempala, Section 2.3 and Theorem B.5](https://sites.cc.gatech.edu/fac/cpeikert/pubs/svp-anynorm.pdf). Add this definition at the first occurrence. With that convention, both the scaling and the LDL normalization are correct. This is an expository omission, not a defect in the rounding or quotient theorem.
