# Stage 1 reviewer lenses

All fifteen reviewers read the entire stage. These primary lenses guide extra scrutiny, not exclusive assignments.

1. Adversarial end-to-end mathematical validity and counterexamples.
2. Convex lifts, parity contacts, arbitrary integer ranges, topology and measurability.
3. Exact square/product constants, strong convexity, finite disjunction formulations.
4. Bilinear graphs, covariance-volume reasoning, fractional-cover LPs and rational precision.
5. Quadratic-system lower bounds: covariance, capacity/Hall argument and principal restrictions.
6. Quadratic-system upper bounds: shrinking, real/complex descent, coordinate exponents and compactness.
7. Smooth maps, matrix evaluations, oscillatory estimates and local/global rank distinctions.
8. Constant scalar Hessian rank, Legendre charts, polyhedral tubes and finite covering.
9. One-sided inertia, epigraph row complexity and positive perspectives.
10. Actual formulation implementation: graph containment, error budgets, endpoints, size and coefficient encoding.
11. Quantifiers, degeneracies, domains, constants and plausible boundary counterexamples.
12. Primary-source attribution, bibliography, novelty wording and imported theorem hypotheses.
13. Repository coverage, dependency completeness, corrections and audit reconciliation.
14. Reader comprehension, mathematical exposition, terminology and organization, with mathematical checks.
15. Independent synthesis audit: reconstruct the main proof chain, check theorem consistency and hidden assumptions.
