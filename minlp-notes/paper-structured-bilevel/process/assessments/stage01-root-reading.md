# Root reading during stage 1

This is a working record, not acceptance of the stage before its five reviews.

The root read the source scalar and block exact proofs, moving-normal and
pessimistic extensions, the polynomial inverse construction, the principal
approximation interfaces, scalar algorithms, and structural scope maps. The
draft foundations were checked for consistency with those result families.

## Coverage decisions

- Include Klee--Minty diagonal follower-path results. They are distinct from
  growing-leader path hardness, and require the telescoping/slab construction.
- Keep the general fixed-core theorem as methodological/supporting context only
  to the extent needed by the bilevel proofs. Pooling application classifications
  are outside this paper.
- Include the general signed monotone-polynomial inverse construction with its
  complex critical values, precision, and rational Taylor encoding arguments.
  An appeal to numerical inverse evaluation would not prove the accuracy-bit
  global optimization theorem.
- Distinguish scalar nonconvex population scaling with two repeated types from
  heterogeneous response-event scaling.
- Stage 6 should investigate an independent whole-leader optimization baseline;
  the existing original-coordinate face oracle only evaluates fixed tariffs.

## Rejected root concern

The root initially suspected the first comparison-table row in
`notes/bilevel-classical-positioning.md` misattributed the fixed-follower-variable
positive result to Ketkov--Prokopyev Theorem 1. Direct inspection of version 2,
Section 2.2, Theorem 1 disproved that suspicion: the theorem explicitly permits
either a fixed number of follower constraints or a fixed number of follower
variables. Corollary 1 instead concerns pessimistic optimization with a fixed
number of upper coupling constraints. The source note requires no correction
on this point. The root informed the author and corrected its user-facing update.

Primary URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2511.15592v2

## First-pass foundation checks

The fixed-normal model provides uniform follower bounds through continuous
polynomial coordinate bounds on compact leader space. It permits empty fibers.
The optimistic and universal-feasibility pessimistic definitions keep upper
constraints out of the follower problem. The substitution lemma correctly clears
positive denominators and exploits fixed compressed dimension to control expanded
polynomial size. Actual degree and input bit length are kept distinct. These
checks do not replace the forthcoming independent reviews or later proof stages.
