## The three new claims

**Support-tuple reconstruction is valid** (`appendices/a-fixed-core.tex:180–247`). Every retained tuple is locally feasible at the sampled core, so its image lies inside the Minkowski sum. Conversely, for every real direction, the actual sign condition at that core and direction occurs in the globally enumerated list. Its selected tuple attains the full support value and survives the feasibility filter. Equality of support functions therefore proves equality of the convex hull and the entire aggregate image. It is safe to retain additional tuples whose original sign conditions are not realizable at the sampled core: feasibility prevents enlarging the image beyond the true Minkowski sum.

The common-field step also holds. Carathéodory reduction supplies a representation with affinely independent lifted columns, hence at most `h+1=k+2` tuples. Enumeration is polynomial because `h` is fixed. On a successful independent subset, the unique weights solve a constant-order linear system whose coefficients and target are in the sampled field. Thus the weights remain in that field. The same weights across blocks preserve every aggregate measurement, including the objective coordinate. Neither growing follower dimension nor independently chosen block fields enter this argument. Lower-dimensional images, singletons, repeated vertices, and the no-block case are covered.

**The fixed-core numerical-degree extension is justified** (`appendices/a-fixed-core.tex:26–38`, `43–101`, `158–178`, `260–266`). Local vertex determinants and score comparisons have degree `O(d delta)` and polynomial explicit size for fixed local dimension. The common support denominator introduces degree `O((B+1)d delta)`, not exponential degree. Its expansion has only polynomially many monomials because the number of variables stays fixed. Coefficient-bit growth is polynomial under the product bound already proved in the main section. The subsequent sign enumeration, quantifier elimination, sampling, and fixed-order reconstruction consequently remain polynomial in `(L, delta)`. Compact fixed boxes and compact `C` make the original feasible set closed and bounded even with moving local normals; this supports attainment without requiring continuity of the individual fiber correspondence.

**The constant-local-Hessian degree bound is justified** (`sections/02-exact-responses.tex:474–510`). With fixed normals and constant rational `Q_b`, all local KKT inverses have rational constant entries. Local response formulas are polynomials of bounded degree in the fixed compressed coordinates. The number of blocks and rows can increase polynomial counts and coefficient sizes, but not the degree of these responses or their sums. Substitution into upper polynomials gives degree `O(delta^2)` independent of follower count. Basu–Pollack–Roy's Theorem 1.3.1 explicitly separates output polynomial degree from the number of input polynomials; Section 3.1.3 supplies the corresponding fixed-dimensional univariate sampling bound. A fixed number of elimination and sampling stages preserves a degree bound depending only on the fixed dimensions and fixed `delta`. Decoding stays in that same field. The corollary correctly requires fixed normals; it does not silently apply to the moving-normal extension.

## Remaining stage claims

- **Local elimination and global candidate construction** (`sections/02-exact-responses.tex:40–234`): polyhedral normal-cone necessity applies without a constraint qualification. Conic support reduction modulo equality normals gives at most `d_b` independent rows. The saddle matrix is invertible under the stated positive-definiteness and independence hypotheses. Squared denominators preserve signs. Valid local formulas agree by strict convexity. Joint sign conditions avoid an exponential Cartesian product of block regimes. The common-denominator follower-value equation includes the correct quadratic, linear, and aggregate terms. Explicit upper substitution and coefficient growth remain polynomial.
- **Globality, optimization, and attainment** (`sections/02-exact-responses.tex:236–309`): every global follower minimizer appears among the feasible KKT candidates, so comparison against all candidate values is sufficient and necessary for globality. The formula keeps a constant number of quantified copies, including when upper rows and regimes grow. Joint sampling returns a leader, value, compressed response, and all decoded coordinates in one field. The fixed-normal Hoffman argument correctly approximates each limiting feasible competitor along a sequence of nonempty fibers; it proves the closed response graph needed for optimistic attainment.
- **Moving normals** (`sections/02-exact-responses.tex:315–371`): enumerating all equality/inequality row subsets covers rank changes. Soundness does not require the chosen equality subset to span the full equality row space, because omitted multipliers can be zero. Completeness chooses a full basis at the particular leader. Determinant guards prevent invalid decoding. The example correctly demonstrates nonattainment and why an empty equality basis is necessary at the singular leader.
- **Pessimistic semantics and infima** (`sections/02-exact-responses.tex:373–472`): the existence conjunct excludes empty followers; `Bad` tests every original global follower optimum against upper rows. `Worst` uses the unfiltered follower set, as required. Fixed-leader compactness supplies its maximum. The infimum predicate is correct for nonempty bounded attainable-value sets, and endpoint membership decides attainment. Joint witness sampling has fixed total quantified dimension. The quartic example correctly retains both endpoint optima and excludes the midpoint stationary point.
- **Low-rank rational specialization** (`sections/02-exact-responses.tex:512–557`): SPD of the full supplied Hessian suffices even when the supplied small matrix `M` is indefinite. The clipped affine formulas agree on cell boundaries. For a realizable affine sign cell, replacing strict signs by weak inequalities gives its closure, and continuity makes the same decoding valid there. The explicit aggregate bounds give bounded cell LPs. KKT sufficiency proves exact coverage and rational recovery, including lower-dimensional cells and isolated feasible leaders.
- **Fixed-core membership and limitations** (`appendices/a-fixed-core.tex:41–178`, `249–277`): bounded polyhedra have enough independent active normals at every vertex, even when lower-dimensional. The squared-denominator support comparisons and universal support predicate correctly reject empty blocks. The nearest-point separation proof gives exact membership. Bounded slack blocks preserve the fixed parameters. The appendix clearly restricts convex mixing to affine measurements of polyhedral blocks and does not apply it to nonconvex follower reaction graphs.
