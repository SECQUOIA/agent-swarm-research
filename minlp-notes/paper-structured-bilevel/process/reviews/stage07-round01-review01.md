## Mathematical assessment

* **Exact compression:** the polynomial local-branch list uses an independent equality basis and an independent conic support modulo that basis. Positive block curvature makes each selected KKT matrix invertible and makes every valid branch the same local minimizer. The realizable sign list lies in fixed `(x,w,lambda,mu)` dimension. Therefore it is legitimate to bound the jointly usable regimes polynomially instead of multiplying local list sizes. The growing product denominator increases numerical degree polynomially; explicit upper monomial substitution remains in a fixed number of variables. The contribution paragraph accurately identifies this quantitative step.
* **Globality and selection:** feasible KKT candidates include every global optimum, and comparison against all such candidates is both necessary and sufficient for globality even with nonconvex aggregate cost. The exact response predicate is eliminated before growing upper-row conjunctions. The pessimistic domain excludes any leader having a violating global response; it does not filter responses by upper rows. The abstract and conclusion preserve this distinction and do not promise unconditional attainment.
* **Moving normals:** the determinant-squared guard and enumeration of equality subsets at all possible ranks are sufficient. Testing every original equality gives soundness even when a selected equality subset is not itself a full basis; completeness uses an actual pointwise basis. The zero-rank example `xz=0` correctly explains why fixed-basis and unguarded formulas would fail. The introductory statement that moving normals preserve compression is supported, and optimistic attainment is properly confined to fixed normals.
* **Arithmetic refinement:** constant local matrices remove growing-degree variable denominators. Moving shared and measurement normals enter effective linear costs and consistency equations, not the constant local KKT matrices. The bounded-degree refinement is consequently consistent with its stated assumptions. Separate elimination of each criterion has fixed dependency depth; growing criterion count does not require a compositum of unrelated witness fields. The overview refers specifically to a selected output field, not an atlas-wide or all-witness field.
* **Near-optimal robustness:** a feasible measurement-fiber candidate under the true global nominal budget realizes an admissible response. A fiber minimum supplies such a candidate whenever the measurement is attainable. The converse needs feasibility and the budget, not global fiber optimality. This remains valid for a nonconvex nominal aggregate and nonstationary unrestricted near-optimal points. Eliminating each criterion separately before conjunction justifies the fixed measurement count per criterion. These are the guarantees advertised by the new prose.
* **Integration:** the new screening overview counts recovery LPs after screening and retains the transition-multiplicity condition. Accuracy prose separates true global upper error, exact base-polytope membership, and conditional exact response-row feasibility. The boundary overview retains the nonconvex upper quadratic row in the path construction and distinguishes inverse-error approximation from accuracy-bit optimization. No new overview sentence I checked weakens a material hypothesis of its cited theorem.
