# Final manuscript delivery

The complete 32-page anonymous paper is `../main.pdf`; its standalone LaTeX
source package is `../certified-minlp-paper-source.tar.gz`. The title is
**Checkable Lower Bounds for Convex Mixed-Integer Nonlinear Optimization
through Rational Outer Approximations**.

The paper develops and verifies the precise model/cut/master/proof contract,
provides focused Lean proofs, positions the contribution against primary
literature, and reports complete historical and uniform campaigns, separately
identified representation repairs, exact failed-step and primal/source audits,
and a catalogue of checked bounds for 222 models. The retained software suite
passes 161 tests; the isolated formal build and kernel audit passed. Claims
remain bounded by documented model, software and formal-coverage assumptions.

All six sequential stages are accepted. Each received five independent reviews;
all valid findings were corrected by a separate agent and inspected by the
coordinator. The final whole-manuscript round found no major issue; its two
valid minor findings are resolved. `STATUS.md` and each adjudication contain
acceptance decisions. `final-delivery-checks.json` records final artifact hashes
and independent checks.
