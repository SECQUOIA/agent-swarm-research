## Mathematical correctness and integration

- **Model and domains (`02-model.tex`, `03-soundness.tex`, and implementation Sections 4.1–4.3).** The theorem concerns the exact loaded expression tree, including binary floating-point leaves interpreted as exact rationals. The source-to-loaded-model distinction is explicit. Curvature and expression domains are checked on the declared box before cancellation; propagated bounds need contain mixed-integer feasible points, rather than the entire continuous relaxation. Rejection of unsupported expressions and conservative cut tests is correctly distinguished from mathematical invalidity or infeasibility. The objective sign convention, affine constants, and free epigraph variable are consistent throughout.
- **Propagation and safe cuts (`03-soundness.tex`, first two subsections).** I rederived the coefficient-sign propagation rules, integer endpoint rounding, finite-box residual maxima, and both half-line sign conditions. The interval endpoint choices use the correct monotonicities when the displacement is nonpositive. Fixed and free coordinates are handled separately and correctly. The row residual includes the affine part and all row variables. The support inequality is an explicit premise, not inferred merely from the existence of unrelated coordinate partial derivatives. The rounding example and its squared-polynomial identity are correct. The sufficient support correction is not presented as the best possible intercept.
- **Discrete proof (`03-soundness.tex`, inference invariant and unconditional master bound).** The restricted feasible set induced by a checked incumbent is essential and is used correctly. The feasible incumbent supplies the comparison needed to lift the bound from that set to the full master; no global minimizer is assumed. Strictly earlier references make induction well founded. Sign-compatible linear combinations, integral rounding, and actual complementary integral assumptions are sufficient for their stated conclusions. The unsplit dependency union retains cross-branch and unrelated assumptions. The text does not confuse a valid earlier bound with permission to ignore an invalid suffix, and it does not use the unsupported stronger integer-objective cutoff.
- **Transfer and primal completion (`03-soundness.tex`, last subsection).** The extension explicitly preserves original coordinates and integrality, assigns the epigraph coordinate the true normalized objective, and assigns the constant coordinate one. Original rows, bounds, and safe cuts therefore hold at the extension. Objective equality includes the affine constant exactly once. The empty-set convention is confined to the pointwise/extended-infimum interpretation; a feasible witness and finite lower bound justify the real-infimum and attainment claims. Reference values and master solutions are not mistaken for original nonlinear primal witnesses.
- **Curvature and derivatives (`04-implementation.tex`).** The global quadratic PSD characterization is correctly distinguished from convexity on a lower-dimensional box. The homogenized quadratic PSD test implies convexity of its square root through a real norm representation. The monomial Hessian and one-positive-exponent Schur condition have the stated signs and hypotheses; the even-power exponent is explicitly integral. The linear-fractional second derivative has the correct numerator and denominator sign dependence. Boundary support requires a justified derivative along feasible segments; the discussion does not claim that finite coordinate partials alone suffice.
- **Formal coverage (`05-formalization.tex` and the three Lean modules).** The rational coordinate enclosure theorems genuinely discharge the shift inequalities, and the finite sum derives the safe affine inequality from support and enclosure premises. The epigraph construction and cutoff lifting are actual semantic results. The manuscript accurately states what remains outside the formal development: support generation, interval evaluation, concrete parsing, matching, and the executable discrete proof checker. It does not present the Python implementation or the VIPR inference invariant as mechanically verified. I reread the transfer and real-infimum theorem hypotheses; no desired final bound is hidden as an equivalent nonlinear-bound premise.

## Scientific contribution and evidence

The introduction and conclusion consistently credit outer approximation, rigorous support minimization, geometric mixed-integer convex certificates, and rational MILP proof checking. The integration, precise model identity, selected formal coverage, and independently reproducible reliability audit are the claimed contributions. The Baes point count is correctly limited to supporting points under the cited hypotheses; it is not a bound on serialized certificate size. The literature comparison does not assert unsupported priority or endorse an externally described interval procedure beyond reporting its published method.

The experimental results support the reliability claims without implying a solver-speed comparison. The complete 289-instance denominator is retained; historical acceptance changes, uniform generation, independent replay, and later repairs are separately identified. The accepted 203 primary replay artifacts are compatible with fewer successful producer returns because completed artifacts can survive interrupted processes. The 222-model catalog is explicitly a strongest-bound merge across source-versioned evidence, not the success count of one uniform run. Reference discrepancies, solver-point infeasibility, invalid individual proof steps, and false final bounds remain distinct assertions. The exact reference and saved-solver metrics have different, explicitly stated denominators. The two matching-primal examples are supported by exact witnesses rather than approximate reference agreement.

The abstract, architecture, formalization discussion, experimental interpretation, conclusion, and reproduction appendix use the same trust boundary. Incompleteness of admission, large proof storage, limited formal coverage, and the lack of comparative timing evidence are disclosed scope limitations rather than missing support for a stronger claim. The source, compact evidence, and bulk proof packages have distinct documented purposes; projected replay outcomes under V3 are identified as expectations rather than completed measurements.
