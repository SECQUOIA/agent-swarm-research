## Precise contribution boundary

The new research artifact and empirical evidence are the controlled ESH/ECP policy comparison in this particular NLP-assisted GDP solver, the measured separation-cost/search-work tradeoff, the executable fixed-geometry diagnostics, and a transparent verification/accounting package. Self-contained proofs document the method's contracts and limitations. No first-ever priority claim is needed or supported for the cut family, radial oracle, disjunctive ESH combination, single-tree organization, exact conic hull, elementary compactness proofs, or representation-sensitivity principle. “To the best of our knowledge” would not cure an overly broad claim, so the current draft uses no such priority assertion.
