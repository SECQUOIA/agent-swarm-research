## Actionable minor findings

1. **Restrict monotonicity to the nonnegative argument interval.** Location: `sections/appendix-formulations.tex:52`, “All laws are increasing, convex and differentiable on a neighborhood of the box.” Quadratic `4z^2` and the trigonometric law are increasing on the stated interval `[0,10/11]`, but not on any open neighborhood of it: their derivatives are negative immediately to the left of zero. Write “All laws are increasing on this interval, and convex and continuously differentiable on an open neighborhood of it.” Convexity on a sufficiently small neighborhood is valid for every listed law. This wording error does not invalidate the box maximum, finite epigraph bound, or witness proof, which only need monotonicity on the nonnegative interval.

2. **Specify the two-dimensional transformed rows explicitly.** Location: `sections/oracle-diagnostics.tex:75`, “the base row and six exponential transformations.” For a standalone experimental specification, write that the rows are `q(x)=x^TQx-1` and `exp(a q(x))-1` with `a` in `{1,2,4,8,16,32}`. The scalar examples and subsequent depth values allow the reader to infer this choice, but the two-dimensional design should state it directly. The count of 896 remains correct.

3. **Avoid the reference line crossing wall-time value labels.** Location: `scripts/regenerate.py`, the `cost_tradeoff.pdf` annotation loop; rendered left panel of `figures/cost_tradeoff.pdf`. The dashed vertical line at one crosses the three-digit annotations placed just outside several bars. Give those labels a small opaque white background or move them inside the bars with sufficient contrast, then regenerate. Both figures are otherwise legible, and their captions correctly distinguish the arithmetic cut-time ratio from the shifted geometric wall-time ratio. This is a local visual correction, not a change to the statistics.
