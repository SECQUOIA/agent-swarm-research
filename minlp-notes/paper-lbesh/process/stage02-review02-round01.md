## Independent mathematical assessment

- **Perspective identity and closure:** Verified the positive-weight convexity calculation, continuity at the sole zero-weight point using boundedness of `g`, the zero tangent constant, and the all-tangents identity obtained by including the evaluation point. The empty-term inactive-origin convention is stated accurately and avoids identifying that origin with the closure of an empty positive-weight feasible lift.
- **Separate hull and sparse variables:** Both projection inclusions follow by grouping convex combinations. Full-copy extension of coordinates absent from the entire disjunction is valid because those coordinates face only their box bounds within its terms. Global constraints and original integrality remain outside the individual convexification. The text does not confuse this relaxation with the full GDP hull.
- **Objective epigraph:** The tangent minimum supplies a lower bound, the finite objective maximum supplies an upper bound, and replacing a lift by the exact original objective is valid because the artificial epigraph has no independent role. The prototype's missing epigraph upper bound is expressly kept outside the compactness claim.
- **ESH margin:** Independently obtained `a_z·(p-anchor) >= delta/s`, `v <= LD(1-s)`, and therefore `ell_z(p) >= delta*v/(LD)`. Together with bounded tangent normals these give the integral packing distances. Mixed ECP/ESH choices still permit a common positive lower margin for each fixed row.
- **Fractional packing and omission:** Multiplying the row margin by the selection weight supplies a uniform transformed margin whenever the weighted residual exceeds its tolerance. The transformed normal bound is independent of the weight. The omission test and the weaker fixed-cutoff bound follow directly from `r <= lambda U_r`; the finite positive cutoff gives a packing result without upgrading residual accuracy. No analogous big-M fractional theorem is claimed.
- **Repair and intersection example:** The proposed repair convex combination cancels the nonlinear upper bounds and preserves affine feasibility. Its weighted sum belongs to the separate union hull. The square-root example has exact feasible set `{(0,0)}` and disproves a linear objective-error rate without joint regularity, including when an identical alternative is duplicated.
- **Value convergence:** Continuity of bounded perspectives and compactness make every cluster point feasible. Approximate minimization of valid outer relaxations provides the complementary objective inequality. Closed integrality gives the integral version. The infeasible-target qualification is correct.
- **Single-tree contract:** The finite-cut conclusion uses the explicit old-cut-feasible generating-point and finite-repetition assumptions, plus the bounded fractional/refinement work. Completeness of a final finite master then yields the stated residual outcome. The paper correctly makes the exact objective-gap conclusion conditional on separately valid bounds and an exactly feasible incumbent, and does not assert that the frozen callbacks satisfy every contract.
- **Arithmetic allowance:** The coefficient-error argument correctly loses at most `E` to coefficient evaluation and a second `E` to relaxing the computed cut; subtracting allowed later violation `rho` gives the claimed distance. The approximate-root example correctly distinguishes the full tangent `x <= 5/4` from the invalid offset-dropped inequality.
- **Oracle examples:** Reconstructed the fixed-anchor invariance via the positive gradient factor at the shared root. Convexity at zero gives `phi(v) <= v phi'(v)`, so the same-point ECP weakening assertion has the correct direction. The scalar recurrence is the actual newest upper bound because the bounds decrease strictly, and each step is smaller than `1/a`; the lower bound and fixed-parameter Newton expansion follow. These are elementary supporting derivations, explicitly framed without unsupported priority claims.
