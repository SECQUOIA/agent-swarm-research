## Minor findings and precise corrections

1. **Monotonicity holds on the nonnegative argument interval, not on an open neighborhood of it.** `sections/appendix-formulations.tex:52` says all five laws are “increasing, convex and differentiable on a neighborhood of the box.” For the quadratic law, `phi'(z)=8z` is negative immediately below zero; the trigonometric derivative is likewise negative immediately below zero. Both are increasing on `[0,10/11]`, and convex and smooth on a sufficiently small open neighborhood of this interval. Split the sentence accordingly. The epigraph-bound proof uses monotonicity only on the box, so its conclusion remains valid.

2. **Specify the numerical comparison tolerances explicitly.** `sections/experimental-design.tex:35` refers to “the comparison tolerance,” and `sections/results.tex:52` to “the declared objective comparison tolerance,” without explicitly identifying their scaling reference. State that cross-witness bound consistency uses `T(f_w)=10^{-6}+10^{-4} max(1,|f_w|)` at the witness objective, and that enumeration agreement uses the same formula at the best enumeration objective. These formulas are verified directly in `lbesh_study_analysis.py:tolerance/audit` and `analysis_v1/derive_ablations_references.py:63`. They differ in role from the tighter primal checker tolerance and should not leave the reader guessing which already-mentioned tolerance is intended. The reported 1.32% fraction is consistent with the stored tolerance values.

3. **Give the reference-solver settings where continuous references are introduced.** `sections/experimental-design.tex:18–22` identifies CVXPY/Clarabel and the benchmark limits, but not the settings for the 420 reference solves. Add the actual frozen reference settings: one thread, 300-second per-solve limit, 300 iterations, and absolute-gap, relative-gap and feasibility tolerances `10^{-9}`. These are explicit in `lbesh_research/conic_reference.py:solve` and its defaults. Distinguish these reference settings from the 120/150-second benchmark limits. This is useful for interpreting the small root differences and inaccurate-status outcomes; Stage 4 can still supply full commands.

4. **Narrow the diagnostic timing exclusion.** `sections/oracle-diagnostics.tex:79` says timing excludes “object setup.” `scalar_run` and `quadratic_trial` create the counted function and oracle before starting the timer, but timed `call_cut` creates an `NLRow`, point/interior dictionaries and NumPy outputs. Replace the broad phrase with “exclude imports and creation of the counted function and oracle objects”; optionally say that per-call row/container construction and Python bookkeeping remain included. The microsecond figures and their limited interpretation are otherwise accurately stated.
