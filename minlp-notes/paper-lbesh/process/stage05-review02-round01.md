The held-out multi-tree pairs have equal solve counts (30/30 for hull and 29/29 for big-M), so there are no additional failures in either of those pairs. Their PAR10 ratios are approximately **0.9944** and **0.9909**, whereas common-solved shifted-time ratios are **0.9550** and **0.8927**. Their relative PAR10 differences are therefore smaller, not larger. The single-tree ratios are approximately **0.1193** and **0.1142**, correctly dominated by one/two extra ECP failures. Suggested correction: “The much larger single-tree PAR10 differences are dominated by one or two additional ECP failures. Multi-tree pairs have equal accepted counts, so their PAR10 differences reflect timing alone.” The tables and central single-tree conclusion are correct; this is a prose quantifier correction only.
