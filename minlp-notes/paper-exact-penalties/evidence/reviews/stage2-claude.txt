**Findings worth fixing** (all presentational, ordered by impact):

1. **Overstated comparison in the introduction.** `sections/01-introduction.tex:47` says Section 5 "improves" the (N+1)2^{O(n)} bound to N^{O(k+1)}. The bounds are incomparable: with n = 1 and many nonlinear rows, Theorem 4.1 gives O(N) bits while Theorem 5.1 gives N^{O(k+1)}. The abstract and Section 5 make no such claim. Replace with "complements".

2. **Symbol F has two meanings inside one argument.** Section 2 defines F as the feasible set (`02-geometry.tex:4`) and Lemma 2.4 refers to "slice meeting F" (`02-geometry.tex:117`). Section 4 redefines F as the objective bound (`04-general-upper.tex:78`) and then applies Lemma 2.4 "with objective bounds −F, F" (`04-general-upper.tex:161`). Also, formula (32) uses v as a free scalar (`05-fixed-count.tex:125`) while v is the optimal value everywhere else, and L, D, M, B, H are each reused across sections. Rename the objective bound and the free variable at minimum.

3. **Radius formulas are not literal instances of Lemma 4.2.** The sparse KKT system is said to use at most s+m+3n polynomials (`04-general-upper.tex:109`) but (eq:RM) uses log(s+m+n+2) (`:118`). The reciprocal graph uses s+2m+1 polynomials (`:146`) but (eq:RD) uses log(s+m+2) (`:150`). The 2^{O(n)} factor absorbs this, and the final bound also silently uses s, m, n ≤ N. Write the actual S+1 terms and add the one clause. The actual count is s+m+2n since g_i = 0 reuses g_i.

4. **Cited work mischaracterized.** `05-fixed-count.tex:19` calls Grigoriev–Pasechnik "few-variable algebraic methods". Their Theorem 1.2 is polynomial for a fixed number of quadratic map components with arbitrarily many variables. Say "few-quadratic", as stage2-sources.md already does.

5. **Evidence note claims something the manuscript does not do.** `evidence/stage2-sources.md:13` says "The bounded OptGP hypothesis is retained in the comparison", but the Kamminga–Rudolph sentence at `05-fixed-count.tex:21–24` never mentions the bounded-solution-set requirement of their Definition 1.15 and Corollary 8.14. Add the clause to the manuscript or correct the note.

6. **Two vacuous assertions in the new check script.** `verification/check_upper_bound.py:59` tests only |c + t⁴ − value| = t, which also passes if the objective sign were wrong, and `value` is hard-coded rather than computed from the objective at the primal point. Line 66 asserts t² ≤ 4 + t², which is always true. The PASS claims in README and stage2-validation.md remain accurate, but these lines add no confidence.

7. **Minor wording.** `04-general-upper.tex:134` uses r_j without stating r(x,z) = Ax+Bz−b in this section. The README paragraph at lines 14–21 describes "the exact arithmetic check" without naming check_lower_bound.py now that two scripts exist.
