\begin{abstract}
We prove that exact separation of hypermetric inequalities is strongly
NP-complete.  The proof gives a rational Gram construction in which
the violated inequalities correspond exactly to exact covers.  A
scaling with polynomially bounded denominator places the hard points
in the metric polytope and the interior of the elliptope.  For any
fixed gonality cutoff, the construction can also satisfy every
rounded positive semidefinite inequality below that cutoff.
The same construction, together with switching and an orthogonal
extension, proves strong NP-completeness for gap-$1$, rounded positive
semidefinite, Boros--Hammer, Boolean quadric clique and cut, pure
hypermetric, and odd clique separation.  Hardness for odd cliques
already holds for the unswitched all-positive inequalities.  The
constructed clique, cut, pure hypermetric, and odd clique inequalities
define facets; we give a direct proof.  We also give polynomial-size
certificates, an exact
rank-parameterized lattice algorithm, and an explicit algorithm for
violations above a normalized threshold.  Finally, we prove
NP-completeness of gap-$0$ separation at metric-feasible points outside
the elliptope; this reduction does not establish strong hardness.
These results distinguish the difficulty of unrestricted
exact separation from the tractability of semidefiniteness tests,
fixed-rank inputs, and restricted searches.
\end{abstract}
