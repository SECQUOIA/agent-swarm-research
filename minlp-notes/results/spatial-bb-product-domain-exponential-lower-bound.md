# Exponential lower bounds for product domains and univariate auxiliary branching

Date: 2026-09-05. Status: independent review passed.
Depends on the reviewed theorem and moment lemmas in
[the higher-order theorem](spatial-bb-higher-sos-exponential-lower-bound.md).

## Product domains with complete univariate polynomial information

Keep the continuous problem `P_n` and parameters `k,m,z,r,epsilon` from the
higher-order result. Replace a spatial box by an arbitrary product domain

```
S = S_1 times ... times S_n,   nonempty S_i subset [0,1].
```

The sets may be disconnected. For a degree-`2r` node relaxation use normalized
polynomial functionals `L`, all products of the global equality through
degree `2r`, and

```
L[g_1 ... g_a p^2] >= 0
whenever sum_j deg(g_j)+2deg(p)<=2r,
```

where each `g_j` is any univariate polynomial nonnegative on its corresponding
coordinate set `S_i`. Include the empty product. One may additionally impose
all products through degree `2r` of univariate polynomial equalities that
vanish on `S_i`. This oracle is deliberately strong: it is given every valid
univariate polynomial inequality of the permitted degree, regardless of how
the set was represented. It need not have a finite efficient representation.

**Theorem.** The same cover bound holds:

```
q_r=min{k-2r+2,z-2r+2,m(1/2-2epsilon)} > 0,
number of pruned product domains >=
min{(n/(n-k))^(q_r/2), (n/(n-z))^(q_r/2)}.
```

The unique-minimizer linear-perturbation corollary also survives, using
`m(1/2-2epsilon-2eta)` in place of `m(1/2-2epsilon)`.

*Proof.* For a product domain containing a witness `w=1_H+p 1_M`, let

```
A={i:0 notin S_i}, D={i:1 notin S_i}, R=A union D, U=[n] minus R.
```

If `|R|<q_r`, use the exact same functional as the higher-order box proof:
fix `x_i=w_i` for `i in R`, and place the fractional-cardinality moments
`E_(s,t)` on `U`. The hypotheses of its Lemmas A/B remain valid because
the number of remaining `H` and `Z` coordinates is unchanged.

Every local inequality on `R` becomes a nonnegative constant. On `U`, both
endpoints lie in `S_i`, so a valid univariate polynomial `g` has
`g(0),g(1)>=0`. Boolean reduction gives

```
g(u_i) = g(0)(1-u_i)+g(1)u_i.
```

Take a product of such local polynomials, combine factors using the same
coordinate, and expand over the `v` distinct involved `U` coordinates. The
result is a nonnegative linear combination of Boolean assignment indicators
on those coordinates. After removing constant factors, `v` is at most the
sum of the original factor degrees. The degree condition therefore permits
Lemma B to show that each indicator times the substituted `p^2` has
nonnegative expectation. Thus every node inequality holds. A univariate
polynomial equality vanishing on `S_i` is zero after deterministic or
Boolean substitution, so all of its allowed products also vanish.

The equality products and objective value are identical to the earlier
construction. Hence a pruned domain containing a witness has `|R|>=q_r`.
Such witnesses satisfy `Z intersect A=empty` and `H intersect D=empty`,
so the same uniform-subset avoidance bound and cover count apply. QED.

## Consequence for branching on a single nonlinear term

A split on `psi_i(x_i)<=theta` versus `psi_i(x_i)>=theta` changes only
`S_i`, so it preserves the product-domain model. In particular, splitting
on the individual penalty term `x_i(1-x_i)` creates a disconnected
endpoint-side domain and a middle interval, both covered by the theorem.
Arbitrary split points and combinations of coordinate and univariate-term
branching are allowed. This claim concerns the node oracle just defined in
the original variables. Additional lifted variables require the degree
accounting below; one must not identify hierarchy order before and after a
nonlinear substitution.

## Polynomial lifts: explicit degree accounting

Suppose the formulation also contains local auxiliary variables
`y_(i,j)=psi_(i,j)(x_i)`, with polynomial degrees at most a positive integer `D>=1`.
Each auxiliary depends on one original coordinate. Node branches may impose
arbitrary local restrictions on `(x_i,y_(i,1),...)`; after exact substitution
these determine a set `S_i`. Assume node relaxations in the lifted variables
use a degree-`2r` moment/preordering oracle generated by:

- the global equality `sum_i x_i=K`;
- the local graph identities `y_(i,j)=psi_(i,j)(x_i)`;
- polynomial inequalities and equalities involving only one coordinate's
  local block and valid on that block's restricted graph.

Products and SOS multipliers are allowed when their total lifted degree is
at most `2r`. Crucially, arbitrary coupled valid inequalities are not given
for free. For example, `sum_i y_i>=1/4` with `y_i=x_i(1-x_i)` is the desired
global objective lower bound itself and would close the root immediately.

The product-domain lower bound applies with **effective order `rD`**.
Indeed, take an original-variable degree-`2rD` functional from the preceding
construction and define a lifted functional by substituting every auxiliary
polynomial before evaluation. A polynomial of lifted degree `j` becomes a
polynomial of original degree at most `jD`. Every lifted preordering term
therefore becomes an allowed original preordering term, since the local
substitutions are univariate polynomials nonnegative on the corresponding
`S_i`. Graph identities become zero identically. Products of the global
equality remain within degree `2rD`, because the equality itself still has
degree one. Thus the pulled-back functional is feasible.

For `y_i=x_i(1-x_i)` take `D=2`. Even a fixed-order lifted SDP/SOS relaxation
combined with arbitrary branching on `x_i` and `y_i` still needs exponential
many leaves on the balanced family. The theorem charges order `2r` in the
original variables, so the restriction threshold is

```
min{k-4r+2,z-4r+2,m(1/2-2epsilon)}.
```

This is sufficient, not optimized. The same unique-minimizer perturbation
can be added and has the same objective estimate because the pulled-back
functional evaluates the intended polynomial objective exactly.

## Limits and novelty assessment

The proof excludes arbitrary multivariable nonlinear cuts and branching on
functions that depend jointly on several original coordinates. It permits
unlimited exact local domain information, but it does not grant an exact
solver for the coupled nonconvex product-domain feasibility problem.
Feasibility-based reductions are covered when they preserve all true
feasible witnesses. Objective-based reductions must be counted by their
certified discarded product domains.

The endpoint-moment construction and avoidance count are inherited from the
reviewed results. The additional observation is that univariate local
inequalities cannot distinguish a Boolean-supported functional when both
endpoints remain in the local set. The [independent audit](../notes/review-spatial-bb-product-domain.md) passes
the product-domain and polynomial-lift statements. The
[literature comparison](../notes/spatial-bb-strengthening-novelty.md) gives
context; no separate priority guarantee is asserted.

## A known cut outside the node model

A [classical Boolean-quadric clique inequality](../notes/spatial-bb-known-clique-cut.md)
closes the single-block root after equality linearization, including the
unique-minimizer perturbation. This gives a concrete way to avoid the
obstruction and clarifies its proof-system scope. The lower bound is not
an inherent hardness claim for optimizing the instance family.
