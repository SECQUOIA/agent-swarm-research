The Hesse proceedings DOI `10.1145/3798129.3800760` was identified,
but its full text could not be inspected. All theorem-level Hesse
comparisons use the versioned arXiv manuscript. The
[Yang access record](yang-source-status.md) records the failed publisher
and repository routes. Neither gap is filled by a claim that a later
version or inaccessible theorem has the same content.

The research paper *PosSLP and Sum of Squares* (FSTTCS 2024) was
screened in the [author's primary manuscript](https://goravjindal.github.io/assets/pdf/SoSposslp2024.pdf).
Its integer-square representation questions do not state a convex
quartic optimizer classification. It is not used as a theorem dependency
and is omitted from the main bibliography.

The overall prior comparison remains finite. Different output encodings,
convexity domains, real versus bit computation, and the supplied
certificates are material assumptions. No search result establishes
publication priority by absence.

The Basu survey has different theorem numbering across versions. The
older proof companion links to the
[early 36-page manuscript](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~sbasu/raag_survey2011.pdf),
where the quantifier-elimination theorem is Theorem 2.16 on page 12.
The report cites the dated
[2014 arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.1534v1),
where the same statement, including the output coefficient-height bound,
is Theorem 2.27 on page 16. The
[author's publication list](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~sbasu/)
confirms publication in *Panoramas et Syntheses* 51 (2017), pages
107–153. The bibliography identifies the inspected 2014 version and
records that later publication; it does not assign a date to the undated
early manuscript from its filename alone.
