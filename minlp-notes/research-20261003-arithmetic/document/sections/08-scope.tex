\section{What the results settle and what remains open}
\label{sec:scope}

The results support one integrated account because their proofs share the
same arithmetic distinction. A value algorithm becomes a point algorithm
only after an effective distance modulus is supplied. A point algorithm
becomes an exact comparison algorithm only after a separation bound is
combined with a representation that can reach the required precision.
An exact circuit can be short even when printing its value is expensive.

\paragraph{Completed extensions.}
The global point theorem treats arbitrary rational polyhedra, sparse input,
and numerical degree dependence, including finite attainment and a fixed
original-coordinate minimum-norm selector. The residual-convex cubic theorem
removes the joint core convexifier on product boxes and supplies one finite
noise law for every precision. The constrained exact results remove the
constraint-rank restriction under an alternative nonlinear-dimension
parameter or under verified box/flow structure. The unconstrained exact
upper theorem extends to explicit unary-degree polynomials and observables.
The circuit-Gram theorem supplies a compact exact representation that
complements the expanded-output lower bounds.

The earlier convex cubic point theorem, quartic point reductions, exact
quartic sign completeness, rational-optimizer hardness, irrational zero
example, and witness-size bounds were audited and integrated. They are
historical results in this repository, not results first created by this
completion pass. The accompanying
\href{../README.md}{result map} links each theorem, its proof, its independent
review, and its verification record.

\paragraph{The unrestricted constrained comparison question.}
This report does not establish deterministic $\mathrm P^{\PosSLP}$ for an
arbitrary globally strongly convex quartic over an arbitrary rational
polyhedron. The constrained Newton recurrence itself is available. What
the present argument lacks is an exact quadratic-subproblem algorithm whose
number of arithmetic operations is polynomial independently of the expanded
bit lengths of circuit-encoded Hessian entries. Ordinary polynomial-bit QP
algorithms do not supply that interface. The new structural results verify
it in concrete families; the nonlinear-dimension theorem uses a different
route. The \href{../constrained-exact/general-boundary.md}{boundary analysis}
also explains why straightforward smooth penalties and accuracy-driven
barriers do not resolve the unrestricted case. This is a remaining question,
not an impossibility result for every possible method.

\paragraph{Other limits.}
The cubic recourse proof uses the product-box geometry and the quadratic
degree of its relevant minor polynomials. It does not settle arbitrary
coupled feasible sets or quartic recourse. The global point theorem requires
global convexity and polynomial dependence on numerical degree; it does not
give a polynomial-input-length result for exponentially large binary-encoded
degrees or arithmetic-circuit polynomial input. Equality in the exact
quartic class has the stated upper reduction, without a matching hardness
claim here. At a zero minimum, rational SOS and real SOS remain different
questions. Compact Gram construction does not imply inexpensive exact
validation or a short expanded certificate.

\paragraph{Prior work and source limits.}
The source comparison credits prior value optimization, effective radii,
invariant directions, error-bound theory, essential-variable extraction,
Newton convergence, and structured QP algorithms. Different output wording
alone is not evidence of a new theorem. In particular, the degree-based
error exponent is not claimed as new. The full primary text of Yang's 2009
paper could not be obtained in this audit, and the inspected version of
\emph{Hesse's Redemption} is arXiv version~1, not its proceedings version.
The \href{../literature/source-ledger.md}{versioned source ledger} records
those limits. Source-level proof-transfer cautions are subordinate to the
theorem comparison and do not assert that the source's main results fail.
Publication priority remains unestablished.

\paragraph{Verification and completion.}
Each new proof has an internal independent review, and the existing central
results have fresh scoped audits. The saved exact-arithmetic diagnostics
exercise identities, degeneracies, and certificate boundaries. The report
and reference implementation have their own targeted checks. No finite
test establishes a universal complexity bound, and internal research-agent
review is not external peer review. The
\href{../VERIFICATION.md}{verification record} lists the commands actually
run and their scope; project-wide checks and CI were not duplicated.

This completes the documented development and review program. It does not
make the broader subject mathematically exhausted: the unresolved cases
above remain explicit research questions, and they are excluded from every
completed theorem statement.
