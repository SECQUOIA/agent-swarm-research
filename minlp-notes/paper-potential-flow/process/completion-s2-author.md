## Proofs and exact scope

- **Representation and validation:** every edge has its own affine coefficient box; each basis is a rational densely encoded continuous piecewise polynomial, with fixed rational breakpoints and value zero at zero. Bases may be nonmonotone and coefficients may be negative. Common breakpoint refinement is polynomial, not a Cartesian enumeration. The pointwise derivative minimum is checked by a univariate derivative-root partition; identically zero derivative factors are omitted. Rational coefficient-matching LPs detect a flat piece for any permitted parameter vector. This proves strict increase for the entire box without excluding isolated derivative zeros.
- **General structural transfer:** centered convolution preserves zero at zero and affine coefficients, and adding a positive linear term gives positive derivative resistances. The acyclic-flow bound, jointly continuous compact parameter family, state uniqueness, and finite closed face family justify the limit. Positive forcing on suppressed paths and the same topology yield the accepted free-coordinate bound. The smoothing is analytical, never an algorithmic polynomial approximation.
- **Growing degree:** only the core and linking dimensions remain fixed in the A03 box lemma. Realizable support-sign enumeration, QE, and sample bounds are polynomial in numerical degree. Dense affine substitution and all expanded sums retain polynomial size. Local algebraic degree can grow polynomially; it is not claimed constant. Recovery uses one common local field and fixed-size affine systems. No unbounded compositum is formed.
- **Polynomial-law optimization:** coefficient independence permits local optimization and summation by face. The pressure output is an additive interval and rational scenario, using the explicitly proved affine-parameter sensitivity estimate. Exact arc extrema are single-block algebraic values, and rational capacity comparisons include equality. Arbitrary polynomial-law rational arc scenarios are not claimed. Rational rounding need not preserve a sampled circulation or polynomial cell.
- **Exact fractional barrier:** a single consistently oriented cycle has base flows `a_i`, resistances `1/a_i`, and final edge `(-1,K)`. Testing its last flow against `-1` is precisely SRS, including equality. Multiplying by the product of radicands yields positive integral resistances. For fixed reduced `p/q>1` with even `q`, positive-edge base flow `a_i^(q/2)` and resistance `a_i^(-(p-1)/2)` give the same radical. Odd-denominator cases and a converse reduction are not asserted.
- **Constructive rational approximation:** a normalized truncated dyadic Gauss rule is written as positive fractions `a t/(t+s)`. The proof supplies tail and complex-disk Taylor error bounds uniform at zero, polynomial-bit individual algebraic coefficient enclosures, positive upward rational rounding including tiny weights, rational normalization, and dense product-expansion bounds. Known positive fractional approximants are credited rather than claimed new.
- **Rational local encoding:** denominators are positive for every real flow. Clearing their product preserves the scalar resistance leaves. One extra objective coordinate and one linking equation encode pressure; flow stays affine. Per-face local algebraic optimizers and additive global face selection are explicitly distinguished.
- **Physical and input errors:** the circulation identity and signed-power strong monotonicity give a uniform sup-norm flow estimate for heterogeneous exponents. A path sum gives pressure error. Original-law electrical sensitivity and the edge endpoint equations give rational nomination/resistance recovery, without evaluating an exact original fractional physical state. The final interval and scenario budgets count both original/surrogate discrepancies, face selection, and rounding separately.

## New development beyond the promoted notes

The original result restricted the fixed finite rational exponent family to `(1,3)`. After a lead suggestion, I independently derived and included an extension to **every fixed finite family of rational exponents greater than one**. The five reviewers must assess this strengthened range explicitly.

For a non-odd-integer exponent write `q=2j+1+2 alpha` with fixed integer `j>=0` and fixed rational `0<alpha<1`. The law

`psi_q(x)=T^(2 alpha) x^(2j+1) R_alpha(x^2/T^2)`

is a positive sum of terms `x^(2j+3)/(x^2+c)`. Their derivative is

`x^(2j+2) ((2j+1)x^2+(2j+3)c)/(x^2+c)^2`,

positive off zero. Odd integer exponents use their exact polynomial. Rational scaling uses a power of two whose exponent is divisible by the denominator of `2 alpha`. Fixed `j` leaves dense sizes polynomial. If `H=ceil(max family exponent)` and `c*=2^(1-H)`, signed-power strong monotonicity has uniform lower constant `c*`, and the physical and rounding budgets use `eta^H`. This closes the artificial upper endpoint without changing any original theorem's restrictions on fixed exponent families.

The water head-loss exponent `1.852=463/250` is a short source-backed example: it lies in both the even-denominator exact barrier and the fixed-family additive theorem. This concerns the specified mathematical law, not physical calibration.
