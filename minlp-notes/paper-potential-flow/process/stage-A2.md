## Corrections and proof details

1. **Wrong single-cycle package in the packet.** `labbe2020-bookings-in-the-european-gas` is the three-author article *Bookings in the European gas market: Characterisation of feasibility and computational complexity results*. Its Section 6 leaves booking feasibility on a single cycle with nonlinear potentials open. The four-author single-cycle result is the separate 2021 Networks article, DOI `10.1002/net.22003`. The section cites the first source accurately and adds a separate bibliography entry for the actual result. The existing package entry is unchanged. This corrects the packet's attribution rather than silently assigning a theorem to the wrong paper.
2. **Space complexity needs a separate source.** The A1 Renegar/Basu bounds quoted in the manuscript are fixed-dimension time bounds; those displays alone do not establish polynomial space when the number of variables grows. The new proposition gives its polynomial-size existential-real encoding, cites A1 for real-algebraic decidability, and cites Canny's polynomial-space theorem directly for the stronger conclusion. Canny is an additional published source, verified through the report version. The two new bibliography entries cannot be transcribed from the named package front matter because the single-cycle package is mismatched and there is no local Canny package. Their metadata provenance is recorded below and in bibliography comments; all existing package entries are retained.
3. **No mathematical obstruction found.** Re-derived aggregation in both directions, the grounded implicit-function calculation, electrical ordering, saturation, smoothing convergence, the one-active-cycle decomposition, and both SRS reductions. Shifted boxes require no zero-inclusion hypothesis. Fixed coordinates, singleton feasible polytopes, all-lower/all-upper balanced vectors, branch gaps, and thresholds at terminals and articulations are explicit. The LP multiplier is also proved directly by feasible exchanges, so no constraint qualification is hidden in an LP citation.
4. **Closed sign cells and degenerate roots.** Closures of full-dimensional arrangement cells cover zero-flow boundaries, including coincident lines and lower-dimensional feasible domains. The signed quadratic expressions agree on shared boundaries. Fixed-cycle roots are isolated piece by piece without assuming a nonzero quadratic coefficient. Strict monotonicity excludes an identically zero polynomial on an open piece; endpoint and linear roots are included.
5. **Witness extraction has an explicit route.** Project the retained feasible active-cycle threshold system onto its nomination coordinate by eliminating the circulation, then use univariate root isolation and sign tests to select an algebraic or rational feasible coordinate. This uses A1's verified quantifier-elimination bound, rather than importing the stale theorem numbers from an older Basu survey version in the research notes. The projection has one quantified and one free variable; its polynomial-count exponent is four. Active decision has two quantified variables, degree two, and polynomial coefficient bits. The proof supplies an explicit total polynomial bound with absolute constant exponents.
6. **Bounds and error budgets.** The main section computes `B` on the aggregated core. Local block aggregation cannot increase its nomination norm bound. Acyclic nonzero physical flows admit source-to-sink path decomposition, giving `|x_e| <= B`; tree routing then gives `|w| <= 2B`. Fixed-block intervals sum to width at most `epsilon/8`, and active bisection adds at most `epsilon/8`. Selecting the greatest face lower endpoint loses at most `epsilon/4`; the extracted parameter attains at least that endpoint, and rounding loses at most another `epsilon/4`, for total loss at most `epsilon/2`. Clipping the rational parameter to its rational interval and recomputing the other pivot preserves exact balance. Greedy disaggregation is exact. `B=0` concerns core flows only; off-core original flows need not vanish.
7. **Arithmetic conventions.** The SRS encoder removes zero/one radicands, handles the empty sum, uses fixed yes/no instances for trivial comparisons, and retains weak inequalities. In the reverse encoder, `T<=0` with at least one radical gives a fixed no instance, ensuring the output has positive integer `K`. Equal branch resistances are handled rationally before rationalization. Denominator products and fixed powers have polynomial binary length; no factorization is needed.
8. **New general-graph upper bound.** Nonnegative complementary `p_e,n_e` encode a signed flow as `x_e=p_e-n_e` and its signed square as `p_e^2-n_e^2` exactly, including zero. The resulting existential sentence has `2n+2m` variables and `O(n+m)` degree-at-most-two constraints. Its equivalence to the passive state uses A1's existence and uniqueness. The section concludes SRS-hardness and membership in `\exists\mathbb R` for multi-entry cactus MPD, without claiming SRS equivalence or NP-hardness there.
