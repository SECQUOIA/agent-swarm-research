## Adversarial mathematical assessment

1. **A01, `thm:a-pre-existence`, `lem:a-pre-incidence`, and `lem:a-pre-block`: accepted.** Strictly increasing laws through zero give positive linear coercive bounds on their primitives even when the laws are bounded. The proof does not accidentally require surjectivity. The empty-edge connected graph is explicitly separated. The incidence-tree and block aggregation arguments handle bridges, two-edge parallel cycles, and shared articulation nominations without duplicate counting. In particular, the construction checks conservation at a cut vertex through the identity `sum_H b^H_v = b_v`; it does not assume the desired restriction theorem to prove itself.

2. **A02, `lem:a-cac-aggregation`, `lem:a-cac-adjoint`, and `lem:a-cac-faces`: accepted.** Core aggregation is exact for shifted and singleton boxes. The adjoint acts on balanced nominations with a grounded potential; the electrical flow has positive branch currents on a cycle even if physical flows vanish or change sign. The multiplier exchange proof covers singleton feasible polytopes. Gap thresholds, terminal/articulation equalities, empty branch interiors, and two equal-valued branch vertices are all included in the face family. The finite-family compactness argument and uniform smoothing limit justify keeping an original optimizer without asserting unsmoothed differentiability or a numerically computable perturbation size.

3. **A02, active/fixed block computation and `thm:a-cac-additive`: accepted.** Conservation leaves the advertised two-variable active cycle formulation. Fixed block nominations really remain fixed because the two pivots have fixed total nomination and are in the same deleted-block component for every other block. The loop polynomial is monotone even under the signed circulation convention. Zero-flow and lower-dimensional cases are covered by closed cells, and the formulas agree on their boundaries. Witness extraction at a retained feasible threshold, clipping inside a rational interval, exact recomputation of balance, and the Lipschitz estimate establish an admissible rational scenario. No algebraic sum is silently treated as a polynomial-size single-root representation.

4. **A02, `thm:a-cac-srs`: accepted.** Both reductions preserve the weak inequality, including exact ties. The triangle chain has `3 mu` vertices, `4 mu - 1` edges, and maximum degree three. Multiplying by `2 product_i a_i` clears every constructed denominator with polynomial bit length. The reverse encoder absorbs a positive radical coefficient into a positive rational radicand, then clears denominators with a polynomial-size product. Equal branch resistances, no remaining radicals, nonpositive transformed thresholds, and zero/one input radicands are separated. The result is SRS equivalence for the stated single-source/sink subclass, not an NP-hardness claim or a claimed classification of general cactus MPD.

5. **A03, `lem:a-blk-order` through `lem:a-blk-faces`: accepted.** Strict block interior ordering follows from connectivity after deleting either terminal. The suppression count includes parallel paths and rank-one blocks, and does not apply its nonbridge bound to rank zero. Positive forcing gives strictly increasing path currents, hence a unimodal adjoint with at most a two-vertex maximum plateau. The path-position enumeration includes adjacent free pivots with no upper segment. Local perturbation is applied after choosing a coarse face, so all other block contributions are constant at fixed resistances. The resulting family is independent of resistances, which is essential for the later joint-optimization transfer.

6. **A03, `lem:a-blk-boxlp`: accepted.** The support formula introduces only the fixed number of direction variables. Enumerating realized signs in `(z,eta)` avoids an exponential enumeration of formal endpoint choices. The objective row is included in the support image, including its core-dependent offset. Compactness applies before projection. Exact recovery uses one common local algebraic field, all exposed zonotope vertices, and at most `k+2` affinely independent images; it does not incorrectly enumerate only the at-most-`k` non-bound coordinates of an LP vertex. Zero columns, singleton intervals, redundant equations, and lower-dimensional zonotopes do not invalidate the argument. Each final determinant system has fixed size, so the bit-length argument is coherent.

7. **A03, `lem:a-blk-resistance-lipschitz`, `thm:a-blk-joint`, and `thm:a-blk-arc`: accepted.** The resistance derivative has the stated positive parameter sign, while its coefficient may itself be signed. The unit adjoint current bound is valid on any connected graph. The unfiltered product domain is needed for the interpolation and is explicit. Nomination rounding preserves balance by rational box feasibility; resistance rounding preserves each interval; physical circulation need not remain on the algebraic sign cell. The zero aggregated-core case correctly allows nonzero off-core flows. For arc optimization, pressure monotonicity transfers nominations only after fixing resistances. The subsequent objective is the actual affine arc flow, so the algorithm does not substitute a pressure-maximizing resistance vector. The parallel-edge example correctly illustrates why that substitution would fail. Bridge extrema are the intersection of the two cut-sum intervals. Exact algebraic comparisons distinguish capacity equality from strict violation.

8. **A03, `prop:a-blk-limits`: accepted.** I checked the primary Thürauf PDF, including the booking data, resistances, equation (3), and Lemma 4.17. The renamed gap formulas agree. The rational gap has polynomial encoding length even when its magnitude is small. The proposed width-two bags and deletion of the hub establish the claimed class restrictions, while the growing block rank explains why there is no conflict with the positive theorem.
