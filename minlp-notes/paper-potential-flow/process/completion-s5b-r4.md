2. **Total-edge-flow hardness, lines 71–164 and 192–249.** Each paired triangle is a physical unit-through-flow device with path resistances in `[1,3]` and direct resistance two. The final paragraph correctly distinguishes total edge flow from delivered throughput, which stays one, and disclaims fixed-rank and relative-error conclusions.
