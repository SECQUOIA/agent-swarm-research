## Finding

**Minor — clarify the quantifiers of the Brandenberg–Stursberg comparison (lines 1078–1083, especially 1080–1082).** The text describes Theorem 18 as universal nondegeneracy of a differential-flow polytope “with fixed elasticities and variable nomination/capacity bounds.” In the August 16, 2023 primary preprint, Theorem 18 explicitly quantifies over all choices of the elasticity vector as well as the two families of bounds: [[brandenberg2025-extremal-solutions-for-network-flow]] p.19. Definitions 1–2 fix the elasticity vector for each particular polytope: [[brandenberg2025-extremal-solutions-for-network-flow]] p.2-3. Those two levels should be distinguished. The current wording can incorrectly suggest that their universal graph characterization keeps elasticity fixed while only the bounds vary.

Repair: say that their theorem concerns nondegeneracy of linear differential-flow polytopes for every choice of positive elasticities and nomination/capacity bounds, while each individual polytope fixes those data. Then retain the valid distinction from nonlinear regions obtained by varying resistance at fixed nominations. This does not change any S4a result.
