## Weighted-stage source update

The lead read the complete local 21-page Wang–Hasler report *Convexity of Resistive Circuit Characteristics*, retrieved in the knowledge base on September 7. Earlier research notes saying that its full text is inaccessible are stale. The introduction and model concern a scalar branch current or voltage as a function of a source value; Theorem 1 expresses second derivatives by an associated linear circuit, and Theorem 4 gives structural sufficient conditions for convex or concave transfer characteristics.
