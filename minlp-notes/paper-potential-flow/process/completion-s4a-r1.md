## Minor issue R1-1: clarify the elasticity quantifier in the prior-work comparison

Location: `06-weighted.tex:1078–1082`, especially “with fixed elasticities and variable nomination/capacity bounds.”

The cited Brandenberg–Stursberg preprint distinguishes two quantifier levels. Definition 10 fixes the elasticity vector and quantifies over bounds. Theorem 18, the cited cactus characterization, quantifies over **all** elasticity vectors as well as all nomination and capacity bounds. I checked its statement against the original PDF, p. 19; Definitions 1–2 define the elasticity vector and linear constitutive relation. The current compressed description can incorrectly suggest that only the bounds are universally quantified in the graph characterization. This does not affect any theorem or proof in S4a.

Repair: say that each polytope has fixed elasticities, while the cactus characterization quantifies over every positive elasticity vector and all bounds. For example: “Their cactus characterization concerns universal nondegeneracy of linear differential-flow polytopes over all positive elasticity vectors and nomination/capacity bounds. Each individual polytope fixes those elasticities; the present regions instead vary resistances within one attainable-state set.” Retain the distinction between the properties being characterized.
