## Finding

### Minor: specify the queried arc in the restricted SRS equivalence

**Location:** `complexity/sections/05-boundaries.tex`, Theorem `thm:a-bound-arc-srs`, lines 687–690; converse proof, lines 717–725.

The statement describes a restricted graph family consisting of a cactus branch with terminal bridges in parallel with a probe, then calls “the weak lower comparison problem” on that family SRS-equivalent. It does not explicitly restrict the queried arc to the probe. The preceding hardness statement concerns comparison of “one quadratic arc flow,” so the graph restriction alone can be read as allowing any designated arc of such a graph.

The converse establishes the equivalence only for the probe flow: it writes that flow as `sqrt(R)/(sqrt(R)+sqrt(beta))`, then converts its threshold comparison to a weak lower comparison of the effective cactus resistance. A target inside a cycle of the cactus branch has an additional internal split factor, and the displayed converse does not cover it. This is a scope ambiguity, not a counterexample to the intended theorem.

**Fix:** In the equivalence sentence, write “weak lower comparison of the designated probe flow” (and, ideally, specify that the probe is oriented from the unit source to the unit sink). The existing proof then supports the statement exactly. The hardness conclusion and the threshold `1/2` remain unchanged.
