# Independent S5a review instructions

Review the complete `complexity/sections/08-design.tex`, its bibliography entries, and the earlier results it uses. The lead will supply the frozen source SHA-256 when dispatching reviewers. Read the applicable AGENTS.md. Do not change manuscript sources or run shared manuscript builds. Write only your assigned review report; scratch computations can use your own temporary files.

Preserve independence: do not read S5a author, lead, check/build, adjudication, correction, or peer-review reports. Do not use broad searches of the process folder that expose them. Repository mathematical result and investigation notes are permitted as supporting material, but their old status assertions do not establish proof correctness or priority. Avoid their historical review reports until your mathematical assessment is complete; if you consult any afterward, document that scope. Use primary literature, including original local PDFs, to check attribution and imported claims.

Every reviewer must cover all of the following, regardless of their additional assigned focus:

1. Exact monotone polynomial-root optimization: all promises, affine parameter dependence, unrestricted polytope dimension, lower-dimensional domains, dense growing degree, fixed breakpoints, uniform vertex and root bit bounds, equality, vertex extraction, and exact algebraic output.
2. Correlated polynomial laws within independent cycles: passivity/existence, reorientation and shifted partitions, exact attainable intervals, rational original endpoint and target profiles, signed/local capacity constraints, singleton and zero-flow cases, and distinctions among exact profiles, local values, and global scalar comparison.
3. Convex polynomial minimization: rational surrogate construction, projection and final recovery, exact feasibility, all error budgets, cube-only convexity, global convex Lipschitz extension and oracle hypotheses, fixed coordinates, and polynomial encoding.
4. Global parameter correlations: precise affine capacity equivalence, exact feasibility and robust violations, original rational witnesses, and individual arc optimization. Check that the independent flow-box conclusion is not extended to globally correlated scenarios.
5. Coupled convex performance hardness: actual graph topology and data bounds, physical cube realization, objective and gaps, strong hardness, approximation output semantics, and membership limited to the proved family.
6. Fixed-measurement maximization: rational normal directions, algebraic lengths, degenerate generators and dimension, complete candidate enumeration, convexity domain, finite-set scope, local capacities, separate algebraic approximation, output and runtime.
7. Explicit new extension: the fixed-measurement algorithm for independent polynomial-law cycle polytopes, with the stated local capacities. Audit its complete proof and degree/bit bounds; do not assume that the older quadratic implementation establishes the extension.
8. Literature, exposition, and consistency: distinguish classical mechanisms from the precise new statements; verify version-specific locators and mathematical dependencies. Identify undefined symbols, unsupported claims, hidden assumptions, missing cases, and readability problems.

Classify each finding as major or minor, explain why, give a precise location and a concrete correction or counterexample. Report all valid issues, including minor ones; do not invent findings to fill a quota. A major issue threatens a theorem, proof, output guarantee, essential scope, or defensible contribution claim. Clearly state what you read and checked, computations run, source access limits, and unresolved uncertainty. Numerical evidence does not prove a universal result. Include the initial and final manuscript hash and an explicit verdict about the new extension.
