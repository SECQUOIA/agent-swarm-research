## Mathematical and algorithmic checks

- **Law validation, lines 16–88.** Independent coefficients justify the pointwise minimum derivative. Refinement and the derivative-root partition have polynomial total size in dense input length. Sign testing establishes nondecrease on all pieces, including unbounded pieces. A continuous nondecreasing law fails strict increase precisely when a polynomial piece has identically zero derivative for an allowed coefficient vector; the proposed coefficient LP detects that case. Isolated derivative zeros and nonsmooth breakpoints do not invalidate the argument.
- **Smoothing and faces, lines 91–140.** Centering preserves the zero value, hence the acyclic physical-flow bound. Compactness supplies the uniform state convergence needed for the limiting face argument without a quantitative smoothing rate. The earlier adjoint ordering and positive-forcing proof use positive derivative resistances, not quadratic homogeneity. Joint optimization is valid on the stated product domain by fixing the parameter at an optimizer before replacing its nomination.
- **Dense degree, polynomial laws, and recovery, lines 142–302.** Fixed dimension, rather than fixed degree, is the essential elimination hypothesis. The arrangement of pulled-back breakpoints does not enumerate an exponential product of pieces. Continuity makes closed-cell overlap harmless. Exact local recovery stays in one common algebraic field; separate blocks need not be placed in a compositum. The pressure sensitivity estimate controls rational input recovery even though the recovered physical circulation changes. Arc optimization correctly fixes coefficients for the face-existence argument and then optimizes the affine flow objective jointly.
- **Exact fractional obstruction, lines 317–380.** The cycle-root comparison has the correct inequality direction and includes equality. Reversing the last arc yields the asserted upper-capacity predicate. Clearing all denominators preserves the root and has polynomial binary size. For reduced even denominator, odd numerator gives integer exponents in the construction and the exponent cancellation is exactly `1/2`. The theorem does not overclaim odd-denominator hardness or NP-hardness.
- **Positive rational approximation, lines 394–495.** The two tail estimates, analytic disk, Taylor remainder, and positive-quadrature error imply the displayed uniform bound, including zero. Upward rounding keeps all poles off the nonnegative axis; the derivative bounds require only polynomially many additional bits. The lower bound on the normalization denominator suffices for the factor 24. Expansion has polynomial degree and coefficient size.
- **Arbitrary fixed rational powers, lines 497–573.** Every nonodd rational exponent greater than one has the stated unique decomposition. The dyadic scale makes its fractional power rational. Multiplication by the additional odd power preserves global strict increase; the derivative formula is correct, including its isolated zero. The error scaling by `B^(2j+1)` is valid for both small and large `B`. Odd integers are handled exactly. Positive denominator clearing preserves both the equations and affine resistance dependence, with only one extra core coordinate and linking equation for pressure.
- **Mixed exponents, physical error, and near-extremizers, lines 575–750.** The bounds using `H = ceil(max Q)` are valid on both `B <= 1` and `B >= 1`. The circulation pairing and the edge attaining the maximum discrepancy give the required uniform flow bound. The proof correctly separates discrepancies above and below one when comparing variable exponents to `H`. Original-law input rounding uses the original adjoint and an inverse increment estimate, avoiding any unsupported Lipschitz claim for inverse flow near zero. The error budgets give the asserted interval width and scenario loss. For an arc, recovery starts from an arc optimizer; varying the target resistance is therefore accounted for.

The explicit exclusions and output qualifications are consistent: independent unfiltered boxes, fixed exponent family, growing dense degrees, additive pressure values across blocks, exact local arc values for polynomial/rational laws, and no exact fractional threshold or rational physical-state guarantee.
