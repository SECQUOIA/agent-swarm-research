The one finding is as follows.

1. **Minor: distinguish fixed data within a polytope from the universal quantifiers of the cited cactus characterization.** Location: `06-weighted.tex:1078–1083`, especially “with fixed elasticities and variable nomination/capacity bounds.” In Brandenberg–Stursberg, Definition 1 defines `b` as the positive elasticity vector. Theorem 18 explicitly quantifies over all choices of `b`, vertex bounds, and edge bounds. Thus the elasticities are fixed within each individual differential-flow polytope, but they are also universally quantified in the graph characterization. The current sentence, followed by “Those quantifiers,” can be read as saying that only the bounds are universally varied. This is an attribution ambiguity, not a problem with the new region theorem. I checked the original preprint page containing Theorem 18, not only a search result or author note. The primary locators are [[brandenberg2025-extremal-solutions-for-network-flow]] p.2–3 and p.19.

   Proposed repair: “It concerns nondegeneracy of linear differential-flow polytopes. Elasticities are fixed within each polytope, and the cactus characterization quantifies universally over positive elasticities and nomination/capacity bounds. The property and the role of those data differ from the resistance-attainable nonlinear state regions studied here.” This retains the valid distinction without implying that elasticity is excluded from the source theorem's universal quantification.
