## Direct mathematical checks before authorship

- Paired triangles: with each two-edge path resistance equal to s and alternate edge resistance two, path flow is 1/(1+sqrt(s)) and drop is 2s/(1+sqrt(s))^2. The former is strictly convex, the latter strictly concave. Two complementary initial bridge resistances contribute constant energy three; one variable bridge would invalidate the energy reduction by adding a linear bias.
- Both reductions use an explicitly encoded affine image of the cube in actual edge resistances. Constant local data, growing cycle count, and a rational threshold of polynomial numerator/denominator magnitude support strong hardness. Fixed-field membership is restricted to the displayed family. Total edge flow counts traversals, while the unit end-to-end throughput is fixed.
- The SOC lift z^2 <= tu, u^2 <= beta z projects exactly to z^3 <= beta t^2 for nonnegative coordinates. If t=0, z=0 and u=0 suffice. At physical optimal potentials the choices (z,u,t)=(beta x^2,beta |x|,beta |x|^3) satisfy both cone equalities. The proposed strictly interior point has tu=2, z^2=1 and beta z>u^2. Affine-hull coordinates and a rational facet-slack bound are needed before invoking convex-body optimization.
- The rational design certificate combines a conserved-flow upper bound and an original-profile potential dual lower bound. Reading the full implementation confirmed unconditional rational input validation, complete encoded box/polytope checks, and no reliance on a numerical solver status. It does not encode separate physical operating bounds.
- The high-circulation-edge return-path argument works by cut balance. On the resulting cycle every difference flow is at least H/m; the scalar crossing inequality has positive remainder 2t^2-(5/2)t+1, minimum 7/32. The proof uses no differential-resistance lower bound. The proposed directional extension preserves this argument by swapping both profiles' directional coefficients under reorientation and bounding increments by beta_L times the symmetric increment. It must receive explicit independent review.
- The five-vertex irrational-resistance example forces both first-cut flows to one using positive-width upper capacities, and then theta=7-4sqrt(2). This is an output obstruction, not computational hardness or failure under strict margins.

## Further development during authorship

Further primary-source finding: the lead read and visually inspected Klimm et al. arXiv:2604.26882v1, Theorem 13's complete proof on PDF/printed pages 21–22 (renderings `/tmp/paper-a-klimm-th13-21.png` and `-22.png`). Its unbounded-conductance construction states that a subset sum S passes `S^((r+1)/r)+2nT^((r+1)/r)-T^(1/r)S <= 2nT^((r+1)/r)` if and only if S=T. For r=1 this is S(S-T)<=0, which also holds for S=0 and every 0<S<T. Thus the displayed equivalence is false as printed. This observation concerns that proof, not a counterexample to the hardness statement; its bounded-conductance part instead invokes Theorem 9. The author was notified not to import the defective equivalence or claim it independently verified. Our Section 9 proof uses its own paired-triangle reduction and is unaffected. The lead derived a stronger coefficient estimate and assigned its completion to the author. For the regularized law g+rho x, let r_e=2 beta_e^(sign) |x_e|+rho. The response to an edge-supported constitutive forcing d_e is (d_e/r_e)(j^(e)-1_e), where j^(e) is the unit electrical flow between that edge's endpoints. Its coordinates have magnitude at most one and its own-edge coordinate lies in [0,1]. Thus the response magnitude is at most |d_e|/r_e. Along a coefficient segment, the forcing is x_e^2 times the active directional coefficient change; at zero flow it is zero. Consequently

`||x'-x||_infinity <= M/(2 beta_L) sum_e max_sign |Delta beta_e^sign| <= m M delta/(2 beta_L)`.

The symmetric sum is simply the coefficient L1 distance. This is four times smaller than the repository's constant; no optimality of the constant is asserted. A complete proof requires the smoothed implicit-function argument and compact uniform convergence as rho tends to zero. The author agreed to develop it. Every independent reviewer must explicitly assess it.

Strict-margin witness size can also be extended to a bounded rational coefficient polytope P with arbitrary affine equalities. Around a real strict profile beta, choose a dyadic center a within delta/4 and intersect P with the rational infinity-norm box of radius delta/2 about a. This nonempty bounded rational polytope has a rational LP vertex of polynomial bit length within 3 delta/4 of beta. It preserves correlations exactly, unlike independent coordinate clipping. This remains a conditional witness-size/recovery statement, not a method for locating the initial strict profile. The author was asked to prove and flag this consequence too.
