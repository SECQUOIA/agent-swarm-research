## Finding

**M1 — Minor: explicitly select a rational radius of controlled bit length in the correlated witness proof.** Location: lines 805–818, particularly the box in lines 814–818. The preceding sensitivity statement permits any real radius satisfying the displayed inequality, while the box proof calls its intersection rational and bounds its vertex size using `log(1/delta)`. The latter alone does not bound the encoding length of an arbitrary rational radius, and a real radius need not define a rational box. Similarly, the dyadic center should be selected at the required precision rather than given unnecessarily many bits. This is a local omission in the encoding explanation, not a false witness theorem: the existential construction is free to make these choices.

Suggested repair: first choose `delta = 2^{-k} <= min{1, beta_L gamma/(4mM)}` with the least nonnegative admissible `k` (or use the sharper radius). Choose the center on a dyadic grid of spacing at most `delta/4`, with the least required grid exponent. Then the box data have encoding length polynomial in the original input and `max{0,log(1/gamma)}`. The determinant argument applies exactly as written, including on a proper affine subspace. For zero nominations use rational LP directly. No change to a theorem or its conclusion is needed.
