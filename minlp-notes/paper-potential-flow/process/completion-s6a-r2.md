## Findings requiring correction

### Minor R2.1 — Qualify the exact rational two-path witness by rational epsilon

Location: `complexity/sections/11-certified-computation.tex:520`, and lines 535–547, Example `ex:a-cert-paths`.

The example specifies only `0 < eps < 1`, then says that the upper dual is attained rationally, exhibits `lambda = 1/(4 L eps)`, and concludes that rational node potentials exist. Those rationality assertions require rational `eps`. They are false for the literal unrestricted real parameter range. For example, `eps = sqrt(2)/10` gives irrational displayed `lambda`; the prescribed drops also contain an irrational term. More decisively, this choice gives rational budget `E(y) = 2L/3 + L/25`, whereas the support value `1 + sqrt(2)/10` is irrational. Any witness with rational `lambda`, `v`, and root enclosures has rational `U_w`, so cannot attain that support value exactly.

The surrounding rational-certificate context makes the intended restriction clear, which is why I classify this as minor rather than a failed support-completeness theorem. The real-variable energy, support-set, and asymptotic formulas remain correct.

Repair: write “For rational `0 < eps < 1`” at line 520, or explicitly limit the exact rational-attainment paragraph to rational `eps`. The asymptotic statement can then be taken along rational `eps` tending to zero; nothing else changes.

### Minor R2.2 — The benchmark coefficient ratio is three million

Location: `complexity/sections/11-certified-computation.tex:767`; corresponding fixture construction in `code/potential_flow_mpd/certified_envelope_benchmarks.py`, `instance`, and `run`'s `ratio1e6` case.

The manuscript says the replay includes “a coefficient ratio of `10^6`.” The parameter named `ratio` scales the base coefficient on selected edges; every edge's upper allowed coefficient is three times its base. In the `instance(15, ratio=10**6)` fixture the smallest allowed coefficient is 1 and the largest is 3,000,000. Exact reconstruction also gives `max(cp+cm)/min(cp+cm) = 3000000` for the actual envelope. Thus `10^6` is the fixture's base-scale parameter, not its global coefficient ratio.

Repair: change the text to “a coefficient ratio of `3 * 10^6`,” or describe it explicitly as a base-coefficient scale contrast of `10^6`. The first repair is simplest and matches the section's coefficient-extrema convention. The stress test and its verified guarantees remain valid.
