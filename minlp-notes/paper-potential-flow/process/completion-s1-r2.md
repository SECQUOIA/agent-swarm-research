## Mathematical acceptance evidence

1. **General laws and block structure.** The coercivity proof in `thm:a-pre-existence` uses strict monotonicity at ±1 and so works even for bounded nonsurjective laws. It does not improperly assume superlinear growth or an everywhere-defined inverse. The block incidence and aggregation proofs cover bridges, parallel edges, and the one-vertex case; summing the incident block nominations at a cut vertex counts its own nomination correctly. The cycle equation is strictly increasing even for bounded laws because all summands have limits with the same strict sign.

2. **Cactus saturation (`lem:a-cac-faces`).** The exchange argument handles shifted intervals, fixed coordinates, and a singleton balanced polytope. Its multiplier orientation is correct: high adjoint values require upper bounds, and low values require lower bounds. The entrance-to-exit electrical order localizes threshold equalities to one cycle or one articulation. The explicit enumeration includes both outside-range thresholds, every branch gap, equality at one or two interior vertices, and bridge gaps. It leaves all-upper/all-lower nominations only if balance permits them; it does not substitute zero for these shifted-box cases.

3. **Smoothing and limiting maximizers.** The common energy comparison bounds minimizing flows uniformly for 0 < rho <= 1. Routing the change in nomination produces valid comparison flows in the limit argument. Joint sequential continuity on the compact nomination box gives uniform convergence; taking a subsequence in one of finitely many closed faces transfers an optimizer. No derivative or strict adjoint ordering is asserted at rho = 0. The same argument works for the block result, and the positive lower resistance bound makes its joint resistance version valid.

4. **One active block (`lem:a-blk-order`, `lem:a-blk-faces`).** Strict endpoint ordering in a biconnected block follows by propagating a hypothetical interior endpoint maximum along a path avoiding that endpoint. Free coordinates of a coarse face all lie in the selected block, whose total nomination stays fixed. Removing another block's edges places these varying coordinates in one component, so that block's aggregate remains constant. Terminal interval shifts in the selected block are fixed translations and preserve its local balanced-box structure.

5. **Suppression and positive forcing.** The marked set includes both distinct local terminals, excluding a suppressed path returning to its only marked endpoint. Degree count and suppression give |K_H| <= 2r_H and p_H <= 3r_H - 1. The adjoint identity j_i - j_(i-1) = delta gives exactly an increasing phase followed by a decreasing phase, with at most one flat edge. Accordingly a threshold has at most two internal pivots on each path. The odd-vertex/even-gap enumeration includes monotone paths, no upper vertices, all upper vertices, and a two-vertex threshold plateau. The free-coordinate and dimension bounds are consistent, including f_H = 0 and rank-zero handling.

6. **Sign cells and exact local state encodings.** Closed full-dimensional arrangement cells cover boundary and lower-dimensional feasible sets. The weak-sign polynomial pieces agree at zero, and the cycle equations plus built-in conservation are equivalent to the physical state. Circulation variables are bounded chord flows; they are not arbitrary cycle coefficients requiring a larger, unproved box. Fixed-dimensional quantifier elimination and sampling avoid assumptions about nonsingular or complex-isolated physical solutions.

7. **Parametric box LP (`lem:a-blk-boxlp`).** Zonotope membership is correctly characterized by all support inequalities. The construction enumerates realizable signs in the fixed joint (z, eta) dimension rather than the exponentially many formal endpoint assignments. Its guarded formula remains exact at zero columns and at degenerate parameter values. Leaf recovery also works for a lower-dimensional zonotope: every vertex has an open set of exposing directions in the ambient space, and one can avoid the finitely many nonzero hyperplanes. The Caratheodory step uses fixed-size affine systems in a single algebraic field. Thus the polynomial-size exact leaf vector is justified rather than assumed from a general algebraic-coefficient LP oracle.

8. **Output contracts and errors.** Inactive block values are isolated separately, with intervals summed instead of combining all algebraic fields. Selecting the largest face lower bound loses at most the stated interval width. Rational nomination rounding intersects isolating intervals with original bounds and exact balance, so admissibility is preserved even on a lower-dimensional box. The resistance derivative has the stated positive sign; the unit adjoint flow satisfies absolute edge current at most one. These facts justify the uniform joint Lipschitz estimate and rational scenario recovery. Off-core resistance choices do not affect the terminal objective. Exact arc optimization stays within one block, and fixing resistance before transferring from arc flow to its strictly increasing endpoint drop is legitimate.

9. **Exact arithmetic and limitations.** Both SRS reductions have the correct weak inequality direction and handle empty sums, nonpositive thresholds, equal branch resistances, and integer scaling. The upper reduction clears rational radical denominators with polynomial bit growth and needs no factorization. The existential-real encoding enforces the two signed flow pieces by complementarity. The broader-parameter obstruction uses an explicitly representable positive gap, so it rules out an accuracy-bit polynomial additive algorithm without asserting an unsupported constant-gap result.
