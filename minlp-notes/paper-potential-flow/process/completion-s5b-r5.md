**Minor finding R5-1: the verifier's accepted string encoding is broader than the stated bit model.** Locations: section lines 490–491 and 523–530; `code/potential_flow_mpd/energy_design_certificate.py`, lines 18–23. The theorem correctly claims polynomial verification for ordinary explicitly encoded rational numerators and denominators, consistent with the preliminaries' binary-rational model. The companion parser, however, passes arbitrary strings to `Fraction`. It therefore also accepts scientific notation such as `"1e10000"`. This seven-character token expands to a numerator of 33,220 bits before the verifier applies its mathematical checks. Generally `"1e"` followed by the decimal digits of an exponent E has length O(log E), but parsing constructs a number with Θ(E) bits. Exact arithmetic remains sound after parsing, and standard fraction-string certificates, including the numerical producer's output, do not exhibit this problem. Repair: specify and enforce integer or explicit numerator/denominator strings, or clearly restrict the complexity guarantee to the expanded rational encoding length.
