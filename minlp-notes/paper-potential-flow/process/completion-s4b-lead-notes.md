## Proposed asymmetric interval strengthening

The lead identified a plausible extension beyond the two promoted results and asked the stage author to assess it independently before inclusion. Allow either one shared positive coefficient interval on an edge or two independent positive intervals for its positive and negative flow branches. On a closed flow-sign cell, only the active coefficient enters the cycle law and objective; the existing one-row LP uses that branch's bounds. Unused independent coefficients can be arbitrary admissible rationals. Zero-flow edges contribute zero with either branch. All local quadratic coefficients remain rational constants after threshold selection, so the candidate and surrogate arguments are unchanged.

For recovery, smoothing the asymmetric law by adding rho*x gives a positive differential resistance and parameter derivatives x_+^2 and -x_-^2. The ordinary unit-terminal adjoint has edge currents bounded by one. Each terminal-potential coefficient derivative is therefore bounded by B^2, uniformly through zero-flow limits. Integrating and taking compact smoothing limits gives the joint potential estimate with the L1 distance over the actual independent coefficient coordinates. For a weighted objective, sum normalized terminal estimates against |c_v|. At fixed coefficients, the already proved asymmetric nomination bound applies.

For any two scalar flows, the asymmetric law with both coefficients at least beta_L satisfies |g(x)-g(y)| >= (beta_L/2)|x-y|^2: same-sign and opposite-sign cases follow directly. Changing the active coefficient at y changes its drop by at most B^2 times the coefficient infinity error. Combined with the terminal estimate this supplies capacity-slack rounding at polynomial precision even across flow reversals. A coefficient coordinate with zero physical flow causes no division.

Shared symmetric intervals must remain explicit: they are not literally independent directional boxes. Recover one shared coefficient from the active local LP coordinate (arbitrary allowed value at zero); its diagonal equality then holds before rounding. For independent directional intervals, recover the active coefficient and choose the unused coordinate separately. Thus the same local physical state can be recovered in the original parameter model. Exact fixed-nomination rational-profile recovery likewise uses rational active coefficients and arbitrary rational inactive coefficients.

This is a proposed new development, not yet accepted. If the author includes it, all five stage reviewers must explicitly audit the extension, its original-parameter recovery, the shared-versus-independent distinction, and encoding bounds.
