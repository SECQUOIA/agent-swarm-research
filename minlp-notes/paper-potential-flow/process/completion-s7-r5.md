## Mathematical review by complete proof group

**A — preliminaries and model, `01-preliminaries.tex`, 9 results.** The existence proof uses coercivity of the primitives, not surjectivity of the constitutive laws: the lower bound from positive values at ±1 covers bounded continuous strictly increasing laws. The feasible affine flow space is nonempty by a signed tree construction. Strict convexity gives unique flow, and the potential ambiguity is exactly a component gauge. The incidence convention, reversed nonodd laws, zero-edge case, cut bound on acyclic physical flow, and compactness/continuity uses are consistent. Block aggregation preserves both the admissible nomination totals and the objective, and disaggregation respects the original intervals. The real-algebraic discussion separates local encodings from cross-block sums and does not promise rational physical states. The model narrative supplies these conventions before using them.

**B — cactus MPD and arithmetic, `02-cactus.tex`, 8 results.** I checked the smoothed adjoint argument, existence of an optimal closed nomination face, the reduction to one active block, the quadratic cycle pieces, bisection and witness extraction, error budgets, and final rational disaggregation. Ties and singleton faces survive the limit; the argument is about existence of a suitably structured optimizer, not every optimizer. The source/sink Square-Root-Sum equivalence handles the stated weak inequality, equality, trivial cases, positive-integer scaling, and the matching restricted upper bound. It does not classify exact MPD on arbitrary cactus nomination boxes. The main arithmetic example and Theorem 3.2 retain that distinction.

**C — fixed maximum block rank, `03-block-rank.tex`, 11 results.** The perturbed internal injections force the needed adjoint ordering on long paths; both sides of a plateau and endpoint patterns are enumerated. Suppression, the bounded coordinate count, and closed-face enumeration are justified for fixed maximum rank per block while the number of blocks is unrestricted. In coefficient elimination, support signs are required to be jointly realizable in the retained core/support variables. This avoids pretending that arbitrary box-corner patterns form a polynomial list. Quantifier elimination has a fixed variable count, and recovery uses a bounded convex combination of exposed vertices in a local algebraic field. Inactive blocks are evaluated separately, without forming their compositum. The exact arc result is local; pressure optimization adds rational enclosures. The exponent is explicitly parameter dependent, not advertised as FPT. The attributed nomination-hardness construction has unbounded rank in one block despite treewidth two. I found no conflict among these scopes.

**D — dense polynomial and fractional laws, `04-laws.tex`, 12 results.** I checked continuity and zero normalization, strictness/admissibility tests including flat derivative pieces, independent affine coefficient uncertainty, positive denominator clearing, growing dense degrees, and parameter recovery. Sparse binary-exponent encoding and moving breakpoints are not silently included. The fractional-law construction preserves monotonicity through positive rational quadrature and uses the odd-power factor to cover every fixed finite rational exponent family greater than one. Its approximation error is connected to physical-state and objective error before rational input rounding. The even-denominator exact arc obstruction is a comparison-hardness result; it does not contradict the additive result or assert NP hardness from Square-Root-Sum. The narrative law theorem accurately states the strengthened exponent range and the dense representation.

**E — finite choices and graph boundaries, `05-boundaries.tex`, 11 results.** The finite secant identity has the correct separate target-edge term; the target's envelope selection is reversed relative to the ordinary adjoint-edge rule. The zero flow-difference case allows an arbitrary positive secant because it multiplies zero. Compact attained envelopes remain strictly increasing under the stated family assumptions, and attainment selects admissible edge laws at the computed state. The electrical sign argument uses the adjacent-terminal series-parallel scope correctly. I checked the pressure gadget, rank-three arc probe, rational gaps, resistance versus nomination scaling, and the quantitative positive-resistance restoration of the K4 obstruction. Restricted NP/coNP membership is supported by the exact fixed-rank verifier and is not exported to general exact arc comparison. The inherited Thürauf construction is credited before its added probe consequences. Exact flow realization, existential filtered capacity design, and unfiltered worst-case validation remain distinct. The main hull and hardness tables preserve these restrictions.

**F — weighted objectives and attainable regions, `06-weighted.tex`, 14 results.** The rational QP witness argument handles singular stationary systems on the relevant face; its certificate claim is not based on a numerical stationary point. On trees, objective cut-flow contraction and the signed-path marks give the stated support/path bounds, including zeros. The fixed-total-rank weighted algorithm retains one bounded algebraic core; that is stronger arithmetic than fixed maximum block rank alone permits. I checked the triangle and theta objectives, their no-instance gaps, the restricted memberships, the positive-objective/absolute-flow variants, and the different effects of resistance and nomination scaling. The universal scalar hull classes depend on the objective: trees for arbitrary weighted potentials, cacti for linear flows and pairwise pressure, series-parallel graphs for an individual arc. The full flow/potential/joint-region convexity classifications are separate statements, with simplicity and the common nonlinear-power restrictions retained. The power-law extension is qualitative and does not acquire an unsupported uniform bit bound. The main presentation distinguishes exact optimizing scenarios from exact evaluation of their scalar sums.

**G — weighted cactus accuracy bits, `07-weighted-cactus.tex`, 11 results.** The two-stage face proof first localizes an active ordinary block and then perturbs only the relevant zero-objective chains. It retains special ports and does not impose a global adjoint sign that the weighted objective lacks. Local resistance elimination and candidate enumeration cover feasible boundaries, switching ties, zero flows, stationary roots, negative or zero objective coefficients, and flat branches. Artificial sign-cell boundaries do not lose an optimum. The candidate surrogates are partitioned and summed in the same original nomination coordinates, with uniform error and a final original-parameter recovery argument. Directional coefficients and shared symmetric intervals are distinguished. Fixed-nomination exact rational coefficient profiles at algebraic circulation endpoints use the full tied optimal face, rather than rounding an irrational physical state. Exact local capacities permit algebraic nomination scenarios; rational recovery with tightening is measured against the tightened optimum. The irrational-nomination example prevents an unrestricted rational-output claim. The main weighted section communicates these qualifications and the implemented solver's narrower scope.

**H — continuous design and performance, `08-design.tex`, 9 results.** The monotone-root LP threshold equivalence and common vertex-height/separation bound support exact rational parameter recovery without enumerating exponentially many vertices. Repeated roots, piece breakpoints, and lower-dimensional polytopes are handled. The final LP threshold identifies an optimizing original vertex, rather than merely a nearby feasible point. Capacity clipping, local polynomial-law polytopes, and the single global correlated capacity LP preserve closed constraints exactly. The convex minimization proof uses an inner rational surrogate domain and exact endpoint/profile recovery, including algebraic singleton circulation intervals. The polynomial convex extension supplies a global rational oracle under the stated convexity promise. The few-measurement result uses rational generator directions with separately represented algebraic lengths; its finite-set conclusion is correctly confined to the unfiltered quadratic setting. The Max-Cut comparison concerns growing coupled measurement complexity. The main design section preserves the minimization/maximization and local/global distinctions.

**I — correlations, dissipation, and feasibility, `09-correlations-energy.tex`, 8 results.** The shared-parameter example establishes curved geometry without an unwarranted hardness inference. I checked the two bounded-data reductions, the positive flow regime, threshold gaps, and the paired-prefix bridge contribution that cancels the unintended energy bias. Maximum dissipation uses the passive energy dual and a valid SOC representation, with an explicit bounded relative-interior setup for the bit algorithm. The cited convex-optimization interface returns a rational point in the original feasible set; it is not merely a weakly feasible point later assumed to meet closed constraints. The global design certificate checks the original polytope and dual bounds. The ordinary upper-capacity example can force an irrational resistance even with positive-width capacity intervals. The uniform coefficient perturbation proof covers reversals and zero flows, and the sharper constant uses the regularized argument legitimately. Strict-margin rational witnesses remain conditional and do not become a general feasibility-search algorithm. The main text gives both the positive maximum-dissipation theorem and the distinct minimum-dissipation hardness.

**J — scalar compilation and higher blocks, `10-weighted-blocks.tex`, 7 results.** I read the entire scalar approximation proof, including the primitive squarefree annihilator, exceptional leading-coefficient/discriminant roots, real parts of complex exceptional roots, padded bad bands, and rational geometric panels. Tracking real parts of all complex roots is sufficient for the claimed disk clearance; tracking only real critical roots would not have been. Lipschitz bounds control the removed bands. The nonvanishing leading coefficient and Cauchy root bound control all local analytic branches with polynomial logarithmic size. The panel-width ratio, interpolation error, rational evaluation precision, and coefficient lengths support polynomial dependence on accuracy bits. The common-coordinate sum does not add one algebraic variable per block. The fixed-law dense extension and the hybrid result retain their different degree/rank hypotheses. The hybrid parameter is total noncactus rank, not maximum noncactus block rank. The final several-parameter question is explicitly external to the proved algorithms; neither the main text nor the conclusion uses its unresolved case as an established result. I found no missing claimed theorem hidden behind that boundary.

**K — certified computation, `11-certified-computation.tex`, 6 results.** I checked the asymmetric cubic conjugate, outward square/cube roots, exact conservation, primal/dual gap, and energy-to-flow constants. The Bregman divergence has the required argument order, and the endpoint inequalities are outward. Support certificates use a nonnegative multiplier and the correct residual signs; multiplier zero requires a cut functional. Positive-gap rational completeness relies on Slater and rational density but makes no unsupported certificate-size claim. The conserved-curvature bound requires exact annihilation on zero-curvature edges, treats zero gap separately, and derives the rational KKT system with the correct block signs. Its sharpness is over the conserved quadratic error set, not every physical or interval-filtered state. In the two-path example the exact gap is `2 L epsilon^2` and exact support width is `2 epsilon`; the different interval centers and the support-versus-physical-Bregman distinction are explicit. The `29/750 > 1/50` example correctly rules out treating every support-feasible flow as a physical-error candidate. Original-instance reconstruction, min/max loss directions, target reversal, endpoint compatibility, optional goal intersections, and scaling by `C B^3` for energy and `C B` for curvature agree with the implementation and replay. The main certificate discussion limits empirical claims to verified widths/losses and modest examples.
