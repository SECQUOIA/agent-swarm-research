## Required gate for every stage

The lead reads the work and adjudicates every finding. A different correction subagent fixes every valid issue, including minor issues. Any valid major issue triggers another five-reviewer round after correction. Remaining valid minor issues must be corrected before the next stage. The same process applies to the complete manuscript.

Review reports are internal research-agent reviews, not journal peer review or formal proof certification. Numerical regression checks supplement rather than establish universal mathematical statements. Open external problems must not be represented as solved without a complete verified argument.
