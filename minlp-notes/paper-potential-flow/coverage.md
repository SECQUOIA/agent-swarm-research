# Paper A coverage inventory

This inventory reconciles the complete standalone Paper A with its research sources. All 43 promoted potential-flow results are developed in the included technical appendices. The detailed theorem and extension map is [completion-coverage.md](process/completion-coverage.md). The main text provides the model, principal statements, proof ideas, examples, literature comparison, and synthesis; no result depends on an unwritten companion.

The source corpus contains 43 promoted potential-flow results, 173 potential-flow notes (83 investigation and 90 review notes), 73 Python programs, and 8 JSON artifacts. Following direct research links and the explicit supporting/excluded dependencies gives 308 inventory rows. The completion audit found every inventoried file byte-identical in the main checkout and the writing worktree. Review notes record internal research-agent review, not journal peer review or formal proof certification.

Formal verification follow-up (2026-09-20): the inventory and byte-identity statement above record the completion audit. Later [topic 03](../formal/topics/03-potential-flow/COVERAGE.md) and [topic 16](../formal/topics/16-potential-flow-certificates/COVERAGE.md) establish the deterministic certificate mathematics of A11, including conditional scenario recovery, support completeness and convergence, curvature bounds and the two-path comparison. A11 and its source notes now include that evidence and the quantitative support-convergence estimate. The original uncertainty-to-envelope mapping, Python implementation, conservation repair, bit-complexity claims and effective-resistance reformulation remain outside the formal coverage. These additions do not change the historical inventory or establish formal verification of the rest of Paper A.

Rows describe source provenance. A historical exploratory or superseded note is not promoted by its inclusion. The full weighted face reduction, one-line bounded-block compiler, and fixed-total-noncactus-rank extension are now proved in A07/A10. Only the precisely stated unrestricted several-parameter higher-block sum remains an external extension. Source-priority statements remain bounded; the 1993 Hasler–Wang tolerance paper is not full-text verified.
