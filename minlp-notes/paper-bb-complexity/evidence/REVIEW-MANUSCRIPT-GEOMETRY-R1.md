## Statement and proof audit

| Result or mechanism | Checked assumptions and mathematical dependencies | Proof location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three spatial certificate classes | Valid-box covers of the root, interior-disjoint rectangular covers, and split-tree leaves are separate minima. Only `N_cover<=N_rect<=N_tree` is claimed. Ideal-incumbent upper bounds exclude incumbent discovery. | `cert:def-classes`, `cert:eq-benchmarks`, explanatory paragraphs |
| Boundary-safe event accounting | Rectangular owners carry every retained boundary forward. First-outside-coordinate slabs partition the discarded owner. Emptying reductions count as one terminal event. Degenerate retained boxes remain legal nodes. Original-feasibility owners contain no feasible point, without asserting this for their closed slabs. | `cert:lem-events`, complete inline proof and counterexample |
| Probe and evaluation accounting | Recursively expanded child tests replace minimum-of-children stopping bounds. Augmented event nodes and actual search nodes are distinguished. Distinct successful source boxes form a fresh possibly overlapping certificate; their deduplicated count is not the original terminal-owner count. Distinct-evaluation charging is explicit. | `cert:eq-work`, `cert:eq-eval`, accompanying arguments |
| Vertex lower bound | Uses only owned pointwise validity, `q_C>=d_i^2`, and all `2^n` vertices of a closed test. Neither regularity of `F` nor disjointness of the family is needed. | `geom:thm-cover-lower`, complete inline proof |
| Stratum arcsine lower bound | Lexicographically selected dominant coordinate projections are Borel, have tangential Jacobian at least `binom(n,d)^(-1/2)`, and require finite selected-sheet multiplicity. AM–GM and each one-dimensional arcsine integral give the displayed constant. Degenerate projected intervals contribute only zero stratum measure. | `geom:lem-arcsine`, `geom:thm-stratum`, complete inline proofs |
| Uniform graph multiplicity | The graph two-point inequality comes from Taylor's remainder on a convex coordinate domain. Singular-value control of a dominant projection excludes repeated fibres below the stated diameter. Uniform graph Hessians give a uniform multiplicity bound; curvature alone is not substituted for sheet control. | `app:geometry:lem-graphs` |
| Constrained upper localization | Relaxed witnesses can be infeasible and infima need not be attained. The error bound is to the full nonempty compact feasible set, including root and exact restrictions. Local LICQ only at minimizers is not used to infer that bound. The nearest feasible witness has objective excess below `Lambda s_j^2-epsilon`. The `5^n` grid-neighbor count and finite coarse levels yield the level sum. | `geom:ass-upper`, `geom:lem-localize`, complete inline proof |
| Running-supremum comparison | At each upper scale, `eta=Lambda s_j^2-epsilon` gives the fixed covering/grid scale ratio. Lower bounds hold for every `eta`. The comparison with `sup_(eta>=epsilon) psi_alpha(eta)` uses the stated factor `2^n`, including the `eta<epsilon` case. The logarithmic number of scales is retained. | `geom:thm-profile`, `geom:eq-running`, complete inline arguments |
| Single-scale counterexample | Objective, derivative bounds, convex underestimator, single-scale localization, separated well count, and the `K/2` certificate bound were checked directly. It refutes a uniform instance-class replacement of the running supremum by one scale. | `app:geometry:ex-wells` |
| Dimension and manifold rates | Quadratic growth is explicit. It gives a tube of radius `O(s)` around the optimal set. Box dimension yields a logarithmic exponent, while bounded chart covers give the sharper manifold `Theta(epsilon^(-p/2))` rate. Finite optimal sets give only an upper logarithm at this stage. | `geom:prop-dimension`, `geom:prop-manifold`, complete inline proofs |
| Constrained stratum integral comparison | Proximity, quadratic doubling, and lower density are stated hypotheses, not consequences of the word stratum. Disjoint `s/3` balls and Tonelli give the upper bound. The displayed two-sided comparison concerns positive-dimensional strata; finite strata have an upper-only logarithmic contribution. | `geom:thm-integral`, complete inline proof |
| Tube witness and run transfer | Admission is required only inside the unchanged exact set. The dichotomy uses all owners, including infeasible owners. Original-feasibility propagation and other independent removal of tube witnesses are excluded. Successful infeasibility sources remain in the fresh evaluation cover. | `geom:ass-tube`, `geom:lem-tube-events`, `geom:eq-infeasible-cover`, inline proofs/arguments |
| Compatible nonlinear KKT transfer | LICQ and KKT refer to the same compatible original exact/relaxed tuple, with root bounds included and a nonzero relaxed multiplier. The IFT preserves exact levels. Strict descent, original violation control, common projection ball, uniform inverse Hessians, uniform coordinate multiplicity, and relative area Jacobian are all proved. Positive-dimensional active-stratum Taylor growth yields the logarithm without strict complementarity or SOSC. | `geom:thm-tube-kkt`, `app:geometry:proof-tube-kkt` |
| Regular KKT and sharp minima | Strict complementarity and SOSC on the common nullspace prove local quadratic growth by a normalized-sequence contradiction; finite minimizers and compactness make it global. A positive-dimensional active graph supplies a lower logarithm under a suitable gap. Zero-dimensional LICQ gives sharp growth, including the equality-only isolated case. Full-feasible-set error bounds and vertex-vanishing errors make a finite grid through the unique minimizer exact. The width-error counterexample and the dyadic middle-position condition prevent broader exactness/bisection claims. | `geom:cor-kkt-rates`, `app:geometry:proof-kkt-rates`, accompanying examples |
| Bilinear formulas and exact faces | Convex/concave envelope formulas are proved from convex combinations. All termwise gaps are nonnegative; endpoint coordinates form a vertex cover exactly when their sum vanishes. The upper coefficient and simultaneous center maxima are correct. The joint-envelope face argument is stated separately; termwise sum lower bounds are not transferred to it. | `geom:lem-mcc-gap`, `geom:eq-mcc-upper`, complete inline proof/arguments |
| Flat and curved graph geometry | Selected-edge coefficient bounds are explicit. Convex rectangular owners support the centroid proof even with open endpoints. Transversality is sufficient and stronger than positive flat `tau` in higher dimensions. Compact `C^1` transverse charts supply uniform projection-slab measure bounds. | `geom:thm-flat-graph`, `geom:prop-curved-graph`, `app:geometry:lem-centroid` |
| Fractional-cover budget | The lower bound is for a feasible near-optimal cube and may use a selected subgraph. The upper construction uses the full relaxed graph and its total gap budget. The logarithmic width program, half-integrality proof, and clipped grid cardinality were checked; no general optimal-set exponent is inferred. | `geom:prop-fractional`, `app:geometry:lem-half-integral` |
| Matching and ray lower bounds | Disjoint matched pairs make the test preimage rectangular. The concave selected edge supplies its chord quadratic loss; other termwise gaps remain nonnegative. AM–GM and arcsine integration give the coefficient. A feasible two-sided chord is required for the logarithmic consequence. The one-sided divergence proof includes a compact limit of finitely many covering intervals and the contradiction near zero. | `geom:prop-matching`, `geom:prop-ray`, complete inline proofs |
| Aligned examples | The two-dimensional result requires a full optimal segment and the fixed convex-plus-one-bilinear decomposition. Its appendix proves constancy of convex transverse derivatives and the correct endpoint products for both coefficient signs. The three-dimensional example has the stated convex decomposition, aligned optimal segment, strip-area lower bound, and explicit dyadic upper count. It remains expensive for that fixed scheme even though its full objective is convex. | `geom:prop-aligned-two`, `app:geometry:proof-aligned-two`, `geom:ex-aligned-three` |
