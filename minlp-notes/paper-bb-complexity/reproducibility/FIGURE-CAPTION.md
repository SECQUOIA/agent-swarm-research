# Suggested manuscript caption

Archived SCIP 10.0.2 processed-node counts for selected instances from a 20-instance synthetic tolerance study. Each point is one recorded run; settings are kept separate. Panels (a) and (b) use the `model` setting: the known optimum is supplied as incumbent, heuristics are disabled, and node selection is best-first; ordinary propagation remains enabled. Dotted guides show arbitrarily normalized powers eps^(-1/2), eps^(-1), and eps^(-1/4), rather than absolute bounds. Legend slopes reproduce the archived finite-window fits: the tail uses the last five available non-limit records with eps <= 1e-3, and the wide window uses all non-limit records with eps <= 1e-2; both require at least three records spanning at least 0.99 decades. Exhausted trees remain eligible. Panel (c) uses a linear node axis to show the additive growth of `model` counts and the saturation of default iso2/iso3; default iso4 is omitted for legibility (3,431 nodes at eps = 1e-7). Panel (d) compares the named branching settings on the aligned McCormick instance; external bisection and x-priority bisection use best-first search with propagation, heuristics, and presolve disabled, whereas LP-point and default use the ordinary search. Tolerance axes are logarithmic and decrease to the right; other node axes are logarithmic. Open squares mark exhausted trees (`optimal`), and triangles mark counts at the 100,000-node limit. The absolute gap is a global stopping test; feastol is 1e-9, and eps ranges from 1e-1 to 1e-7 in half decades. These floating-point observations are not rigorous certificates or proofs of asymptotic exponents.

Source: `archive/research-20260928b/bb-complexity/solver-validation/results/runs.jsonl`; exact grouping, source line numbers, statuses, fit values, and source hash are in `presentation/figure-metadata.json`. The figure contains 230 recorded observations in 19 distinct instance/setting curves, covering 14 named instances.
