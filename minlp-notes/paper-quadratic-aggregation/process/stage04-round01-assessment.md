## Accepted strengthening

Replace the strict part of Theorem `thm:no-finite-quadratics` by the stronger
statement that **no countable conjunction** of arbitrary original-variable
strict quadratic inequalities describes the ordinary hull. Equivalently,
every such exact strict description needs uncountably many inequalities.

The existing proof already shows that each nonzero quadratic restriction has
only finitely many zeros on the uncountable compact boundary arc. In an
arbitrary strict conjunction describing the open hull, each arc point is
excluded, so at least one quadratic has value at least zero there. Continuity
from the feasible side makes every value at most zero, so at least one is
zero. A countable union of finite zero sets cannot cover the arc. No
uniform-neighborhood argument is needed for this strict step. This is an
elementary countability consequence of the fully reviewed proof, not a
repair of a flawed theorem.

Keep the arbitrary **nonstrict** conclusion limited to finite conjunctions:
its neighborhood argument uses finiteness, and countable dense good
aggregations already describe the closed hull. Update the subsection title,
local explanatory statements and live coverage record consistently. Do not
rewrite historical author reports or reviewer snapshots. Make no new broad
