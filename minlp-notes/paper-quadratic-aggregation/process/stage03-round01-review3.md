## Mathematical audit

- **Closed systems and regimes.** The certificate bounds the closed system
  by a proper closed convex sublevel set. The inclusion of the strict system
  gives the reverse properness implication. The proof does not equate the
  ordinary closed-system hull with the closure of the strict hull. Emptiness
  of the strict system is equivalent to emptiness of the strict homogeneous
  system because openness permits perturbing a `t=0` point. HHC implies
  full homogeneous image convexity in the stated dimension `n>=2`.
  The complete good-aggregation hull description retains its distinct
  `n>=3` restriction.
- **Finite SDP characterization.** The simplex/PSD slice is compact. The
  nonnegative PSD trace detects all nonzero quadratic coefficients, and
  the paired signed coordinate maxima detect all nonzero linear
  coefficients. Common infeasibility correctly corresponds to the zero
  certificate cone. No numerical zero test, complexity bound, or implemented
  classifier is claimed.
- **Shor closure.** The covariance formulation yields actual membership
  `-f(x)` in the convex cone `image(PSD)+orthant`, whose positive dual is
  exactly the nonnegative PSD multiplier cone. Bipolarity gives closure
  membership for the aggregation intersection. The strict feasible point
  supplies an interior cone point. The ball argument for adding a closure
  point to an interior point is sound, and the quadratic mixing identity
  has the correct positive covariance sign. It proves closure equality
  without asserting cone-image closedness. Extrapolation to `2x-x0` proves
  actual, rather than merely dense, whole-space Shor membership when all
  certificates are trivial.
- **Both nonclosed projections.** In either example, a PSD covariance
  with zero first diagonal has zero cross entry. A positive first diagonal
  permits the displayed covariance completion for every second coordinate.
  This gives exactly the claimed missing and included endpoint fibers.
  In the compact version, `x1>=1/4` and the bounded product imply bounded
  `x2`; the four inequalities are all strict at the stated point. The
  convex-multiplier condition `lambda1=lambda3` and the resulting strip
  are correct. Compactness of the original set is not confused with
  boundedness or closedness of its SDP projection.
- **Stable convexity and hierarchy examples.** The perturbation formula
  converges to the quadratic-part tuple for each fixed normal. Openness
  of positive definiteness and the three-form convexity theorem give the
  stated criterion. The A-block/Q-block span equivalence in the trivial-
  certificate case uses strict feasibility and a valid Schur complement.
  The AHC-not-HHC example has `A1=I`; its bad restriction is the binary
  square cone. The reverse stable/HHC example gives arbitrarily small
  perturbations with a midpoint requiring nonzero squared coordinates
  and zero product. Both nonimplications are proved.
- **Other boundary examples.** The four-row ordinary-HC example is bounded
  in its first two coordinates and has only traceless aggregate quadratic
  parts. The homogeneous full image and limiting-hyperplane image are
  convex, but every nonzero level of the specified swept family is not.
  Deleting the fourth row preserves the image argument and the trace
  obstruction; the two-sided linear bound and unbounded feasible line
  are correct. The closed-system example has interior around zero,
  automatic HHC, and only trivial convex certificates; any putative good
  closed-system multiplier has two negative homogeneous eigenvalues.
  The strip example correctly separates existence from hull exactness.
- **Application appendix.** Nonnegative original-row aggregation is global
  even when the estimators are local. The tangent residual and first-order
  condition at a box minimizer justify the two distinct exclusion claims.
  The DD LP, negative-part box-minimum lift, and rational sum-of-squares
  identity are exact. Affine equality weights have exact signed estimators
  and share the normalization; the unbounded-margin example explains why.
  Activation conditions are retained. I checked both estimator factorizations,
  the termwise lift, the global tangent, feasibility/activation witnesses,
  the DD interval, both margin formulas, and the optimum and normalized
  comparison. The pure-bilinear PSD obstruction and common-Shor limitation
  are correct. No unimplemented method or algorithmic novelty is claimed.
