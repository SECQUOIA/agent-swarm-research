## Mathematical development and choices

Created `sections/02-certificate.tex` with the complete main theorem and proof,
its HHC specialization, supporting lemmas, and the quantitative alternative.
The main argument uses the shorter route from canonical §3.4 and the formal
package. I independently checked its crucial directions:

- Strict feasibility gives `c_k < L(A_k,b_k)`, with fixed continuous linear L.
  Its coefficient in the sweep inequality is nonnegative, so replacing c by
  this upper bound preserves the inequality in the needed direction.
- The nonconstant coefficient pair is nonzero before normalization. At a zero
  pair strict feasibility makes c negative, and evaluating the original sweep
  at alpha contradicts nonnegativity.
- Unit normalized pairs lie in the actual finite coefficient cone. Closedness
  is proved without falsely treating every linear image of a closed cone as
  closed: support reduction expresses a finite cone as a finite union of cones
  generated by linearly independent subfamilies.
- A single coefficient subsequence converges. For any fixed x all terms except
  the limiting quadratic form vanish. No x-dependent subsequence or bound on
  c/rho is needed. The unit limit is nonzero and cone membership recovers actual
  nonnegative weights, even though normalized weights may diverge.
- Hyperplane exclusion treats t=0 using the common-negative-direction lemma;
  dehomogenization uses t squared, so it works for either sign of t.
- Hyperplane separation uses one open set (the negative orthant), so no
  closedness of the homogeneous image is assumed.
- Both easy-direction cases are proved: positive quadratic eigenvalue, or
  nonzero affine slope. The implication does not need feasibility or AHC.

The original uniform cone estimate is preserved and fully proved as an
independent lemma. The spectral distance identity is justified by diagonalizing
A, and the min-eigenvalue estimate is written with a positive part so it is valid
for arbitrary A. The resulting level bound is `s <= 2 sqrt(n)||alpha||/kappa`.
The full older contradiction route is completed by the simplex subsequence and
strict negativity of the limiting constant. This is supporting development,
not a separate novelty claim; it occupies a concise subsection (roughly one
page in the current PDF). A later full-paper synthesis can move it to an
appendix if that better balances the manuscript.
