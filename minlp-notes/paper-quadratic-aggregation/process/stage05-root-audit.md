# Stage 5 coordinator audit

The coordinator independently checked the angle-mesh upper proof, the original logarithmic lower proof, the replacement finite-grid lower proof, the rational construction, and the supporting-hyperplane proof of objective-specific exactness. The matrix tested in nonnegative directions need not be PSD. Endpoint cuts supply both unit-ball bounds, including N=2. The finite-grid proof allows arbitrary interior good multipliers and arbitrary positive rescalings.

For the replacement argument, put a=1+1/(40 N^2). Exclusion at two grid parameters implies, by adding the two inequalities and minimizing in the positive multiplier ratio, (tau+sigma)/sqrt(tau sigma)<2a. Hence (tau-sigma)^2<16(a^2-1)=4/(5N^2)+1/(100N^4)<1/N^2 for N>=1. Distinct grid points are at least 1/N apart. The gradient bound 5 sqrt(2) gives the stronger manuscript lower constant sqrt(2)/2000. The concurrent formal supplement uses the valid weaker constant 1/2000 and also derives the older logarithmic constant; formal scope must distinguish these constants.

The coordinator opened the primary author PDF of Rote, The convergence rate of the Sandwich algorithm for approximating convex functions (Computing 48, 1992, 337–361; DOI 10.1007/BF02238642). The inspected file is author technical report 118, version 2b, whose title page identifies the slightly shortened journal version. Its abstract and introduction describe optimal quadratic-order error for univariate tangent/chord approximation. This supplies context for, not a direct proof of, the prescribed quadratic aggregation family.
