## Scope and revision assessment

An independent source comparison confirms that the only section changes from accepted Stage 1 are in `03-structured-oracles.tex` and `07-fixed-state-chains.tex`. They clarify the default recovery object as a cycle-coordinate vector and expand the three-label circuit classification. The changes agree with the author's description; the independent diff and current section hashes are retained in `source-diff.txt` and `source-check.json`.

The expanded classification closes the explanatory gap without changing the theorem. Its case distinction is sufficient: without the negative full normal, minimality leaves one positive subset and its negative singleton rows; with the negative full normal, support has at most four rows. The one-negative-singleton case forces the two overlapping pairs through that coordinate. With no negative singleton, a singleton excludes its complementary pair plus any further row by minimality, and other overlaps produce unequal coordinate coverage. The remaining pair-only case forces the triangle and its doubled full-normal weight. I independently enumerated the positive dependencies and checked the occurrence exclusions used subsequently for product coefficients.

The default-vector clarification is correct. A theta block needs two scalar cycle coordinates, and a rank-one block needs one. The compact representation can share a normalized merged vector among its constituent global states while retaining separate exceptions for observed labels. Dense materialization remains a distinct output cost.

## Independent executable evidence

I wrote and ran three new checks. None imports an author verification program or an earlier reviewer audit.

1. **Exact separator and recovery contracts — `exact_contracts.py`, `exact_contracts.json`.** This generated 219 models and 2,196 queries: 989 accepted, 1,207 rejected. Graphs include cycles, theta and four-path blocks, arbitrary orientations, articulation joins, bridges, loops, disconnected components, isolated vertices, zero capacities, and flat chains with zero through three observed labels among as many as nine explicit labels. Of the queries, 1,117 have zero weights and 498 have positive weights smaller than `10^-20`.

   For each acceptance, the checker independently verifies rational state weights, all normalized flow balances and capacities, retained products, and the aggregate reconstruction. For each rejection it checks strict exact violation, unit flow/product coefficients, and the cut on every independently enumerated integral-flow/simplex generator for that test graph: 17,584 exact generator evaluations in total. Integral network flows generate the base hull by the manuscript's checked integrality argument, so these are exact validity checks for the finite instances, not floating-point support optimizations. The tests exercised 11 rejection families, including transportation subsets and flat zero rows. Membership-only and decomposition-inclusive calls agreed.

2. **Exact compressed-model rows and projected certificates — `compressed_contracts.py`, `compressed_contracts.json`.** This checked 60 initial/eliminated formulations on K4, a K3,3 transportation network, and a disconnected multigraph containing parallel edges, a bridge, and a loop. Capacities and balances were scaled by `1/7`; observations were sparse, with zero weights and unused labels present. Independently known state flows supplied exact auxiliary coordinates, and all 3,466 assembled equality/inequality rows were checked, along with native bounds and unit non-simplex coefficients. Thirty eliminated-model counts agreed with cycle ranks computed independently from unobserved edge lists.

   Perturbed queries yielded 58 `certified_outside` statuses and two `numerically_feasible` statuses. Every returned cut was checked exactly against all enumerated scaled integral-flow/simplex generators, for 1,740 further exact evaluations. I do not treat the two numerical statuses as exact membership evidence. The valid-extension tests establish containment for those examples, while the theorem's reversible substitution proves the general equivalence.

3. **Independent three-label circuit enumeration — `circuits.py`, `circuits.json`.** Exact rational row reduction on the normal universe, independently of the production circuit library, found 11 normals and 16 positive circuits with family counts `(7,5,3,1)`. The unique doubled primitive weight belongs to the negative full normal. The checker also verifies the two circuit occurrence exclusions used in the original-product coefficient proof. This finite enumeration corroborates the revised direct argument; the argument itself establishes why the list is complete.

These tests are deliberately distinct from the author's examples: they check exact returned evidence against original-space generators and independently known extensions, including tiny positive weights and mixtures of degeneracies. They do not prove asymptotic bounds by sampling.
