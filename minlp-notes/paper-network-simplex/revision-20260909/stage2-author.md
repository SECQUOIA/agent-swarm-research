## Changes and mathematical development

1. **Complete three-label circuit case argument.** The proof of `thm:three-state-chain` previously passed quickly from coordinate equations to the overlapping-pair and partition cases. The revision gives the actual inequalities: with one negative singleton, either positive subset omitting that coordinate would force the other subset to overcover a different coordinate. Both subsets must therefore be the two pairs through that coordinate, and their weights are forced. Without negative singletons, the proof separately excludes each overlap when a positive singleton occurs, using either a proper positive dependence or unequal coordinate totals. Three pair-only rows then force equal weights and the doubled negative-full weight. This establishes the four displayed forms directly, without relying on enumeration.
2. **Compact recovery representation.** `prop:theta-recovery` now says that each block stores a default *cycle-coordinate vector*. A theta default contains two scalar entries, while a cycle default contains one. This resolves an ambiguity in “one default coordinate per block”; neither complexity nor recovery changes.

No mathematical summaries or references needed alteration. In particular, I did not introduce new fixed-rank sharpness claims, new nested series–parallel theorems, or numerical library counts as theoretical results.
