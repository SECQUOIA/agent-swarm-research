1. **S1-R2-01 — Minor: “all state products” overstates the individual-product completion result.** Location: `sections/00-introduction.tex:63–65`, particularly “minimum number ... needed to recover all state products on the ambient circulation space.” The supporting proposition, `sections/02-compression.tex:314–322` (`prop:minimum-completion`), is explicitly for a fixed block and a label in `J_B`. The sum therefore concerns determination of the state circulations for the locally observed labels. It does not determine the separate state circulations of the labels already merged with the residual state. The recovery discussion at `sections/02-compression.tex:366–372` instead chooses a proportional refinement of that merged state.

   For an exact counterexample to the unrestricted wording, take two parallel unit-capacity arcs from a source to a sink, with total flow one. Let `m=2`, `y_1=y_2=1/3`, `x=(1/2,1/2)`, and observe only `z_(arc 1,1)=1/6`. The locally observed label is 1; its unobserved graph consists of the other arc and has cycle rank zero. Thus the claimed sum is zero. Its state flow is determined as `f^1=(1/6,1/6)`, but for every sufficiently small `epsilon`, the other state flows can be

   `f^2=(1/6+epsilon,1/6-epsilon)` and `f^0=(1/6-epsilon,1/6+epsilon)`.

   They satisfy the scaled balances, capacity bounds, aggregate sum, and the one observation. Hence the individual missing products for label 2 are not determined even on these nondegenerate feasible slices, and in particular are not determined on the ambient circulation space. This distinction does not invalidate the exact hull formulation or its constructive recovery: those require a consistent refinement, not identification of every possible unobserved state flow.

   Repair the sentence to say that the count is the minimum for determining each locally observed block–label state circulation from individual products and balance equations; state separately that the merged state follows from the aggregate and can then be proportionally refined. A shorter repair is to replace “all state products” with “the full state products for each locally observed block–label pair.” I classify this as minor because the correctly scoped proposition and the exact refinement proof are already present, and the repair changes a summary sentence rather than the mathematical result or the paper's substantive novelty claim. Nevertheless, leaving the literal “all” would conflate determination with constructive selection.
