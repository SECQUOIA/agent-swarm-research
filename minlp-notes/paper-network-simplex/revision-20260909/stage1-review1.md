Enumerated findings:

1. **S1-R1-01 — Minor: carry the locally observed-label qualification into the completion summary and conclusion.** Locations: `sections/00-introduction.tex:63–65` and `sections/09-conclusion.tex:4–8`. The introduction says the summed residual count is the minimum number of individual products needed to recover “all state products.” Proposition `prop:minimum-completion` instead fixes a block and a label in `J_B`; it concerns determination of those locally observed labels' state circulations. Unobserved labels are merged, and proportional refinement selects a feasible split rather than determining every possible individual state flow. The conclusion similarly counts cycles of “each unobserved block–state subgraph” without excluding unobserved labels and the merged state, although the abstract now includes the necessary qualification.

   For a concrete distinction, take a directed unit-capacity cycle with zero balances, aggregate flow 1/2 on each arc, two explicit weights 1/4, and just one observation in label 1 equal to 1/8. The count in the theorem is zero: the complement of the observed arc is a forest. Label 1's flow is determined, but label 2's flow can be either 1/8 or 1/5 on each arc, with residual flow respectively 1/4 or 7/40. Both satisfy all scaled bounds, balances, aggregate values, and the same observation. Thus zero additional coordinates suffice for the sparse hull representation and for constructing a completion, but do not uniquely determine all individual state products from linear balances. These rational values were checked and recorded in `stage1-review1-evidence/completion-scope-example.txt`.

   Repair the introduction to say that the count determines the full state circulation **for every locally observed block–label pair**; retain the existing ambient-space and individual-coordinate qualifications. In the conclusion, restrict the counted subgraphs explicitly to labels observed in their block. This is minor because the formal proposition, construction, and abstract already impose the correct scope; no mathematical result needs changing.
