# Whole-manuscript independent review brief

Read the complete anonymous manuscript, including all proofs, examples,
computational claims, references, and its accompanying source and supplement.
The final-round1 snapshot is the frozen submission candidate. Review the paper
as a journal referee who has not seen the notes or conversation. Do not assume
that previous internal acceptance establishes correctness, novelty, or quality.

Five fresh reviewers work independently. Do not read each other's reports or
prior review reports/adjudications before forming your own judgment. Primary
literature, the literature-audit source evidence, code/data, and independent
verification programs may be inspected. Treat those as evidence to examine,
not authorities to accept. Use online primary sources whenever necessary to
check the closest prior work or a precise imported result.

Each reviewer reviews the whole paper; a requested emphasis is not a restriction.
Check scientific and mathematical correctness first, including essential
hypotheses, local sufficiency in coefficient sections, lower-dimensional and
zero-weight cases, complexity and bit-length qualifications, and the difference
between a valid component relaxation and the hull after adding side rows.
Check that novelty is precisely identified and qualified against known full
convexification, graph-specific predecessors, common-factor elimination,
transportation, Minkowski geometry, and general coefficient constructions.
Assess significance, overall organization, consistent terminology, reader
accessibility, anonymity, and whether the complete deliverables are usable.

Write a self-contained report with an explicit verdict, stable IDs for findings,
major/minor severity, source locations, exact reasoning or a counterexample,
and a concrete correction. Separate optional preferences from actual defects.
Report actual checks and their limits; do not count another person's execution
as yours. No need to inflate the report or repeat the author narrative.
Send a potential major problem early while continuing your own audit.

Do not edit source, frozen files, or other reviewers' evidence. Use private
copies for builds, imports and commands that write reports. Do not spawn agents.
After all five reports, the root assesses every finding and a separate correction
author fixes every accepted issue. Any accepted major issue triggers another
complete five-reviewer round after correction. No external submission occurs.
