# Whole-manuscript review plan

Begin only after Stage 7 is accepted and all its valid findings are corrected.
Freeze a new complete source snapshot. Five fresh agents each review the entire
manuscript independently. Their different emphases must not partition the scope.
They may consult primary literature and create private executable checks. They
must not read each other's current-round reports or edit shared paper/code/data.

The root will read all five full reports, adjudicate every finding, and assign
a separate correction agent to all valid issues. Any accepted major finding
requires another complete five-reviewer round after correction. All valid minor
issues must also be resolved before closure.

## Full scope and interfaces

- Every theorem, proof and construction, including empty/zero-weight cases,
  arbitrary orientations where asserted, capacity degeneracy, affine-hull
  restrictions and rational encoding.
- Known simplex disaggregation and shared-simplex gluing versus the claimed
  sparse formulations and coefficient boundaries. The literature comparison
  must not inflate novelty or confuse projection with an actual coordinate section.
- Observation-sensitive cycle coordinates, fixed-arc deletion, minimum individual
  product completion and its ambient-space scope; no optimal-EF claim.
- Cycle, theta and parallel-path exact separation and recovery.
- Bounded-rank support libraries, coefficient scaling and finite-basis recovery;
  sharp five-product K4 example and the ambient-facet implication.
- Three-way transportation transfer, sparse Fibonacci construction and simple
  degree-three realization. Large magnitude is distinct from large bit length,
  separation hardness and extension complexity.
- Shared flat-chain profile, five tests at two observed labels, 16 circuits at
  three, balance repairs and the failure example at four. Parameterize by
  globally observed labels, while preserving all original simplex variables.
- Code scope and rational certificate semantics, strengthened numerical controls,
  complete measured case grid and source/data provenance. Inspect the fixed-weight
  native-bound formulation and elementary reductions before judging performance.
- Scientific completeness against the repository, cross-section assumptions,
  consistent notation and terminology, literature metadata, PDF readability,
  runnable reproduction instructions and limitations of the application example.

Fresh reviewers should assess the manuscript on its merits rather than inherit
prior acceptance decisions. Earlier records remain available for provenance, but
a previous review verdict is not evidence that a theorem is correct.
