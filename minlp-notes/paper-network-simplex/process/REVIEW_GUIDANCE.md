# Independent review instructions

Review the assigned frozen snapshot as a potential journal referee. Each of five
reviewers examines the complete assigned stage or manuscript; an emphasis is a
way to diversify attention, not a restriction on what to check.

Do not read other reports from the current round or coordinate judgments with
other reviewers. Do not edit manuscript sources. Write your report only to the
assigned report path, and place optional executable evidence in your assigned
verification directory. Build a private copy of the snapshot when necessary.

Check statements, assumptions, proof implications, edge cases, computational
claims, attribution, completeness against repository sources, and readability.
Try to disprove claims rather than only reproduce author examples. Numerical
agreement does not replace a proof or exact certificate. Cite exact manuscript
locations and show the reasoning behind every criticism.

Classify findings as major or minor. A false theorem, missing essential proof,
unsupported central claim, misleading novelty/performance claim, or material
scope omission is major. A local repair that does not change the mathematics or
scientific interpretation may be minor. Explain the classification. Report
uncertain concerns as such, with what would resolve them. Do not manufacture
issues to fill a quota; distinguish optional preferences from valid defects.

End with a clear verdict, an enumerated findings list with stable identifiers,
checks actually performed, and limitations of the review. A verdict of no major
issues may still have minor findings that must be resolved before acceptance.

All five reports are evaluated by the root. A separate correction agent fixes
every accepted issue. Accepted major issues require another five-reviewer round;
remaining accepted minor issues are also corrected before the stage closes.
