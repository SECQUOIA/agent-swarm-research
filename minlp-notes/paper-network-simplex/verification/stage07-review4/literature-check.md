- [Fiorini author manuscript](https://samuel.fiorini.web.ulb.be/papers/howto_rev.pdf): abstract explicitly gives transfer from a facet of an arbitrary 0/1 polytope to an acyclic-subgraph facet. The introduction explains the dicycle-covering transformation. This supports the new comparison without identifying it with the manuscript's continuous sparse bilinear sections.

Printed page 52 identifies the original 1956 chapter in Linear Inequalities and Related Systems, Princeton University Press, pages 223–246. The new network argument is self-contained and consistent with this attribution.
- Local primary full text `literature/papers/khademnia2025-convexification-of-bilinear-terms-over/fulltext.md`, Section 4.2 (lines 438–442): supplies, demands, capacities, route-specific service incentives/costs and bilinear products occur in the transportation-with-conflicts example. The manuscript correctly describes its common-simplex two-supplier model as a motivated specialization, rather than claiming that arbitrary conflict systems themselves are one simplex.
