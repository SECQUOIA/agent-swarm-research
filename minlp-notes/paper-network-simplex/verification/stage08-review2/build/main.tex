\begin{abstract}
We study the convex hull of selected products between a bounded
equality-constrained network flow and a simplex vector. Starting from known
simplex disaggregation, we give an observation-sensitive formulation whose
additional coordinates are counted exactly by the cycle ranks of unobserved
subgraphs, with unit flow and product coefficients. Forest complements remove
all additional coordinates. For bounded block cycle rank we develop finite
support libraries for exact separation and compact recovery without online
linear programming; a $K_4$ with five observed products already forces a
product-coefficient ratio of two. A complementary result holds on arbitrarily long
chains of parallel arc pairs with one bypass: unit flow/product descriptions
exist whenever at most three simplex labels are observed, and four labels can
force a coefficient ratio of two. After local checks and bound grouping,
two observed labels need five profile tests; three use 16 positive circuits
and flow-balance repairs. Sparse
Fibonacci constructions give exponential coefficient ratios on simple
series--parallel graphs of maximum degree three. A coordinate-section transfer
of transportation universality gives unrestricted ratios with only two labels
on general networks. Exact implementations and strengthened numerical LP
comparisons show substantial model-size reductions, while global state merging
and native flow bounds remove some apparent runtime advantages.
\end{abstract}
