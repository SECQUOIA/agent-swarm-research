# Deterministic potential-flow certificates: coverage map

This map assigns every obligation of [CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md) to actual Lean
declarations and records every difference between the source statement and the
proved statement. Build, axiom and replay evidence is in
[VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md); this document fixes the statements to which
that evidence applies.

All declarations are in namespace `PotentialFlow`. Within it, those taking a
`Network` argument are in `PotentialFlow.Network`; those taking a
`RationalNetwork` argument are in `PotentialFlow.RationalNetwork`; the scenario
hypothesis bundle and its lemmas are in `PotentialFlow.Scenario`; the two-path
example is in `PotentialFlow.TwoPath`; and the worked certificate instance is in
`PotentialFlow.CertExample`. Names below are written relative to these
namespaces. **Correction:** an earlier version of this convention named only
`PotentialFlow` and `PotentialFlow.Network`, so it did not reach the other four
namespaces and left unqualified citations in CC19, CC32 and CC40 unresolvable as
written; those citations are now qualified as well. Proof sources are in
[`Formal/PotentialFlow`](../../Formal/PotentialFlow).

Throughout, "the physical flow `x`" means a flow that is feasible for the
nominations and minimizes the energy among feasible flows. Topic 3 proved that
such a flow exists, is unique, and is exactly the solution of the potential
equations (`exists_unique_energy_minimizer`, `energy_minimizer_iff_potential`),
so the hypotheses `Feasible b x` together with minimality are the faithful Lean
form of "`x = x*`". Verified statements are about that flow, not about a
numerical approximation of it.
