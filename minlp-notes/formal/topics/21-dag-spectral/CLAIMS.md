# Topic 21 frozen claims

The authorized scope is the complete explicit-DAG spectral-cover algorithm,
its rational implementation and complexity, criterion consequences, and
conditional finite-memory sandwich transfer. The 33 obligations below are
frozen from the [independent source inventory](SOURCE-REVIEW.md). The broader
stochastic finite-memory producer and represented-matroid topic 22 are excluded
as explained there. No supplied normalization or spectral-cover certificate
substitutes for construction and coverage of the actual producer.


The identifiers below label source obligations. Final claims may regroup
them, but every substantive conclusion remains required.
