# Topic 21 coverage

This maps all 33 [frozen obligations](CLAIMS.md) to the current declarations.
The original-matrix cover, exact cardinality, whole-producer cost bound and
criterion selectors have passed targeted builds and independent reviews.
The source-frozen machine-check record is in [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md).

The original-matrix endpoints are
[`rawDagSpectralCover_isRelativeCover`](../../Formal/DAGSpectral/RawHeadline.lean)
for arbitrarily numbered acyclic input graphs and
[`dagSpectralCover_isRelativeCover`](../../Formal/DAGSpectral/Headline.lean)
for explicitly ordered graphs. Their inputs are the actual rational prior and
edge matrices, their PSD promises, and a positive rational accuracy. The raw
front end constructs a topological order and preserves the original edge
identifiers. Both invoke the factor producer, sorted normalization trials,
signed-label dynamic program, and rank-zero branch. No maximum-volume basis,
accepted trial, or cover is supplied as a premise. `eta<1` is additionally
required for kernel preservation and the source approximation scheme. The
finite sets `feasiblePaths` and `rawFeasiblePaths` define semantic comparison
domains; the producer does not enumerate those sets.

The original-input cost endpoint is
[`coverInputRun_polynomial_work`](../../Formal/DAGSpectral/CoverInputExecution.lean).
The runner executes and stores factorization before enumerating trials; its
output is proved equal to the mathematical cover. The bound uses only original
matrix/accuracy encoding bounds, explicit graph sizes, and fixed dimension.
Its final targeted build and independent whole-runner review passed.

“Mapped” below means the listed declarations implement the stated mathematical
or local algorithmic conclusion. Final module builds, kernel replay and axiom
audits belong in [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md). All 33 obligations are
discharged within the stated scope; local bounds are connected to the actual
original-input producer rather than left as supplied certificates.

Dimension-dependent coefficients may be large. No dimension-independent FPT
running time, numerical stability guarantee, stochastic graph-generation
certificate, or topic-22 represented-matroid result is claimed.

checks that the executable closed formula preserves the exact recorded charge.
