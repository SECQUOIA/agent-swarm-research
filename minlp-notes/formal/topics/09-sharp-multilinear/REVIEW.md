Two agents reviewed the final sharp-growth integration and found no
mathematical or specification issue. One reviewer was independent of all
new implementations. The other authored the scalar asymptotics module
and independently reviewed the probability, envelope, box-transfer, and
supremum integration. Additional component authors reviewed adjacent
interfaces and the final cube upper bound.

The reviews checked that the finite laws have the prescribed means and
that the harmonic and threshold laws depend only on the full mean vector
and shared parameters. One common mixture works for all monomials. The
support classification covers constants, singleton supports, zero gaps,
and boundary points. Every bound needed by the final coupling theorem is
proved rather than left as a hypothesis at the endpoint.

The reviewers also checked that the scalar upper certificate has leading
constant one, explicit lower witnesses exist at every sufficiently large
integer allowance, and the ratio sets are nonempty and bounded before
their suprema are compared. The limit is a full-sequence statement, not
a result restricted to a dyadic subsequence.

For general boxes the proof preserves the actual continuous hull slice,
including zero-width coordinates. The original termwise gap is at most the
expanded gap; the proof does not incorrectly replace this inequality by
equality. The dimension supremum requires exactly n ambient coordinates,
using padding that preserves both actual gaps. Padding does not assert
that all coordinates occur or that padded points are interior.

The final theorem uses an alternative rational harmonic gain factor and
does not verify the manuscript's optimized fixed-point, Lambert W, or
second-order bounds. Both reviewers recommended that stronger finite
upper certificate as a next project, while distinguishing it from a
matching second-order lower bound.
