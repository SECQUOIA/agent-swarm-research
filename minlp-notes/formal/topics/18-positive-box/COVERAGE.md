# Positive-box multilinear gaps: coverage map

This map assigns every obligation of [CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md) to actual Lean
declarations and records every difference between the source statement and the
proved statement. Build and axiom evidence is in
[VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md); the three independent reviews are in
[REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md).

All declarations are in namespace `MultilinearGap`, the radix ones in
`MultilinearGap.Radix`; proof sources are the nineteen modules under
[`Formal/MultilinearGap`](../../Formal/MultilinearGap). Names below are given
without the `MultilinearGap.` prefix.

`CLAIMS.md` requires that reuse of existing modules be identified here and that
nothing be silently reproved. The reuse inventory is the last section.

## Conventions actually implemented

- Boxes are `coordinateBox l u` with `0 < l i ≤ u i ≤ rho * l i`; the point is
  `x ∈ coordinateBox l u`; the ratio is `boxTermwiseGap / boxHullGap`, admitted
  into the class only when the hull gap is strictly positive. Coefficients are
  nonnegative on the support; dimension, degree and term count are unrestricted.
- **Fixed coordinates are not removed.** `CLAIMS.md` says they are "permitted
  and are removed first"; the Lean transfer needs only surjectivity of the
  affine map, not injectivity, so the proved statements simply allow `l i = u i`
  with no preprocessing. See PB23 and PB25.
- Moments are taken at integer order `j : ℤ` throughout, but only two of the
  four families are `binomMoment s μ j` of an actual law — `thresholdMoment`
  and `orientMoment`, as is Tier F's `balancedOrientMoment`. `elementaryInt` is
  the elementary symmetric polynomial and `lowerMomentInt` the floor/fraction
  formula, so their PB12 conventions are re-established from their own
  definitions. An earlier version of this bullet said the moments were
  `binomMoment` for all four families and that the PB12 conventions were one
  set of lemmas rather than four; that was wrong.
- `sSup` of an unbounded set is junk in Lean, so every supremum statement is
  either preceded by an explicit `BddAbove` hypothesis or by
  `boxAspectRatios_bddAbove_of_lt` / `commonAspectBoxRatios_bddAbove_of_lt`.
- **Universe restriction.** `boxAspectRatios` and `commonAspectBoxRatios`
  quantify over `(I : Type)`, that is `Type 0` only. Every finite type is
  equivalent to some `Fin n`, which lives in `Type 0`, so no generality is lost
  mathematically — but there is no transport lemma in the tree, so the
  restriction is literal. Recorded by the third reviewer; see
  [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md).
