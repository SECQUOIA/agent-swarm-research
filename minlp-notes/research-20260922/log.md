- Ridge scan of MINLPLib (`ridge-envelopes/minlplib-ridge-scan.md`): 245 instances (128 open) contain a ridge occurrence that needs a nontrivial envelope, but 97% of those occurrences have two-variable arguments (`(x_i - x_j)^2`, `sin/cos(theta_i - theta_j)`), and most sin/cos occurrences lack finite variable bounds in the file. Only 83 instances have arguments with three or more variables. Consequence: in MINLPLib the box-aware envelope mostly matters in the two-variable (triangle) case; high fan-in applications are trained neural networks, which MINLPLib barely covers. The concave envelope of a convex function of a linear form, such as `(x_i - x_j)^2` in packing constraints, is the known staircase/Lovász result.
- Checked idea from the brainstorm scout, negative: "box-aware rank-one cuts" `b^T X b <= Lambda_b(x)` with `Lambda_b` the concave envelope of `(b^T x)^2` over the box are implied by McCormick (RLT) inequalities. After reflecting coordinates so that `b >= 0` on `[0,1]^n`, `Lambda_b` is the Lovász extension `c^2 + 2c b^T y + sum_ij b_i b_j min(y_i, y_j)`, and `Y_ij <= min(y_i, y_j)` termwise gives the cut.
