Reviewer: independent agent, 2026-09-23.  Scripts: curve-hulls/code/review/.

OVERALL VERDICT
The headline holds up. The saved waterno2 cuts are exactly valid. The substituted model is a correct
relaxation of the original. In an independent rebuild (no model.py), the saved waterno2_06 cuts raise
Gurobi's 300 s dual bound from 94.4 to 212.4, above MINLPLib's listed best dual bound of 165.19.
Every table entry matches the raw results files. The problems found are provenance and reproducibility
defects, not validity defects (see "Defects" below). The dual bounds are still uncertified
floating-point Gurobi output, as the report says.

SCRIPTS (all written by the reviewer; none of them use the author's validate.py or check_point.py)
- rbuild.py      own OSiL reader and Gurobi builder for waterno2 (linear, quadratic, power(var,3) only;
                 anything else raises).  "sub" mode: y=x^2, z=x^3 with exact bounds.  Cuts are translated
                 from the saved funcs (k*t**p) to (x, y, z).

- rowcheck.py    row-by-row equivalence of the author's model.build(...,"sub") and the OSiL model.

CLAIM 1  Cut validity (waterno2_06, waterno2_24; also _09/_12/_18)          VERDICT: CONFIRMED
- Each cut claims p(t) = c0 + c[0] t + c[1] k2 t^2 + c[2] k3 t^3 >= 0 on [l,u].  The check converts all
  floats to Fractions exactly.  The minimum is taken over the endpoints and the real roots of p'
  in the interval; each root is (-B +- sqrt(D))/(2A), and p(root) is reduced to X + Y sqrt(D) with an
  exact sign test.  The interval is the union of the float bounds in the cut file and the exact decimal
  bounds in the OSiL file (for example, ".58").  The checker was tested on t^3 - t and on cuts with c0 lowered by 1e-9.
- waterno2_06: 673 cuts on 72 variables, 0 violated; worst slack +1.46e-11 (absolute), +1.2e-12 (relative).
  waterno2_24: 2479 cuts on 289 variables, 0 violated; worst slack +2.5e-12 (absolute), +4.1e-13 (relative).
  _09 (1006), _12 (1102), _18 (1751): 0 violated; worst slacks 2.5e-12, 6.6e-12, 5.7e-15.
- For every cut, [l,u] equals the OSiL bounds of variable v.  Each v is continuous and has a diagonal x_v^2
  term and an x_v^3 term in the OSiL model.  All selected variables have l >= 0.
- In the author's model, each t{v}_j is defined from x_v only (checked from Gurobi's stored QC rows
  and NL expression trees).  For the _24 file, t{v}_j equals funcs_j(x_v) exactly at random points.
- Caveat: the _12/_18/_24 cut files are from the seed-1 runs (1102/1751/2479 cuts).  The seed-0 cuts
  behind the table (1232/1664/2202) were overwritten, because run.py writes cuts/<name>.json regardless
  of seed.  The validity of the seed-0 cuts for _12/_18/_24 therefore cannot be checked.

CLAIM 2  The substituted model plus cuts is a relaxation of the original        VERDICT: CONFIRMED
- rowcheck.py (_06 and _24) sets x to random points in the box, computes each t from its defining
  constraint, and evaluates all rows.  Results:
  - Rows 1234/1234 (_06) and 4942/4942 (_24) are present, with matching senses and right-hand sides.
  - The maximum relative row mismatch against the reviewer's OSiL evaluation is 0 (_06) and 1.3e-17 (_24).
  - The objective matches, and the original variable bounds and types are unchanged.
  - The model.py selection equals the reviewer's selection (78 and 312 variables).
- The auxiliary bounds contain g([l,u]) exactly (156 and 624 variables).
- Together with claim 1, this gives the argument: for any feasible x of the original, setting t = g(x)
  satisfies every definition, row, bound and cut.  The new model is therefore a relaxation.
- Fixing the MINLPLib best point (p4, obj 282.888; p5, obj 7332.72): the reviewer's orig, sub and sub+cuts
  models are all feasible, with identical objectives, on _06 and _24.  The author's sub+cuts model is feasible on _24.
- DEFECT: the author's sub+cuts model is INFEASIBLE at the _06 best point with the current code.
  check_models.py waterno2_06 now prints "sub+cuts status 3"; the IIS contains a cut, x366, t366_0 and t366_1.
  Cause: the saved _06/_09 cuts were generated under an earlier scaling (for example, t = 4x^2 on [0,0.58]).
  The current model.py defines t = x^2 there, so run.add_static puts the saved coefficients on differently
  scaled variables.  131 of the 673 _06 cuts are affected.
  This is a stale-artifact problem, not an invalid run: within each run, the cuts and the model came from
  the same code, and the cuts are valid for the funcs recorded with them.  However, the report's statements
  that "waterno2 was not affected" by the rescaling, and that check_models passes on every reported
  instance, are no longer true of the files on disk.  In fact, the seed-0 table runs (cut files dated
  10:29-10:32) used the old scaling, and the seed-1 replicates used the new one.
