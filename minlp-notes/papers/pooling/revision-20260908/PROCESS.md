# Submission revision

This revision follows the user-requested author/five-independent-reviewer/correction process. Earlier reviews are historical context, not acceptance evidence for this revision.

## Stages

1. Foundations and algebraic complexity (Sections 1–2): verify proofs, audit primary literature and exact-arithmetic claims, develop missing arguments, improve exposition.
2. Restricted hardness (Section 3): audit source reductions, physical realizations, strong versus ordinary hardness, approximation, and contribution comparisons.
3. Structural and contract algorithms (Sections 4–5): audit polynomial size and arithmetic, decomposition, recovery, exceptional cases, and prior-work comparisons.
4. Synthesis and complete submission manuscript (Section 6, abstract, introduction, bibliography, packaging): verify geometry and cost results, remove dependence on inaccessible adjacent notes, sharpen literature comparison and significance, build standalone deliverables.
5. Whole-manuscript review: five independent complete reviews, adjudication, separate-agent corrections, repeat after any accepted major issue.

Each stage is completed before the next begins. All valid minor findings are corrected; major findings trigger a fresh five-reviewer round after correction. Reports record actual evidence and limitations. Open research directions may remain only when they are outside the proved claims; gaps within claimed results must be resolved or the claims corrected.
