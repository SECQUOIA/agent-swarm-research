\caption{Physical arcs in reciprocal emission and quality conversion.
A star marks a node whose upper capacity $b$ is attained at the profit
threshold. Here $\lambda$ is an input quality, $q$ a pool quality, and
$\mu$ a product quality specification; $[0,1]$ is vacuous.
Arrow labels give the flows at saturation. Dashed arrows are physical
input--pool arcs whose other endpoint is supplied by the calling gadget;
they carry the logical continuation. No arrow joins two pools.
The storage pool $Q$ has quality-one intake $a$; its feed and diluent
arcs, slack outlet, and other uses are omitted. The receiving pools'
other arcs and markings are determined by the caller.}
