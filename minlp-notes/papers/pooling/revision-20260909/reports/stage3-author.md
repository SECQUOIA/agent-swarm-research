## Complete proof audit

### Structural section

**Fixed core (`s4:fixed-core`).** A vertex of a bounded polyhedron has ambiently spanning active normals even when the polyhedron is lower-dimensional. Thus enumeration of fixed-size bases covers nonempty blocks, with explicit determinant nonzero tests. Candidate validity and score order are constant on sign conditions; connectedness is unnecessary. Every support direction has exactly the relevant retained condition, and an empty block makes the universal formula false. Squared products of denominators preserve support-inequality signs. Support membership in the compact Minkowski sum is equivalent to aggregate and objective attainability. The degree after multiplying denominators grows linearly with the number of blocks; dense size remains polynomial because the variable count is fixed. Optimality uses only a fixed number of duplicated variables. The compact original feasible set supplies attainment. A common algebraic field of polynomial degree permits polynomial-bit LP recovery; bounded boxes provide vertices/full column rank. Slack bounds are computable from compact coordinate projections and root bounds. I also checked the local-binary subset-sum obstruction and the multiquadratic degree obstruction in `s4:core-limits`; neither is misstated as an approximation theorem.

**Pooling corollaries (`s4:fixed-inputs`, `s4:fixed-qualities`).** Inlet fractions give correct positive-flow mixtures and harmless inactive mixtures. Product blocks, feed bounds, source withdrawals, pool bounds, and all actual arc costs have the claimed ownership and dimensions. Arbitrarily many attributes add local rows. In the fixed-quality no-bypass model there are only `p(K+1)` linking equations. A fixed number of bypasses may enter the core; unbounded bypasses are not covered by that corollary alone. Pools missing an inlet or outlet are removed only after checking their bounds, while adjacent external contracts remain.

**Vertex integrity (`s4:vertex-integrity`, `s4:structural-corollaries`).** Rational affine compression preserves all quality equalities; product specification vectors need not lie in the input affine hull. Every actual flow belongs to exactly one core or component block. Covered-product delivery and coordinate-mass variables are necessary to keep aggregate dimension independent of the ambient attribute count. Their bounds and equations give the stated core/block/aggregate dimension bounds. The finite subset search is polynomial for each fixed integrity bound. The rank-zero and no-pool LP cases and each listed specialization were checked.

**Planar composition and path lifting (`s4:polygon-composition`, `s4:path-projection`).** Clipping to the shared-coordinate interval is necessary. Convex/concave slice envelopes and the clamped minimum formula give additive piece-count growth; level sets have at most two boundary points, including plateaus. The stated constant 64 safely exceeds the displayed envelope count and degenerate descriptions. One-step Fourier–Motzkin candidates include same-child pairs. Rational intersections and sequential redundancy deletion give polynomial arithmetic and per-composition bit bounds. Balanced depth makes both recurrences polynomial. Each interval lift is a rational affine combination of parent endpoints; exact comparisons introduce no new algebraic field. Points, segments, empty sets, and zero-length slices are included.

**Attachment and objective theorems (`s4:bounded-attachments`, `s4:two-two-feasibility`, `s4:fixed-support`, `s4:equality-support`).** At most `4a` core variables suffice, including inlet fractions and all bypasses incident to attachments. All remaining local rows are rational and independent of the mixture. Every boundary-connected component is a path with at most two boundary arcs; detached cycles are LPs. A cut cycle whose boundary arcs meet the same attachment is still valid. Retaining an objective coordinate cuts at an arc flow, preserving adjacent node constraints; a singly cut cycle becomes `R(x,x)`. Closed extra constraints preserve compactness. Dense costs are handled only through the supplied exact equality identity or bounded support. The revenue-change example counts changes along paths, not the number of distinct revenue values.

**Hardness comparison (`s4:degree-boundary`).** I reread the current Section 3 construction through `s3:physical-threshold`, the subsequent source splitting, and `s3:completion`. Before completion, the source threshold is a physical circuit, exact contracts remain, the two actual feeds/outlets are unchanged, and all advertised alphabets and input/output degrees hold. Thus the feasibility conclusion is valid and NP membership follows from the accepted scalar one-pool certificate. Zero-lower-bound feasibility is correctly distinguished from threshold decision.

### Contract section

**General parameter paths (`s5:quasipoly-path`).** The minor signs determine candidate intersection feasibility and validity of an omitted row at every potential vertex. This holds for any retained subset because its minors are among those of the full list. Lower-dimensional cases admit bounded-size *subsets*, preserving symbolic coefficient bounds. Open-cell refinements combine by adding breakpoints in one parameter; multiplying partitions would give an unnecessary larger bound. Balanced depth yields the claimed quasipolynomial number of cells and polynomial coefficient degree/bit length per row. Singletons are evaluated in the field of the one parameter. Feasible interval samples are rational with polynomial bit length; feasible boundary parameters and original-LP witnesses have a common field of polynomial degree. No dense objective projection is claimed.

**Strips (`s5:strip`).** The normalization has correctly ordered intervals for either product sign. Nonnegative two-edge excursion lengths make the unique simple path shortest. The all-pairs bound is necessary and sufficient via the minimum of shifted upper bounds. Retained states become exact intervals. Zero gains remove the predecessor relation and retain the head interval even when the original edge points toward the chosen tree root. Products/reciprocals of gains have polynomial dense encoding in fixed parameter dimension. Removing a fixed number of nonforest edges and retaining their endpoints preserves every original strip constraint. Infimum, nonattainment, and unbounded parameters are not conflated with compact optimization.

**Fixed outlets (`s5:fixed-products`).** Exact source and nonreceiving product mass/quality contracts give the displayed boundary identities for every local bypass lift, including detached components that feed the pool. Fractions allocate both total mass and every attribute mass correctly. Nonnegative recovered feeds imply `Q=0` at zero throughput. Receiving-product intervals and all common/individual bounds remain. Standard economics is affine on the retained core, whereas arbitrary feed or bypass costs can remain dense. The counterexample to substituting an upper specification for an exact quality contract was checked directly.

**Conservation and signed cuts (`s5:conservation`, `s5:cuts`).** Summing exact product equations plus the global constants recovers both physical pool balances, but does not derive common capacity. The root-arc circulation reduction has the correct outgoing-minus-incoming signs. Connected induced subsets suffice by additivity, including whole components and isolated vertices. Prescribed boundary arcs shift node intervals with their signed divergence contributions and retain their own bounds.

**Quadratic field (`s5:quadratic-field`).** Singular qualities include every source quality in the feed interval, even qualities of bypass-only inputs. Fixed-quality branches are original physical LPs. Nonsingular scaling retains signed original orientation. Two distinct local inlet qualities turn an outlet interval into the stated quadratic interval; equal qualities, one inlet, no inlet, missing outlets, and zero demands require the separate displayed conditions. Each cut crosses at most two edges, so all lower/upper candidate combinations are polynomial. Feasible univariate quadratic sets have a rational interval witness or a quadratic boundary witness. Incidence recovery and division preserve that common field. Affine-rank-one reduction checks positive-demand vector compatibility and ignores homogeneous zero-demand qualities. The restrictive-capacity example has `T=1/[q(2-q)]>=1` and was verified.

**Exceptions (`s5:quality-chart`, `s5:fixed-rank`, `s5:arbitrary-exceptions`).** Retained external masses plus global equations derive actual pool balances for every internal lift. The moment-curve direction list excludes at most `|I|(K-1)` bad choices when no full source vector equals `q`; full-vector singularities use original LPs. Residual coordinate equations are exact, with affine `a_h` and quadratic `f_h`; zero coefficients retain `f_h=0`. Per-cut clearing uses at most two affine denominators, giving the stated safe degree-four bound without multiplying all graph denominators. All chart signs, including equality cells, are retained and actual charts are never closed across singularities. Compactness belongs to the union before chart partition, so the maximum is attained. An active ordinary outlet restricts `q` to an affine space of dimension at most two. If no ordinary outlet is active, the exceptional-only conserved-mass/fraction construction is exact and independent of quality rank. The common-bound and standard-economics restrictions are material. Section 3's five-exception hardness permits three bypass inlets at ordinary collectors; it does not contradict bypass maximum degree two here.

**Symbolic throughput (`s5:clamp`, `s5:box-base`, `s5:restrictive-capacity`, `s5:throughput-optimization`).** The binary recurrence and shifted clamp endpoints were rederived. The fixed polynomial list determines all selections and final labels, including ties; fixed first labels reduce cycles to two path problems. The box rank function is jointly submodular before partial minimization. Nonemptiness gives `g(empty)=g(V)=0`, both directions of `D=B(g)` hold, and the greedy proof works for signed/tied costs. For each quality chart the order of reciprocal source-quality costs is fixed. Prefix ranks reduce to binary submodular path/cycle energies. Scalar breakpoint unions across all prefixes/components remain polynomial. A single product of distinct quality factors clears the support denominators with known sign and polynomial degree/bit length. The full attainable bypass totals form a compact interval. Its intersection with the common-capacity interval is sufficient by interpolating two attaining flows in the same field. The two-variable throughput projection plus singular LP intervals is exact, with attained extrema. No arbitrary dense arc-cost result is inferred.

**Two vectors (`s5:two-vector-theorem`, `s5:two-vector-extensions`).** Full-vector affine reduction, exact class totals under variable withdrawals, signed source/feed/bypass intervals, and the outlet identity were rederived. Positive feed and bypass lower bounds remain affine. The common upper bound has the same concave quadratic factor as outlet upper bounds.
