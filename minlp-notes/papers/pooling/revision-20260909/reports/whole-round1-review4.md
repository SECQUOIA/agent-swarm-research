## Mathematical and arithmetic issues examined closely

These are conclusions from the review, not requests for new proofs.

1. **The algebraic hardness is a statement about physical arc flows.** In Section 2 the forced source and product totals, storage diluent, and slack formulas determine all arcs from a source solution. Inactive relay qualities do not invalidate the stated flow bijection. The bounded-data refinement preserves the designated variable feed needed for the field-generation conclusion. The one-pool construction uses separate attribute pins rather than covertly adding mixing feeds. Lower quality specifications are converted to an additional complementary attribute only where that increased attribute count is acknowledged.

2. **The fixed-dimensional NP certificate does not imply a polynomial search algorithm.** `s2:fiber-np` enumerates no bases in its verifier: the basis is guessed, determinant/Cramer polynomials have polynomial dense size in the fixed parameter dimension, and their verifier has a polynomial bit bound. The distinction from a rational physical-flow certificate is preserved throughout the later hardness results.

3. **The fixed-core optimization theorem addresses the principal output-size difficulty.** The support comparisons live in fixed dimension even though there are many blocks. Cleared denominator products have growing degree but polynomial dense size. Sampling the core and value in one field, then solving a linear system over that field, avoids the independent-square-root obstruction illustrated in `s4:core-limits`. I checked the cited algebraic LP source for this rational-machine qualification rather than treating real-arithmetic polynomiality as sufficient.

4. **The path and contract algorithms retain the information their objectives require.** A two-endpoint feasibility relation does not retain a dense interior cost. Sections 4 and 5 repeatedly restrict objective scope for that reason. In the fixed-outlet and exceptional-only branches, exact contracts make the otherwise dense pool mass and attribute mass functions of retained boundary variables. In the restrictive-capacity branch, the total unscaled bypass flow is recovered by support optimization. These are distinct valid arguments, not interchangeable assumptions.

5. **The two-vector convex-QP reduction handles exact feasibility rather than only closure.** The affine transformed polytope alone can have spurious points at quality zero or one. Those values are handled by the original physical LPs. For interior quality, the negative-minimum interpolation argument and the zero-minimum uniqueness-of-quality argument provide rational interior witnesses where required. The sign of the common quadratic coefficient is exactly why positive outlet or common lower bounds remain outside this theorem.

6. **The isolated margin set has different threshold and optimizer arithmetic.** In `app:margin-certificates`, fixing suitable margin patterns makes the rational threshold a univariate quadratic inequality. A rational endpoint or stationary point supplies a rational witness, even when minimizing the original reciprocal objective chooses an irrational square root. This distinction is valid and scientifically useful.

7. **The conic claims do not overstate their quantitative strength.** Exact fixed-order PSD and SOC lower bounds are exponential in the correlation dimension; unrestricted exact PSD order uses the weaker stated exponent. Approximate SDP order has the logarithmic loss from the supplied quantitative argument. The text does not convert the last bound into an exponential-in-dimension approximate SOC lower bound.

Section 1 currently includes substantial sign, conic, cyclic, and facial developments before the central ETR theorem appears on page 15. **O2 — Optional: distinguish the general path tools from the contract algorithms at their entry point.** Locator: `sections/05-contract-algorithms.tex:100–315` (`s5:quasipoly-path`, `s5:strip`). The text accurately says the first result is quasipolynomial and does not supply the later polynomial physical classifications. **O3 — Optional: add a compact dependency map for the restricted-hardness refinements.** Locators: `sections/03-restricted-hardness.tex:665–1427`, especially `s3:base-bypass`, `s3:half`, `s3:cycles`, `s3:upper-only`, `s3:linear-circuits`, `s3:constant-thm`, and `s3:five-thm`. I would trim wording only where the same warning is repeated without a new nearby inference, and would preserve the objective, contract, rank, and output-field qualifications.

## Source-access update: Xiang et al. (9 September 2026)

After completing the review, I independently inspected the raw original PDF supplied at `/tmp/pooling-xiang2026/paper.pdf` and its raw `metadata.json`. I generated a fresh extraction at `checks/whole-review4/xiang2026.txt`; I did not use another review or an existing interpretation of the source. This update supersedes the earlier statement that I could not retrieve and check the paper. All unrelated findings and the overall verdict remain unchanged.

**Bibliographic verification.** The PDF has 12 pages and identifies itself as part of the refereed proceedings of the 2026 INFORMS Optimization Society Conference. Its title, seven authors, and DOI match the manuscript's bibliography. The raw record identifies record `19984634`, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.19984634`, publication date 20 March 2026, and record creation on 2 May 2026. The supplied PDF is 293,149 bytes; its independently computed MD5, `b19bcb6c38fd643a76e3f8b7e55278b6`, agrees with the metadata's file checksum. The bibliography's year, proceedings title, DOI, and pages 1–12 are supported.

**Exact result locators and assumptions.** Section 2, printed pp. 2–3, expressly permits source–pool, source–terminal, pool–pool, and pool–terminal arcs. Its objective is linear in arc flows, its flow restrictions are nonnegativity and upper arc/node capacities, and it permits lower and upper terminal quality bounds. Section 3.1, printed p. 5, explains that the restriction to a single terminal is an LP: equation (4g) identifies its terminal-indexed and actual flow variables, making (4f) redundant. Remark 1 on that page states that the full optimum is no greater than a single-terminal optimum, which is no greater than zero. Proposition 4 and Theorem 2 are both on printed p. 8. Proposition 4 gives the sum-of-single-terminal-optima lower bound. Theorem 2 then explicitly tests zero optimal value by solving one LP for every terminal and distinguishing whether any of their optimal values is negative. These are the exact result numbers cited in the pooling manuscript, and they support its treatment of polynomial zero-optimum detection as established work.

**Cyclic-model scope.** The source includes pool–pool arcs and imposes no explicit acyclicity restriction in the inspected model or zero-test theorem. This supports the manuscript's phrase “including pool–pool arcs.” It should not, however, be read as an independent proof of equivalence with every algebraic steady-state circulation admitted by Section 1 of the pooling manuscript. Xiang et al. index terminal proportions by the terminals reachable from each pool. In their original TP formulation, equation (1g) requires those proportions to sum to one; an empty reachable-terminal set needs separate treatment. In their projected formulation, equation (3) assigns zero incoming actual flow when that set is empty. Consequently the paper's displayed terminal-indexed formulation does not represent a positive isolated circulation in a component with no reachable terminal. An isolated circulation in the positive-flow support may still have a reachable terminal in the original graph, so these two situations must not be conflated.

The pooling manuscript already makes precisely the material distinction: it credits the existing generalized terminal-indexed zero test and separately proves the physical reconstruction, isolated-circulation, and conic-hull statements under its stated algebraic semantics. The new source inspection removes my access and locator limitation; it does not reveal an attribution error or require a change to the review verdict.
