## Mathematical examination

Every proof and additional assertion in Section 3 was read and checked against the physical model and Section 2 certificate scopes.

1. **Orientation.** Cyclic implications force equal variable copies and keep each literal's occurrence count at most two. Private weight-two triangles are saturated in every feasible orientation, so their unit clause edges point toward clauses. Literal and clause degrees are at most three. Subdivision preserves feasibility in both directions, including the case when neither half-edge enters the subdivision vertex, and puts all degree-three vertices on one side. The source has linear size.
2. **Local degree pooling reduction.** Mass balance gives cost a-s-t, and any positive strict flow forces dirty intake zero by nonnegative quality mass. Equality at minus the total weights forces one pure endpoint mode per pool. Input/output capacities match orientation loads. In the omitted-pool-capacity variants, equality need not force integrality, but the selected endpoint still carries at least the required weight; this suffices for the orientation. The two-node Partition specialization is ordinary rather than strong hardness. The one-inlet/one-outlet LP boundary correctly substitutes quality mass coordinate by coordinate.
3. **All degrees two.** Cleanup preserves s+d while decreasing every resource use. Once modes are fixed, throughputs form a bounded bipartite matching polytope, giving integral replacement. Twice subdividing matching E3 yields exactly the mode conflicts. Deleting an original endpoint and adding its adjacent subdivision vertex preserves cardinality and independence; E3 being a matching prevents interference. Hence alpha(H)=n/2+alpha(G). Merging lax outputs adds only the existing shared-dirty-input constraint after cleanup, and makes all four physical degrees exactly two. The approximation recovery loses only n/2 and uses alpha(G)>=n/4. NP membership is the exact endpoint disjunction for the full stated restriction class, not a general rational-flow assumption.
4. **Weighted modes.** The linked four costs give -alpha*s-beta*d. Cleanup preserves both weighted quantities even for signed rewards. Integral source/output and retained-mode capacities yield an integral weighted bipartite flow optimum. With unit capacities, each pool supplies one color with two disjoint mode edges, and at most one may be chosen. The theorem does not assert integrality for arbitrary arc objectives.
5. **Positive tolerance.** Low-quality pools lose at most 2d; high-quality pools lose at most 2s. Summed quality mass gives loss at most 2n(eta+delta/eta). Taking delta=eta^2, eta<gamma/32 and epsilon<gamma/6 leaves an approximation ratio strictly better than the source gap. Fixed data imply strong hardness of exact optimization. The K4 example satisfies every shared input/output capacity and gives 3+2delta versus the zero-tolerance integral bound 3. Shifting all qualities and upper bounds by one preserves constraints by conservation. Neither integral optima nor exact source value identities are asserted at positive tolerance.
6. **Matsui source.** With N=n+n^2 variables, active integral matrices have entries bounded by one; Cramer/Hadamard gives fractional coordinates in [h,1-h]. Terms with an index above the largest fractional index cancel, while the highest diagonal contributes at least p^(2k)h/2. The remaining signed terms yield D>=p^(2k-1)(ph/2-n^2)>p for p=n^(n^4), n>=5. Concavity extends this to all points when no binary source solution exists. The product identity, positive factor bounds, yes-case strict inequality, and O(n^5 log n) coefficient encoding hold. The matrix row count is unrestricted and remains part of the source size. The displayed n=5,p=2 counterexample to the report's arbitrary-p estimate is valid; the manuscript does not rely on that estimate.
7. **Two-pool tree.** Rational LP normalization makes U>=2 without changing UV; cutting V<=K retains every yes witness. The simplex embedding includes the individual coordinate upper bounds. A positive pool outlet imposes each polytope row; the anchor cancels in those rows. The distinguished attribute and output capacities imply T<=1+1/a, attained at y1=1/a,y2=1,h=1-1/a. The value-to-product equivalence is correct for K>0. Three actual outlet fractions (at most four in the full class) give the Section 2 certificate despite unbounded attributes. Suppressing the single-input/single-output anchor pool creates exactly one bypass. Normalized qualities, standard nonnegative economics, redundant source/pool bounds, and exact unit demands all preserve the asserted value; no strong-hardness consequence is drawn from these numerical normalizations.
8. **Represented-family FPTAS.** The floor grid in reciprocal a keeps an optimizer feasible in one of N+1 LPs. Its optimizer cannot decrease b and has reciprocal a at least that grid value. The loss is at most OPT/N, including zero OPT. Bounded rational LPs have rational optimal vertices of polynomial encoding length. The result requires the supplied polytope/affine representation, and claims neither recognition nor a general two-pool FPTAS.
9. **Copy/row gadgets.** Subtracting midpoint times demand forces equal endpoint flows; exact middle supply makes the other pair complementary. Exact-two zero links copy the same bounded signal, and the conversion complement forces the actual intake. Distinct occurrences avoid repeated physical arcs. A row's grouped source total is c*x+B, so its upper supply is precisely c*x<=0. Homogenized Matsui cone rows have polynomially bounded integer coefficients. Generator U_i>2 and V_i<K ensure every a_i>1,b_i>0 even at the unrestricted simplex generators. The all-zero original signals fill all copy contracts even for an empty source polytope. Scalar exact qualities convert to two upper attributes by negation and normalization.
10. **Full/half bounded-degree networks.** Full averaging uses four ports; half averaging uses one full port and two complementary half ports. Full and half zero-port formulas coincide. Averaging-tree roots enforce the original sum bounds with no extra restriction. Separate closed full and half cycles force all normalized upper-quality inequalities tight by aggregate equality; unlike mixed cycles, their aggregate coefficients are uniform. Full/half coupling uses separate occurrences and enforces signal equality. Complement-flow source splitting preserves exactly the old total equation and achieves input degree two, output degree three, and the stated capacities.
11. **Explicit error bound and penalty.** Projection onto the nonempty polyhedron has a conic representation using independent active normals. The singular-value product is at least one because det(B B^T) is a positive integer, giving the stated coarse H. Clearing only quality/upper rows leaves every positive contract residual bounded by the total deficit. The exact copy polytope remains nonempty at zero original intakes. The primary subsystem is feasible iff F(x)=S(T-1)-T<=0 on x>=0,T<=2, including zero throughput. Its gradient is bounded by 3a_max+1. Radial scaling of the projected point loses at most L H delta, preserves every homogeneous row, and extends to the exact copies. M0=C+1 strictly dominates lost base profit. The separate private-input objective argument controls its relaxed discrepancy rather than assuming copied flows remain equal. All penalty coefficients have polynomial bit length; standard economic offsets cancel globally. The proof preserves exact optimum up to an additive constant, not a fixed approximation ratio.
12. **Constant-data circuits.** Binary bits are processed least significant first, yielding t_L=c*x/2^L. Scaling a signed integer row by B>sum|c_i|+|d| keeps both totals and every partial sum below two. The affine constant comes from a unit signal. Separate source splitting for average/coupling and addition gives collector demands two and four; quality and capacity alphabets are fixed. This is an LP representation theorem, not LP hardness.
13. **Two actual feeds and completion.** Power-of-two D0 bounds put a_i in [1,19), b_i positive integral and rho_i dyadic in [0,1). Signals T,h,l and the mass identity l+33h=sum a_i*x_i implement the original mixture with precisely two pool feed arcs. The threshold is encoded as a physical affine circuit and forces positive throughput. The forward radial witness and reverse throughput bound give exactly the original product decision. After splitting all allocated gates, division by 33 gives the seven-value alphabet. Summing upper-bounded contract totals reaches S0 iff each is saturated; arc reward is at most two and the economics become input costs {0,1}, output revenues {1,2}. Only S0 grows, linearly in the network size. Strong NP-completeness refers to exact threshold attainment and does not establish an inverse-polynomial completion gap.
14. **Five exceptions.** Every comparison becomes an exact slack addition with slack in [0,2]. Each unused quality-three port is grouped with mates of a single exact gate; total complementary supply is automatically sum capacities minus the original supply. Ordinary zero ports already belong to closed cycles. Splitting original and complementary three-port sources gives only exact inputs and exact collectors; the latter have demand two or four. Exact cycle qualities impose no new restriction because aggregate saturation already forced equality. Only the two private positive conversion fillers and anchor lack exact input supplies; only the two primary outputs lack exact product contracts. Reserved original-coordinate output ports are omitted. Thus exactly the stated five possible exceptions remain, degrees and data alphabets persist, the pool bound is redundant, and the product threshold is wholly inside the physical network. The fixed scalar parameter gives NP membership.
