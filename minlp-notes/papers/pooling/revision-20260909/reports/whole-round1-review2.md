**Overall assessment.** The paper's strongest contribution is the pair of physical existential-real completeness reductions. They change the general exact-decision classification, rather than merely adding an NP-hard restriction. The fixed-parameter certificate theorem then explains why several restricted classes can still be NP-complete. The degree-two/degree-three feasibility boundary, the simultaneous four-degree-two hardness construction, and the exact structural and contract algorithms give this classification considerable depth. The rank-one margin results are also substantial: a precise old hardness conjecture is answered, and the same set supports unexpectedly simple threshold certificates and exact optimizers. The correlation-face and approximation transfers are meaningful uniform-representation results.

This is not simply a collection of observations imported from other papers. The reusable ingredients are mostly established, but the physical realizations, the uniform elimination arguments, and several quantitative transfers require the new arguments supplied here. The manuscript generally identifies that division of credit correctly. No experimental section is necessary to support these theoretical claims, and it appropriately avoids implying that the algebraic algorithms have useful practical running times.
