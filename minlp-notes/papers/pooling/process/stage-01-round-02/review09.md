## Main checks

1. **Facial recognition (`s1:face-alg`).** The forbidden-input LP is an exact characterization, including empty intersections and repeated or nonvertex input qualities. If its optimum is zero, an interior segment point in the permitted intersection cannot have an endpoint mixture using a forbidden input: combining that endpoint representation with the other endpoint would produce positive forbidden weight. Both endpoints are consequently mixtures of allowed inputs and belong to the convex product region. A positive optimum supplies precisely the forbidden mass needed by the converse integrality construction. The LP has polynomial size even when the ambient attribute count grows.

2. **Fixed-dimensional face enumeration (`s1:face-alg`, final proof paragraph).** Working in the rational affine hull is essential and is explicitly done. A facet of a full-dimensional hull in dimension `d` contains `d` affinely independent input points; exact supporting-side tests therefore enumerate all facets. For any nonempty proper face, a basis of its active facet normals determines all its active equalities because their offsets agree at a point of the face. Intersecting those at most `d` hyperplanes with the hull recovers the face. The loose bound `1 + |I|^(d^2)` is sufficient. Rational elimination and determinant bounds give polynomial coefficient lengths for fixed `d`; testing the finitely many generating input points suffices for both membership and containment. The explicit `d = 0` case avoids an invalid facet construction for a singleton. Fixed `p,d` yields polynomially many tuples; it does not imply a uniform fixed-parameter exponent, as the encoding subsection correctly warns.

3. **Exactness of face states and lower bounds (`s1:facial`, `s1:face-alg`).** The smallest face containing an active pool quality contains every positive feed and is contained in every receiving product face. This establishes coverage by the enumerated branches, while convexity establishes sufficiency. Inactive pools can choose any nonempty face because all their incident flows vanish. The manuscript explicitly rejects a branch if it omits an arc with positive lower bound; surviving node contracts remain in the network problem. Thus deletion does not silently remove mandatory flow. Node splitting and the bounded return arc give a valid network representation with integer data when the original flow bounds are integer.

4. **Integrality converse (`s1:facial`).** Nonfaciality gives a permitted mixture with positive forbidden-input weight. Unit input, arc, pool, and product capacities admit this mixture, while every integral feasible nonzero flow selects a single permitted input and has cost zero. The negative fractional objective is therefore a strict obstruction to an integral optimum. Rationality follows from the rational single-product LP. The stated quantifier is universal over networks and objectives, and the manuscript correctly avoids turning this example into a hardness claim or a claim about every particular network.

5. **Encoding, affine rank, and exact certificates (`s1:encoding`).** The affine reduction preserves all original product rows. Column independence justifies the reduced mixing equations; unused pools can be assigned bounded coordinates. Rank zero reduces to the common-quality linear case. The initial compactness argument is restricted to standard and acyclic networks, and the later cyclic compactness argument separately handles isolated circulation qualities. The manuscript does not infer general NP membership from rational inputs or from an existential-real formulation.

6. **Destination decomposition, sign tests, and witnesses (`s1:destination` through `s1:paths`).** Using the arc head in the destination multiplier preserves each pool's incoming proportions and gives conservation via the backward recursion. The selected product retains its original inflow, so its quality remains valid. Zero lower bounds make coordinatewise domination sufficient for all capacity restrictions. Single-product reconstruction and cancellation of internal quality masses justify the LP projection. The approximation inequalities have the correct direction for minimization with nonpositive optimum. The support argument uses at most `K` independent active quality rows plus normalization. Shortest-path costs, an optimal rational mixture, common capacity scaling, and reconstructed qualities all have polynomial encoding. In particular, reconstructing qualities as one rational triangular system gives a valid determinant bound without relying on a potentially misleading recursive fraction-size estimate.

7. **Conic hull and recirculation (`s1:cone-theorem`, `s1:cyclic-single`, `s1:cyclic-sign`).** The scaling proof distinguishes the conic hull from a closed cone and then proves polyhedral closedness through an extended formulation. Positive-capacity support is required for scaling back into the capacitated set. In the cyclic proof, a terminal pool component without product outflow has zero external inflow and is isolated. Every remaining active pool reaches a product, giving absorption probability one. The backward component matrix has a deficient row exactly when boundary inflow exists, and irreducibility supplies the nonsingularity argument. Isolated circulations are assigned constant quality rather than being subjected to that argument. Cycle removal preserves the single-product aggregate quality constraints. Empty input/product sets and merging circulation flow into one component are handled consistently.

8. **Other scope and endpoint claims (`s1:model`, `s1:rank-one`, `s1:endpoint-or`).** Homogeneous specifications impose no composition requirement at zero delivery. Physical feed/outlet costs produce additive matrix coefficients, so the rank-one discussion does not overstate reductions from arbitrary matrix objectives. The endpoint class explicitly excludes additional nonredundant quality restrictions. Its disjunctions and throughput-based binary formulation are exact; rational branch vertices and, with integer bounds, integral optimal flows give polynomial-size threshold certificates. Pool integrality does not imply input purity at throughput greater than one.
