## Checks supporting the verdict

1. **Model and encoding conventions, lines 11–159.** The arc restrictions keep inputs as sources and products as sinks. The homogeneous specification convention consistently makes zero delivery unconstrained in composition. Finite effective arc bounds justify boundedness; restricting inactive qualities to the input box preserves the flow projection. The acyclic convex-combination argument supplies the remaining quality bounds and attained optima. The affine-coordinate substitution retains the original product inequalities and their potentially growing number. Its equivalence is an equivalence of physical flow feasibility after assigning unused pool qualities, rather than a claim that arbitrary inactive quality coordinates must originally lie in the affine hull. The text explicitly supplies that reassignment. The general existential-real upper bound is not used as an NP claim.

2. **Rank-one correspondence and costs, lines 163–187.** For a nonzero nonnegative rank-one matrix, multiplying its margins and dividing by its total reproduces every entry. The zero-total case is separated. Feed and outlet arc costs give exactly additive matrix coefficients, so the warning against importing arbitrary-matrix-cost hardness into physical pooling has the correct direction.

3. **Destination decomposition and single-product projection, lines 191–278.** The multiplier must be evaluated at the arc head, as written. This both preserves incoming mixture proportions and gives outgoing conservation by the recursion. At the selected product its entire original inflow is preserved. Coordinatewise domination preserves node and arc upper capacities precisely because this subsection excludes positive lower bounds. Aggregate quality cancellation includes bypasses and internal pool arcs. Conversely, topological reconstruction makes any single-product LP flow physical. Acyclicity also handles zero delivery and an empty product set. The quality reconstruction is one rational triangular linear system with polynomial-size coefficients, so a determinant bound controls the encoding length despite repeated averaging.

4. **Sign, approximation, and destination relaxation, lines 259–340.** Both chains of cost inequalities have the correct direction for minimization with nonpositive optimum. Selecting a destination component supplies a physical feasible solution; it does not assert physicality of their arbitrary sum. The shortest-path test uses source proportions only after establishing positive single-product delivery. Removing zero capacities and scaling by the minimum positive capacity/load ratio handles all supplied node and arc capacities. At a mixture vertex the independent active quality rows contribute at most K to the supported system, even though there can be both lower and upper bounds on every coordinate. Together with normalization this proves the K+1 support bound. Polynomial rational witnesses follow from LP vertex bounds, polynomial-length paths, rational scaling, and reconstruction.

5. **Uncapacitated hull, lines 343–389.** The cone is defined as finite conic combinations. Scaling closure and the inclusion of zero correctly imply that the convex hull is already a cone, without presupposing closedness. Destination decomposition proves one inclusion into the sum of single-product cones, and physical reconstruction proves the reverse inclusion into the conic hull. Closedness is established subsequently by the polyhedral extended formulation. The equality with cone(P) uses positive-capacity support and zero lower bounds; it is not generalized to arbitrary contracts. In the capacity counterexample, any positive outlet requires the pool to have that outlet's exact quality, so both outlets cannot be positive. The two-unit conic-hull point nevertheless respects the separate capacities as asserted.

6. **Cyclic reconstruction and inherited corollaries, lines 393–526.** A source-free component of the positive pool support with no external inflow has no external outflow by summed conservation. Every remaining strongly connected component has a deficient row in its backward substochastic system. Strong connectivity makes every row transient, giving an invertible system and boundary-weighted qualities. The determinant argument applies to the combined nonsingular rational system. In the forward decomposition, a terminal pool component with no product outlet has zero external inflow and hence is isolated; no other positive-flow component can feed it. Thus removing these components leaves every active pool able to reach a product, which justifies absorption probabilities summing to one. Isolated circulations are physical under the expressly chosen algebraic semantics. They can be merged into one designated destination component because their active pool supports are separate. This supports the cyclic approximation and relaxation inequalities as well as the sign and hull statements. With no negative cycles, removing cycle components preserves aggregate source withdrawals and output deliveries while not increasing cost. With a negative cycle, common scaling gives a feasible circulation directly. The bounded-lift argument for attained cyclic optima is valid, including no-input instances, because isolated components can use zero quality. I also checked the displayed two-pool matrix, determinant, and residual.

7. **Facial specifications and the converse, lines 530–601.** Applying the face property twice gives exactly the stated feed–outlet incompatibilities, including arbitrary bypasses. Every compatible allowed support leaves only a bounded network flow system; node splitting and the common return arc preserve the network structure. Integral bounds give integral vertices through total unimodularity, including the projected physical arc coordinates. For a nonfacial nonempty product region, the segment witness necessarily gives positive weight to at least one forbidden input. The constructed unit-throughput blend has negative cost, whereas any integral positive-throughput flow must select one allowed input and has zero cost. The rational LP observation supplies a rational strict improvement without assuming that the initially chosen segment witness is rational. The converse's quantifier is appropriately over networks and objectives, and no hardness claim is inferred from fractionality.

8. **Recognition, face enumeration, and endpoint certificates, lines 603–685.** The forbidden-input LP has optimum zero exactly when the intersection is a face: an interior segment point in the intersection would otherwise expose a positive forbidden contribution in an endpoint expansion. Fixed affine dimension suffices to enumerate supporting facets from affinely independent input subsets. A basis of the active facet normals determines all active equalities at a nonempty face, justifying enumeration using at most d facets. Face membership and containment reduce to input generators. The smallest face containing an active pool's quality retains its positive feeds and outlets, proving completeness of the pool-face enumeration. The explicit check for positive lower bounds on omitted arcs is essential and present. The endpoint disjunction follows from nonnegative quality mass, and the finite throughput bound is valid for both big-M rows. The integer and rational certificate claims follow from an optimal branch vertex, with polynomial encoding; neither claim is extended to arbitrary product specifications.
