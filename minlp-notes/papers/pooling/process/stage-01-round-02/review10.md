## Mathematical checks

- **Model and encoding, lines 9–160.** The treatment of zero delivery and zero throughput agrees with homogeneous quality constraints. The capacitated-model convention supplies bounds on every arc, so the compact-lift argument is valid for standard and acyclic generalized networks. Restricting inactive qualities to the input box preserves the flow projection. The empty-input exception is handled. Affine-rank reduction preserves every original product row; full column rank of the coordinate matrix gives the stated equivalence. The general existential-real upper bound is not incorrectly promoted to general NP membership.

- **Rank-one block and objectives, lines 162–187.** For positive throughput, the proposed matrix has the stated margins and delivered quality masses. The converse factorization works for every nonzero nonnegative rank-one matrix; zero throughput is separately included. I checked the objective identity directly: `sum_i a_i y_i + sum_j b_j v_j = sum_ij (a_i+b_j) W_ij`. Consequently the restriction to additive matrix costs is necessary, and the manuscript correctly declines to transfer arbitrary-matrix objective hardness to this physical cost class. Missing feed or outlet arcs are accounted for through zero margins. The argument does not claim that arbitrary matrix side constraints describe a physical pool.

- **Destination decomposition, single-product projection, and sign tests, lines 189–347.** The use of the arc head in the component multiplier is correct: it scales all incoming quality masses at a pool equally, while the recursion restores outgoing conservation. Product quality masses at the designated destination are unchanged. Coordinatewise domination preserves the stated arc and node upper capacities, with zero lower bounds essential and explicitly imposed. Aggregate quality conservation plus topological reconstruction proves both directions of the single-product LP projection. The cost inequalities have the correct direction for minimization with nonpositive optima. Shortest-path mixtures preserve source proportions, and positive common scaling respects all retained capacity rows. An optimal mixture vertex has support at most `K+1`; determinant bounds and the explicit scale give polynomial rational encoding.

- **Conic hull, lines 349–395.** Nonnegative scaling of the uncapacitated physical set justifies equality of its convex and conic hulls. Destination decomposition and the physical realizability of each single-product cone establish both containments with their Minkowski sum. The positive-capacity support is needed for the scaling back into the capacitated set and is retained in the hypotheses. The extended formulation proves polyhedrality and closedness without assuming a conic hull is closed by definition. The two-pure-product example correctly separates intersecting the uncapacitated convex hull with capacities from the capacitated convex hull.

- **Cyclic extension, lines 397–520.** I checked the SCC reconstruction, including isolated positive circulation, deficient-row invertibility, and rational bit complexity. A component with no external inflow has no external outflow, so isolated circulations can receive a constant quality without changing any delivered quality. Removing terminal pool components without product exits leaves an absorbing forward chain. Its destination components preserve mixing by the same incoming-mass calculation. The negative-cycle alternative is necessary under the stated algebraic semantics. Without negative cycles, deleting nonnegative-cost cycles from a profitable single-product component gives a profitable path mixture. The compact-lift argument can include zero together with the input-quality box, covering the empty-input case. The residual example has determinant and residual exactly as stated.

- **Facial specifications, lines 522–650.** The face property applies first to contributing pool qualities and then to their positively weighted input qualities. This gives the union of compatible network supports; node splitting and bounded circulation preserve integrality. The converse construction has a negative feasible mixture while every integral flow uses at most one unit from one acceptable input and has zero cost. Recognition by forbidden-input weight is equivalent to faciality, including the empty face. For fixed affine dimension, facet enumeration and intersections of a basis of active facet hyperplanes give polynomially many face descriptions. Selecting the minimal face of each active pool proves completeness of the tuple enumeration. The explicit check for positive lower bounds on omitted arcs is sufficient.

- **Endpoint formulation and certificates, lines 652–685.** The special case explicitly excludes additional nonredundant quality restrictions and inherits standard pooling, bounded rational flows, and permitted lower flow bounds. Nonnegativity of attributes makes a zero product upper bound force every positive contribution to have zero mass in that coordinate. Thus, after incompatible bypass deletion, the disjunction is equivalent to physical quality feasibility, including zero flow. Each of its two sides sets a sum of nonnegative arc flows to zero and therefore produces an ordinary network branch. There are `pK` pairs, at most `2^(pK)` branches, and one binary per pair in the stated MILP. Both sums are bounded by pool throughput, so a valid finite throughput upper bound is a valid common coefficient. Such a bound can also be derived from finite effective incident-arc bounds under the model convention. Lower bounds remain enforced in each branch, including when an incompatible arc must have zero flow. Integer bounds give an integral optimal branch flow, hence an integral threshold witness whenever the answer is yes. With rational bounds, minimizing the objective over the successful branch supplies a rational vertex witness of polynomial encoding. Checking the physical bounds, threshold, bypass compatibility, and disjunctions verifies that witness without supplying pool qualities. The NP conclusion concerns this restricted endpoint class only.
