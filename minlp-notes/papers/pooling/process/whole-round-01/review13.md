## Finding: omitted zero-lower-bound refinement of margin-block hardness

**Severity: minor, related-result coverage.**

**Locations:** frozen `sections/06-synthesis.tex`, the general-cost margin-block discussion at lines 746–756 and the following unit-margin-hull discussion beginning at line 758; `source-index.md`; `bibliography.bib`; and the canonical-result inventory in `process/coverage.md`.

The repository contains `results/rank-one-zero-lower-hardness.md`, titled “Strong hardness with only unit upper bounds on rank-one flows.” This distinct refinement is absent from the inventory, bibliography, source index, and manuscript discussion. The indexed `results/rank-one-row-column-hardness.md` explicitly points to it in its third remark. The manuscript reports strong hardness with general margin bounds and then discusses the unit-capacity hull, but never reports that strong cost hardness already holds for that zero-lower/unit-upper family.

This is a meaningful restriction, rather than a duplicate proof or an inference from the conic lower bounds. I checked the omitted note's argument directly. If `r_0,c_0` are the distinguished margins and `delta=2-r_0-c_0`, its repair produces a matrix with both distinguished margins one and entrywise distance at most `2 delta`. For total mass at least one, raising each distinguished margin while preserving the total costs `2(1-r_0)` and `2(1-c_0)` in margin distance. For total mass below one, replacing the matrix by `E_00` gives the same bound using `W_00 >= r_0+c_0-S`. Therefore a penalty `-L(r_0+c_0)` with `L>2 max|C_ij|` forces both margins to one and shifts the optimum by exactly `-2L`. Applying this to the earlier biclique instance removes its only positive margin lower bounds with polynomially bounded integer costs. The negative threshold, rather than zero-flow feasibility, carries the hardness.

**Correction:** add a short, explicitly scoped sentence to the general-cost margin-block paragraph stating that strong hardness persists with all lower margin bounds zero and all upper bounds one, using a negative threshold. Cite the separate note and add its title, path, and hash to the source index and coverage inventory. Keep the existing warning that the cost matrix is nonadditive and that this does not prove hardness of physical pooling with standard economics. A complete reproduction of this adjacent proof is unnecessary under the paper's stated coverage policy.
