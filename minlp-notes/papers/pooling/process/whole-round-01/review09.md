## Detailed check of contract exceptions

The principal focus was `sections/05-contract-algorithms.tex`, Lemma `s5:quality-chart`, Corollary `s5:fixed-rank`, and Theorem `s5:arbitrary-exceptions`.

The entries have polynomial bit length even with growing ambient dimension. Restricting the pool to an affine space of dimension at most two does not require source vectors to belong to that space. - **Residual attributes.** I independently derived `s5:residual-quality` by substituting `w_2=R_j-w_1` into each original coordinate equation and multiplying by `gamma_1 gamma_2`. The coefficient of `w_1` is affine: its quadratic terms cancel. The bracket in `f_h` is affine, making `f_h` quadratic. Keeping `f_h=0` when `a_h=0` is necessary and is expressly done. Nonzero residual coefficients give signed rational equality bounds. One-inlet and zero-inlet products retain their full vector conditions. Equal projected source qualities therefore do not discard ambient restrictions.
- **Boundary elimination.** A residual bound placed on a retained arc remains in the core. Removing exceptional nodes leaves degree-two components, and every connected cut crosses at most two internal arcs. Even polynomial candidate lists produce only polynomially many pairs. Known signs justify clearing the selected denominators; no multiplication of all residual denominators is needed. The claimed fixed degree and fixed retained dimension survive growing attribute count.
- **Actual pool balance.** Subtracting all bypass quantities from `s5:exception-balances`, together with the ordinary product equations, recovers both pool mass conservation and every coordinate of pool mixing. Arbitrary feasible internal lifts cannot change these identities. Nonnegative incident flows vanish at zero throughput, so no absent or inactive outlet silently introduces a composition requirement.
- **Strict cells and optimization.** The proof optimizes the exact union of attainable-value descriptions on actual sign cells and the singular LP branches. It does not infer an optimizer from a chart closure. The model with its supplied closed affine quality restriction and box is compact, which gives attainment for the union. - **Arbitrary quality rank.** An ordinary outlet with positive flow has positive demand, and its exact product equation puts `q` in the affine hull of its contract vector and at most two bypass source vectors. Enumerating these spaces covers every flow with such an outlet. A branch remains sound without imposing positivity of its designated outlet, because all original constraints are retained.
