## Projection and attachment proofs

- **`s4:polygon-composition`.** I checked the slice formula `g(x)=max{L(l(x)),D(u(x))}` in all three positions relative to the minimizer interval. The two compositions are convex with the stated monotonicity conventions. A convex or concave univariate piecewise-affine inner function encounters each outer knot at at most two boundary points, including a plateau; this gives the additive piece bound. Taking the maximum does not require a product bound because each convex function is the maximum of its supporting piece extensions. Reflection gives the upper boundary. The singleton-domain, singleton-intermediate-domain, segment, point, and empty cases are addressed. Fourier–Motzkin elimination supplies an exact candidate description, and redundancy deletion plus the separate lower-dimensional descriptions preserve the coefficient bound. The loose constant 64 safely dominates the explicit envelope count.
- **`s4:path-projection`.** Both row count and coefficient height satisfy additive child recurrences multiplied by a universal constant. Balanced logarithmic depth makes their growth polynomial; sequential elimination is not silently substituted. Interval-slice lifting needs only rational affine combinations of the two parent endpoints. It therefore preserves one supplied algebraic field and has polynomial coefficient length along the balanced tree.
- **`s4:bounded-attachments`, `s4:two-two-feasibility`, `s4:fixed-support`.** The counts of at most `a` pool flows, `2a` incident bypass flows, and `a` inlet fractions are valid, including shared attachments. Deleted attachment nodes leave at most two boundary arcs per remaining path; a detached cycle has no boundary. One-boundary components and repeated attachment endpoints are explicitly covered. All attachment quality rows remain in the core even with growing attribute count. At zero pool throughput the simplex fraction choice is harmless. Designated interior coordinates split relations without deleting local node constraints, and a cut cycle gives a relation evaluated at repeated retained coordinates. Compactness and common-field lifting support both feasibility and the stated objective scope.
- **`s4:equality-support` and the section's final scope paragraphs.** The supplied identity reduces the objective on the entire feasible set. The price-change example counts changes along the path, rather than distinct prices. The text does not infer arbitrary dense-cost optimization from endpoint feasibility.
- **`s5:quasipoly-path`.** I checked opposite-sign elimination, retention of zero-coefficient rows, the augmented minors used to stabilize intersection feasibility, redundancy selection on open cells, and separate treatment of singleton cells. Keeping the finite box makes vertex-based equivalence valid for each retained subset. Lower-dimensional fibers admit bounded-size row subsets, and empty fibers have a constant contradiction. Row counts grow polynomially with balanced depth, whereas scalar parameter partitions obey the larger quasipolynomial recurrence. Polynomial coefficient degrees and heights survive because degree doubles only at logarithmically many levels. Symbolic rows at singleton parameters avoid joining unrelated algebraic fields. The final fixed-parameter LP gives the claimed polynomial-size witness even though the full cell list is quasipolynomial.
- **`s5:strip`.** Equal gains on the two sides are essential and explicitly assumed. Dividing by cumulative gains produces bounded differences with the correct order for negative factors. The all-pairs inequalities are necessary, and the minimum of upper bounds plus path distances supplies a feasible lift. Zero gains are removed in their original direction and impose an interval on their head. On an arbitrarily oriented tree, reciprocal factors satisfy the original edge relations; a polynomial product clears the denominators in fixed parameter dimension. Removing a fixed number of cycle edges and retaining their endpoints leaves the claimed fixed-dimensional projection. The distinction between infimum analysis and attained optimization is maintained.
