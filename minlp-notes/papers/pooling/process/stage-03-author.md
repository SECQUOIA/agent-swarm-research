## Source verification and substantive repair

The author read `literature/AGENTS.md` before consulting local literature. Local imported papers and generated literature indexes were not modified. The appended manuscript bibliography preserves all earlier entries and uses separately verified printed author names and version notes. In particular Jesper Jansson is recorded as printed on the orientation article; Baltean-Lugojan's full surname is retained.

Matsui's open METR95-13 report, pp. 2–7, is the proof version actually used. The journal article metadata are distinguished from that report's page locators. Root checked the original PDF and found that its Theorem 2.1 arbitrary-positive-integer-p inequality is false: n=5, p=2, all r_i=1/2, diagonal s_ii=1/2, off-diagonal s_ij=0, epsilon=1/4 gives `Y-X^2=-279`, whereas the claimed bound is `-99/2`.

The manuscript does not rely on this false general claim. At a rational polytope vertex, Cramer's rule gives fractional separation `h=(n^3)^(-n^3)`. Retaining the factor `p^(2k-1)` at the largest fractional index gives `Y-X^2 >= p^(2k-1) (p h/2 - n^2) > p` for the applied choice `p=n^(n^4), n>=5`. Concavity extends this to every no-instance point. The exact identity `UV-K=(Y-p)^2+4p^(4n)(Y-X^2-p)` proves the no direction; the binary yes point has a separately bounded negative value. Positivity and polynomial bit length are also shown. Thus the needed source theorem is recovered without the defective estimate. Root's independent exact checker and source audit are separate evidence, not premises of the proof.

For the independent-set gap, the Chlebik–Chlebikova author manuscript Section 5(A), pp.25–26, explicitly constructs a proper three-edge-coloring of the cubic graphs while preserving the objective and gap. Root inspected the original diagram/text independently. Manuscript locators are not presented as the final journal's pagination. No numerical source gap constant is imported.

The final 2016 Haugland article Section 6, printed p.214, is used for historical scope, not the earlier 2014 report. Haugland–Hendrix Section4.5, printed p.607, supplies the explicit bypass question. Baltean-Lugojan–Misener Remark4.6 is acknowledged as an earlier broad hardness assertion; no claim of first general one-pool hardness is made. The author's local reading checked the quoted scopes.
