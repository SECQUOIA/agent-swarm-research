## Finding 1 — endpoint formulation needs an upper-bound-only hypothesis

- **Location:** `sections/01-foundations.tex`, lines 641–658, with consequences at lines 660–672.
- **Severity:** Major as written: a missing essential hypothesis makes the asserted equivalence and exact formulation false. The repair is local and does not require a new proof.
- **Reason:** The general model allows lower and upper product quality bounds, and the current subsection permits rational polyhedral product regions. The special-case introduction restricts the input attributes to `[0,1]` and the product *upper* bounds to `{0,1}`, but does not exclude positive lower quality bounds or additional product inequalities. The disjunction checks only the zero upper bounds. An upper bound of one does not imply that every mixture is accepted if a positive lower bound remains.
- **Counterexample:** Take one input of quality zero, one pool, and one product; include the feed and outlet arcs, with all upper flow bounds one and zero lower flow bounds. Give the product lower quality bound one and upper quality bound one. This meets the two restrictions explicitly listed at lines 641–642. The unit feed and outlet flow satisfies the displayed disjunction because both `D` and `S` are zero, but its delivered quality is zero and violates the lower bound. Thus the claimed quality-feasibility equivalence and exact MILP admit a nonphysical flow. The example even has `F_j` empty, hence facial under the subsection's convention.
- **Proposed correction:** State that the *only* product quality restrictions in this special case are coordinatewise upper bounds in `{0,1}`, with no positive lower quality bounds or other product-region inequalities. Equivalently, allow only redundant lower bounds at most zero. The existing disjunction proof then establishes precisely the claimed result.

## Specific checks of the new cyclic development

1. **Reconstruction, lines 397–422:** For a positive-support SCC, external inflow equals external outflow. A source-free SCC is therefore isolated in the positive support. Every other SCC has a deficient row in the backward substochastic matrix, and irreducibility gives invertibility. The solution is a convex combination of boundary qualities; considering the whole nonisolated system also gives the claimed polynomial rational encoding without accumulating an uncontrolled sequence of algebraic extensions.
2. **Destination decomposition, lines 438–456:** A terminal pool SCC without a product outlet has no external inflow. Consequently its removal does not strand another positive-flow component. Every remaining pool reaches a product, so absorption probabilities sum to one. Multiplication by the head probability preserves the incoming quality balance exactly. Arc and node upper capacities are preserved by coordinatewise domination.
3. **Merging circulations, lines 477–483:** The isolated circulation is disjoint from the product-serving flow at the level of positive-flow vertices and arcs. Its qualities can therefore be retained or reassigned independently when it is merged into a designated component. This yields exactly `m` components whose costs sum to the original cost, so the `m` denominator is justified, including when some components deliver zero.
4. **Shared-capacity LP, lines 484–488:** Every decomposed physical flow obeys the capacities on the sum. Conversely every LP component is physically reconstructible by the cyclic single-product lemma, and nonnegativity makes each component individually satisfy all upper capacities. Arbitrary linear arc costs are additive, so choosing the cheapest component gives the displayed inequalities with the correct directions for nonpositive minimization objectives.
5. **Compactness, lines 489–493:** Isolated circulation qualities can be reset to zero without changing any delivered mass. All other active pool qualities are source-derived convex combinations. Inactive pools can also be set to zero. Therefore the coordinate box containing zero and the input qualities preserves the arc-flow projection. Together with finite flow upper capacities and polynomial equality/closed inequality constraints this gives a compact lift and an attained optimum. No continuity of a particular reconstruction across changing supports is needed.
6. **Negative-cycle sign criterion:** Removing nonnegative-cost cycles preserves source withdrawals and the sole destination delivery; cyclic single-product reconstruction supplies physical qualities after this ordinary-flow operation. The proof does not wrongly assume that arbitrary cycle removal preserves each original intermediate quality.
