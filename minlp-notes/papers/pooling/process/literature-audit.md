## Initial checks

- The local package `dey2015-analysis-of-milp-techniques-for` contains presentation slides, despite article metadata. Article-based theorem locators must be checked against the open manuscript at https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/04/3849.pdf. The publisher confirms Operations Research 63(2), 412–427 (2015), DOI 10.1287/opre.2015.1357. Its abstract attributes the output-count approximation and relaxation bounds to Dey and Gupte; these must not be claimed as new here.
- The local package `boland2016-new-multi-commodity-flow-formulations` contains a June 2015 manuscript. The known closed-circulation counterexample concerns that inspected manuscript. A claim that the final journal article has the same defect requires inspecting that version. The projected-feasible-flow equivalence is distinct from uniqueness of the auxiliary mixing system.
- The final Haugland 2016 complexity paper supersedes the unsupported two-pool wording in the 2014 conference proceedings. The earlier source audit records the discrepancy and the final paper's bounded-pool open question. New comparisons must use the model and quantifiers in the final source.
- Initial fresh web searches for pooling and existential-real complexity did not identify a matching completeness theorem. This is limited negative search evidence, not proof of publication priority. The manuscript should state its contributions directly and attribute their imported ingredients.
