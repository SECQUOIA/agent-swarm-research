## Verified scope

- **Slack gates, lines 1192–1199.** Replacing the comparison source by the exact source on `u,s,2-v` gives `u+s=v`. Since both compared signals lie in `[0,2]`, every satisfying comparison has an admissible slack `s=v-u`. Giving the slack its own cycle does not introduce a further restriction.
- **Port identities and complement grouping, lines 1201–1225, depending on lines 736–768.** The closed-cycle argument uses exact zero-link totals, exact middle supplies, and exact output demands. For full cycles these totals force equality in every `v_j <= u_j`; half cycles similarly force equality in every `2v_j <= u_j`. These arguments do not use the private positive-port supply equations. The complement supply is therefore justified before grouping: the full pair sums to two and the half pair to one. Distinct requested occurrences avoid double use of a port. The table gives the correct complement supplies for all listed gate types.
- **Supply-four splitting, lines 1227–1235.** For capacities `(2,2,2)`, splitting an original supply-two source produces demand four, while splitting its supply-four complement produces demand two. For capacities `(2,1,1)`, supply two produces demand two. Exact per-branch supplies enforce the complement flows, and the collector equation enforces precisely the old total supply. Inputs have degree two and collectors degree three, including these newly grouped sources.
- **Exact ordinary contracts and exception count, lines 1237–1252.** Full and half cycle qualities are already tight; the conversion cycles remain covered because their positive endpoint qualities are one and 33. Collector quality is automatic from its common input quality. Every ordinary output consequently has exact demand and exact quality. Every gate, mate group, middle source, zero link, split source, and conversion source has exact supply. Only the two private conversion fillers and the anchor retain variable supply, and only the two primary outputs retain variable demand/upper-only quality. The explicit omission of extra coordinate-designation ports avoids introducing extra fillers.
- **Physical threshold and data restrictions, lines 967–1155 and 1254–1263.** The binary recurrence produces `cx/2^L` in the stated bit order. Clearing a rational row and dividing by a power of two larger than the coefficient sum keeps every nonnegative partial sum below two, including the constant term. Thus the affine threshold comparison is implemented by the same bounded-signal gates. It excludes zero intake. The dyadic normalization, two-feed identity, radial throughput bound, and product-threshold algebra preserve both reduction directions. The normalization by 33 yields exactly the asserted quality palette; all final flow bounds remain in `{0,1,2,3,4}`. The two outlet capacities alone imply the pool bound. Constant local data and polynomial circuit size support the strong-hardness claim.
