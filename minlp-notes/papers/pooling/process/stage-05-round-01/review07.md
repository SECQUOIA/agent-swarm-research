## Finding 1 — minor: make absent exceptional outlets explicit in the exceptional-only branch

**Location:** `s5:arbitrary-exceptions`, lines 776–802, especially “these r outlet flows” and “Keep their demand and quality bounds.”

Here `r` counts the allowed exceptional outlets, whereas `E_J` may also contain exceptional products with no pool outlet. The fraction variables are introduced only for those `r` outlets. After all exceptional nodes are deleted, the paragraph at lines 799–802 explicitly retains demand and quality bounds for “their” products, apparently referring to the exceptional outlets. The proof should explicitly state that it retains requirements for **every** product in `E_J`. For a product without an outlet, its mass and quality expressions must be bypass-only; the displayed `theta_j Q` expression needs a zero convention because no such fraction was introduced.

This is a local indexing and constraint-preservation ambiguity, not a counterexample to the theorem or a need for a new algorithm. The initial absent-flow convention covers `v_j=0`, but does not define an absent `theta_j`. The canonical argument's Section 4 explicitly says to retain all exceptional output requirements, making the intended construction clear.

The distinction matters: take a quality-zero input of exact supply one, a feed of capacity one, an exceptional product X with an allowed outlet of capacity one and demand interval [0,1], and an exceptional product Y with no outlet, demand interval [2,3], and a single bypass from the input of capacity one. Give both products quality bounds [0,1]. All lower arc bounds and the common lower bound are zero; take redundant common upper bound one. The physical instance is infeasible because Y cannot receive two units. Dropping Y's requirements would admit feed/outlet flow one to X and zero bypass to Y. This example only illustrates why the intended preservation must include products without outlets; it is not evidence against the correctly interpreted construction.

**Proposed correction:** Define `J_E` as the exceptional products with allowed outlets and `r=|J_E|`. Introduce fractions for `J_E`, set `v_j=theta_j=0` by convention on `E_J\J_E`, and explicitly retain the demand and homogeneous quality bounds at every `j in E_J`. This adds no variables or complexity.
