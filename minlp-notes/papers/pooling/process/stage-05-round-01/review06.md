## Vector-quality chart audit

The proof at `s5:quality-chart`, especially `s5:directions` and `s5:residual-quality`, preserves its claimed scope.

- **Coverage in growing ambient dimension.** For any fixed real vector `q` unequal to every full `C_i`, each polynomial `beta(k)·(C_i-q)` is nonzero and has at most `K-1` roots. The explicit `|I|(K-1)+1` integer directions therefore contain a simultaneous nonsingular direction. This argument works over real coefficients, not only rational sample points. The number of directions and the bit lengths of all entries remain polynomial when `K` grows. The retained variable dimension depends on `d,s`, while `K` increases the number of rows and coefficient sizes.
- **Singular vectors.** The fixed-vector physical LPs cover every full `q=C_i` in the supplied affine space and box, including vectors of inputs without pool feeds. A projected collision `beta·C_1=beta·C_2` is handled separately from a full-vector singularity. Strict `gamma_i != 0` conditions are retained on charts. No claim relies on closing a chart after denominator clearing.
- **Cancellation.** Put `p=beta·q`, `c_i=beta·C_i`, and `bbar=beta·B_j`. Direct expansion gives

  ```text
  a_h = C_1h c_2 - C_2h c_1
        + (c_1-c_2)q_h + (C_2h-C_1h)p,

  f_h = b_j gamma_1 [B_jh c_2 - C_2h bbar
          + (C_2h-B_jh)p + (bbar-c_2)q_h].
  ```

  Thus `a_h` is affine and `f_h` is quadratic after any supplied affine substitution for `q`. No assumption that the ambient source or product vectors belong to that supplied quality space is needed.
- **All quality equations.** Substitution of `w_2=R_j-w_1` and multiplication by the nonzero `gamma_1 gamma_2` is reversible. Keeping every residual equation on the same selected arc is valid. On `a_h=0`, the required condition is exactly `f_h=0`; on `a_h != 0`, the equality is exactly a matching lower and upper rational bound. The one- and zero-inlet equations preserve all ambient coordinates. Equal projected qualities do not remove these residual obligations.
- **Projection and degree.** Every residual component is a path, cycle or isolated node. Connected induced cuts cross at most two internal arcs. Expanding all combinations of polynomial-size candidate lists therefore remains polynomial. A bound attached to a removed boundary arc remains explicit; shifted node intervals retain its signed contribution. Clearing only the selected, known-sign denominators preserves constant degree. Degree four is a valid safe bound. Arc consistency comparisons, whole-component cuts, isolated nodes, and zero residual coefficients remain present.
- **Physical correspondence and optimization.** All ambient global quality equations in `s5:exception-balances` are needed and retained. Together with local output equations they imply actual pool mixing for every residual lift. The independent coordinate rows of `D` provide polynomial-bit parameter bounds. The affine-space restriction and feed-coordinate box make the original branch compact before chart partitioning. Optimization over actual attainable values, followed by sampling an attaining chart or singular LP, handles open charts correctly. Recovery stays in the sampled common field.

The arbitrary-quality theorem also checks out: a positive ordinary outlet puts `q` in an affine space generated by its exact product vector and at most two bypass source vectors. Enumerating these spaces is complete without imposing positivity on the designated outlet in each branch. The exceptional-only branch covers all remaining flows, including an inactive pool. Its conserved `T,Q` expressions are valid for every detached-component lift, and zero `T` forces `Q=0`. Its fixed fractions preserve the full ambient product inequalities.
