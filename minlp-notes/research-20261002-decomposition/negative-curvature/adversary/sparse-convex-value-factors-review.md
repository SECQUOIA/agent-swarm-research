# Independent review of sparse convex value factors

Date: 2026-10-02. Reviewed the saved
[composition theorem](../sparse-convex-value-factors.md) and the updated
[exact diagnostic](../check_affine_convex_recourse.py).
Verdict: no substantive gap found in the stated theorem.

The main scope restrictions are necessary and explicit: private feasible
polytopes are fixed, blocks are disjoint after conditioning on the retained
variables, complete attachment scopes fit in supplied residual bags, and
the original optimum is unique with full-vector growth. The method does
not apply unchanged to a private feasible set that varies with a parameter.

Each local value function is an infimum of affine parameter functions,
so it is concave. Adding the direct retained quadratic gives the claimed
coordinate upper curvature. The response can cross active faces or have
ties away from the global optimum; neither interferes with concavity or
exact convex-QP evaluation. Convex polyhedral KKT certificates remain
valid without a strictly feasible private point because a polyhedral
normal cone is generated by active constraint normals. Rational primal
and multiplier solutions of polynomial encoding length exist.

The interpolation and retained-coordinate pruning steps use semiconcavity,
exact finite table values, and point growth. They do not require the
value function itself to be a quadratic polynomial. Conditional optimal
responses lift retained incumbents to feasible original points with equal
objective, so the original growth premise implies retained point growth.
The DP bit calculation correctly bounds the denominator length of a sum
along one selected assignment. It does not incorrectly multiply by the
total number of alternative table entries or assume a common denominator
across all active sets.

The exact-output argument uses the original quadratic over its bounded
rational product polytope, rather than applying a polynomial-height bound
to a nonsmooth value function. Uniqueness yields a rational optimizer of
polynomial height through its minimal-face stationarity system. A certified
value interval narrower than `1/(4Q^2)` isolates its exact objective.
Retained coordinate windows of radius `1/(4R^2)` contain at most one
rational of denominator at most `R`; differences of two such rationals
are at least `1/R^2`. The stated accuracy `g/(32R^4)` places every true
retained coordinate in its window. The inequality `1/sqrt(32)<1/4`
is correct.

Premature coordinate reconstruction cannot falsely certify optimality:
acceptance also checks original feasibility and exact objective equality
with the already isolated original optimum. Local convex-QP responses at
the accepted retained vector supply all private coordinates. Original
uniqueness ensures that any such attaining lift is the same optimizer.
The unknown growth constant affects how long refinement runs, while all
accepted bounds and the final equality remain independently valid.

The theorem properly separates direct retained curvature from cancellation
by an affine response. In particular, for `M(y-z)^2` its general local
value-oracle argument still has direct diagonal `2M`; one must first use
the affine certificate to remove that curvature. The extra condition
`L<=C beta` is therefore substantive, not implied by `H>=-nu I`.
