The full KTK original is accessible from MathNet, but the PDF read here is the Russian original; the English translation is identified by its DOI. GLS was read in the actual 1988 first-edition scan, so its locators above are first-edition page numbers. Del Pia’s exact theorem contract was read in the author’s 2024 arXiv v2 preprint; the 2025 journal identity and pagination were confirmed from SIAM, but the journal full text was not available here. No source was ingested into the shared literature knowledge base. The Pardalos–Vavasis publisher URL and abstract should go to its sole literature owner if a primary package is still needed. No manuscript file was changed.
