# Root integration decisions

These decisions implement the user's request for one coherent complete paper.

1. Include the constant-base component solver and both strong-field families.
   Give the full constructive proof as a reusable algebraic interface. Its
   mathematical correctness is supported by the fresh Sol audit; priority
   remains subject to Luna's nearest-source comparison. Do not claim that
   rational univariate representations or critical-point methods are new.
2. Retain the two-negative-direction linear numerical bound. Incorporate
   `reviews/prewrite-two-inertia-sol.md`, including its explicit deterministic
   exponent derivation. The finite Gaussian-like-law variant may be stated
   with its additional accuracy budget.
3. Use the sharper mixed-order transport bound for continuous orders as well
   as mixed orders. It specializes an existing proof and does not need a
   separate priority claim.
4. Use the attaining-witness inequality to remove unnecessary kernel inclusion
   from supplied-factor quadratic closure. The globally valid upper model is
   enough; do not impose differentiability on a nonsmooth envelope.
5. Present TU feasible rounding only at its proved scope. Do not infer an
   unrestricted smoothed TU optimization theorem from it or from a chamber
   count with uncontrolled constants. The developed small-core TU integer
   recourse theorem is a separate result with a complete oracle contract.
6. Define charted exact outputs for polynomial and implicit graph constraints.
   Rational free coordinates can have an irrational exact dependent lift.
   Do not promise an exactly feasible fully rational point on every graph.
7. Distinguish the three recourse sampling-bit regimes. Preserve the
   parameter-dependent precision of general nonlinear boundary flow/TU.
8. State overlap with the existing repository manuscripts honestly. Unpublished
   companions can be cited as such, but they do not replace proofs essential
   to this manuscript or establish public priority. Avoid private file paths
   in submission text.
9. The scientific paper contains theory and worked examples, not a performance
   benchmark. No saved optimization experiment is rerun. Existing proof
   diagnostics are not evidence of practical speed.

The inventory's 69 in-scope entries include supporting lemmas, external
oracles, examples, and limitations; they are not 69 independent original
contributions. Final coverage must link these entries to actual theorem and
proof locations and record supersession or deliberate omission.

10. Retain the small bridges identified by the draft coverage audit: B10's
    explicit conditional-recourse count and X16's deterministic flow-core
    error bound. Each follows from a shared lemma and needs only a short
    precise statement and proof; qualitative discussion alone loses useful
    information about when the ambient dimension factor disappears.
11. Retain the direct Square Root Sum boundary at width two with bounded
    coefficient magnitudes from the exact-arithmetic companion. Its
    structural scope is relevant to the sparse and core-noise results and
    is stronger than the indirect PosSLP implication. State the precise
    implication, acknowledge the overlap, and give the necessary
    self-contained construction in Appendix G. Do not claim its novelty
    here or assert a treewidth bound for the different PosSLP construction.
