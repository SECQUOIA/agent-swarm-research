Section~\ref{sec:computations} reports five campaigns, each run with solver
code, limits and protocol fixed in advance. All 274{,}489 cut records of the
last three campaigns passed a fresh-process replay, which re-executes the exact
support routines against the source model and the SCIP row recorded when the
cut was generated. Constants taken from the separator's samples or improved by
local minimization would have cut off feasible solutions, including known
optima, and even a numerical global solver once reported optimality for a
wrong constant. On MINLPLib models, including 20 larger models that SCIP does
not solve within 60 seconds, the cuts did not help SCIP, and SCIP's
disabled-by-default separators were stronger. On constructed families with the
structure of Section~\ref{sec:composition}, the cuts closed nearly all of SCIP's
root gap. A configuration chosen after a post hoc diagnostic and fixed before
fresh instances were generated solved all 20 of them, against at most 10 for
SCIP in three settings and 5 for Gurobi; with a binding coupling row, SCIP
without its implicit-discreteness presolve step did as well, faster. On
constrained stars with up to 16 leaves, star blocks made the root bound optimal
to within $10^{-6}$, which neither smaller blocks nor SCIP achieved, but
Gurobi, which found good solutions faster, solved more instances.
