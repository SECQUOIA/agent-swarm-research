\subsection{Validity, and what certification prevents}\label{sec:validity}

Every recorded cut of campaigns 3 to~5 passed replay: 7{,}498 cuts in the
prospective parts of campaign~3, 30{,}998 in Part~3P, 104{,}771 in campaign~4
and 131{,}222 in campaign~5, 274{,}489 records in total, of which 5{,}267 are cuts
on MINLPLib models and the others cuts on the constructed families; the 1{,}135
cuts of the informational rerun of Section~\ref{sec:star-bench} passed replay as
well. The records contain 92{,}222 distinct exported rows. Rows recur because
the separator runs only at the root, so that the root and full runs of a path
instance record the same cuts, because the runs of Part~5C-b with the caps
$32n$ and $64n$ nearly coincide, and because overlapping blocks of a star share
source rows. In every part and cut mode, each of the fourteen
corrupted records of Section~\ref{sec:replay} was rejected. Every incumbent
returned in campaigns 3 to~5 passed the original-model check, with one
exception in a run without certified cuts: in a Part~4D root run of
SCIP-extra, the incumbent satisfied all constraints, but SCIP's reported
objective value differed by 34\% from the value recomputed on the source model.

The checks also changed what reached SCIP. The stored-row check (C4) rejected
certified, violated rows in every part, because presolve had aggregated or
fixed a block variable or because SCIP had dropped or rounded a coefficient
(Section~\ref{sec:funnel}). Binary64 rounding changed at least one coefficient
in 13.5\% of the exported rows on the MINLPLib models, in 92.9\% on the path
family and in 51.5\% on the star family; the bound correction of Proposition~\ref{prop:export} was at most
$8.5\cdot10^{-16}$ in absolute value and never destroyed a violation.

To measure what the certificate itself buys (Part~4U), we recomputed, for the
recorded binary64 direction of each cut, the constant that an uncertified
pipeline would use. U1 is the minimum over the separator's own sample set,
which is what the direction LP sees. U2 is the better of U1 and local
minimizations by SLSQP \citep{Kraft1988} from the three best samples, on the
block box and the affine domain rows. Up to binary64 rounding, both are upper
bounds on the true support value. The MINLPLib set comprises all cuts whose
certified value is the exact support value; the 151 cuts certified by
Bernstein or ball-arithmetic lower bounds, all on models of Part~3A (95 in
Part~3A and 56 in its Part~3P root runs), are excluded, because a larger
uncertified constant would not show an error. Table~\ref{tab:ablation}
compares the constants with the certified values. On the MINLPLib models the
sample minimum was wrong for 38\% of the cuts, and 405 of the resulting rows
would have cut off a feasible point that SCIP had returned for the same model.
Local minimization reduced the share of wrong constants to 4.5\%, but not all
errors disappeared. On \code{nvs02}, SLSQP stopped at its starting point after
one iteration, because the directional derivative along its first step was
below its default tolerance; on \code{kall\_ellipsoids\_tc02b} it started at
stationary points that are not local minima. On the path family, where the
blocks are nonconvex by construction, the sample minimum was wrong for almost
every cut, 40\% of the uncertified rows would have cut off the known optimal
solution, and local minimization still left 7.3\% wrong constants, some of
whose rows cut off the optimum, because SLSQP converged to non-global local
minima. Every wrong constant is refuted by an explicit point: the certified
minimizer is exactly feasible and attains a smaller value. With ten times the
removal threshold the four ``cuts off'' counts become 389, 128, 503 and 27, and
the lists of models do not change.

A numerical global solve is a much better substitute on these small blocks
(Part~5U). Gurobi's best bound with default tolerances exceeded the exact
support value, by rounding-level amounts, for 45\% of the MINLPLib cuts, so even
these constants would need a safe correction, and it was materially wrong for
one distinct cut, recorded four times on \code{nvs02}. There Gurobi reported
optimality with zero gap for a constant $1.8\cdot10^{-4}$ above the true
minimum, because a slope of $-8.9\cdot10^{-7}$ in one variable fell inside its
optimality tolerance, and over that variable's range of 200 this lost
$1.8\cdot10^{-4}$. A safety shift of $10^{-6}$ would not
have covered this error, although none of these rows cut off a recorded
feasible point; Gurobi's objective value instead of its bound was materially
wrong for three of the sampled path cuts. The certified rows themselves were
violated at recorded incumbents only within the feasibility tolerance of these
incumbents: for example, incumbents of \code{st\_e22} with objective value
$-85.0000017$, which satisfy the constraints only up to a scaled violation of
$10^{-8}$, violate the certified objective bound $t\ge-85$, the true optimum, by
$1.7\cdot10^{-6}$.
