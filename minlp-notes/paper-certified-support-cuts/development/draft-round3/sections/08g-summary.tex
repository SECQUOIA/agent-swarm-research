\subsection{Summary and limitations}\label{sec:comp-summary}

Every one of the 274{,}489 recorded cuts passed replay against the source
model and the row stored by SCIP. Constants taken from the separator's samples
would have cut off feasible points on seven MINLPLib models and on 58 of the 60
sampled path-family instances, and constants improved by local minimization
still did so on two models and 19 instances; a numerical global solve was
almost always right, but not always. On MINLPLib models the cuts were neutral
for SCIP: they changed neither which models it solved in campaigns 2 to~4 nor
its own solving time, the Python separator made the runs slower, and SCIP's
disabled-by-default separators improved the root bound more often than the
certified cuts did. On the constructed families, exact block support closed
the root gap that SCIP leaves. With a non-binding coupling row, a separator
configuration chosen after the campaign-3 diagnostic and fixed before the fresh
instances were generated solved all 20 of them, against at most 10 for each
SCIP setting and 5 for Gurobi. With a binding row, both direction rules solved
all instances, but so did SCIP without implicit discreteness, faster. On the
star family, star blocks certified by the star oracle made the root bound
exact, which neither blocks of four variables nor any SCIP setting achieved;
the solved count was then limited by SCIP's search for good solutions, and
Gurobi solved more instances. We therefore do not recommend enabling the
separator by default; its value is on models whose joint block structure
SCIP's relaxation misses, and an activation rule that recognizes such models
is not available.

\paragraph{Limitations.} The MINLPLib parts use models with at most 1{,}000
variables, and their blocks have at most four variables; star blocks occur only
in the constructed star family. The implementation is in Python, so the
measured cost is an upper bound on that of a compiled separator. The host was
shared, with different loads in different campaigns and one load spike during
Part~5S, so we compare times only within paired runs of one part, and time
differences of a few percent are not meaningful \citep{LodiTramontani2013}.
Runs used one seed, except Parts~3A and~3B, and one thread. Gurobi was run with
one thread and default settings apart from its nonconvex mode. The constructed
families favor block cuts; the post hoc diagnostic of campaign~3 was designed
after its results were seen, and its finding was then tested prospectively on
fresh instances.
