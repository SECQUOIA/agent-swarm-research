# Switching-control paper process

The user requested a comprehensive LaTeX paper, further development of incomplete ideas, and sequential stages with one author followed by five independent reviewers. Each valid finding is adjudicated by the primary agent and corrected by a different agent. A stage with a valid major finding receives another five-reviewer round after correction. All valid minor findings are corrected before acceptance. The same process applies to the whole manuscript.

## Planned stages

1. Foundations, source positioning, uniform controls, and exact continuous one-switch minimax; establish the LaTeX project and notation.
2. Heavy-mode reduction and exact continuous two- and three-switch minimax, with complete four-block certificate foundations and relevant structural counterexamples.
3. Arbitrary budgets: mode removal, general and seeded bounds, plateau and asymptotics; investigate remaining reach questions and present only justified conclusions.
4. Exact finite-grid minimax: arbitrary-grid one-switch formula and extremizers, special cases, five-cell two-switch theorem, and precise published-source corrections.
5. Exact instance algorithms, dwell constraints, sharp grid transfer, and development of certified coarse-grid approximation.
6. Reproducible computations, complete literature comparison, introduction and synthesis, and a comprehensive coverage audit.
7. Whole-manuscript independent review and revision to acceptance.

The order may be refined when dependencies require it; stages remain sequential. Superseded bounds are covered through derivation, comparison, or an explicit subsumption record, not repeated as competing final claims. Genuine open problems are investigated without treating a desired theorem as established. A conjecture may be discussed as an open problem, but no proved result may depend on it.

## Review requirements

Review the actual manuscript and supporting artifacts. Historical reviews are context, not substitutes for a fresh review. Check hypotheses, quantifiers, measurable-input arguments, repeated modes, endpoint conventions, exact arithmetic, certificate coverage, source attribution, complexity, and readability. Reports must state the precise files reviewed, independent checks performed, findings with severity and locators, and limitations. No reviewer edits the manuscript. Reviewers write only their assigned report and verification directory.

The primary agent reads the work independently, adjudicates all findings, and records acceptance only after corrections and required repeat reviews. No claim of external peer review or guaranteed acceptance is made.

## Status

- Stage 1: accepted after five reviews and verified correction of all minor findings; see `process/stage01-acceptance.md`.
- Stage 2: five round-1 reviews complete, no major findings; correction and analytic exact-instance strengthening assigned, followed by a fresh five-reviewer round.
- Stages 3–7: pending.

## Artifacts

Author reports, reviewer reports, adjudications, stage snapshots, source records, and verification outputs are stored beneath `process/` and `verification/`. Manuscript content is kept separate from process history.
