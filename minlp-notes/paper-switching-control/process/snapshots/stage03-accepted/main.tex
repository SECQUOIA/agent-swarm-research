\title{Rounding switching controls under a hard switch budget:\\sharp minimax bounds and exact algorithms}
\author{}
\date{}

\begin{abstract}
Combinatorial integral approximation replaces a relaxed mixture of control
modes by one active mode at a time while controlling cumulative error. A hard
bound on the number of switches changes the approximation problem: even on
arbitrarily fine time grids, the worst-case error can remain proportional to
the horizon. We study this error as a function of the number of modes, the
switch budget, and the time grid. We establish exact uniform-input formulas
and, for at least three modes, the full continuous one-switch minimax value,
with matching extremizers and constructive proofs for measurable relaxed
controls. These results give
explicit counterexamples to a published switching-budget conjecture. The
continuous and grid problems are formulated separately, so that bounds for
individual inputs and worst-case bounds have unambiguous meanings.
\end{abstract}
