## Proof and scope audit

- **One-switch dominance and crossing** — `sections/10-instance-algorithms.tex:19–68`. The three candidates cover all distinct ordered pairs, including tied terminal masses. The prescribed-initial-mode extension correctly changes the candidate set. Constant controls are included when no boundary is allowed. The crossing difference increases by at least the elapsed time, while its left and right objective branches have the required monotonicity. The query bound explicitly excludes input loading and permitted-boundary preparation. The restriction to common dwell durations for this dominance shortcut is necessary and correctly stated.
- **Fixed-prefix completion** — `10-instance-algorithms.tex:70–89`. Negative residuals cause no omitted absolute-value term: inactive negative endpoint errors and active positive endpoint errors are bounded by the already incurred prefix error. Replacing the final label with a larger eligible residual cannot increase either remaining term. Eligibility is held fixed, so no unsupported greedy rule for constructing the prefix is implied.
- **Subset assignment and full-budget coverage** — `10-instance-algorithms.tex:93–156`. The component cost covers disconnected occurrences of the same mode and charges omitted modes their terminal masses. The recurrence partitions block indices rather than physical modes, and its transition count is exactly the stated sum yielding (3^k). Padding by subdivision covers fewer-switch schedules with (k=\min(s+1,N)), including (N=1), (s=0), and budgets exceeding the available boundaries. Traceback storage and fixed-budget rational bit complexity are consistent with the implementation. No fixed-parameter claim in the switch budget alone is made.
- **Dwell restrictions and candidate-label exchange** — `10-instance-algorithms.tex:160–205`. Dwell admissibility is applied after merging adjacent equally labeled blocks, including both horizon-end runs. Unused modes impose no dwell obligation. This property survives subdivision, which is essential for extending fixed-boundary optimization to all schedules. The candidate-set exchange is valid even with ties: an outside label forces at least one top-(d) mass to be omitted; replacement by an unused member of the role's cheapest (d) labels bounds both the changed role cost and the newly omitted mass. The (n=d) case is handled separately.
- **Continuous LP enumeration** — `10-instance-algorithms.tex:207–244`. The time-cell decomposition covers zero-length blocks, repeated labels, multiple switches in one input cell, and switches exactly at input knots. Unlike the grid algorithm, its block count must not be truncated at (N). Endpoint monotonicity justifies omitting constraints at input knots inside an activation block. The full and one-sided LP signs match their respective definitions. Compact LP domains have optimal vertices; lower-dimensional domains still have (k) independent active normals in the (k)-variable representation. The enumeration count and polynomial bit complexity for fixed (k) follow. The deliberately exponential dependence on block count is disclosed.
- **Supported uniform-grid transfer** — `sections/11-transfer-and-coarsening.tex:11–77`. The network carries cumulative counts along mode chains, has integral lower and upper capacities, and admits the original occupations as a fractional feasible flow. Integral feasibility therefore supplies supported selections. Floor/ceiling constraints give strict endpoint error below one normalized cell, including zero error at integral prefixes. Cellwise monotonicity extends the bound to every time. Positive-measure support supplies strictly increasing sample times in original blocks; this proves the chronological subsequence property even when modes recur. The strict minimax inequality is valid because the grid maximum is attained; merely taking suprema of strict instance inequalities would not suffice.
- **Binary transfer and sharpness** — `11-transfer-and-coarsening.tex:85–137`. The two binary choices are separated by the current cell width, so at least one remains in the interval of radius half the largest width. Degenerate occupations preserve support and leave the discrepancy unchanged. The universal sharpness family has the stated switch count, grid optimum, and continuous competitor error. Its use establishes an instance-gap lower bound, not an exact continuous optimum or a minimax-gap identity. The four-mode, five-cell, three-switch specialization lies in the cited budget range and gives the stated gap of at least (9/16).
- **Coarsening and perturbation** — `11-transfer-and-coarsening.tex:141–241`. Exact coarse cumulative values suffice to evaluate the original objective of any coarse schedule. Nonaligned input/coarse grids have at most (N+M-1) common-refinement cells. The exact grid optimum yields the strict lower certificate through transfer and the feasible upper certificate directly. The (1/\varepsilon) complexity is stated as a numeric-accuracy dependence, not polynomial dependence on its logarithm. The half-width improvements are limited to one switch or two modes; the implementation deliberately uses the general bound. Strictness is retained when the raw lower bound equals zero, and removed when a negative bound is clipped. Nested and nonnested prescribed-grid feasibility are distinguished. The odd-grid dwell obstruction is valid even with arbitrary refinement, and the perturbation inequality correctly incurs one tolerance on the certificate endpoints and two on suboptimality.

## Fresh independent verification

The new script `verification/reviewer02/stage05-round01/independent.py` imports the relocated implementations but constructs its own objectives and comparison problems. Its output is retained in `independent.log`.

1. **375 grid optimization cases:** exhaustive enumeration of all physical cell words on rational nonuniform grids, including zero allocations, tied data, budgets from zero past saturation, mode-specific dwell requirements, and complete infeasibility. Dwell admissibility is computed by directly merging the physical word's maximal runs. Both the returned objective and reconstructed schedule matched the exhaustive optimum.
2. **95 restricted one-switch cases:** direct word enumeration with prescribed or free initial mode and independently selected permitted boundaries. All objectives and returned schedules matched.
3. **150 fixed-prefix completions:** direct enumeration of all eligible constant suffixes, including mandatory changes from a nonempty prefix. All returned values and modes were optimal.
4. **Six continuous instances, covering both objectives:** independently formulated every word/cell LP in **block-length variables** with an exact total-length equality, rather than the implementation's switch-time variables. These include unequal two-cell binary inputs and a one-cell three-mode input with two switches. For **all 118 individual LPs**, SciPy was used only to propose primal and dual values; rational reconstruction then checked primal feasibility, dual signs, nonnegative reduced costs, and exact equality of primal and dual objectives. The minimum certified LP values agreed with the new entirely rational vertex-enumeration solver.
5. **12 coarsening certificates:** compared certificates on four coarse grid sizes against the independently certified continuous optima from the full-error cases above. All strict/closed lower endpoints and feasible upper bounds were valid.
6. **729 original three-mode integer controls:** exhaustively enumerated words on six microintervals, grouped into three uniform coarse cells but split in different unequal rational proportions inside each cell. Every original word admitted a supported prefix-rounded witness. Every witness tested had no more switches and had full continuous discrepancy strictly below one cell, checked directly at all microinterval endpoints.
7. **120 candidate-label exchange cases:** exhaustive injective role assignment for independent synthetic bottleneck cost and terminal-mass data, including ties. Restricting to the stated candidate union preserved the optimum.

These checks are separate from the author's existing examples and do not merely rerun their outputs.
