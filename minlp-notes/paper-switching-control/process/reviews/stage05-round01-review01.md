## Algorithm proof audit

- **Three candidates per boundary:** Final-mode dominance preserves the omitted-mass bound when a label is replaced. Outside the two leading terminal masses, both remaining terms are constant except for the initial cumulative allocation, so its largest value identifies the third candidate. Three mass leaders suffice to evaluate every omitted maximum, including ties; the binary case and constants are handled separately. Prescribed initial modes and subsets of eligible boundaries preserve the argument.
- **Crossing search:** The difference of the increasing initial deficit and decreasing final deficit has increment at least the time increment. The minimization over permitted boundaries is therefore covered by the two neighboring boundaries, with extreme boundaries when appropriate. Adding the constant omitted-mass term cannot invalidate this selection. The preprocessing and random-access qualifications are necessary and are stated.
- **Final residual lemma:** The prefix already bounds the extrema that disappear under monotonicity on the final block. The newly relevant terminal extrema are exactly the two residual terms. Replacing the selected eligible label by one with a larger residual decreases its final deficit and cannot increase the omitted maximum, including negative residuals. This is correctly scoped to a fixed prefix and fixed eligible set.
- **Subset assignment and full-budget enumeration:** A mode's assigned subset determines its entire component service, including disconnected occurrences and its unused cost. The partition recurrence assigns every nominal block exactly once. Its cost construction, `3^k` transition count, and traceback storage are correct. Padding a feasible schedule by subdivisions into equally labeled adjacent blocks justifies enumerating exactly `min(s+1,N)` nominal blocks, including zero switches and budgets at or above the cell count. Rational sums, differences, and comparisons have polynomial bit lengths for fixed budget. The paper does not mislabel the `N^{k-1}` algorithm as fixed-parameter tractable in the switch budget alone.
- **Dwell constraints:** Maximal consecutive strings of a mode's subset coincide with its physical runs once the partition is complete. The checker merges those nominal blocks before comparing their total duration with the mode's dwell requirement. Empty subsets carry no dwell obligation. Subdivision preserves these physical runs, so completeness of the boundary enumeration remains valid. Initial and terminal runs and infeasibility are expressly covered. The extension to other restrictions is properly qualified by mode-local dependence and subdivision invariance.
- **Candidate-label exchange:** An outside label implies that a top-mass label is currently omitted. Replacing it using an available member of its role's `d` cheapest labels controls both the role cost and the newly omitted terminal mass. Repeating the exchange removes every outside label. The result is stated only for unrestricted injective role assignment and does not promise arbitrary constraint support.
- **Exact continuous enumeration:** There are `k-1` time variables and one error variable. Each fixed word/time-cell choice yields affine endpoint allocations and services. Endpoint monotonicity makes input-breakpoint constraints unnecessary, even when the relaxed rate changes during a block. Closed input cells, nondecreasing cell choices, repeated labels, and zero-length blocks cover every feasible schedule. The count `n^k binom(N+k-2,k-1)` includes `N=1` and `k=1`. Every bounded nonempty polytope has an optimal vertex with `k` independent active normals in the ambient space, including lower-dimensional feasible sets; enumerating nonsingular active systems is complete. The rational elimination and determinant bound justify the fixed-budget bit claim. The code implements the two discrepancy objectives separately and does not truncate continuous block count at the number of input cells.

## Transfer, sharpness, and certificates

The supported prefix-flow network is feasible using the original cell occupations. Integer supplies and bounds give an integral cell assignment on positive-support edges. Its prefix counts lie between floor and ceiling, giving strict error below one at all finitely many endpoints. Within a cell the selected component of `V-W` increases and every other component decreases, so the same strict bound holds throughout. A supported cell selection admits an interior time in an original block; increasing cell times give nondecreasing original block indices. Removing repeated selections of one block and merging adjacent equal labels shows that the resulting block word is a chronological subsequence and cannot use more switches.

The instance transfer uses an attained continuous optimum. The minimax upper inequality uses an attained grid maximizer, which is exactly what allows strictness to pass to the outer maximum. The binary nonuniform construction maintains its interval invariant: its two candidate discrepancies cannot both lie outside an interval of width at least their separation, and forced support choices leave the error unchanged. The one-cell witness proves its half-width constant.

For universal sharpness, the first-cell grid error is at least `1-1/n`, and the constant terminal-mode schedule attains it. The displayed continuous competitor has precisely the claimed first-cell error, then zero discrepancy. Its switch count fits the stated budget range. A competitor upper bound on the continuous optimum suffices for the resulting gap lower bound, so no unsupported exact continuous optimum is required. The repeated-cycle extension and the `9/16` example have correct arithmetic and are explicitly instance statements, not a minimax-gap identification.

Certified coarsening uses the original cumulative values at its actual endpoints. The common-refinement integration handles nonaligned grids and has at most `N+M-1` segments. The strict universal interval, epsilon rounding, sharper non-strict half-width special cases, zero-switch equality, and nested-grid implication have the right inequality directions. Polynomial bit complexity for fixed budget and the parameter-only factor statement follow from the stated finite grid algorithm. The lower-endpoint clipping conventions remain correct when the raw endpoint is exactly zero. The perturbation certificate loses one cumulative tolerance on each side, giving the stated strict `h+2 delta` suboptimality bound. The implementation distinguishes supplied piecewise-constant rates from arbitrary measurable inputs requiring an actual cumulative oracle.

The dwell example is a genuine obstruction: both nonempty runs must have duration exactly one half, an odd uniform grid has no such boundary, and the feasible constants retain discrepancy one half. Thus refinement alone cannot justify carrying the unrestricted certificate to dwell-constrained optima.
