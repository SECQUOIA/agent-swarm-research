# Independent whole-manuscript review: stage 7, round 1, reviewer 1

## Verdict

**No major or minor issue found.** I found no correction to request in the frozen manuscript or its supporting proof artifacts. This is a fresh assessment of the complete manuscript, not an inference from previous stage approvals.

The results stated as proved have adequate proofs or specified, reproducible exact certificates. The remaining research questions are clearly separated from those results. In particular, the general five-block reach inequality, the exact value of the three-mode three-switch minimax problem, and general constraint-preserving coarsening are not prerequisites for the theorems claimed here.

## Mathematical audit

- **Foundations and uniform/one-switch results (sections 01–02).** I checked compactness and attainment, including zero-length activation intervals; the cumulative representation of measurable simplex controls; endpoint reduction; grid averaging and the direction of the minimax comparisons. I rederived the uniform recurrence and its floor/ceiling boundaries, including more nominal blocks than grid cells. The three-term one-switch reduction correctly controls both signs and includes constant controls and endpoint switches.
- **Heavy reduction and analytic reaches (section 03).** The prescribed-heavy integral-prefix construction and the first-repeat reordering prove different statements, and the manuscript keeps that distinction. I checked the prefix counts, latest-inverse flat portions, every admissible position of the adjacent pair, the deadline ordering, and preservation of the suffix. The two- and three-block arguments account for capped inverses, omitted coordinates, and dimension boundaries.
- **Four-block certificates (section 04).** The event linear programs give necessary conditions; their relaxation does not require every feasible assignment to be a control. Symmetry averaging is valid because each distinguished objective is fixed before averaging. The orbit list covers all pair/last-mode incidences. The stable quotient is justified from the number of fixed and row-local bulk labels for n at least 9; the finitely exceptional dimensions are covered separately. I checked the signs in the dual implication, the mass multiplicities and right-hand sides, and the passage from the weighted inequality to the excluded-triple sum. The symbolic certificates cover every integer n at least 23, rather than only tested dimensions.
- **Small-budget formulas and structural examples (section 05).** I checked that the one-sided reaches yield the stated full-discrepancy minimax formulas. In the three-mode example, the capped-inverse sensitivity estimate excludes every distinct three-block word below the exact instance optimum, including thresholds below 1 by monotonicity. The repeated-word and shorter-word exclusions apply at the claimed threshold, and the displayed word and switching times attain it. The exact instance identity is not presented as an exact minimax identity.
- **General budgets and equal masses (sections 06–07).** I checked the minimum-mass, maximum-mass, equality, and constant branches of mode removal, the positive denominators and time horizons, the seed hypotheses, and the stated asymptotics. The all-light induction supplies the advertised endpoints and omitted-mode bound. The equal-mass result is an identity for each input in its stated regime, not merely a worst-case result; its lower bound follows from an omitted mode. The classical small-mode comparison uses the correct number of cells and a permissible hard switch budget.
- **Finite grids (sections 08–09).** I checked both arbitrary-grid LP families, their coverage, elimination conditions, and reconstruction, including coincident breakpoints and a one-cell grid. The bit-complexity claim distinguishes compressed formula evaluation from expanded input construction. I also checked the all-length floor-history realization, density argument for boundary histories, exhaustive state interpretation, the exceptional six-history orbit and three repair words, and the continuous lower examples. The claimed five-, six-, and seven-cell values and the bounds F(3,2)=T/5 and T/7 <= F(3,3) <= T/6 have the stated scope.
- **Algorithms and transfer (sections 10–11).** The one-switch algorithm handles endpoint and constant schedules. The subset assignment includes omitted modes, its label reduction has a valid exchange argument, and its dwell assumptions are explicit. Continuous LP enumeration includes repeated labels, zero blocks, all relevant cells, and more blocks than cells; endpoint constraints suffice. The transfer proof combines supported integral allocation with chronological selection without adding switches. Its strict estimate, binary improvement, sharpness examples, coarsening intervals, and dwell obstruction use consistent hypotheses.
- **Experiments, narrative, and appendix (sections 00 and 12–14).** Headline formulas, figure ranges, switch/block indexing, and tabulated comparisons agree with the proved statements. The public experiment separates the normalized source, quantization, grid restriction, and switch budget. The general exclusion argument remains conditional on its stated weighted premise. The negative event assignment is explicitly a relaxation obstruction, and the chronological chamber result proves only its specified order and maximizers. The overall sequence makes these dependencies understandable.

## Independent verification

The following checks supplement hand verification. They do not replace proofs by sampling.

1. **Independent four-block certificate reconstruction.** `independent_four_block.py` imports no manuscript routines. It reconstructs the event constraints and quotient coordinates independently and verifies all 179 finite dual identities and signs. For all ten polynomial cases it verifies the bounded-degree identities at enough exact integer evaluation points to establish polynomial identity. It proves the required integer-range signs through nonnegative Newton-basis finite differences at 23, independently of the supplied shifted-monomial sign check. The degree argument and quotient stability were also audited analytically. Result: PASS.
2. **Heavy reduction and reach stress tests.** `independent_structures.py` independently constructs half-cell rational inputs and checks full discrepancy at their input knots. Across 45 profiles it found 2,969 feasible words, checked 1,930 admissible first-repeat pair-position repairs, and verified 74 prescribed-heavy integral-prefix witnesses. Exact inverse calculations with flat portions checked 70 reach profiles, 16,190 weighted pair-exclusion inequalities, and 50 four-block maxima. Result: PASS.
3. **Independent floor-history enumeration.** The same script uses full-word bitset intersections, not the supplied small-state switch-cost recurrence. Its switch-count distributions are 5 cells: {0:3, 1:138, 2:255}; 6 cells: {0:3, 1:255, 2:1377, 3:237}; 7 cells: {0:3, 1:414, 2:4542, 3:3891, 4:6}. It verifies that the six exceptional histories form the stated permutation orbit. Result: PASS.
4. **Arbitrary-grid formula and algorithms.** An independently implemented rational formula and reconstructed input were compared with full-cell LPs retaining every mode and cell: 71 grids and 1,364 programs. Reconstructed discrepancies were evaluated exactly; comparisons with SciPy LP optima used floating-point tolerance. Independent exhaustive checks covered 160 subset/dwell instances, 14 continuous one-switch comparisons against separately computed crossings, and 56 coarsening/interval cases. A repeated-mode one-cell example independently gives continuous optimum 1/10 for both objectives. Result: PASS.
5. **Transfer.** Exhaustive supported-rounding checks covered all 19,683 three-mode words for coarse widths (3,3,3), and all 4,096 binary words for widths (2,4,6). Result: PASS.
6. **Public and synthetic computations.** Using the pinned CSV, an independent implementation verified its hash, quantization, and perturbation summaries. Separate scalar root searches and full signed endpoint evaluations reproduced the six continuous pair values and the public fine optimum. A cumulative-count DP independently reproduced all four budgets at each of 12, 24, and 48 cells and every uniform convergence point. Headline thresholds, classical comparison, case counts, and stored timing medians agree. Result: PASS.
7. **Portable reproduction and build.** The complete offline runner passes. A clean `latexmk` build succeeds and produces 56 pages with no undefined-reference, overfull, underfull, or warning entries in the final log. This verifies the bundled, relocated source rather than relying on an existing PDF.

Logs and independent scripts are in `verification/reviewer01/stage07-round01/`. Some independent algorithm and experiment scripts were retained from my earlier independent work and rerun against this frozen snapshot; the certificate reconstruction and combined structural checker were developed for this whole-manuscript review.

## Source attribution and limits

I checked the relevant original Sager–Zeile statements against the final primary PDF, including the additional equality/attainment conditions, the lower-bound proposition, and the conjecture. The manuscript's correction of the stated finite-grid corollary is precise and does not incorrectly discredit the separate continuous lower bound. I checked primary material for supported rounding, graph-based rounding, switch-time optimization, and the classical small-mode bound. The targeted attribution and restrained novelty wording are appropriate. Access to the final Abbasi–Esfeden article was limited; the stated caution about general dynamic-programming optimality is supported by its available primary indexed text, not a new theorem claimed by this manuscript.

This review is not formal proof-assistant verification or an exhaustive search of the entire external literature. The all-dimension certificate conclusion relies on the audited structural and degree arguments as well as exact arithmetic. The supplementary randomized/exhaustive finite tests cover the cases reported above, not all admissible inputs. Archived timing medians were recomputed from the stored timing data; they were not treated as machine-independent performance guarantees. None of these limits exposes an unsupported claim in the manuscript.

**Final assessment:** no valid major or minor finding remains from this independent full-manuscript review.
