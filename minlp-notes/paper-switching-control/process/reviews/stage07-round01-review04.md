## Mathematical audit

The following records the substantive checks behind the verdict. Locators are source filenames and stable LaTeX labels; section numbers in the PDF differ from filename prefixes.

- **Foundations and uniform inputs** (`01-foundations.tex`; `02-uniform-one-switch.tex`). I checked the equivalence of measurable controls and cumulative allocations, the compact parameterization with zero-length blocks and repeated labels, the uniform continuity estimate in switch times, and attainment of all four types of extrema. The exact averaging argument compares the different continuous and grid input classes correctly. Endpoint monotonicity applies between activation endpoints even when the input changes inside a block. The uniform lower recurrence allows repeated competing modes; the grid recurrence and integer rounding argument include short grids and surplus nominal blocks. The one-switch three-term identity and its continuous upper construction account for omitted modes and constant schedules.

- **Heavy modes and reach** (`03-heavy-and-reach.tex`, especially `lem:prefix-repeat`, `lem:first-repeat`, `thm:heavy`, and `thm:one-sided-reduction`). The integral-flow repetition argument, earliest-repeat prefix counts, deadline ordering, and two adjacent copies give the claimed full-error bound. Reordering preserves the occupations at the prefix endpoint, so the original suffix remains valid. Extension, scaling, and restriction prove the arbitrary-horizon heavy theorem without a hidden mode-count assumption. The reduction distinguishes a strict heavy mass from the complementary all-light case. Latest capped inverses and monotonicity justify greedy reach for distinct unused modes, including flat intervals. I checked the sums and positive coefficients in the analytic two- and three-block arguments.

- **Four-block computer-assisted proof** (`04-four-block-certificates.tex`, `lem:weighted-pair`, `thm:reach-four`). I checked that every event inequality used by the LP is necessary for actual reaches, that maximizer exclusions give the reverse allocation inequalities used, and that no unproved converse or global event chronology enters the proof. The three pair types and ten distinguished-mode types exhaust the cases, with nine at (n=5). Symmetry averaging preserves both constraints and objective. The orbit representation distinguishes allocation to the interchangeable excluded index from allocation to another interchangeable index. The stabilization explanation at (n\ge9) covers the maximum of three interchangeable indices in one row. Only mass sums and objective multiplicities vary with (n), as required by the affine reconstruction. I read the exact finite and polynomial checker, including multiplier signs, positive denominators, coefficient identities, shifted polynomial positivity, and explicit coverage of 179 finite and ten symbolic cases. The final analytic substitution from the weighted-pair inequality to four-block reach has the required positive coefficient for every (n\ge5).

- **Sharp small budgets and structural counterexamples** (`05-small-budget-minimax.tex`). The full minimax formula follows from matching one-sided and omitted-mode constructions in its stated spare-mode range. Equal-mass consequences remain distinct from unrestricted minimax values. In `prop:three-mode-failure`, the component slope bound gives the capped inverse Lipschitz constant four; the first and second reach changes are bounded by (4\delta) and (20\delta). The final threshold comparison uses (21\delta). Terminal support forces the two relevant modes into repeated-word candidates; the middle-service inequality excludes those words at the attained threshold. The explicit 021 schedule attains (18673/18396). This proves an instance optimum and a minimax lower bound, not an exact three-mode minimax value. The prescribed-heavy and prescribed-pair-position examples have the narrower grid scope claimed in their text.

- **General budgets and predecessor results** (`06-general-budgets.tex`; `07-predecessors-and-frontier.tex`). I checked both mass-selection branches in mode removal, the constant-mode branch, positivity of the shortened horizon/error, and preservation of distinctness. The transformed recurrence telescopes correctly, including its zero and negative seed cases. The closed coefficient, plateau factor, sufficient seeded plateau test, and asymptotic coefficients agree with independent algebra. The general bounds establish the sharp first correction but do not assert an unresolved second-order minimax coefficient. The all-light construction gives the stated boundary results. In particular, the inequality ((n-k)^2\le k) implies sufficient reach for **every** equal-mass input, and the omitted-mode argument gives the matching instance lower bound. The third-largest-mass lemma and its example remain useful input-dependent information after the older global coefficients are subsumed. The classical small-mode consequence uses an unrestricted CIA upper bound on a grid of (k) cells; it does not import that source's tightness assertion into the hard-budget problem.

- **Arbitrary-grid minimax and three-mode chambers** (`08-finite-grid-one-switch.tex`; `09-three-mode-floor-chambers.tex`). I checked both closed LP families, their obstruction and coverage arguments, interpolation of feasible cumulative data, and the limiting treatment of cutoff equality. The high-family reduction and low-family elimination supply the stated maximum formula and rational reconstruction. The number of scalar coordinates is independent of the mode count, supporting the bit-complexity and compressed-extremizer claims. The three-mode residue formula includes its exceptional one-cell case and both nonzero residues. The all-length floor-history representation has incoming and outgoing edges at every node; averaging complete paths produces strict fractional prefixes with valid simplex increments. The exact finite enumeration then has complete scope. I independently verified the five-, six-, and seven-cell distributions, the exceptional six-history permutation orbit, all three repair words, and the lower witnesses. The averaging inequality for the repairs gives the exact seven-cell value (4/3). The separate continuous lower constructions and grid-domination argument yield (F_{3,2}(T)=T/5) and only the stated band (T/7\le F_{3,3}(T)\le T/6).

- **Instance algorithms** (`10-instance-algorithms.tex`). I checked the terminal-mass exchange behind the three candidates, fixed initial mode and permitted boundary contracts, the crossing search, and signed residual completion. The subset recurrence includes empty and disconnected subsets; the omitted masses are not lost. Enumerating exactly (\min(s+1,N)) positive nominal blocks covers shorter controls by subdivision. The operation and traceback bounds follow from the actual loops and the (3^k) transition count. Dwell tests apply to maximal adjacent assigned runs, including horizon endpoints, and remain invariant under subdivision. General coupled transitions are correctly excluded. The role-candidate exchange does not assume a label is unused without accounting for the other roles. The continuous LP enumerates all words and ordered closed input-cell assignments, allows (k>N), and covers coincident switches and repeats. Its endpoint inequalities are sufficient. Bounded feasible lower-dimensional polytopes still have vertices with (k) independent active inequalities, so the stated rational vertex enumeration is complete. Fixed-budget polynomial bit complexity is supported; FPT in the switch budget alone is not claimed.

- **Transfer and coarsening** (`11-transfer-and-coarsening.tex`). Integral prefix rounding gives strict endpoint error below one cell, including zero error at integral prefixes. Positive occupation permits an interior original-block witness in each cell; chronological source-block indices give the switch-count bound. Whole-cell monotonicity extends the discrepancy estimate between endpoints. Strict minimax transfer uses an attained grid maximizer rather than taking a supremum of strict pointwise bounds. The binary nonuniform proof uses supported choices and handles pure cells. The sharp coefficient family and repeated-cycle extension establish instance gaps without claiming an exact minimax gap. Exact integration over the common refinement justifies nonaligned coarsening. The certificate's open endpoint, zero clipping, nested fine-grid use, additive complexity, and perturbation term (h+2\delta) have the stated contracts. The odd-grid dwell example correctly demonstrates failure of convergence under the specified minimum-run rules.

- **Higher reach appendix** (`14-higher-reach.tex`). The general exclusion inequality has a valid coefficient sign in its stated mode range. Its additional weighted premise is explicitly unproved. The negative event assignment is not promoted to a counterexample control. Coordinatewise chronology is sufficient for interpolation of allocations, but the text explicitly does not infer inverse-reach identities from interpolation. The strengthened certificate applies to one fixed event order and maximizer pattern. Its dual residual sign and matching uniform primal prove precisely that chamber minimum. Canonical row normalization makes the supplied certificate independent of builder insertion order. Nothing in the main results depends on solving other chambers.
