Exact Arithmetic in Polynomial Optimization:
Values, Optimizers, and Certificates

The anonymous manuscript starts at main.tex. All sources and references are
included. No repository files, figures, external data, or custom styles are
required.

Recommended build (from this directory):

    latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex

Alternatively:

    pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
    bibtex main
    pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
    pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex

The supplied main.bbl contains the formatted bibliography. With that file,
repeated pdflatex runs can build the manuscript without running BibTeX.

Use a standard TeX Live or MiKTeX installation with pdfLaTeX and BibTeX.
Required packages: fontenc, inputenc, lmodern, geometry, amsmath, amssymb,
amsthm, mathtools, mathrsfs, booktabs, array, longtable, tabularx, enumitem,
microtype, graphicx, natbib, url, and hyperref. The bibliography style is
the standard plainnat style.
