## Section 01 proof correction

`rem:models-socp` (`01:454–491`). Tarasov–Vyalyi Theorem 3 proves
equivalence of the predicate `v(S1) ≥ v(S2)` across bases, and their
Theorem 4 reduces that predicate to semidefinite feasibility. The earlier
remark used the strict predicate `A > B`. That version is reachable only
through an unstated complement step: `PosSLP` reduces to its complement, and
the complement of `≥` is `<`. The remark now reduces `PosSLP` to `A ≥ B`,
because an integer is positive exactly when it is at least one. It replaces
the two-by-two block with the variable `s` by the linear inequality
`d ≥ e`, and it justifies dual attainment by the conic duality theorem under
strict primal feasibility and finite value. I rechecked each step:
monotonicity of feasible points, attainment of the primal value at the gate
values, weak duality in both directions, existence of a strictly feasible
point, and the second-order cone form of two-by-two semidefinite constraints.
