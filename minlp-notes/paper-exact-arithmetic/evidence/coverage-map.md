# Exact-arithmetic source coverage map

This is a source inventory for the manuscript brief, dated 2026-10-05. It records what the repository claims, the available proof and review records, and the relationship between those claims. It is not a new correctness verdict or literature search. No mathematical computation script or experiment was run while preparing it.

The scope is exact comparison, exact optimizer and certificate representation, and the value/point/recourse boundary needed to explain those outputs. All 97 concept rows are in the manuscript scope, including Q13–Q14's common-field, finite-infimum and algebraic-cone developments. Structural optimization results enter only where they supply an exact-arithmetic interface or an explicit output boundary. Every reference below uses an absolute repository path. Dependencies stated by identifier refer to other entries in this map.

“In report” means that the October 3 LaTeX report contains a stated theorem or substantial argument. “Mentioned” means that it gives only a short statement, discussion, or dependency. “Missing” records absence from the October 3 benchmark and the need for new manuscript coverage when this inventory was assembled. Current statement/proof coverage is recorded separately in [final-coverage.md](/workspace/minlp-notes/paper-exact-arithmetic/evidence/final-coverage.md). The benchmark is [the October 3 source](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20261003-arithmetic/document/main.tex), particularly its eight files under `document/sections/`. Internal independent review means review by another research agent. It does not mean journal peer review. A recorded diagnostic is evidence from an earlier run, not a run performed for this inventory.

The principal indexes read were [the root README](/workspace/minlp-notes/README.md), [September 27 README](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20260927/README.md), [September 27 closeout](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20260927/closing-research-results.md), [its scope review](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20260927/closing-research-scope-review.md), [September 28 algebra README](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20260928/algebra/README.md), [October 2 closeout](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20261002/CLOSEOUT.md), [October 3 README](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20261003-arithmetic/README.md), and [October 3 progress/closeout](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20261003-arithmetic/PROGRESS.md).

## 8. Corrected and failed attempts that must stay distinct

1. **Naive gate lifting is not globally convex.** For x²+x⁴+ε(y−x²)², the xx Hessian entry at (0,1/ε) is −2. A nonsingular residual Jacobian at the zero does not fix this. [The arithmetic-reduction review](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20260927/quartic-exact-arithmetic-reductions-review.md) also explains why one-real-conjugate field closure does not imply a polynomial-time invocation of a dense realization theorem for a succinct root tower, and why preserving an unscaled repeated-square coordinate conflicts with a short normalized radius bound. A3 and A10 solve restricted sign/realization tasks; they do not validate the failed unrestricted lifting argument.

2. **General degenerate quartic upper bound remains unproved.** [Degeneracy frontier](/workspace/minlp-notes/research-20260928/algebra/degeneracy-frontier.md) proves one exact tractable case: if a globally convex quartic has an optimizer with entirely zero Hessian, its optimizer set is the rational affine space D³f=0, recoverable by rational linear algebra. It also gives three separate counterexamples: ordinary Newton only converges linearly for x⁴; a convex bivariate quartic has a Newton map discontinuous at its partially degenerate optimizer and defeats 3N∘N−2N; and a convex ternary quartic has an irrational Hessian-kernel line. Infinitesimal quadratic regularization supplies a valid value estimate but not polynomial-size initialization/refinement. The note records second-agent reconstruction and symbolic diagnostics; no PosSLP upper theorem for all convex quartics follows. This positive preprocessing proposition and all three counterexamples are missing from the October report.
