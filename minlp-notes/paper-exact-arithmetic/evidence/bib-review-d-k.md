## Key issues

- Jiang2021 has a material key-year mismatch: it names the rational-minimizers source, whose journal version is from 2023, while SODA 2021 published a different integral-minimizers work.
- GaertnerMatousekRustSkovron2008 is an alias for manifest key GaertnerMatousekRuestSkovron2008. Use the latter as the single bibliography key.
- JeyakumarLi2014’s 2014 suffix has no publication date established here; the cited article is online from 2013 and in the 2015 journal issue.
- FranekRatschanZgliczynski2016’s issue year is 2016 and online year is 2015; the specific arXiv version used for theorem pinpoints remains unresolved.
- DavisPapp2022’s local source was downloaded from an unversioned arXiv URL. Hatcher2002 and HildebrandGoess2024 have unread KB source packages. KojimaKimWaki2005 has a local source package for an unread 2003 report rather than the cited 2005 journal article.
- DelPia2023 and KolmogorovNaldiZapata2024 use preprint-year key suffixes; their records give the final journal year and identify the preprint/version distinction.
