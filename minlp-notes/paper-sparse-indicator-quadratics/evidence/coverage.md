# Repository source and claim map

Paths below are relative to the repository root. This map groups mathematical
claims, rather than treating every intermediate note as a separate result.
Historical review verdicts are evidence about their drafts, not validation of
this manuscript. In particular, the two old complete-message reviews do not
pin the exact reviewed text by digest.

The primary theorem handles prescribed bounded boxes by solving each message
directly. It does not need the invariant-domain property required by a recursive
message algorithm. A dictionary containing all active support labels, a necessary-formula envelope,
and connected winning regions are distinct outputs. Corollary `cor:subdivision`
constructs a sign-invariant subdivision with complete argmin labels; its cells
need not be maximal connected winning regions. Corollary `cor:atomicregions`
separately counts those actual regions. The sharper continuous scalar/planar
bounds and discrepancy transfer remain useful for their better constants.

The accompanying `paper-sparse-indicator-quadratics/checks/spectral_reference.py`
implements the primary spectral algorithm: direct supplied-decomposition grid
DP, continuous support reoptimization, certified partition enumeration,
parameter nets, full noisy quadratics, and the original-objective certificate.
`paper-sparse-indicator-quadratics/checks/check_spectral.py` compares this implementation with independent grid
enumeration and exact support solves on small instances. The finite boundary
probes do not replace the proof of completeness over a box. CAD, the direct
recursive SDD algorithms, and the analytic geometry arguments are not
implemented. The three other checkers retain their separate count, lower-bound,
moment, and discrepancy checks; their exhaustive subroutines are diagnostic
comparators, not the spectral algorithm's optimization oracle.
