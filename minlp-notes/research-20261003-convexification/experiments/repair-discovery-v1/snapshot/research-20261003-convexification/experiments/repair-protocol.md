# Matched discovery-correctness supplement

The original prospective campaign completed all 282 jobs. Preserve every
original record and report that campaign separately. It exposed two defects:
using all source variables in dense polynomial construction crashed four
large-diagnostic workers, and the existing discovery time budget was checked
only after complete model discovery instead of between its units of work.

Before any corrected optimization outcome, freeze the following selection
rule. Select every original model/phase/seed group where an all/auto record:

- spent more time in discovery than
  `min(config.max_separation_seconds, config.separation_budget_fraction *
  (nominal time_limit - preparation_seconds))`; or
- failed with the identified `split_affine` global-generator recursion error.

For each selected group, repeat all three original modes with the same
phase, seed, time limit, node limit, process limit and relative mode order.
The completed original records produce **25 groups, 75 jobs and 50 triggering
records**. Their soft budgets sum to 1,575 seconds. The exact criteria,
triggering measurements, original hashes, ordered jobs and freeze timestamp
are saved in `repair-plan.json`, generated by `freeze_repair_plan.py` and
independently checked by the experiment reviewer.

The repairs use only symbols present in a term for polynomial construction,
keep exact nonrational coefficients outside the rational affine part, and
check the already configured discovery deadline between rows and groups.
They introduce no instance-name filter, new size threshold, activation policy
or parameter value. A single nonpreemptive symbolic or exact support operation
can still overshoot a soft budget. Measure and report that residual; do not
claim a hard real-time limit.

After independent semantic and replay clearance, `run_repair.py` copies the
corrected source and unchanged original model files into a separate output
directory. It runs one worker and one solver/BLAS thread at a time after the
original campaign has finished. It preserves the original source manifest,
all failed/unknown original cut logs, its own source manifest, the amendment,
all six original protocol fields and independent checks. Complete every job;
do not replace unfavorable outcomes.

This is a correctness-regression cohort selected by observed failures and
budget violations. It is **not** a new prospective holdout, and its records
must not be spliced into the original 30-model result table. Independently
replay the saved added cuts, recheck original-model incumbents and all metrics,
and report corrected outcomes and remaining limitations in their own table.
