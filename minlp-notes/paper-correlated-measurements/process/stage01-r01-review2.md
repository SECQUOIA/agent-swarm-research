2. **MINOR — specify which quantity has the stated supremum.**
   File: `sections/01-foundations.tex:345–348`.
   The displayed construction is correct. Its efficiency approaches `1/alpha` from above, so this is an infimum of efficiency; the corresponding log-information loss approaches `log(alpha)` from below, giving a supremum of loss. The phrase “the bound is sharp as a supremum” immediately after the efficiency discussion does not identify the latter quantity.
   Fix: write that the efficiency lower bound is sharp as an infimum, or explicitly say that the log-information loss has supremum `log(alpha)`. No proof change is needed.
