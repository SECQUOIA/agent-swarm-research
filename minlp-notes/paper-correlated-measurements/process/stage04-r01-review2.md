## Finding

The present phrase “compact convex relaxation of the feasible indicators” alone also permits sets outside the cube, whereas the stated global concavity domain, nonnegative virtual variances, and subsequent all-split common-point argument are on the cube. All examples and intended applications already have the required containment, so this is a hypothesis clarification rather than a substantive failure. The covariance innovations and reverse-adjoint derivation yield the same resolvent rows, including zero-weight rows.
