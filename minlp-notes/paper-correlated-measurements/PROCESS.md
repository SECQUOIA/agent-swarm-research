# Correlated measurement selection manuscript process

This manuscript is being prepared under the user's staged authoring and review
protocol. Internal research-agent reviews are not external peer review.

## Stages

1. Scope inventory, primary-source comparison, observation model and foundations.
2. Exact information representations, finite-history error bounds, spacing,
   general covariance and partial observations.
3. Approximation guarantees: weighted trace, relative spectral sets and
   rationally represented matroids, with complete supporting proofs.
4. Relaxation comparisons, rational certificates, robust standardization and
   the latent-separator hierarchy.
5. Computational investigation, independent validation, and portable supplement.
6. Integrated introduction, discussion, complete coverage and submission packaging.
7. Independent whole-manuscript review and final corrections/validation.

Each development stage has one author followed by five independent reviewers.
The coordinator adjudicates all findings. A separate correction author addresses
every accepted finding. Any accepted major issue triggers another five-reviewer
round after corrections; accepted minor issues are resolved before proceeding.
The same review and correction gate applies to the complete manuscript.

## Evidence policy

Mathematical proofs, finite exact checks, solver-numerical outcomes, rational
certificates, and internal reviews are distinguished. Numerical certificates
apply to their stated input model, not to unverified physical assumptions.
Publication priority is qualified by the precise sources actually inspected.
Historical experiment timings retain their original provenance. Fresh experiments
report their own configuration, failures and complete cost accounting.
