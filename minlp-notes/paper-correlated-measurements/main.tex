\noindent\textit{Formal verification scope.}
Lean reference constructions accompany the explicit-DAG and represented-matroid
spectral covers in Sections~\ref{subsec:spectral-set}
and~\ref{subsec:matroid-cover}, with proved correctness, exact singular ranges,
and bit-work bounds from rational inputs. The DAG core takes verified
topological vertex indices; the matroid core computes row reduction from
the supplied representation. The upstream stochastic graph construction
is outside this scope. The algebraic finite-memory transfer is conditional
on its stated sandwich assumption.
