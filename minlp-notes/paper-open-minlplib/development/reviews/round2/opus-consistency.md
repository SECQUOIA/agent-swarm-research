(31/9/28+3/14 families, 7+9+15 prior status, 15 staged = 11+4, 12+15+1+3
branching, 158/131/46, 19+12+12+63+25, 22/18/16 LINDO, 129/109 =

    or map a status with any "partial second", "weaker second" or "proved"
