**Round 3 referee review: claim status and consistency**

Reviewed 2026-10-05. Verdict: **minor revision required before treating this as a final revision**. The main family-status corrections are sound, including the downgrade of `ann_cumene_tanh` to *proved*. Five remaining issues concern the scope of verification, the description of a replay, or the evidence behind reported-point comparisons. I found no discrepancy in the ten sampled register numbers. This review does not establish correctness of every checking program or replace a certificate replay.

Paths below are relative to `paper-open-minlplib/`, except paths beginning with `research-20260929/`, which are relative to the repository root. Line references identify the reviewed sources, not proposed replacements. No paper source was edited, no repository script was executed, and no commit was made.

**Definitions applied**

The operative definitions are `sections/02-semantics.tex:183`–202:

- *Proved* includes a written argument or a computer-assisted proof at an identified evidence level. It does **not** require two implementations (`:198`). The trust base includes correctness of checking code; the code is inspected and rerun, not formally verified (`:163`).
- *Verified by a separately written implementation* adds reproduction of the computer-assisted part by a second implementation (`:199`). In the tables, *verified* abbreviates this phrase. *Weaker second* and *partial second* mean *proved*, with weaker or partial additional certification (`tables/tab-trust.tex:11`–14).
- *Computed* is a single-implementation result that is not presented as proved and is not used as a premise (`sections/02-semantics.tex:200`). A single-implementation rigorous enclosure need not be downgraded to *computed*.
- Solver output and published values are *floating-point output* (`:201`); high-precision evaluation and sampling are numerical evidence (`:167`). Interpretation is labelled and is not a premise (`:202`; `sections/09-interpretation.tex:27`–28).
- *Separately written* means later code from a separate agent session, with no import or execution of the first implementation in the checking computation and, as far as the records show, no copied code (`sections/02-semantics.tex:184`–185). Reading the first code is explicitly allowed. The named shared readers and arithmetic cores are exceptions expressly disclosed at `:189`–191.

The current definition differs from the reading-based rule requested in `development/reviews/round2/adjudication.md:207`. `development/build-r4.md:441`–450 explicitly records this as an unresolved lead-author decision. I used the definition actually printed in the paper. Reading first code is therefore not, by itself, grounds for downgrading a current *verified* label. Before submission, the authors should record their decision on that definition; adopting the adjudication's alternative requires the broader status review described in the build record.

**Inconsistencies and concrete fixes**

1. **The tighter EMFL enclosures are labelled verified, although the other implementation proves wider enclosures.** `sections/I-reproduction.tex:216`–219 gives one *verified* status to the spring optimum, EMFL enclosures and rocket refutations. `artifact/claims.json:15392` repeats that status for `register-23`. But `sections/E-audit.tex:412` claims the first-line, tighter EMFL intervals, while `:436`–439 gives wider intervals from the audit implementation, and `:452` expressly says the displayed enclosures lie inside the audit's. For example, the claimed `emfl050_5_5` enclosure is `[18.9136329557291, 18.9136329557624]`; the other implementation establishes only `[18.9136329529, 18.9136329563]`. The latter does not verify the former. Even the third check of `emfl050_3_3` at `:445` has a weaker lower end than the claimed first-line enclosure.

   **Fix:** split or qualify the register row: spring optimum and rocket refutations *verified*; the displayed EMFL enclosures *proved*, with a separately written implementation establishing wider enclosures. Preserve *verified* for the qualitative statements about listed dual bounds and unattainable listed primal values where both enclosures establish them. Update `artifact/RUNS.md` R23 and the builder's component/status mapping, then regenerate `claims.json`; the current empty per-instance maps for R23 cannot express this distinction. Alternatively, report a common enclosure certified by both implementations, if changing the displayed enclosure is desired. Do not infer verification of the tighter enclosure merely from overlap.

2. **The pricing status has an unresolved scope distinction, and the “one implementation” description undercounts the archived checking evidence.** Table 1's *verified* label is appropriate for the **dual bound** (`tables/tab-trust.tex:30`). The register instead cites the entire theorem (`sections/I-reproduction.tex:168`–171); that theorem includes exact feasibility, the primal end and the gap (`sections/05-other.tex:107`–108). `artifact/claims.json:3881` includes the primal value, while `:3989` applies *verified* to the row without a component qualification. Yet the paper says the pricing primal claim has one implementation (`sections/02-semantics.tex:193`; `sections/06-points.tex:77`; `sections/B5-small.tex:358`, `:367`; `tables/tab-points-all.tex:29`) and the points register maps it to *proved* (`sections/I-reproduction.tex:211`). As written, the reader cannot tell whether R11 verifies the dual statement only or the whole enclosure theorem.

   There is also stronger archived evidence than the single-code wording acknowledges. `development/dossiers/small-checks/pricing_check.py:104`–125 and `development/dossiers/small-checks/r2/pricing_check.py:150`–168 both check the **same saved 17-digit point**, its bounds, its five row slacks and its exact objective. Their logs record positive row slacks and the value `-1813.8290784519730769`. `development/dossiers/small.md:724` credits both the earlier dossier and the current dossier; `:571`–577 distinguishes their implementations. The point constructed by `research-20260929/reviews/wave2-small-verification/v_pricing050.py:255`–297 is a different, 25-digit point and should not be described as a recheck of the saved point.

   **Fix:** reconcile the archived checker/session provenance before assigning one status to the whole theorem. If the earlier and later saved-point checkers meet the printed definition, name both, change the pricing primal wording to two implementations, and make its points-register status *verified*. If that provenance is not established, retain the conservative primal *proved* label and mark R11's whole-theorem status *proved*, explicitly adding “dual bound verified; primal feasibility proved by one credited implementation.” Table 1 can remain *verified* because it concerns the dual bound. Apply the same component scope to JSON and `RUNS.md`. ETAMAC's *weaker second* dual label and its conservative *proved* primal label are mutually consistent; its different point constructions should likewise remain distinguishable.

3. **The ANN replay is called separately written although its checking computation imports and calls the original certifying implementation.** `sections/B9-ann-kan.tex:65` says “A separately written replay of both runs”. In fact `research-20260929/reviews/ann-extension-review-checks/replay.py:3`–6 describes use of the original bounding code as a black box; `:43`–48 loads the original snapshot/current modules, and `:97` calls `M.fbound_combo`. This use is part of reconstructing the proof partition, not an unused side comparison. It fails the printed criterion at `sections/02-semantics.tex:185`.

   **Fix:** replace that sentence's opening with “A replay driver written in a later agent session, using the first implementation's bounding routines, reproduced …”. State that the coverage argument trusts this reconstruction. This leaves the ANN *proved* label unchanged. The later per-region bounding code is already correctly described as not separately written because it copied the saturation idiom (`sections/B9-ann-kan.tex:147`, `:150`, `:164`).

4. **Some “proved” returned-point comparisons rest on ordinary high-precision objective evaluation, without a rigorous objective enclosure.** The clearest cases are ETAMAC: `artifact/claims.json:25130` and `:25241` assert that the BARON/Gurobi returned points lie below the certified bound, with *proved* labels at `:25227` and `:25338`; `reported-08` does the same for the listed point (`:20947`, status at `:21041`). The campaign data come from `research-20260929/publication/solver-runs/check_points.py:37`–40, which evaluates with ordinary 50-digit mpmath arithmetic and prints 40-digit values. Its evaluator uses ordinary `mpmath.log` (`research-20260929/bound-audit/audit_eval.py:49`–52). The listed-point ETAMAC value is likewise an ordinary evaluation (`research-20260929/reviews/wave2-small-verification/v_etamac.py:347`). The generator treats these output strings as exact rational operands (`data/make_tables.py:1148`–1150 and `:1166`–1181), and the claim builder assigns *proved* unconditionally (`artifact/build_claims.py:327`). This proves a comparison of the stored **numerals**, not an error bound for the objective evaluated at the corresponding point.

   The distinction matters because `sections/H-solvers.tex:74`–75 labels the residuals numerical evidence but says point infeasibility follows from the objective values. Under `sections/02-semantics.tex:167`, numerical evaluation alone cannot establish the rigorous objective bound needed for that inference. The large separation compared with likely 50-digit error makes the conclusion plausible, but does not supply the missing enclosure.

   **Fix:** enclose the ETAMAC objectives at the saved/listed points with outward interval logarithms, store the enclosure endpoints and sources, and derive the margin from the appropriate endpoint. For other evaluated-point entries, use exact objective evaluation where the objective is rational/polynomial, or a rigorous enclosure where needed. Alternatively, keep *proved* only for the explicitly scoped comparison of reported decimal numerals, label the actual-point evaluation *computed*, and remove the claim that this evaluation proves point infeasibility. This is not a request to relabel all reported solver numbers as proved: the raw solver output remains *floating-point output*. The generic builder should distinguish these claim components rather than rely on the word “comparison”.

5. **The display-check claim is broader than its evidence, and its evidence-level cell is empty.** `sections/I-reproduction.tex:232`–235 labels “All displayed numbers” *proved* with level `--`. `artifact/claims.json:19303` and `artifact/RUNS.md:441` say every displayed numerical value has a directed-rounding check. The generator's actual promise is narrower: every **generated certified** bound, primal, gap and margin display (`data/make_tables.py:13`–21). Solver outputs, run times, metadata and numerical observations also appear in the paper; they are not all directed certified bounds, and R26 correctly labels the campaign output *floating-point output*. `sections/J-displays.tex:9`–11 repeats the overly broad wording.

   **Fix:** use “Directed rounding of generated certified displays” for R27 and its machine-readable statement; distinguish separately checked handwritten certified displays, literal solver-output strings and numerical evidence in S7.5. Give the display-preservation check an evidence level, such as *stored*, rather than `--`, while preserving the underlying certificate's own status and trust base. Regenerate the register artifact after correcting `RUNS.md` and the source row.

**ANN: why proved is justified**

There is no logical implication “not separately written, therefore only computed” in the paper's definitions. S1.9 supplies reduction, coverage and per-box bounding arguments (`sections/B9-ann-kan.tex:57`–63, `:70`–112), a finite target `-7447080719734483 * 2^-41`, and a computer-assisted argument that every region meets that target (`:120`–132). Its rounding assumptions, transcendental enclosures and saturation correction are stated at `:136`–151. The archived region logs report success on 278,495 first-run regions, 1,365,615 second-run regions and 208,223 unsplit boxes, matching the total 1,852,333. These are recorded evidence, not replays performed for this review.

Thus *proved* at the *rerun* level is warranted conditional on the disclosed trust base and coverage reconstruction. Copying the saturation idiom prevents the second bounding code from adding the paper's stronger *verified* status; it does not invalidate a computer-assisted proof. Table 1 (`:37`), the main certificate sentence (`sections/05-other.tex:294`), S1.9 (`sections/B9-ann-kan.tex:150`, `:164`), R18 (`sections/I-reproduction.tex:199`), JSON (`artifact/claims.json:7975`) and the abstract/introduction all agree. Do not restore *verified* or change the result to *computed* merely to address finding 3.

**AI-use disclosure**

The substantive AI-use sentences are identical in `sections/02-semantics.tex:177` and `sections/11-conclusion.tex:53`. They disclose instance selection, constructions, proofs, code, re-implementations, checks, experiments, audit, literature search and drafting. The records supporting that breadth are explicitly described in `development/reviews/round2/adjudication.md:103`–116. The paper does not invent a model identifier for every session (`sections/02-semantics.tex:178`), does not imply external human review (`:179`; `sections/11-conclusion.tex:26`), and states the shared-author/shared-filesystem/read-first risks (`sections/02-semantics.tex:187`, `:204`). This is internally consistent with the printed code-based definition and its shared-component exceptions.

Two submission actions remain, not additional mathematical inconsistencies: resolve the alternative definition recorded in `development/build-r4.md:441`, and replace the explicit author-confirmation placeholder in `sections/11-conclusion.tex:56` with a truthful statement of which proofs/code the authors themselves checked. “The authors take full responsibility” does not itself establish that they personally checked them. This review cannot supply that information. Also avoid interpreting the disclosure's ordinary phrase “re-implemented them separately” as a blanket technical certification that every re-implementation satisfies the defined term; the existing qualifications and finding 3 should remain visible.
