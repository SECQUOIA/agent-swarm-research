               Verified bounds for positive cubic relaxation gaps


                                                     Abstract
        For multilinear polynomials of degree at most three with nonnegative coefficients, we compare
     the sum of the individual monomials’ envelope widths with the width of the full graph hull.
     Over all finite nonnegative boxes, the supremum of this ratio lies between 1610000/743033 and
     31/12. The upper bound uses a fixed mixture of three probability laws that preserves every
     coordinate marginal and works for all terms at once. Its weights are optimal within that three-law
     family for a uniform termwise guarantee. An explicit integer-coefficient family gives the lower
     bound through a rational Bernstein certificate. The accompanying Lean project connects both
     arguments to the actual graph hulls and includes exact finite witnesses. The exact supremum
     remains undetermined.

3.2   Bilinear terms and boxes
For two means u ≤ v, T = min(u, 1 − v) and DO = T /2. If both are low, DI ≥ T /2. If just one
is low, DB = min(u, 2(1 − v))/2 ≥ T /2. If both are high, DB = T /2 and DI = uT ≥ T /2. The
mixture therefore captures at least 12T /31 in every case. Constant and singleton terms have zero
gap. Summing the common-law inequalities proves the cube bound.
    For a nonnegative box, substitute xi = ℓi + (ui − ℓi )yi . Each original positive monomial expands
into nonnegative cube monomials of degree at most three. The sum of the expanded term widths is
at least the original term width: taking a supremum of a sum is bounded by the sum of suprema,
and taking an infimum has the reverse inequality. The full hull width is preserved by the affine
change of coordinates. This remains true with zero-width coordinates. Apply the cube result to the
expansion to prove the upper bound in Theorem 1.

7    Formal verification and boundaries of the result
The standalone project in formal/ contains the dependency closure of the canonical proofs. Its
claim map identifies declarations for the graph-hull interpretation, the three actual laws, scalar
inequalities, all-box transfer, fixed-mixture obstruction, finite analytic family, limit and supremum,
and finite witnesses. The verification record gives the pinned Lean and Mathlib versions, commands,
module and declaration counts, axiom audit, kernel replay, and source fingerprints. Source export
identity and the paper build are checked separately. Standard axioms propext, Classical.choice,
and Quot.sound are allowed; unfinished proofs and additional axioms are rejected.
    Formal proofs establish the stated mathematical claims about the specified relaxations. They
do not establish publication priority, determine the exact value of R(3), or cover every recursive
relaxation. General coefficient removal, homogeneous approximation of the supremum, equal-
marginal classification, and the general two-group asymptotic family are separate results outside
this paper. The finite two-group examples above require none of those extensions.
