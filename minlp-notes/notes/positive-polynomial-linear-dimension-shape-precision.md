# Separable convex graph precision

Promoted after two independent proof audits and a focused literature review.

See [the complete result](../results/separable-convex-graph-linear-dimension-precision.md):
finite gaps of `7r` for continuous convex scalar sums and `4r` for independent
outputs, with polynomial rational constructions giving gaps of `12r` and `9r`
for dense positive polynomials.
