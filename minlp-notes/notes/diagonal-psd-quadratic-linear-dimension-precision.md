# Diagonal PSD quadratic precision

The independently reviewed theorem is now in
[results/diagonal-psd-quadratic-linear-dimension-precision.md](../results/diagonal-psd-quadratic-linear-dimension-precision.md).
It proves a trace-allocation characterization with additive `O(r)` error
and a polynomial rational construction using at most `p_conv+5r+1`
binaries. Two proof audits and a source/novelty assessment are linked there.
