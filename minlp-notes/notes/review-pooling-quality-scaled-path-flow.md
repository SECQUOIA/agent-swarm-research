# Independent review: quality-scaled contracted pooling

Date: 2026-09-05. Reviewer: `pooling_degree_two`.

**PASS** for the complete scalar theorem and the fixed-boundary lemma in
[the author note](pooling-quality-scaled-path-flow.md), including its
all-candidate cut formulation. This review is independent of the author's
implementation and of the separate review by `benders_review`.

I checked the physical elimination in both directions. Summing exact
source and product contracts gives actual pool mass and quality balances;
zero throughput forces every nonnegative pool arc to vanish. Missing
pool arcs retain a zero-flow constraint. A restrictive common pool bound
cannot be inferred from these identities and is explicitly excluded.

For nonzero `gamma_i=C_i-q`, signed scaling is invertible. Input bounds
become node-divergence intervals. At a two-inlet product, solving the
quality equation and bypass-total equation gives exactly
`w_1=gamma_1[R-gamma_2 s]/(C_1-C_2)`. The unequal-quality denominator is
a nonzero rational constant. Equal-quality, one-inlet, and isolated
product cases in the note retain the missing conditions and avoid
division by zero. All singular pool qualities are handled by the original
fixed-quality LP, so no transformed limit is used as a substitute.

The signed Hoffman inequalities have the correct orientation. Adding
root-to-node arcs with divergence bounds proves necessity and sufficiency
by ordinary circulation. Node sums and cut terms add over disconnected
induced subsets, so connected subsets suffice. A path or cycle has
quadratically many such subsets, and each cut crosses at most two arcs.
Effective upper capacities are minima and effective lower capacities
are maxima. Thus imposing every candidate combination is exactly the
required conjunction in both cut inequalities, including negative bounds.
Lower-versus-upper candidate comparisons separately ensure valid arcs.

After this expansion, every sign interval has polynomially many rational
quadratic inequalities. Rational sign-cell samples and quadratic roots
give the claimed degree-two parameter witness. The incidence system has
an integral coefficient matrix; a bounded feasible vertex is a rational
linear combination of its bounds. Recovery therefore stays in the single
field generated by the selected pool quality. Polynomial encoding is
preserved by the one division per original bypass flow.

The boundary extension correctly removes prescribed arcs, shifts their
signed contributions from both node-divergence bounds, and retains all
their capacity conditions separately. This leaves the cut inequalities
linear in boundary coordinates and quadratic in the quality parameter.
The affine-rank-one reduction also passes: exact mass conservation
cancels the common affine offset, and positive-demand product vectors
must lie on the input affine line. Rank zero reduces to an LP.

The author's finite checker provides additional evidence, distinct from
