Closed at the user's request on 2026-09-04. This is an internal assessment of
mathematical substance and potential impact. Independent review means another
research agent checked the work. None of these notes has received external peer

My strongest overall research program is the **positive multilinear gap
characterization**. It refutes the universal-constant conjecture and replaces it
with sharp asymptotic laws in degree, dimension, incidence width, marginal
interiority, and positive-box aspect ratio. The verified results include leading
constant one in these parameterizations, a joint characterization, and exact
incidence-treewidth-two constant two. The positive-box theorem now gives the
finite bounds `max{2,rho} <= C_box(rho) <= rho+2`, with an additional reviewed
finite-dimensional refinement. The explicit cubic counterexamples and exact
equal-marginal companion identify where constant-two intuition fails. The new
two-TU-row partition lemma may be useful beyond scalar gap bounds. Classical
envelope and integrality ingredients are credited separately. See the
[main theorem](../results/positive-multilinear-gap.md),
[joint characterization](../results/positive-multilinear-joint-gap-growth.md),
[width-two theorem](../results/positive-multilinear-treewidth-two-exact.md), and
[positive-box bound](../results/positive-multilinear-positive-box-sharp.md).

The **switching-control program** is the next strongest coherent direction.
The published conjecture has exact counterexamples, and continuous one-, two-,
and three-switch minimax formulas passed independent review. The general
three-switch reach proof uses exact finite and symbolic certificates; that
computer-assisted dependency is explicit. The final analytic universal
heavy-mode theorem gives an exact reduction from full to one-sided minimax for
every block budget `1<=k<n`. Its global consequence establishes a general exact
plateau and the sharp first mode-count correction for arbitrary profiles and
every fixed switching budget. The exact one-sided finite-mode values for larger
budgets remain open. See the [general reduction](../results/cia-universal-heavy-mode-rounding.md)
and [global bounds](../results/cia-arbitrary-switch-global-bound.md).

The **rank-one matrix hull program** gives a different kind of obstruction:
a correlation-polytope face, quantitative face stability, and conic extension
size lower bounds, including approximations at a specified error scale. Its
embedding and source transfers have substantial independent review. The
extension-complexity machinery is established literature; the candidate
contribution is the transfer through the continuous unit-capacity rank-one
block with explicit stability. See the [exact conic result](../results/rank-one-correlation-face-conic-lower-bounds.md)
and [approximate SDP result](../results/rank-one-approximate-sdp-lower-bound.md).

A separate **single-monomial complexity result** proves NP-completeness of exact
midpoint-envelope threshold evaluation on arbitrarily narrow unequal positive
boxes. Its positive rank-one multimarginal optimal-transport consequence answers
an explicit published logarithmic-precision question negatively in the standard
rational bit model unless P=NP. Both the mathematical/source audit and a focused
later-literature check are complete. No matching later resolution was found;
priority remains unestablished. The gap is small: fixed-accuracy hardness,
strong NP-hardness, and unrestricted unit-cost arithmetic conclusions do not
follow. See the [result](../results/positive-box-single-monomial-hardness.md) and
[later-literature assessment](rank-one-mot-precision-later-literature.md).

The final **disjunctive epigraph geometry** result is also worth retaining.
For two rational unit balls, a fixed rational orthogonal change of coordinates
turns the tightest auxiliary-image convexification from arbitrarily inaccurate
into exact. The negative side survives every valid convex strengthening confined
to the chosen auxiliary function space while retaining the coordinate epigraph
links. The positive side has an explicit conic repair. Every section passed
independent review. This is a quantitative representation effect, not a general
coordinate-selection algorithm or a new capsule hull formula. See the
[complete note](common-factor-p-split-rotation-gap.md).

sign and conic-hull results use known destination formulations; positive

transfers transportation universality. Common-factor reciprocal hulls use
classical convex order, and the integer extension gives an explicit compressed
separator. These are retained with their precise constructive consequences and
novelty limits. The reviewed FBBT hardness and convergence results are additional
complexity findings whose primitive-contractor scope matters.
