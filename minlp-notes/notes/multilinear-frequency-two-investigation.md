# Frequency-two multilinear investigation

Date: 2026-09-04.

The complete independently reviewed theorem has moved to [Positive multilinear gaps when every variable appears in at most two terms](../results/positive-multilinear-frequency-two-gap.md). It proves the sharp factor `3/2`, the dual odd-girth refinement `g/(g-1)`, and exactness for bipartite dual graphs. The proof retains the required coverage baseline using a half-integral degree-slab decomposition and Jensen's inequality.

The result applies to the unit cube and boxes with zero lower bounds. No extension to arbitrary positive lower bounds is claimed. The result note records the primary matching foundations, adjacent multilinear-polytope work, the independent audit, and computational checks. Application-level novelty remains unverified.

A further candidate consequence is polynomial-time exact convex-envelope evaluation in this frequency-two class: the binary linear-optimization oracle reduces to minimum-cost edge cover with penalties for uncovered vertices. This consequence still needs a written reduction, independent audit, and literature attribution before promotion.
