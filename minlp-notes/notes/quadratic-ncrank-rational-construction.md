# Rational noncommutative-rank construction: promotion record

Date: 2026-09-05. The independently verified result is now maintained in
[the result file](../results/quadratic-ncrank-rational-construction.md).
Its independent review is
[the rational construction audit](review-quadratic-ncrank-rational-construction.md).

For rational quadratic systems of total input encoding length `s` and
dyadic accuracy `2^(-k)`, the minimum integer dimensions equal `(r/2)k`
up to an additive polynomial in `s`, where `r` is noncommutative rank.
A deterministic algorithm constructs a rational MILP attaining this
rate in time and total encoding length polynomial in `s+k`.

The result uses the established constructive rank algorithm of Ivanyos,
Qiao, and Subrahmanyam and the integral capacity lower bound of Garg,
Gurvits, Oliveira, and Wigderson. Exact primary-source references,
all assumptions, and the proof are in the result file. Publication
priority is unestablished.
