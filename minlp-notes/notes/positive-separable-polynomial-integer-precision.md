# Positive separable polynomial precision

The independently reviewed theorem is now in
[results/positive-separable-polynomial-integer-precision.md](../results/positive-separable-polynomial-integer-precision.md).
It gives a finite allocation law and a polynomial rational MILP with
additive overhead `O(r+sum_i log(D_i))`; dense degrees may grow.
Both proof audits, the source/novelty assessment, and exact checks are
linked there.
