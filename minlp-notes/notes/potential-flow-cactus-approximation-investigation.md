# Multi-entry cactus MPD approximation investigation

Date: 2026-09-05. The complete theorem and proof were promoted to [polynomial additive optimization of quadratic cactus MPD](../results/potential-flow-cactus-additive-optimization.md) after two independent mathematical reviews, root review, a separate bounded novelty audit, and reproducible structural/numerical checks.

The result contains the one-block saturation lemma, at-most-two-free-load refinement, fixed-dimensional algebraic algorithm, and Lipschitz rounding proof for rational nomination output. Its companion [exact arithmetic theorem](../results/potential-flow-cactus-square-root-sum.md) explains why additive precision does not settle arbitrary equality-sensitive thresholds.
