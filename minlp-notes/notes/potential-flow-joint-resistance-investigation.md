# Joint nomination/resistance investigation

Date: 2026-09-05. The theorem has been promoted to [polynomial additive optimization under joint nomination and resistance uncertainty](../results/potential-flow-joint-resistance.md) after two independent mathematical reviews and a separate bounded novelty audit.

It applies to arbitrary shifted rational nomination intervals and independent positive resistance boxes on graphs with fixed maximum block cycle rank. The proof combines the resistance-independent nomination-face family with the fixed-core/polyhedral-leaf theorem. The older, narrower [resistance sign-pattern route](potential-flow-joint-resistance-sign-patterns.md) remains archived as useful exploratory evidence.

The [exact arc-flow capacity corollary](../results/potential-flow-exact-arc-capacity.md) is separately stated and included in both reviews.
