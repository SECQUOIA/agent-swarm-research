# Nonconvex polynomial binary-versus-integer gap

Promoted after two independent full proof audits and a focused literature review.
See [the complete paired theorem](../results/polynomial-graph-binary-integer-degree-gap.md):
a rational polynomial family has fixed-error general-integer count at most two
and binary count within one of `ceil(log2 M)`. Finite and compact upper bounds
show that logarithmic degree overhead is the correct worst-case order.
