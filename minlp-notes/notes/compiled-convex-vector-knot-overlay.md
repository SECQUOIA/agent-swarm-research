# Compiled convex-vector knot overlay

The complete construction, proof, two independent audits, source credit, and exact checker links are preserved in the promoted [convex-vector theorem](../results/convex-vector-compiled-integer-precision.md).

For dense rational convex polynomial outputs on one input with box errors, it gives a polynomial-size rational MILP with at most `p_conv+11+ceil(log2 m)` binaries. The arbitrary-polynomial extension is recorded in [its own result](../results/polynomial-vector-compiled-integer-precision.md).
