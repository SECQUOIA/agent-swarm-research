# Small-exponent conic formulation separation

The twice-reviewed theorem is promoted to
[the result](../results/small-exponent-milp-soc-encoding-separation.md).
It contrasts rational MILP encoding length `Theta(2^B)` with polynomial-size
rational MISOCP encoding for the fixed-error graph of `x^(1/2^B)`, while
four binaries suffice in both constructions. The exact conic constraints,
standard rational encoding, and source qualifications remain explicit.
