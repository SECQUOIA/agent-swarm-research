The independent mean and sensitivity calculation starts from the three mass
balances, including the unobserved product:

\[
x' = Jx,\qquad
J=\begin{pmatrix}-k_1&0&0\\k_1&-k_2&0\\0&k_2&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad x(0)=(A_0,0,0)^T.
\]

For parameter order \(\theta=(\log A_0,\log k_1,\log k_2)\), the sensitivity
matrix obeys

\[
S'=JS+
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-k_1 A&0\\
0&k_1 A&-k_2 B\\
0&0&k_2 B
\end{pmatrix},\qquad
S(0)=\begin{pmatrix}A_0&0&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This gives a constant 12-dimensional augmented linear system. The review
integrates it with DOP853 and checks the observed B row against all 864 saved
sensitivity entries. The largest discrepancy is `1.644e-13`. Mean values agree
within `8.10e-14`; total mass and the derivatives of total mass agree within
`2.23e-16`. A second calculation uses 70-digit matrix exponentials of the same
independently assembled system at 16 candidate rows. The largest discrepancy
from a saved mean or sensitivity decimal is `1.48e-16`. Neither calculation
imports the producer or differentiates its closed-form expression.

The rate-swap caveat is correct. Taking Laplace transforms of the balances gives

\[
\widehat B(s)=\frac{A_0 k_1}{(s+k_1)(s+k_2)}.
\]

Thus replacing \((A_0,k_1,k_2)\) by
\((A_0 k_1/k_2,k_2,k_1)\) preserves the entire B trajectory. Independent ODE
solutions of the original and transformed systems agree within `7.83e-14`
on the saved grids. The saved sensitivity matrices nevertheless have full
column rank at all four nominal instances; local rank does not remove this
global ambiguity. The regularized selected information condition numbers are
about 121 and 126 for the early-peak cases, and 12,266 and 13,056 for the
late-peak cases. The high-precision objective check below covers the latter
cases without relying on their floating-point conditioning.

All dimensions, times, parameter ordering, cardinalities, and covariance
entries match the stated model. In particular, each observation has variance
`0.0025` and standard deviation `0.05`; its first-lag correlation is `0.2`,
while `0.4` is the latent AR(1) correlation. The covariance is positive definite
and can be generated by a stationary scalar Gaussian process with innovation
variance \(P(1-\rho^2)\), plus independent Gaussian observation noise of
variance r. It is an externally specified error model, not a consequence of
the reaction mass balances. The mean-information criterion
\(J_0+F_S^T R_{SS}^{-1}F_S\) is appropriate under the explicitly fixed Gaussian
covariance assumption. The prior is the stated regularizer `0.01 I` in log
coordinates. Holding the per-grid latent correlation fixed when n changes
changes the physical correlation scale, as the application note explains.
