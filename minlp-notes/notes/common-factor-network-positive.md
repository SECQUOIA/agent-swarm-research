# Positive network–simplex investigation

The completed, twice-reviewed theorem and constructive decomposition are maintained
in [the standalone result](../results/network-simplex-cycle-theta-hull.md). It gives
an exact original-space hull on equality-constrained flow networks assembled from
bridges, cycles, and theta blocks, with arbitrary simplex dimension and sparse
products. The arithmetic count, compact-output qualification, and known foundations
are stated there.

The first review is [here](review-common-factor-network-positive.md), and the
second review including the constructive decomposition is
[here](review-network-positive-second.md).

Broader graph directions and the complementary universality obstruction are
tracked in [the network investigation](common-factor-network-simplex.md).
