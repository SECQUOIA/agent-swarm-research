## Bounded developments and reconciliations

The coefficient-regularity candidate is valid. The proof sums the already established fixed-ambient coefficient inequalities with nonnegative binomial-basis coefficients satisfying a_(j+1)<=L a_j for j>=2. It yields a single mixture with total denominator L+1+beta_N for entire cardinality factors. Affine coefficients are irrelevant to widths, L=0 is included, N>=2 is explicit, and at most one nondeterministic coordinate gives zero gap. No optimality or independent priority is asserted.

The general-radix capacity observation also admits exact attainment. The two adjacent digit-reversal resource profiles have the prescribed means after cyclic shifting; mixing at the capacity cutoff attains every upper bound used in the surrogate. This proves the exact formula outside the b>=L simplification, and explains the fixed-radix logarithmic limitation without applying the simplified formula out of range. The 120-case checker is supporting evidence for this analytic completion.

The broader link screen recovered the later bipartite epsilon family, forest canonical-pair and compression lemmas, the asymmetric box predecessor and fixed-I/O optimality proof. All are retained as distinct developed results. The older 7/6 example remains useful as a small rational certificate, while the larger bipartite lower endpoint is now 3/2. Stale open statements about width two were not carried forward.

## Retained limitations

Exact W_k for k>=3, universal balanced/TU partitions at larger width, sharp planar constants, the exact finite positive-box constant, and the unequal-aspect bipartite value in [3/2,2] remain open. The fixed-mixture optimality theorem is not an optimum theorem for polynomial gaps. Generic nonnegative factor payoffs and positive-coefficient monomials remain distinct. The hardness family has one original factor and therefore factorwise width ratio one; its difficulty is exact individual-envelope evaluation. No spatial-certificate conclusion is drawn from these positive gap constructions.
