**Findings.**

1. **R15-01 — MINOR: qualify the introductory common-upper claim by its domain.** Location: frozen `main.tex:44–46`. “For positive multilinear functions” is too broad without “on nonnegative boxes.” The formal propositions later include this condition correctly. An exact counterexample is
   `f = x1*x2*x3 + x2*x3*x4` on `[-1,1] × [0,1]^3`, evaluated at `(-1,1/3,1/3,1/3)`. The first term's upper envelope is zero and the second's is `1/3`, whereas the sum's upper envelope is zero. Indeed, all admissible laws fix `X1=-1`, and the sum is `-X2*X3*(1-X4) <= 0`; a law with disjoint successes of `X2` and `X3` attains zero. Thus the individual upper optima cannot be attained together. This is also the example in [[luedtke2012-some-results-on-the-strength]] p.9. **Repair:** insert “on nonnegative boxes” in the introduction. No change to the formal proposition or proof is needed.

2. **R15-02 — MINOR: finish the tolerance conventions in the certificate definition.** Location: frozen `sections/01-foundations.tex:180–183`. The statement that worse incumbents require weakly higher targets is valid for the relative target only when `theta <= 1`. No range for `theta` is given. For example, with `f*=1`, a worse feasible value `U=2`, and `theta=2`, the target decreases from `-1` to `-2`. In addition, granting `U=f*` in the relative convention requires `f*>0`, since the preceding definition assumes `U>0`. **Repair:** explicitly specify `epsilon >= 0` and `0 <= theta < 1` (or `<=1` if that endpoint is desired), and qualify the best-incumbent statement for relative certificates by `f*>0`. This is a local definition issue; Stage 1 asserts no spatial lower-bound theorem using the omitted ranges.

3. **R15-03 — MINOR: make the cited author-manuscript theorem numbers externally locatable.** Location: frozen `sections/01-foundations.tex:124–125,493` and the `Luedtke2012` bibliography entry. The text deliberately cites Theorems 4–5 and 8 “in the authors' manuscript,” while the only bibliography entry identifies the published article and gives its DOI. The local original does contain those numbered theorems, and the author record acknowledges that published numbering differs. A reader with only the paper cannot identify which manuscript version supplies these locators. **Repair:** cite the corresponding published theorem numbers after checking the published version, or add a stable URL/version reference for the exact author manuscript. The mathematical attribution itself is supported.
