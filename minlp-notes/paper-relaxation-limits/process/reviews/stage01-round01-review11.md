## Independent verification

- **Envelope foundations:** independent endpoint rounding gives `E f(X)=f(x)` for every multiaffine graph point, so the finite vertex graph hull is exact. Its lower/upper polyhedral surfaces yield the claimed boundary continuity. The consecutive failure arcs have union length `min(1,sum(1-x_i))`, including wrapped arcs, giving the single-product lower envelope. The common-threshold law attains every nonnegative monomial upper envelope. Subtracting the anchor marginal gives a nonnegative indicator deficiency. Both independence classes and their endpoint cases satisfy the stated `c_0/2` bound.
- **Box transfer and certificate convention:** expansion keeps all coefficients nonnegative and never increases degree. The full hull is invariant under the affine bijection, while subadditivity of widths gives the original-termwise-to-expanded-termwise inequality in the stated direction. Common upper attainment survives the expansion. The region-cover definition, incumbent targets, and distinction between a specified oracle and stronger certificates are sound for the usual nonnegative absolute tolerance and relative tolerance between zero and one. No spatial lower bound is currently claimed.
- **Induced-cut characterization:** at half means, pairing `s` with `-s` attains the unconstrained quadratic-character extrema while preserving every singleton mean; hence `H=R/2` and `T=L/2`. Outside fixed-one coordinates produce only affine terms. On each cell, the signed equality/complement relation graph either fixes the coordinates to `0,1/2,1` or leaves a perturbable parameter; thus all cell vertices are half-integral. Concavity of `H` and affinity of `T` justify extending the bound from those vertices. Orthogonality makes `R>0` on nonzero support.
- **Upper constants:** polarization gives `R=max_S ||A_{S,T}||_(infinity to 1)` with no missing factor of two. Sharp Khinchin gives each side's row estimate. A cut locally maximal for squared weights gives `X+Y >= sum_i ||a_i||_2/sqrt(2)`, and then `R >= sum_i ||a_i||_2/4`. Fractional-orientation loads of at most `rho` follow from the explicit flow cut capacity `m-|E(U)|+rho|U|`; weighted Cauchy–Schwarz then gives `L <= sqrt(rho) sum_i ||a_i||_2`. The maximum-degree and bipartite constants follow with the stated factors. The separate reverse-order degeneracy proof is retained and valid; it is not silently replaced by the stronger fractional argument.
- **Lower theorem and support quantifiers:** the exponential estimate sums `2^h` signed exponentials after fixing one vertex sign, giving `E Z <= h log(2)/lambda + m lambda/2` and the stated optimized finite bound. Exposing a densest induced face allows every other edge to keep coefficient `+1` or `-1`. Compactness of `L=1` and strict positivity of `R` justify the arbitrary-real center maximum and perturbation to full support without asserting continuity of `c*` at disappearing edges. The diluted-core, coefficient-rescaling, and arboricity consequences are correct.
- **Exactness and finite witnesses:** the edge sets that cross every cycle evenly are precisely cuts, by the path-parity construction. Thus the signed-cycle criterion follows in both directions. The frustrated unit four-cycle has `L=4`, `R=2`, `rho=1`, and degeneracy two, which certifies the stated finite universal-constant lower bounds.
