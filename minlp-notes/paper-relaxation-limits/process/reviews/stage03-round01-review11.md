1. **R11-1 — MINOR: explicitly reset the separate-group upper values for the asymmetric cutoff.** Location: `sections/appendix-positive-box-predecessors.tex:147–155`, referring back to the definitions at lines 25–32. The symmetric proof defines `C_L` and `C_H` by integrals over `[0,1/2]`. The asymmetric proof permits low means as large as `1-delta` and reuses `C_L`, `C_H`, `A`, and `B` without explicitly redefining those integrals. The intended separate-group common-threshold values are clear from the source proof, but the preceding displayed integral definition cannot literally be retained. For example, at `rho=64`, `delta=1/8`, a low singleton of mean `3/4` would give the inherited `C_L=1+t/2`, while `P_L=1+3t/4`; the asserted nonnegative separate deficiency would then fail. With the intended `C_L=1+3t/4`, it is zero, as required.

   **Repair:** after the new classification, say that `C_L,C_H` are again the common-threshold upper values of the two separate normalized groups, now with
   `C_L=1+integral_0^(1-delta)(rho^L(s)-1) ds` and
   `C_H=1+integral_0^delta(alpha^H(s)-1) ds`.
   Equivalently use `[0,1]` for both integrals. Then define `P_L,P_H` by the same products with the newly classified means. This is a local definition repair; I verified that the asymmetric estimates themselves work with these values.

- Incoming ownership is compatible because only high coordinates are assigned owners, each has one owner per round, and all low anchors use the same threshold variable. Capped-sum subadditivity has the needed direction. For the outgoing degree alternative, the reduced polynomial's deficiency is pointwise bounded by the original total deficiency under every common law; thus a polynomial-level degree bound is enough.
- In the radix family, the two profiles have means `M_s` and `M_(s+1)` and bracket one. They attain the same affine cap at every anchor level, including levels below the cutoff. Digit reversal gives exactly `min(b^j,R)` occupied prefixes; the coordinatewise cyclic shift makes all leaf means equal. The factor elimination counts are `(j-1)+(L-j+1)=L`. The deepest-factor contractions supply the stated complete bipartite minor only in the asserted range. The degeneracy construction deletes degree-two deepest factors before the leaves, and therefore supports `k+1` lower bounds without asserting treewidth `k`.

For the joint lower family, the lower bound on `b` and the upper bound `m<=q/2` avoid any uncontrolled error from raising a rounded radix to the power `L`. - Feedback repair leaves residual singleton masses summing to exactly the residual feedback mass. Conditional forest gluing therefore preserves every repaired factor law.
