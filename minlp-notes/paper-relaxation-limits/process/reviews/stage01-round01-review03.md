## Independent verification

The following checks were analytic unless explicitly described as numerical.

- **Vertices, affine terms, and fixed coordinates.** Independent endpoint rounding preserves the value of every multiaffine polynomial as well as all coordinate means. Hence the full graph hull equals the finite vertex graph hull. Removing fixed coordinates leaves a box of its affine dimension, possibly dimension zero. A singleton box has a unique vertex law and both gaps zero. Substituting fixed nonnegative coordinates preserves positive coefficients; substituting arbitrary fixed values into a bilinear function creates only affine terms in the remaining incident factors.
- **Single products and zero gaps.** Consecutive circular failure intervals work when their total length is exactly one, and when individual lengths are zero or one. Singletons are affine; empty supports are constant and are explicitly discarded before anchors are introduced. When an anchor mean is zero, both its deficiency and termwise width vanish. When all other support means equal one, the same is true. Nonnegative coefficients make the simultaneous deficiency representation valid even with zero coefficients and tied anchor means. The independence argument never divides by a mean or a deficiency.
- **Positive-box direction.** For each original factor `g = sum_q g_q`, `vex g >= sum_q vex g_q` and `cav g <= sum_q cav g_q`; therefore its width is at most the expanded total width. The full objective hull commutes with the coordinate bijection. Common-threshold attainment supplies the reverse inequality needed for exact common upper attainment. These facts also cover expansions with zero coefficients and purely affine residual polynomials.
- **Half-integral grid and its extension.** At grid points, outside zeros kill their incident factors; outside ones add affine terms. Mixing a sign vector with its negative gives every active singleton mean 1/2 and attains each quadratic extreme. This yields `H = R_W/2`, `T = L_W/2`. The cell argument is valid: an unanchored component with consistent equality/complement relations retains a perturbation direction; an inconsistent signed cycle fixes it to 1/2. Consequently all cell vertices are half-integral. Concavity of `H` and cellwise affinity of `T` extend the grid inequality without dividing by `H`, including its zero set.
- **Cut bounds.** Reconstructed `Q(s)-Q(t) = 2 v^T A u` and the converse sign construction. The locally optimal squared-weight cut gives crossing squared mass at least half at each vertex, so the two Khinchin row estimates yield `R >= (sum_i ||a_i||_2)/4`. Fractional loads bounded by induced density imply `L <= sqrt(rho) sum_i ||a_i||_2`; full-row Cauchy–Schwarz gives `L <= 2 sqrt(Delta) R`. Empty rows contribute zero. The theorem proof only divides by graph parameters after requiring nonempty weighted support. The bipartite proof and frustrated four-cycle have the claimed sharp constant.
- **Fixed-graph lower bound and support.** The exponential-moment argument counts at most `2^h` signed terms after global reversal, giving `E Z <= h log(2)/lambda + m lambda/2`. The chosen positive lambda is available because the densest induced graph has an edge. Localization permits arbitrary signs on all other edges. Orthogonality ensures `R > 0` for every nonzero coefficient vector, making the normalized whole-center maximum continuous on `L = 1`. Perturbing zero coefficients there proves the full-support supremum claim without continuity of `c*` across disappearing edges.
- **Exactness and graph consequences.** The two sign classes must each be a cut for `R = L`; their cycle parities are precisely the stated criterion. The property survives induced restriction. The coloring probabilities, complete-graph center ratio for `n >= 2`, arboricity comparison, diluted dense-core example, and scale-invariance argument have the stated constants and scope.
- **Schur transfer.** The rectangle count is valid even when the two index sets overlap: edges inside their intersection are the only ones counted twice. Taking equal densest sets proves equality of the two density parameters. Pairing the sign matrix with the weighted adjacency counts each edge twice. The second polarization uses `p,q` inside the signed cube and yields `||A||_(infinity->1) <= 4R`, hence the stated `4 K_G sqrt(rho)` transfer. No source theorem here implies a formulation-size claim.
