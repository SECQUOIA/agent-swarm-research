## Independent verification

1. **Optimum, chords, covers, and tolerances.** At a vertex of the balance slice, exactly one coordinate is 1/2 and k are one; the penalty is 1/4. Endpoint interpolation bounds any convex univariate underestimator by its chord, including degenerate intervals. The use of an infimum for arbitrary underestimators is necessary and correct. Evaluating chords at a witness leaves only restricted middle coordinates, each contributing less than 1/(2m). Thus a positive target forces strictly more than m(1/2−2ε) endpoint-excluded coordinates. The avoidance argument requires the minimum of the reciprocal bounds, as printed, and never needs independence of H and Z. It applies to overlapping covers. Counting four new nodes per surviving prefix gives 2^(n+2)−3 nodes; the midpoint recurrence gives exactly binomial(n+1,k+1) leaves. The fixed-complement exponent n−k is correct. A tolerance interval contained strictly between k and k+1 has optimum 1/4−δ²; the manuscript imposes both δ<1/2 and ε+δ²<1/4. Charged discarded sets are explicitly included in the cover; closure of an open slab is not silently assumed certified.

2. **Full SDP–RLT.** With c=t/s and d=t(t−1)/(s(s−1)), the free covariance is (c−d)(I−J/s), while all restricted covariance rows vanish. This certifies the full augmented matrix. The distinct-free-index slack products are d, c−d, c−d, and (s−t)(s−t−1)/(s(s−1)); repeated indices give c,0,0,1−c. Deterministic rows also cover degenerate intervals. Every row balance and the restricted penalty calculation hold. The diagonal mixed slack implies chord domination. The affine Farkas expansion includes a nonnegative constant and only original-space affine inequalities, so it does not inadvertently supply the clique cut.

3. **Fractional positivity and truncation.** The cardinality recurrence is valid up to squarefree multiplier degree s−1. Here 2d−1≤s−1, so no zero denominator is requested. Homogenization gives P−H=eQ with degree Q≤d−1 in the Boolean quotient; multiplying by P+H consumes precisely multiplier degree at most 2d−1. The homogeneous Gram entry identity follows by falling-factorial Vandermonde after multiplication by (s)_(2d), with no cancellation of a possibly zero numerator. Each incidence matrix is a Gram matrix and all coefficients are nonnegative in the stated range. For assignment indicators, the uncancelled weight is (t)_a(s−t)_b/(s)_(a+b). If the square is nonconstant, a+b≤2r−2 makes this weight strictly positive. Conditional positivity then follows from s−a−b≥2d, t−a≥2d−1, and s−t−b≥2d−1. Constant squares, zero weights, repeated/conflicting slacks, and complete assignments are separately covered. The negative-indicator argument correctly distinguishes the full preordering from stronger classical moment-matrix positivity.

Every auxiliary is defined and finite on the whole original interval. The affine endpoint substitution sends each lifted variable to degree at most one. Thus an assignment indicator of degree |J| times the substituted global square stays within |J|+2 degree Φ(P)≤2r. Local equality products reduce to zero.
