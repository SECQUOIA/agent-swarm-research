## Independent verification

The analytic checks below do not rely on prior audit labels.

- **Scalar envelopes and positive sums.** Independent endpoint sampling preserves every coordinate mean and every multiaffine value, proving equality of the graph hull with its vertex graph polytope. The failure intervals on the circle have the asserted union length even when their total length exceeds one. The shared threshold law then attains all positive upper envelopes simultaneously. Subtracting the affine anchor expectations gives the stated deficiency maximization. I interpret “discard supports of size at most one” as redefining the polynomial for this proposition; under that stated reduction its displayed upper-envelope identity is correct. The independence estimates cover the equality boundary at 1/2 as written.
- **Nonnegative boxes.** Each original factor expands into nonnegative unit-cube monomials. Summing their lower envelopes underestimates that factor and summing their upper envelopes overestimates it. Thus the inequality is `T_original <= T_expanded`, while the full H is invariant. Common upper attainment survives the expansion. The manuscript correctly warns that incidence structure can change.
- **Half-integral cells.** The active equations at a cell vertex connect coordinates through equality and complementation, or fix them at 0, 1/2, or 1. An unfixed component with no odd-complement cycle admits a sufficiently small two-sided perturbation, since inactive inequalities have positive slack. Therefore every cell vertex is half-integral. The function `T-cH` is convex on each cell, so its nonpositivity at cell vertices extends throughout that cell. Fixed-one coordinates contribute only affine terms on the free face. This supplies the full argument, including boundary points.
- **Constants.** Polarization gives precisely `R=max_S ||A_(S,S^c)||_(infinity->1)`, with no extra factor two. The squared-weight local cut gives crossing row sums at least the full row sum divided by sqrt(2); combining both Khinchin row estimates gives `R >= sum_i ||a_i||_2/4`. Fractional orientation then gives `L <= sqrt(rho) sum_i ||a_i||_2`. Counting each edge twice in the full row l1 sum yields the stronger maximum-degree constant 2. The bipartite proof counts each edge once, and the frustrated four-cycle attains its claimed constant. The distinct degeneracy argument is compressed but its outgoing-edge counting and coloring order are sufficient to reconstruct every step.
- **Fixed-graph lower bound and support.** The exponential sum has `2^h` terms after quotienting by global reversal and adding both exponential signs. Jensen and the selected lambda give exactly `sqrt(2mh log 2)`. Extending a signing off the chosen induced face preserves its witness. The whole-center supremum equality follows by zeroing off-face coefficients; compactness of `L=1` and positivity of R justify the full-support perturbation without requiring continuity of c* at vanished coefficients.
- **Exactness and interpretation.** Equality `R=L` requires the positive and negative edge sets to be cuts, equivalently even intersection with every cycle. The stated positive-coloring bound, complete-graph limiting value, nonempty-forest exactness, arboricity comparison, and global-average-density counterexample are consistent. The manuscript separates coefficient suprema from a fixed coefficient vector and separates finite extremizers from growing-density constants.
