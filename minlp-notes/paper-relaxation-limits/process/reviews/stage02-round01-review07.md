2. **Exact dyadic family and representation size.** In `thm:dyadic-exact`, selecting all anchors from upper tails is simultaneously feasible because only their singleton means constrain them. The distinct arbitrary-partition Jensen argument requires no such realization. The dense count LP has both a projection and a uniform-subset lifting, so it is exact for the dense model only. Counts of `2^(L+1)-2` monomials and `L*2^L+2^(L+1)-2` occurrences are correct; homogenization preserves the monomial count but can raise occurrences to quadratic order. The manuscript does not confuse these size measures.
