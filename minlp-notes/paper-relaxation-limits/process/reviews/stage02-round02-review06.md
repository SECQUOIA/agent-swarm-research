1. **Envelope geometry and the precise relaxation.** For a multiaffine function, independent endpoint interpolation puts each graph point in the hull of the vertex graph. A feasible law with fixed means therefore describes a vertical section of that same hull. The termwise model permits a different law for each factor, so its vertical width can exceed the width obtainable from a common law. All Stage 2 uses of `T` and `H` respect this distinction. Positive coefficients give common upper attainment; consequently `H` is a maximum of the weighted deficiencies, while `T` is the sum of separately attainable deficiencies. This establishes the direction used by every coupling proof.

   Positive expansion on a nonnegative box preserves the full scalar hull and gives `T_original <= T_expanded`. The paper uses that inequality in the correct direction for upper bounds and equality of degree/dimension suprema. It does not identify expansion with an unchanged original decomposition, or transfer the result to a recursive product relaxation on a positive box. Count-polynomial identities are restricted to binary vertices. The auxiliary continuous cubic minorants are used as valid inequalities for those counts, not as replacements for multilinear factors on the continuous cube.
