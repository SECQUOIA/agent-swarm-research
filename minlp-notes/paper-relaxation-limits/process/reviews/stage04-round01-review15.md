## Independent verification

1. **Optimum, chords, and counting.** Opposite perturbations exclude two fractional coordinates at a slice vertex. The half-integer balance then leaves k ones and one half, giving penalty 1/4. Convexity between interval endpoints gives domination by the chord even for singleton intervals. At a contained witness, only restricted middle coordinates can contribute; their individual penalty is strictly less than 1/(2m). This forces the stated endpoint-exclusion threshold. The two avoidance events need not be independent: either marginal bounds their intersection. Taking the larger marginal upper bound after the q/2 dichotomy yields the minimum of the reciprocal bases in `lem:endpoint-count`. Zero-sized endpoint classes and q=0 give the stated trivial bound. Overlap of regions does not affect the union bound.

2. **Upper bounds and discarded sets.** Each positive-tolerance coordinate step creates four nodes for each surviving low/high prefix, including the intermediate upper child. Summing gives `2^(n+2)-3` nodes and `2^(n+1)-1` leaves. The exact midpoint stopping thresholds are k+1 high intervals or n−k low intervals, giving `binomial(n+1,k+1)` leaves and the correct complementary exponent. The quadratic chord error follows by maximizing each interval product. The augmented-cover count is valid only with certified discarded sets, as expressly assumed. The tolerance-slab minimum is `1/4−delta^2` because the permitted sums remain strictly between k and k+1; the stated delta and positive-target restrictions are essential and present.

3. **Full SDP–RLT.** On free coordinates the covariance is `(c−d)(I−J/s)`, where `c−d=t(s−t)/(s(s−1))`. All deterministic restricted rows have zero covariance. This proves augmented PSD, including the constant row. The unrestricted off-diagonal slack expectations are d, c−d, c−d, and `(s−t)(s−t−1)/(s(s−1))`; repeated indices give c, 0, 0, 1−c. A restricted slack factors out as a nonnegative constant. The row balances and objective therefore hold in every case. The diagonal mixed product gives chord domination. The original-space affine Farkas expansion is valid on degenerate nonempty polytopes and includes a nonnegative constant; its degree-bounded balance terms vanish. It does not imply availability of lifted clique cuts.

4. **Fractional moments and positivity.** For a squarefree multiplier of size a, the cardinality recurrence is `a m_a+(s−a)m_(a+1)=t m_a`, with a≤2d−1≤s−1. Homogenization divides by a strictly positive binomial coefficient. The difference of the monomial and its homogeneous replacement is the balance times a polynomial of degree at most d−1 in the Boolean quotient. Multiplication by P+H consequently stays within the proved equality range. In the homogeneous Gram identity, factoring `(t)_(2d−ell)` is multiplication, not division; Vandermonde remains valid at zero numerator factors. Each incidence matrix is a Gram matrix, and every coefficient is nonnegative under the stated sufficient conditions.

5. **All assignment localizers.** Inclusion-exclusion gives the uncancelled indicator moment `(t)_(a+c)(s−t)_b/(s)_(a+b+c)`. Repeated slacks collapse and contradictory assignments vanish. A nonconstant square multiplier leaves at most 2r−2 assigned coordinates, making its indicator weight strictly positive. After conditioning, `s−v≥2d` and both residual cardinalities are at least 2d−1. Constant, zero-weight, and all-assigned cases require no undefined conditional functional. The argument applies to any coefficient vector of the square polynomial, including globally coupled ones. The negative product with `floor(t)+2` distinct factors also confirms why stronger moment-matrix positivity alone cannot remove the full-preordering restriction at noninteger t.

6. **Order and perturbations.** Integrality of |R| converts `|R|<k−2r+2` into at least 2r−1 surviving unit witness coordinates, and similarly for zeros. The r=1 dimension boundary is handled separately. The constructed objective is strictly below the non-strict target when |R|<q_r, including R empty. This gives exactly the printed q_r and the regimes `r≤3t/8+1` and, for the explicit perturbation, `r≤13t/32+1`. The strict-concavity and sorted exchange arguments prove uniqueness; the separate mass-transfer argument correctly proves that the same point minimizes the linear cost over the whole slice. First moments are within [0,1], so the perturbation contribution is at most eta. Adding the conserved sum preserves absolute tolerance but does not preserve a fixed relative obstruction in a growing single block.

7. **Arbitrary domains and degree-free lifts.** On an unrestricted coordinate only endpoint membership is needed. Each local polynomial reduces to its two endpoint values, with nonnegative coefficients for a valid inequality. After grouping repeated factors, every active Boolean coordinate consumes at least one original lifted degree. Hence the indicator degree plus twice the substituted square degree stays at most 2r. Restricted coordinates are evaluated at genuine witness graph tuples. In the lifted construction every variable has an affine or constant image, irrespective of the defining function's degree or regularity. Original affine balances therefore retain their multiplier budget. Local equalities have zero Boolean reduction, as do their allowed global multiples. More generally full-graph identities vanish on every substituted Boolean assignment. Full-graph objective agreement, not just feasible-graph agreement, is what identifies the objective reductions at those assignments. Both objectives remain within degree 2r. These observations prove the advertised absence of order loss; they do not assert that a nonlinear graph equals its endpoint chord continuously. Finite values on all of [0,1], retained originals, exact preimages, and independent local restrictions are explicitly required. Literal polynomial substitution separately gives the valid coarser rD estimate.

8. **Global tensor and relative estimate.** For each fixed product of local constraints and square degree d, each block's full degree-d localizing matrix is available because `deg(g_b)+2d≤2r`. Tensoring those PSD matrices and selecting indices with global total degree at most d produces the required global localizing matrix. Entries outside that principal submatrix need no interpretation as available global moments. This proves positivity for all globally coupled squares, rather than only products of block squares. Balance and local equality products factor monomial by monomial. Light blocks use fractional moments; heavy blocks use true witness evaluation. Their common penalty bound is `|R_b|/(2q_0)`. Rearranging the certified objective inequality gives `sum_b |R_b|≥2q_0 G tau`; independence of block witness draws then yields the exact exponent `q_0 G tau`. The example gives tau=17/128 and exponent 17n/384. For graph lifts, the same affine substitutions and total-degree tensor argument apply. Full-graph agreement also identifies a written objective that couples blocks with the intended separable objective after Boolean reduction.
