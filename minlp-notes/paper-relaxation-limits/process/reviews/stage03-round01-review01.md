- **Radix attainment and structure (`thm:variable-radix` and following generalization).** For general cutoff `s`, the integrated cap certificate is `s+(L-s)b^(-s)`. - **Marginal floor and simultaneous asymptotic (`thm:marginal-floor`, `thm:joint-growth`).** The clipped probability integrates to at most the target marginal, and its residual completion is in `[0,1]` and integrates exactly to that marginal.
