## Independent verification

1. **Foundations and signed bilinear bounds.** In `sections/01-foundations.tex`, I checked the vertex-law reduction, the distinction between termwise and joint distributions, and the common-upper-envelope identity for positive monomials. The nonnegative-box expansion only supplies the claimed inequality for the original termwise gap; equality with the gap of the expanded representation is not silently assumed. For `lem:induced-cut`, `lem:cut-row`, `lem:fractional-orientation`, and `thm:graph-density`, I reconstructed the cut localization, polarization, row estimates, fractional ownership argument, and random-sign union bound. The induced-density parameter is the right one for the flow allocation. The center and worst-coefficient quantifiers are kept separate. The Schur-multiplier discussion identifies an antecedent route rather than claiming the order of growth as a new discovery.

2. **Universal positive growth and cubic refinements.** In `sections/02-universal-positive.tex`, the dyadic construction's level-capacity certificate and nested realization agree. The harmonic mixture defines one global law with the required marginals, so summing the termwise guarantees is legitimate. I checked the finite optimization and Lambert-bound direction, and the cloning argument's uniform control over all vertices. The manuscript claims a sharp leading term, not a matching second-order lower expansion. In `sections/03-cubic-equal-means.tex` and its two supporting appendices, I checked the three-law cases, scalar certificate, finite affine majorants, analytic family, and two-level family. I checked the roles of the Bernstein lower bound `901/120000` and the stated Hessian bound, and the resulting strict improvement. The equal-mean formula follows from adjacent-cardinality interpolation and a common upper law; it is not extrapolated to unequal means.

3. **Structural parameters and compatible coupling.** In `sections/04-incidence-interiority.tex`, ownership determines compatible endpoint prescriptions because each variable is assigned at most once. The variable-radix capacity proof, incidence-minor lower bound, marginal-floor completion, and joint asymptotic use their stated ranges. I distinguished the exact simplified cutoff under the stronger radix hypothesis from the general cutoff handled in `appendix-structural-auxiliary.tex`. In `sections/05-feedback-frequency.tex`, the feedback-law domination is by `2^f`; the residual forest can then be glued with the shared marginals. The degree-slab extreme-point argument leaves the stated odd cycles, not arbitrary fractional components. Convex cardinality costs, edge-cover reduction, mandatory-slot matching bonus, and scalar LP bit-complexity claims fit their respective hypotheses.

4. **Incidence treewidth two and positive physical boxes.** I checked the series-parallel coloring invariant and its composition cases in `sections/06-treewidth-two.tex`, including why the condition on all cycles gives the required total-unimodularity conclusion. Merely asserting balancedness would not suffice; the manuscript supplies the stronger argument. The factor-two sharpness construction and the separate width-three obstruction have different scopes. In `sections/07-positive-boxes.tex` and `appendix-positive-box-predecessors.tex`, I checked the finite coefficient-spreading move at a fixed ambient dimension, the physical-box normalization, the coefficient-regularity improvement, and the softened lower construction. The restricted-law lower benchmark is not stated as an unrestricted optimality result. The rational bipartite example gives `7/6`, and its limiting family approaches `3/2`; the unresolved general unequal-aspect frequency-two upper bound remains open.

5. **Exact envelope complexity.** For `sections/08-exact-complexity.tex`, I checked the partition reduction at the printed perturbation scale `epsilon = 1/(16 K A^3)`, including the strict separation between the two remainder estimates. The certificate support can be limited to `n+1` vertices with rational weights of polynomial bit length. The hard case has unequal positive interval ratios; it does not contradict the fixed-number-of-ratio-types algorithm. The rank-one transport consequence concerns precision polynomial in `log(1/epsilon)`, which is stronger than the inverse-accuracy dependence in the cited approximation theorem.

6. **Cardinality spatial certificates.** In `sections/09-cardinality-spatial.tex`, the optimum is `1/4`, the fractional endpoint count produces the stated node witness, and the tolerance hypothesis excludes the boundary where that witness would no longer separate. The leaf count and upper construction charge the same resource. In `sections/10-cardinality-preordering.tex`, I reconstructed the fractional-cardinality moment functional and Gram decomposition. The falling-factorial weights stay nonnegative at the stated order, including zero boundary weights. Assignment substitution respects total degree. The proof supports the full truncated preordering, not merely the basic moment matrix.

7. **Product domains, local graph lifts and relative blocks.** In `sections/11-coordinate-domains-lifts.tex`, interpolation on each local two-point set makes every lifted coordinate affine in the original endpoint variables. Thus the stated local graph-lift transfer has no extra monomial-degree loss, while the retained original objective and the definition of the local hull remain essential.
