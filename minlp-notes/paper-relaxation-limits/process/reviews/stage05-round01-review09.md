**Independent verification.**

1. **Affine first/second moments and witness mass: section 15, lines 125–145.** Uniform signs have mean sum zero and second moment of the sum equal to `n`. Any actual law supported on `S>=0` with zero mean has `S=0` almost surely. Thus it cannot realize the prescribed second moment, including at `n=1`. Equivalently, the normalized affine coordinate has moments `0,1/n`, violating the valid `[0,1]` inequality `z-z²>=0`. The Boolean halfspace mass is exactly `1/2` for odd `n`, and `1/2 + binom(n,n/2)/2^(n+1)` for even `n`. This statement concerns the retained moments, not impossibility of constructing a different functional.

2. **Affine substitution necessity: section 15, lines 147–186.** Suppose `t<min(r-1,ceil(n/2))`. For both parities of `n`, `n-t>=t+1`. A negative fixed sum `a` is detected by the constant square. For `a>=0`, the indicator of `a+1` selected negative free signs exists and has degree at most `r-1`; the unreduced localizer degree is at most `2r-1`. Direct expectation is `-2^(-a-1)`. This proves a necessary bound only. In particular, satisfying the displayed numerical bound does not assert that all halfspace localizers hold. At `r=1` the bound is vacuous, as the text says. Linear order consequently forces linear substitution cost for this construction despite at least half the witnesses remaining. No affine-tree upper bound for the XOR objective follows.

3. **Unlifted local oracle: section 13, lines 67–156.** For a witness-containing coordinate product, fix every coordinate excluding an endpoint. This is deterministic substitution followed by the untouched marginal, and needs no positive pseudo-mass for the fixed signs. Every univariate factor on an untouched coordinate reduces to a nonnegative combination of its two endpoint indicators. If the total generator degree is `a` and square-polynomial degree is `d`, `a+2d<=2r` gives indicator-times-polynomial degree at most `a+d<=2r`. Its square fits degree `4r`. Repeated, opposite and overlapping factors satisfy the same idempotence calculation. Equalities vanish at all used endpoints or fixed witnesses. Marginalizing the actual degree-two law and adjoining fixed coordinates matches every cross moment and has support in the actual node, even when the sets are nonclosed. Changed clause expectations lie in `[0,1]`; untouched ones remain zero. Thus `LB<=Delta|R|/m` and the witness fraction is exactly `2^(-|R|)`. The reciprocal union bound applies to arbitrary finite covers, including overlapping regions. The earlier certificate convention correctly charges discarded feasible regions.

4. **Signed realization and width: section 13, lines 159–243.** Unit Gram vectors with entries in `{0,+1,-1}` divide into orthogonal signed classes. Fixing the constant class to one and sampling independent unbiased signs for the other classes reproduces the augmented matrix. For the width construction, every transitivity or orientation product has endpoint symmetric difference of size at most `2 floor(w/2)<=w`; conflicting signs yield a contradiction. Hence the signed character matrix is a Gram matrix for arbitrary polynomial squares, not just functions on one small common variable set. Moments at odd top degree are defined directly by the closure rule. The required even source degrees `4r` and `4rD` therefore fit precisely as stated.

5. **Random family and constants: section 13, lines 245–333.** Independent clause signs give `Binomial(8n,1/2)` violations at every fixed assignment. The exponential moment calculation at `t=1` gives `exp(-n)`; multiplication by `2^n` tends to zero. The occurrence generating function gives `E[D_i 2^D_i]=48(1+3/n)^(8n-1)`. The displayed deletion estimate follows, and `384*3^24<2^64` verifies its last strict inequality without a floating-point approximation. The deletion event has failure probability below `1/8`, so it intersects both events of probability `1-o(1)` for every sufficiently large `n`; no independence is required. Retaining at least `7n` clauses and at least `n` violations gives `OPT>=1/8`. Both best-incumbent targets are at least `1/16`, yielding exponent `7n/1024`. One source functional supplies all smaller admitted orders. Derivative and Hessian bounds count one first derivative and two mixed derivatives per incidence, with the stated normalization.

6. **Lifted transfer, including order one: section 14, lines 7–168.** Every restricted monomial becomes its witnessed sign after fixing the union `C` of its original support. Literal pullback defines the functional through degree `2r` using degree `2rD`. An allowed localizer reduces to a nonnegative sum of parity-indicator products times squares. Its auxiliary square has degree at most `4rD`, including repeated or dependent supports. Every graph identity vanishes as an ordinary polynomial after pullback, and its allowed multiplied pullback has degree at most `2rD`. Lifted linear squares pull back to degree at most `D`; all first/second entries are signed characters of degree at most `2D`. The signed realization lemma therefore applies, and restricted means force support inside the lifted coordinate product. The realizing law is not assumed to satisfy the graph equations pointwise. The affected-clause argument is available because `rD>=2` gives square-polynomial capacity `2rD>=4>=3`. No independent step requires `r>=2`; the original cubic objective's availability is the separate unlifted restriction.

7. **Rank, dimension and order: section 14, lines 139–228.** A column absent from a basis of the restricted parity rows is absent from their span, so the basis supports have exactly the full support union. Thus `|C|<=D rank(A_R)`. A witnessed affine binary system is consistent and has fraction `2^(-rank(A_R))`, giving the claimed `2^(mT_*/(Delta D))` count without an auxiliary occurrence assumption. Retained originals make the witnesses distinct. The two equations per clause define the exact continuous pair/clause graph, with `D=3`, `N=n+2m<=17n`, source degree `12r`, exponent `7n/3072`, and dimension exponent `7N/52224`. The polynomial-region consequence is necessary only and stays within `4rD<=an`; arbitrary inflated `N` is not substituted for original `n`.

8. **Both upper certificates: section 15, lines 9–118.** For every positive epsilon, `M=ceil(3/epsilon)>=1` and width `h=2/M` satisfies `3h/2<=epsilon`, including `M=1`. Each clause's eight-corner interpolation gives nonnegative coefficients times three bound slacks. Order two supplies those cubic expressions, and the stated cubic graph identity transfers them to the linear objective. Independently, the full-box degree-two law and the two graph moment equations bound the error in `L[v]` by `h+2h`. The explicit `q_e` is a genuinely quadratic valid full-box polynomial, and its identity uses only constant equality multipliers. Thus order one is sufficient without a cubic moment or graph-supported law. Averaging gives `LB>=OPT-epsilon`, even if a node feasible-functional set were empty. The `48^n` count and the relative-tolerance implication follow.
