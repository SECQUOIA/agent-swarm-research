Summing capped masses across rounds gives the required incoming bound. No unproved simultaneous per-term degree guarantee is needed. The zero-outdegree and zero-gap cases are covered.

2. **Radix, width, and joint parameters.** Reconstructing the quantile bound gives the integrated constant `(L-s)b^(-s)`. Both count profiles attain the pointwise affine certificate, including levels below and at the cutoff. The digit-reversal prefixes give exactly `min(b^j,R)` hits for composite as well as prime bases; the digit shifts are transitive on leaves. Deleting deepest factors before leaves gives degeneracy `L-1`, while the stated orientation requires `L>=3`. The corollary restricts its stronger claim accordingly. In the joint law, `L<=phi(q)` implies `b>=(1-o(1))log q`, so `L/b` vanishes uniformly and the same example meets all four restrictions.

3. **Marginal floor.** The completion coefficient is in `[0,1]` and integrates the clipped probabilities to exactly the intended marginal.
