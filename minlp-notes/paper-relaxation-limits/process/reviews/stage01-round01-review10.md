The manuscript's explicit decomposition convention correctly excludes applying its simple-graph comparison to that uncombined decomposition. Adding affine terms shifts both envelopes equally. Independent endpoint sampling represents every multiaffine graph point by vertex graph points; the finite vertex polytope supplies attained envelopes, including boundary continuity. The consecutive failure-interval construction has the required union measure both below and above total length one. The common threshold law uses one complete mean vector, and the deficiency identity correctly subtracts a fixed common upper value. Zero anchor means and zero deficiencies create no division. 2. **Nonnegative boxes and fixed coordinates.** Positive affine expansion preserves nonnegative coefficients and degree. Its termwise gap is at least the original termwise gap, whereas the whole hull gap is equal under the bijection. This direction is essential and correctly stated. Common upper attainment follows term by term from the expanded common threshold law. Fixing coordinates in a nonnegative product either removes a factor or leaves a nonnegative lower-degree factor. For signed bilinear functions, fixed-coordinate terms become affine and free-coordinate coefficients scale by positive products of interval widths. Thus no support-zero or fixed-coordinate obstruction was found.

3. **Half-valued sufficiency reconstructed.** At a point with half-valued coordinate set W, fixed zeros remove cross terms and fixed ones add affine terms. Mixing a maximizing or minimizing sign vector with its global negative satisfies all active means and gives `H=R_W/2`, `T=L_W/2`. Every cell vertex of the stated arrangement is half-valued: an unanchored relation component with consistent equality/complementation equations admits a nonzero infinitesimal motion; an inconsistent cycle forces one-half. Since `H` is concave and `T` is affine on each cell, `T-cH` is convex for `c>=0`. Its grid-vertex bounds therefore extend to the full closed cell. This also handles points where H vanishes, without taking a ratio there.

4. **Norm constants reconstructed.** With `Q(s)=s^T A s/2`, polarization gives `Q(s)-Q(t)=2v^T A u`, so half the oscillation equals the maximum complementary-block infinity-to-one norm.
