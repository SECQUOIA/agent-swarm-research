**Findings.**

1. **R07-01 — MINOR: qualify the best-incumbent statement for relative certificates.** Location: frozen `sections/01-foundations.tex`, lines 180–183, subsection “What a spatial certificate counts.” The implication from a target `(1-theta)U` to `(U-f*)/U <= theta` is correct for `U > 0`. The following sentence, however, says without qualification that worse incumbents require weakly higher targets and that the best incumbent `U=f*` may be granted. Monotonicity of the relative target requires `theta <= 1`; for example, with `theta=2`, changing `U` from 1 to 2 lowers the target from -1 to -2. Also, the stated normalized relative gap is undefined at `U=f*=0`, and its required positivity fails if `f*<0`, both allowed by the opening assumptions. Repair: specify the intended tolerance range, for example `epsilon >= 0` and `0 <= theta <= 1`, and say that the best incumbent can be granted for absolute certificates, and for these relative certificates when `f*>0`. This is a local clarification: no Stage 1 spatial lower-bound theorem depends on the unrestricted sentence.

- **Constants and flow.** The exact identity is `Q(s)-Q(t)=2 v^T A u`, so the factor in the rectangular cut formula is correct.
