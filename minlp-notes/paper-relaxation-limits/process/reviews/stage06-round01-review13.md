2. **Cubic and finite signing certificates.** I read the count-polynomial derivations and marginal-attainment argument before executing the complete programs printed in the two appendices. 3. **Structural and positive-box arguments.** I checked that the frequency-two proof preserves the coverage baseline involving the largest marginal, rather than substituting a different generic coverage ratio. The half-integral slab and odd-cycle/matching arguments handle parallel incidence edges. 4. **Exact complexity and spatial lower bounds.** The PARTITION construction's separation is between the stated `b+epsilon^2/8` and `b+epsilon^2/2` bounds, allowing threshold `b+epsilon^2/4`; it is not a fixed-accuracy hardness claim. Indicator localizers condition onto the stated admissible range. Tensor-product arguments use principal moment submatrices and therefore cover globally coupled squares of the permitted degree. 5. **Lifts, XOR, and certificate boundaries.** Coordinatewise endpoint interpolation controls lifted polynomial degree without requiring a bound on the original univariate graph function's polynomial degree. I checked the signed-character positive-semidefinite construction, the constant class, and the source expansion parameters against the Schoenebeck passages. The finite-region and integer-comparison arguments do not license stronger arbitrary cuts or affine branching.

6. **Point packing.** Independently summing pair distances in one coordinate gives `k tr(X)-e^T X e <= k^2 L^2/4`, hence the pair-average bound `k L^2/[2(k-1)]`. Adding coordinates gives the two SDP upper bounds. The proposed covariance blocks are PSD analytically because each is a nonnegative multiple of an orthogonal projection or a nonnegative diagonal block. My checker reconstructs all four feasible constructions for `2<=n<=24`, where applicable, and verifies all four rational RLT inequalities, including repeated indices, and the minimum pair distance. All 86 cases pass.

7. **Scaling and P-split.** I reconstructed the scale-dependent-cost counterexample with value one versus the erroneous envelope value zero. The extensive perspective formula uses the correct size-biased law; it does not silently replace it by independent scale and intensive laws. For rectangularity, mixing zero, interior, and maximum scales propagates the interior fibre toward the full intensive projection. The Shapley–Folkman application leaves at most the ambient dimension many exceptional summands. For P-split, the retained box `[0,3] x [-1,1]` has all four vertices in the union; the shared auxiliary interpolation gives feasibility throughout its convex hull. Strict Jensen in one coordinate does not imply strict Jensen in every auxiliary coordinate, and a perturbation is not guaranteed to remain in the retained domain. I also checked the truncated-square auxiliary hull, downward substitution, monotonicity locating the translated-ball distance, the limit `sqrt(2)-1`, the rational orthogonal rotation, and the concave decreasing auxiliary-image boundary used for the rotated capsule description.

8. **Rank-one face and approximation.** For a nonzero rank-one generator, `W=rc^T/S` and trace one force Boolean equal marginals. The three successive faces select exactly one member of each pair, giving the displayed correlation-polytope inverse. The exposure estimate `S<=m+2mB+4mD` implies `g>=B+D`. Rounding and repairing pairs gives the intermediate bound `4SB+58SD+5|S-m|`, hence `136m+10`; retaining the signed total deviation before averaging gives the stronger section constant `184m+6`, and block scaling gives `A_m=m(184m+6)`. My checker constructs and checks 1,124 rational-grid generators for `m=1,2` as a finite falsification attempt. Independently substituting the LRS pseudo-density bounds gives the bracket power `k^{-13/2}` and prefactor `k^{-23/4}`; choosing `k` proportional to `(m/log m)^{2/13}` yields the approximate bound, distinct from the exact `2^{Omega(m^{2/13})}` bound. Minimal-face reduction and the extra scalar factor address potentially improper PSD lifts.

9. **Primitive FBBT and integer comparisons.** Fairness suffices for eventual domination of each finite Kleene iterate; no bounded-delay assumption is needed. I followed the positive/negative circuit normalization and detector through the Etessami–Yannakakis source and the manuscript's direct contractor realization.
