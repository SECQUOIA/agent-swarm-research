# Instructions for an independent manuscript reviewer

Read the entire assigned frozen stage, its mathematical dependencies, and the relevant primary sources. Your focus area is additional emphasis, not permission to ignore the rest. Existing repository reviews are context, not proof. Do not read other reports from this review round before submitting yours.

Review theorem truth, proof completeness, constants, quantifiers, endpoint and degenerate cases, computational claims, attribution, scope coverage, and clarity. Try to falsify central claims. Distinguish a demonstrable defect from an unsupported suspicion or a request for a different research project. Follow source attribution and local literature instructions.

Do not edit manuscript files. Write only your assigned review report, and any independent checker under your assigned verification path. Record whether each check is exact or numerical and what it does and does not verify. Do not claim a finite computation establishes a universal theorem.

Use this report structure:

- **Verdict:** PASS, MINOR, or MAJOR.
- **Coverage:** exact manuscript files and dependencies read; any inaccessible source or unverified dependency.
- **Findings:** numbered IDs with severity, a precise location, the mathematical or editorial reason, and a concrete repair when available. Do not invent findings to meet a quota.
- **Independent verification:** enough derivation or check evidence to make the review assessable.
- **Remaining limits:** open mathematical questions or external verification limits; these do not automatically make a correctly scoped paper defective.

A major finding affects truth, proof completeness, necessary assumptions, a central attribution, required scope coverage, or the reader's ability to verify a main argument. A minor finding is local and does not affect those conclusions. The coordinator makes the final severity and repair decisions on substance.

Send a concise final message with verdict, major/minor finding IDs, and the path to your report. If a major defect is discovered before the review is finished, notify the coordinator promptly and then complete the review.
