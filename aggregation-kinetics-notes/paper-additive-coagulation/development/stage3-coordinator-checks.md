# Coordinator checks during Stage 3 authorship

These checks supplement, and do not replace, the required five reviews after authorship.

The controlled first-event construction has the correct normalization. A fragmentation-only path started from normalized size q has mean q exp(-remaining coagulation activity). Its integrated killing hazard has mean q times 1-exp(-remaining coagulation activity), including an infinite horizon by Tonelli. Parent dependence does not invalidate the mean calculation because the retained daughter's conditional mean is one half. The future-path coupling must continue the comparison process using its own state-dependent daughter rule after divergence.

For constant rates, both benchmark hazards have mean one. The generator inequalities follow in the correct directions from convex Jensen and the endpoint chord bound. Finite-horizon normalized sizes are deterministically bounded, so the killed-test compensation is justified without second moments. The terminal test error is bounded by q exp(-bt), which is enough to pass to the infinite horizon even when the normalized fragmentation-only path is not monotone.

The unequal-split limit has expected post-small-daughter hazard q epsilon sigma/(b+sigma epsilon), tending to zero. Together with the common-clock dominated convergence before that daughter, this proves the claimed L1 hazard limit within positive daughter laws. The general overlap coefficient 1-w_ext(1), not just its critical specialization, belongs in the manuscript.

For the equal-split exact tail, q Psi(q+v) is bounded by one. Total-variation continuity of the accepted solution therefore gives continuity of its pair expectation and an everywhere derivative in the homogeneous equal-split case. The general parent/time-dependent last-event density should be justified in integrated form by conditioning at event times, with an almost-everywhere derivative.
