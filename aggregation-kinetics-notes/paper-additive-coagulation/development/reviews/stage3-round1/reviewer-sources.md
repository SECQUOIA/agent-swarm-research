**S3-SRC-001 — MINOR — the finite total count conclusion concerns coagulations.**

- Location: `sections/last-event.tex:95`, final sentence of the proof of `thm:last-controlled`.
- Finding: “Finite-horizon nonexplosion now implies a finite total event count” is too broad. The preceding limit controls the last coagulation time. Fragmentation events can remain infinite in number over the whole half-line.
- Evidence: Choose positive constant fragmentation rate, including the critical case. The fragmentation clock then has infinite total intensity and infinitely many fragmentation events almost surely. Since every daughter is strictly smaller than its parent, these are actual jumps. This is compatible with the proved finite last coagulation and contradicts a literal claim about the total count of both event types. The theorem statement correctly limits its conclusion to `K_infinity`.
- Proposed correction: Replace “a finite total event count” with “a finite total coagulation count.” No change to the theorem or its argument is required.
