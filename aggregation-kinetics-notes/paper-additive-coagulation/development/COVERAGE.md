# Manuscript coverage and stage plan

This map is the scope contract for the paper. Existing notes are evidence to examine, not authorities to cite in proofs. Each manuscript claim needs a complete proof or a checked external theorem. Earlier independent reviews remain useful, but do not replace the five manuscript reviews required at each stage.

The paper's central topic is additive coagulation–fragmentation: separation of number and mass sampling, finite nonlinear log-size corrections, last-event behavior, sharp daughter-law comparisons, and finite-population implications. The inverse Fourier results are included as an application of this theory. Unrelated extinction and coarse-graining investigations are excluded.

## Exclusions and residual questions

The extinction coupling, near-critical extinction, Perron tie-breaking, coarse-graining, and continuous-semigroup dimension work form other topics and are excluded. Their novelty corrections remain preserved in the repository.

The exact calendar-time exponent of the last-event tail is not established. The paper can completely state and prove its upper/lower bounds and its obstruction to a scalar closure without resolving that distinct asymptotic problem. Stable recovery of an entire daughter law from noisy local frequencies is likewise outside the proved identification application. These boundaries must be explained accurately; the manuscript must not present them as solved.

General-parent existence and nonlinear uniqueness, formerly open in the repository's final report, are addressed by the stage 1 proof under a finite initial second-moment hypothesis. This development beyond transcription has completed five independent manuscript reviews with zero accepted major issues. All accepted minor corrections were verified by the coordinator; stage 1 is accepted.
