## Proof and notation decisions

The new log-characteristic function is `chi_t`, distinct from the accepted raw-size Laplace `phi_t`. The limiting amplitude is `C_infty(k)`, distinct from the controlled clock `C(t)`. The sampling attenuation is `d`, distinct from the earlier Poisson rate `r=2 sigma`; the supporting initial-rate linear term is `beta`, distinct from `b=lambda m`.

The Fourier source follows from the accepted bounded-test normalized log equation, applied to real and imaginary parts. Its paired Lipschitz increment is bounded by `|k| lambda H(t)^2/N(t)`, hence `|k| a0 exp(-omega t)`. No separate unbounded logarithmic source moment is used. Variation of constants gives a physical remainder `exp(-omega t)` and a rescaled remainder `exp(-delta(k)t)`. Uniform convergence of continuous finite-time functions proves amplitude continuity without assuming smoothness of a source representative. The amplitude has no asserted characteristic-function or independent-shift interpretation.

The quotient condition is checked before division and the phase is fixed on a neighborhood of zero. The one-sided proof places the transform in the lower half-plane, subtracts the total signed jump mass at zero, uses Schwarz reflection and the identity theorem, then restricts the resulting zero measure to the open negative half-line. This explains both the zero-selection and strictly-smaller-daughter boundaries. The uniqueness statement compares candidate models on the intersection of their local intervals. It uses exact frequency continua and allows an unknown initial distribution.

The instability example preserves both daughter constraints: nearby complementary atomic laws have uniformly close exponents on bounded real-frequency intervals, but the full signed variation norm of their difference is four. This is not the probability supremum-set TV distance. The complex identity `psi(-i)=-sigma` is separated explicitly from noisy analytic continuation.

The sampling proof checks Hoeffding's four-component constant and both exact quotient identities. The directly observed denominator condition is strict. The bias/noise bound uses the bounded exact ratio to avoid an unnecessary second attenuation factor. The schedule is predetermined and requires the relevant unknown scale to be supplied; at fixed confidence it gives a sufficient upper rate, not minimax optimality, adaptation, or full-measure recovery. The zero-attenuation case remains covered.

For preparation shells, the positivity/interior, full row rank, and nonzero constraint vector hypotheses are used in the proof. The finite-grid theorem does not imply a continuum converse under two constraints. Full-trajectory count neutrality in the sufficient continuum family is conditional on a valid solution and a locally integrable scalar coefficient, ensured by the stated bounded coefficient functions. Unknown-source noise propagation is included as a direct extension of the supplied formulas. The finite-difference minimizing time is asserted only for positive error and curvature, and only inside the interval where the bound is valid.
